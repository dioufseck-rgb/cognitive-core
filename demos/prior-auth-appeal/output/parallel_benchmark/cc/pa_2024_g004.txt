Dear Ms. Oduya,

This letter serves as the determination for your appeal regarding the prior authorization denial for Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 (CPT 22551), requested by Dr. Patrick Walsh. After a comprehensive review of your clinical record, HealthFirst's clinical coverage criteria, relevant California regulatory provisions, and generally accepted clinical guidelines, the initial denial is **UPHELD**.

**Summary of Clinical Information and Initial Denial:**
Your presenting diagnosis is cervical radiculopathy C5-C6, right-sided (Clinical Record, presenting_diagnosis). Imaging from 2024-09-10 shows mild to moderate disc herniation at C5-C6 with right C6 nerve root contact, but no cord compression or myelomalacia (Clinical Record, imaging.mri_1.findings). Your neurological examination on 2024-09-15 revealed mild right C6 dermatomal tingling but no weakness, no upper motor neuron signs, and full functional capacity, allowing you to work full-time without activity limitations (Clinical Record, neurological_exam.findings). The initial denial, issued on 2024-10-15 by Dr. Maria Rivera, cited that imaging did not show cord compression or neurological deficit, and that the conservative treatment pathway had not been completed, thus not meeting medical necessity criteria for surgical intervention at this time (Denial Info, reason_text).

**Rationale for Upholding the Denial:**

1.  **Failure to Meet HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria:**
    HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria for Spinal Surgery — Cervical Procedures (Document: CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2) requires completion of ALL of the following conservative treatment components prior to surgical authorization:
    *   **Physical Therapy:** A minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(a)). Your record indicates completion of 4 weeks of physical therapy (Clinical Record, conservative_treatment.physical_therapy_summary), which does not meet this requirement.
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** A trial of at least two pharmacotherapy agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(b)). Your record documents the use of Naproxen 500mg BID for 8 weeks (Clinical Record, conservative_treatment.pharmacotherapy), which is only one agent and therefore does not meet the requirement for two agents.
    *   **Interventional Treatment:** Completion of one interventional treatment (e.g., cervical epidural steroid injection) (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(c)). Your record indicates no interventional treatment was completed (Clinical Record, conservative_treatment.interventional).
    The myelopathy exception to conservative treatment (CC-SPINE-2024, Exception 2.2(A)) does not apply, as your primary diagnosis is cervical radiculopathy, not myelopathy (Classify Clinical Presentation, radiculopathy_primary).

2.  **Consistency with Generally Accepted Clinical Standards:**
    Generally accepted clinical guidelines, such as the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for Management of Degenerative Cervical Myelopathy (2023 Update), do not support surgical intervention at this time based on your current clinical presentation (Clinical Evidence Document, Section 3.1).
    *   Your condition is classified as cervical radiculopathy, not myelopathy (Clinical Evidence Document, Section 1.1). Objective findings from your MRI (no cord compression, no myelomalacia) and neurological exam (no weakness, no UMN signs, working full-time) do not meet the criteria for Tier 1 (Strong Indication) for surgery (Clinical Evidence Document, Section 3.1, Tier 1(a,c,d)).
    *   While radiculopathy can be a Tier 2 (Reasonable Indication) for surgery, this typically applies after 6-12 weeks of persistent symptoms following an adequate conservative trial (Clinical Evidence Document, Section 3.1, Tier 2(b)). As noted above, your conservative treatment pathway is incomplete.

3.  **Regulatory Compliance of Denial Notice:**
    The appeal raised concerns regarding the procedural adequacy of the denial notice. However, review of the denial information confirms that the notice provided a specific clinical reason for the denial, stating: "imaging shows mild-moderate disc herniation with no cord compression and no neurological deficit on examination. Conservative treatment pathway not completed. Request does not meet medical necessity criteria for surgical intervention at this time." (Denial Info, reason_text). This satisfies the requirement of California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b), which mandates that a denial notice specify either the clinical criteria applied *or* the specific clinical reason for the denial. Therefore, the denial notice is procedurally compliant.

4.  **Inapplicability of Regulatory Waivers for Conservative Treatment:**
    *   Your condition does not meet the criteria for an urgent myelopathy condition under DMHC All-Plan Letter APL 22-014, as there is no objective evidence of cord compression with myelomalacia or progressive pathology (Investigate Regulatory Compliance, no_myelopathy_urgency; Clinical Record, imaging.mri_1.findings).
    *   The requirements for a waiver of physical therapy under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 are not met. There is no documentation from your treating physician that physical therapy is contraindicated, that you reached a functional plateau without adequate symptom resolution, or that your condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address (Investigate Regulatory Compliance, no_functional_plateau_documented; Clinical Record, conservative_treatment.physical_therapy_summary.note).

**Conclusion:**
Based on the comprehensive review, your request for ACDF C5-C6 does not meet HealthFirst's medical necessity criteria, nor is it supported by generally accepted clinical standards for your current presentation. The denial notice was procedurally compliant. Therefore, the prior authorization denial is upheld.
