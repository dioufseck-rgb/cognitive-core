Dear Ms. White,

This letter serves as the determination of your appeal regarding the prior authorization denial for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551), requested by Dr. Paul Lee, MD, at UCLA Medical Center.

**Summary of Original Denial:**
The initial prior authorization request was denied on October 5, 2024, with the reason cited as: "Does not meet criteria. Conservative treatment incomplete. Physical therapy and medication requirements not satisfied." (Denial Info).

**Your Basis for Appeal:**
Your appeal highlighted several concerns, specifically that the denial notice: (1) did not specify which criteria were not met, (2) did not include an Independent Medical Review (IMR) notice as required by California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31, and (3) prevented a meaningful appeal due to the lack of specific information (Appeal Basis).

**Review Findings:**
Our review considered your clinical record, HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria (Document: CC-SPINE-2024), the California Regulatory Framework, and generally accepted clinical evidence.

**1. Application of HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1):** Your clinical record indicates a presenting diagnosis of "Cervical radiculopathy C6-C7" with MRI findings of "C6-C7 disc herniation, moderate right C7 nerve root compression" and neurological exam findings of "Right C7 dermatomal numbness, tricep weakness 4/5 right, tricep reflex diminished" (Clinical Record). This meets the plan's diagnosis criteria for cervical radiculopathy (Section 2.1(a) or 2.1(c)).
    *   **Conservative Treatment Requirement (Section 2.2):**
        *   **Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)):** The clinical record documents 4 weeks of physical therapy (Clinical Record). HealthFirst's criteria require a minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(a)). Therefore, this criterion was not met.
        *   **Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)):** The clinical record documents a trial of Ibuprofen 800mg TID for 6 weeks (Clinical Record). HealthFirst's criteria require a trial of at least two pharmacotherapy agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(b)). Therefore, this criterion was not met.
        *   **Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)):** The clinical record indicates no interventional treatments (Clinical Record). HealthFirst's criteria require one interventional treatment (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(c)). Therefore, this criterion was not met.
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (Section 2.2(A)):** Your clinical presentation is classified as primary radiculopathy, not myelopathy, as there is "No cord contact. No myelomalacia" on MRI (Clinical Record; Classify Clinical Presentation). Therefore, the myelopathy exception to conservative treatment requirements does not apply (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(A) NOTE).
    *   **Conclusion on Plan Criteria:** Based on a substantive review of the clinical record against HealthFirst's criteria, the conservative treatment requirements outlined in Section 2.2 were not fully satisfied.

**2. Compliance with California Regulatory Framework:**
    *   **Defective Denial Notice:** Our review identified critical procedural defects in the original denial notice, as raised in your appeal (Appeal Basis; Clinical Record - procedural_defects). The denial notice failed to:
        *   **Include IMR Notice:** The denial notice explicitly stated that an IMR notice was not included (Denial Info - imr_notice_included: false). California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(a)(2) mandates that a denial notice based on medical necessity SHALL include notice of IMR rights (Regulatory Document, CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2)).
        *   **Specify Clinical Criteria:** The denial notice provided only a "Generic reference to plan criteria — no section number, no specific criterion" (Denial Info). CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2) requires the denial notice to specify the exact clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reason for the denial (Regulatory Document, CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2)).
    *   **Remedy for Defective Notice:** CHSC § 1374.31(b) explicitly states that a denial notice failing to specify the clinical criteria applied or the specific clinical reason for the denial "is DEFECTIVE and shall be treated as if no denial was issued for purposes of appeal timelines" (Regulatory Document, CHSC § 1374.31(b)).

**3. Clinical Evidence Standard:**
    *   Your condition is cervical radiculopathy, not myelopathy, as confirmed by the absence of myelomalacia and cord contact on MRI (Clinical Record; Clinical Reference Summary Section 1.1, 1.2). For cervical radiculopathy, generally accepted clinical standards recommend a trial of conservative treatment, typically 6-12 weeks, before considering surgery (Clinical Reference Summary Section 2.2, 3.1 TIER 2(b), Section 4). While you completed 4 weeks of PT, the clinical evidence supports a longer trial for radiculopathy. Your claim of a functional plateau is noted, but the PT discharge does not explicitly use this language, which is required by CIC § 10169.5(b) for this exception to apply (Clinical Record; Regulatory Document, CIC § 10169.5(b)).

**Resolution of Conflicts and Determination:**
While a substantive review of your clinical record against HealthFirst's criteria indicates that the conservative treatment requirements were not fully met, the procedural defects in the original denial notice are critical and take precedence. California state law (CHSC § 1374.31) mandates specific content for denial notices to ensure members can meaningfully exercise their appeal rights, including the right to IMR. The failure to provide specific criteria and IMR notice renders the original denial procedurally invalid.

Therefore, the prior authorization denial for ACDF C6-C7 (CPT 22551) is **REMANDED**.

**Required Action:**
HealthFirst Health Plan must issue a new, compliant denial notice to you within 5 business days of the date of this letter. This new notice must:
1.  Clearly specify the exact clinical criteria from HealthFirst's policy (CC-SPINE-2024) that were applied to your case.
2.  Provide the specific clinical reasons for the denial, referencing your clinical information against the cited criteria.
3.  Include a clear and prominent notice of your right to Independent Medical Review (IMR), as required by CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2).

As per CHSC § 1374.31(b), the original defective denial is treated as if no denial was issued. Once you receive a compliant denial notice, you will have the full opportunity to understand the basis of the denial and pursue further appeal rights, including IMR, if you choose.
