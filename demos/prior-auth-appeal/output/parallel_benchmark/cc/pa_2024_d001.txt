This letter serves as the determination for your appeal regarding the prior authorization denial for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551).

**Background:**
Your request for prior authorization for ACDF C6-C7 was initially denied by HealthFirst Health Plan on October 5, 2024. The denial notice cited that the procedure "Does not meet criteria. Conservative treatment incomplete. Physical therapy and medication requirements not satisfied." You subsequently filed an appeal on October 18, 2024.

**Determination:**
Upon review of your appeal and the associated clinical and regulatory documentation, the prior authorization denial for ACDF C6-C7 (CPT 22551) for Case ID: PA-2024-D001 is hereby **REMANDED**.

**Rationale for Remand:**
The decision to REMAND is based on critical procedural defects in the original denial notice issued by HealthFirst Health Plan on October 5, 2024. These defects violate California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(a)(2) and (b).

Specifically, the denial notice failed to:
*   Specify the exact clinical criteria applied from the HealthFirst Health Plan's Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024).
*   Provide the specific clinical reason for the denial with sufficient detail.
*   Include notice of your Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights.

CHSC § 1374.31(b) explicitly states that "A denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied or the specific clinical reason for the denial is DEFECTIVE and shall be treated as if no denial was issued for purposes of appeal timelines." This procedural non-compliance prevents you, the member, from meaningfully understanding the basis of the denial and hinders your ability to exercise your full appeal rights, including the right to an Independent Medical Review.

It is acknowledged that a clinical review of the submitted documentation indicates that the requested procedure, ACDF C6-C7, does not meet HealthFirst's conservative treatment criteria as outlined in Section 2.2(a) and (b) of the HealthFirst Health Plan Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024). Specifically, the clinical record shows only 4 weeks of physical therapy completed, whereas 6 weeks are required for radiculopathy, and only one pharmacotherapy agent (Ibuprofen) was used, whereas a trial of at least two agents is required. However, the critical procedural defects in the denial notice, as mandated by state law, take precedence in this determination.

**Directive to HealthFirst Health Plan:**
HealthFirst Health Plan is hereby directed to reissue a compliant denial notice for the prior authorization request for ACDF C6-C7 (CPT 22551) for Sandra White (Member ID: M-334466, Case ID: PA-2024-D001). This reissued notice must fully comply with California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(a)(2) and (b) by including:
1.  The specific clinical criteria from your plan's policy that were applied to this case.
2.  The specific clinical reason(s) for the denial, detailing how the submitted clinical information failed to meet each cited criterion.
3.  A clear and comprehensive notice of the member's right to Independent Medical Review (IMR), including instructions on how to apply for IMR.

This reissued denial notice must be provided to the member and their treating provider within the timeframes specified by California law.

**Next Steps for Member:**
Once HealthFirst Health Plan reissues a compliant denial notice, you will have the opportunity to review the detailed reasons for the denial and exercise your appeal rights, including the right to request an Independent Medical Review, based on the complete and accurate information provided.
