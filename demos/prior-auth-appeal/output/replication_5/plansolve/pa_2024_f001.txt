## 1. OFFICIAL DISPOSITION
**DECISION: OVERTURN**  
The initial adverse determination dated November 2, 2024, is **OVERTURNED** in its entirety. The requested single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) is **APPROVED** as medically necessary.

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This formal appeal review evaluates the medical necessity of a single-level ACDF (C6-C7) for a 51-year-old female member with bilateral C6-C7 cervical radiculopathy. The initial request was denied on November 2, 2024, by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, citing a failure to satisfy the plan’s physical therapy (PT) requirements. Specifically, the plan had received only 4 weeks of PT records from Valley Physical Therapy, while the remaining 4 weeks of claimed therapy at Mountain Physical Therapy were missing from the initial submission.

On appeal, the complete clinical records from Mountain Physical Therapy have been submitted and successfully integrated into the review file. These records confirm an additional 4 weeks (12 sessions) of supervised cervical physical therapy, establishing a verified total of 8 weeks (24 sessions) of physical therapy. Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5**, a health plan cannot condition surgical approval on additional physical therapy once a member has completed a course of physical therapy and a "functional plateau" is documented. Because the clinical record now demonstrates complete compliance with both the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) and California statutory mandates, the initial denial is overturned.

## 4. CLINICAL EVIDENCE EVALUATION

### A. Diagnostic Classification & Clinical Severity Verification (Step 3)
To establish the correct clinical pathway, the member's clinical presentation must be classified as either Cervical Myelopathy or Cervical Radiculopathy:
*   **Imaging (UCLA Medical Center MRI, 2024-09-01):** Demonstrates a large C6-C7 disc herniation, bilateral C7 nerve root compression, and bilateral foraminal stenosis. Crucially, the MRI notes **"mild cord contact without compression"** and does not identify any T2 spinal cord signal changes (myelomalacia).
*   **Neurological Examination (2024-09-05):** Reveals bilateral C7 dermatomal symptoms, including bilateral tricep weakness (graded 4/5), diminished bilateral tricep reflexes, and bilateral finger tingling. There are no upper motor neuron signs (e.g., positive Hoffman sign, hyperreflexia, clonus, or gait disturbance) documented in the clinical file.
*   **Classification:** Because there is no objective spinal cord compression, no myelomalacia, and an absence of upper motor neuron signs, the patient is clinically classified as having **Cervical Radiculopathy (Bilateral C6-C7)**. The standard plan coverage pathway applies.

### B. Standard Radiculopathy Pathway Evaluation (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2)

#### 1. Physical Therapy Requirement (Section 2.2(a))
*   **Plan Requirement:** Minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy directed toward the cervical spine.
*   **Valley Physical Therapy Records:** Document 4 weeks of treatment (June 1, 2024, to June 28, 2024) spanning 12 sessions. The discharge summary notes "moderate improvement then plateau."
*   **Mountain Physical Therapy Records (Enclosed with Appeal):** Document an additional 4 weeks of treatment (July 15, 2024, to August 9, 2024) spanning 12 sessions. The discharge summary notes "no improvement in neurological symptoms" and explicitly documents a "plateau at discharge."
*   **Total Verified PT:** 8 weeks (24 sessions) completed across two providers. This exceeds the plan's 6-week minimum requirement.
*   **Statutory Compliance (CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)):** California law prohibits health plans from conditioning surgical approval on further physical therapy when the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the provider documents a "functional plateau" without adequate resolution of symptoms. This exception is fully triggered by the plateaus documented at both Valley PT and Mountain PT.

#### 2. Pharmacotherapy Trial (Section 2.2(b))
*   **Plan Requirement:** Trial of at least two specified drug classes for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
*   **Agent 1 (Neuropathic):** Pregabalin 150mg BID, trialed for 3 months, resulting in "partial relief."
*   **Agent 2 (NSAID):** Naproxen 500mg BID, trialed for 2 months, resulting in "inadequate" response.
*   **Findings:** The member completed a trial of two distinct, clinically indicated pharmacotherapy classes for a duration exceeding the 4-week minimum. This criterion is met.

#### 3. Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c))
*   **Plan Requirement:** Minimum of one interventional treatment (e.g., cervical epidural steroid injection).
*   **Completed Procedure (2024-09-20):** Bilateral C7 epidural steroid injection.
*   **Clinical Outcome:** Left side achieved 50% temporary relief; right side achieved 30% temporary relief. Symptoms subsequently returned to baseline.
*   **Findings:** The interventional treatment requirement has been fully satisfied.

#### 4. Imaging Congruence (Section 2.3)
*   **Plan Requirement:** Current imaging within 6 months demonstrating pathology consistent with symptoms and the proposed surgical level.
*   **Findings:** The MRI dated September 1, 2024, confirms a large C6-C7 disc herniation and bilateral C7 nerve root compression, which directly correlates with the bilateral C7 dermatomal symptoms and tricep weakness evaluated on September 5, 2024. This criterion is met.

## 5. HIERARCHY OF AUTHORITY & LEGAL EXCLUSION ANALYSIS
Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(c)**, a health plan’s internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy cannot be mechanically applied without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau. 

Furthermore, under the **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b)** and **Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) APL 22-014**, generally accepted clinical standards govern over more restrictive internal plan criteria. The North American Spine Society (NASS) and American Association of Neurological Surgeons (AANS/CNS) guidelines confirm that for radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits (such as the member's documented 4/5 tricep weakness and diminished reflexes) that fail to resolve after 6–12 weeks of conservative care, surgical decompression is strongly indicated.

The member has:
1.  Failed an 8-week course of supervised physical therapy with a documented clinical plateau.
2.  Failed dual-agent pharmacotherapy.
3.  Experienced only transient relief from a bilateral C7 epidural injection.
4.  Demonstrated severe structural pathology on MRI that perfectly correlates with her objective neurological weakness.

To delay surgical authorization further in the presence of an active motor deficit (tricep weakness) and a documented functional plateau would violate California statutory mandates and depart from generally accepted standards of medical practice.
