### III. STEP-BY-STEP EVALUATION ANALYSIS

#### **Step 1: Procedural Adequacy & Defect Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))**
*   **Analysis:** The initial denial issued on October 30, 2024, by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, cited Reason Code "MED-003" and stated: *"Myelopathy exception does not apply... Standard 6-week PT requirement applies; member completed 5 weeks."* However, the denial notice failed to cite the specific clinical coverage criteria document (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024) used to make this determination, nor did it outline the specific regulatory exceptions available under California law.
*   **Finding:** **POSITIVE FINDING (Procedural Defect).** Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b), a denial notice that fails to specify the exact clinical coverage criteria applied is procedurally defective. Because the plan failed to cite policy CC-SPINE-2024, the denial is procedurally invalid. 
*   *Note: To ensure a comprehensive review, the clinical and statutory merits of the case are evaluated below.*

#### **Step 2: Diagnostic Classification & Clinical/Imaging Analysis**
*   **Analysis:** The plan's denial asserted that upper motor neuron (UMN) signs are not conclusive for myelopathy without imaging confirmation showing cord compression or T2 signal changes. 
*   **Clinical Findings:**
    *   **Objective Exam (2024-09-05):** Bilateral positive Hoffman's sign, bilateral hyperreflexia of the upper extremities, sustained ankle clonus (3 beats), positive finger escape sign, and a mildly wide-based gait.
    *   **MRI Findings (2024-09-01):** C5-C6 disc herniation with "mild cord contact." No quantifiable compression on standard sequences; no T2 signal change; no myelomalacia.
*   **Clinical Standard Application:** According to the American Association of Neurological Surgeons and Congress of Neurological Surgeons (AANS/CNS 2023) Joint Guidelines, the diagnosis of cervical myelopathy is primarily *clinical*, established by the presence of objective UMN signs. Imaging-negative myelopathy is a well-recognized clinical entity where clinical signs of cord dysfunction precede visible structural changes or signal intensity changes on standard MRI.
*   **Finding:** **Myelopathy Confirmed (Clinical Definition).** The member presents with five distinct, objective UMN findings. Under generally accepted clinical standards, clinical cervical myelopathy is established regardless of the lack of severe cord compression or T2 signal changes on MRI.

#### **Step 3: Plan Criteria & Exception 2.2(A) Application Check**
*   **Analysis:** The Plan denied the procedure because the member completed 5 weeks of physical therapy (PT) instead of the standard 6 weeks required under Section 2.2.
*   **Plan Policy CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(A) (Myelopathy Pathway):** For members with confirmed cervical myelopathy, the conservative treatment requirement is modified:
    1. Physical therapy minimum duration is reduced to **4 weeks**.
    2. Interventional treatment (injections) is **waived** if the physician documents a risk of neurological deterioration.
*   **Application:** The clinical record shows the member completed 5 weeks (15 sessions) of a specialized myelopathy PT protocol. The PT was halted by Dr. Adams due to safety concerns regarding the patient's neurological presentation (UMN signs remained unchanged).
*   **Finding:** **POSITIVE FINDING (Plan Criteria Misapplied).** Because the member has a confirmed clinical diagnosis of myelopathy, Exception 2.2(A) applies. The member's completed 5-week course of PT exceeds the modified 4-week minimum requirement. The plan misapplied its own criteria by demanding a 6-week trial.

#### **Step 4: Statutory Override Evaluation (California Law)**
*   **Analysis:** Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5, a health plan cannot mechanically enforce a minimum physical therapy duration when specific statutory exceptions are met.
*   **CIC § 10169.5(a)(1) & (2) Application:**
    *   *Subdivision (a)(1):* Dr. Adams documented that continued PT was halted because it was unlikely to benefit the patient and posed a risk of neurological decline.
    *   *Subdivision (a)(2):* The physical therapy summary notes that after 15 sessions over 5 weeks, the patient's neurological signs were unchanged, establishing a clinical "functional plateau" where further conservative management would not resolve the structural cord irritation.
*   **Finding:** **POSITIVE FINDING (Statutory Override Triggered).** Under CIC § 10169.5, the plan's 6-week PT requirement is legally waived. The plan's mechanical insistence on an additional 1 week of PT violates California insurance law.

#### **Step 5: Generally Accepted Clinical Standards Review (IMR Standard)**
*   **Analysis:** Under CHSC § 1374.32(b), the standard for California Independent Medical Review (IMR) requires that the necessity of a service be evaluated based on generally accepted standards of medical practice, not the plan's internal policies.
*   **Clinical Evidence (AANS/CNS 2023 / AO Spine 2021):** National clinical practice guidelines establish that for patients with clinical cervical myelopathy and objective UMN deficits, there is no high-quality clinical evidence supporting a mandatory course of PT. Delaying surgical decompression to complete an arbitrary PT trial is clinically contraindicated, has no evidentiary basis, and risks permanent, irreversible spinal cord damage.
*   **Finding:** **Surgically Indicated (Tier 1).** The requested single-level ACDF (CPT 22551) at C5-C6 is medically necessary and standard-of-care under national guidelines.

---

### IV. CONCLUSION & CLINICAL RATIONALE FOR OVERTURN

The initial denial of coverage for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) is **OVERTURNED** based on the following determinations:

1.  **Procedural Defect:** The plan's denial notice failed to cite the specific clinical coverage guidelines applied, violating CHSC § 1374.31(b).
2.  **Misapplication of Plan Exception:** The member has clinical cervical myelopathy established by five distinct upper motor neuron signs (Hoffman's, hyperreflexia, ankle clonus, finger escape, and gait disturbance). Under the Plan's own policy (CC-SPINE-2024, Exception 2.2(A)), the PT requirement is reduced to 4 weeks. Having completed 5 weeks, the member satisfied the Plan's criteria.
3.  **California Statutory Violations:** Mechanical enforcement of a 6-week PT requirement violates CIC § 10169.5, as the member reached a documented functional plateau and the treating neurosurgeon appropriately halted therapy due to safety concerns.
4.  **Clinical Medical Necessity:** According to AANS/CNS and AO Spine guidelines, delaying surgical decompression in a patient with progressive UMN signs to satisfy a physical therapy requirement is clinically contraindicated and increases the risk of permanent neurological deficits.

The requested ACDF C5-C6 (CPT 22551) at UCSF Medical Center, to be performed by Dr. Robert Adams, MD, is **approved in full**.
