### I. FINAL DISPOSITION
**DECISION:** **OVERTURN** the initial denial. Prior authorization for CPT Code 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) is **APPROVED**.

---

### II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This formal appeal review evaluates the medical necessity of a single-level anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 for a 63-year-old male with a mixed cervical myelopathy-radiculopathy presentation. The health plan initially denied the procedure on October 5, 2024, citing its internal clinical coverage criteria (**HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024**). The plan argued that because the member's symptoms were primarily unilateral (right-sided), "radiculopathy" remained the primary diagnosis, thereby rendering the member ineligible for the plan’s Myelopathy Exception (Section 2.2(A)) and requiring standard conservative therapy compliance.

Following a comprehensive clinical and statutory review, the health plan's denial is **OVERTURNED**. Under the strict **Hierarchy of Authority** governing prior authorization appeals in California:
$$\text{California State Law (CIC, CHSC, DMHC APL)} > \text{Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS, AO Spine, NASS)} > \text{Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)}$$

The plan's denial is legally and clinically unsustainable due to:
1. **Procedural Deficiencies** under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b).
2. **Statutory Overrides** under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(3) and California Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014, which explicitly prohibit health plans from requiring conservative treatment when objective imaging documents spinal cord compression and myelomalacia.
3. **Clinical Standards Deviation** under CHSC § 1374.32(b), where national professional standards (AANS/CNS 2023, AO Spine 2021) classify myelopathy with T2 signal change as a Tier 1 strong surgical indication where delaying surgery is clinically contraindicated.
4. **Factual Errors** regarding the member's actual conservative treatment history, which fully met even the plan’s standard criteria.

### III. DETAILED EVALUATION PLAN ANALYSIS

#### Step 1: Procedural Defect Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
* **Evaluation**: The initial denial notice issued by Dr. Patricia Huang on October 5, 2024, denied coverage under reason code "MED-003." The notice failed to cite the specific clinical coverage policy identifier, version, or exact section numbers applied (e.g., *HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2*). It also failed to provide the mandatory statutory disclosures regarding the member's right to an Independent Medical Review (IMR) through the DMHC.
* **Finding**: **POSITIVE PROCEDURAL DEFECT**. Under **California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b)**, a denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied or fails to provide the required IMR rights notices is legally defective and "shall be treated as if no denial was issued" for purposes of the appeal timeline, triggering an immediate procedural **OVERTURN**.

#### Step 2: Diagnosis and Clinical Classification Verification (Radiculopathy vs. Myelopathy)
* **Evaluation**: The plan's initial denial asserted that the member's pathology is "primarily radicular" and that the "myelopathy exception requires myelopathy as the primary diagnosis." This review evaluated the clinical record dated September 5, 2024, and the MRI dated September 1, 2024, against the **AANS/CNS Joint Section Guidelines (2023)**.
    * *Objective Imaging*: MRI demonstrates "C6-C7 moderate cord compression" and "T2 signal change (myelomalacia) within the cord."
    * *Neurological Examination*: Objective upper motor neuron (UMN) signs are documented, including **bilateral hyperreflexia** (right > left), a **positive right Hoffman sign**, an equivocal left Hoffman sign, and a **mildly wide-based gait**.
* **Finding**: **POSITIVE CLINICAL DIAGNOSIS OF CERVICAL MYELOPATHY**. Under AANS/CNS diagnostic standards, the presence of objective spinal cord compression on imaging combined with objective UMN signs (Hoffman's, hyperreflexia, gait disturbance) establishes a definitive clinical diagnosis of cervical myelopathy. The presence of concurrent right-sided C7 radicular symptoms (weakness 4/5, numbness, dermatomal pain) indicates a mixed myelopathy-radiculopathy presentation. The plan's clinical assertion that the presentation is "primarily radiculopathy" because the symptoms are lateralized is a fundamental clinical category error; myelopathy is defined by cord compression and UMN signs, which are objectively present.

#### Step 3: Evaluation of State Law Statutory Overrides (CIC § 10169.5 & DMHC APL 22-014)
* **Evaluation**: California statutory mandates strictly regulate when a health plan may condition surgery on physical therapy (PT).
    * **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(3)**: Explicitly prohibits a health plan from conditioning surgical approval on physical therapy where "the condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address."
    * **DMHC All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014**: Mandates that health plans *shall not* require full conservative treatment when objective imaging shows progressive pathology (with **myelomalacia** specifically identified as a qualifying finding) and the treating physician documents surgical indication under generally accepted clinical guidelines.
* **Finding**: **POSITIVE STATUTORY OVERRIDE**. The member's MRI dated September 1, 2024, provides objective evidence of structural neurological compromise (moderate spinal cord compression and T2 signal change/myelomalacia) which PT cannot structurally resolve. Conditioning surgery on further conservative care violates CIC § 10169.5(a)(3) and DMHC APL 22-014. The plan's conservative therapy criteria are legally overridden.

#### Step 4: Evaluation of Clinical Standards vs. Plan Criteria (AANS/CNS 2023 & AO Spine 2021)
* **Evaluation**: Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)**, the standard of review for medical necessity is defined by generally accepted standards of medical practice, not by the plan’s internal guidelines.
    * **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023)**: Classify moderate-to-severe cervical myelopathy with objective cord compression and T2 cord signal change (myelomalacia) as a **Tier 1 - Strong Surgical Indication**. Delaying surgical decompression is associated with irreversible spinal cord injury and permanent neurological deficits.
    * **AO Spine Clinical Practice Guidelines (2021)**: State there is "no high-quality evidence supporting a mandatory course of physical therapy prior to surgical intervention for patients with moderate to severe cervical myelopathy." Requiring physical therapy is clinically contraindicated in the presence of myelomalacia.
* **Finding**: **POSITIVE CLINICAL STANDARD OVERRIDE**. The requested ACDF (CPT 22551) is the established, gold-standard treatment for this pathology. Forcing a patient with active myelomalacia to undergo prolonged conservative therapy is clinically contraindicated and deviates from national standards of care. Under CHSC § 1374.32(b), the clinical standard of care overrides the plan's restrictive criteria.

#### Step 5: Assessment of Plan Criteria Application and Myelopathy Exception 2.2(A)
* **Evaluation**: Even if the plan's clinical criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024) were applied without statutory/clinical overrides, the plan’s interpretation of its own criteria was factually and logically flawed.
    * *Misapplication of Exception 2.2(A)*: The plan denied the exception because "primary diagnosis remains radiculopathy." However, Section 2.1(b) and Section 2.2(A) do not allow the plan to ignore a documented, severe co-existing pathology (myelomalacia) based on symptom lateralization. 
    * *Factual Error on Conservative Care Completion*: The plan claimed the member failed to satisfy standard conservative treatment criteria. However, the clinical record demonstrates:
        1. **Physical Therapy**: The member completed **6 weeks (18 sessions)** of physical therapy under a myelopathy protocol, resulting in a documented **functional plateau** (no improvement in UMN signs or radicular symptoms). This satisfies standard Section 2.2(a).
        2. **Pharmacotherapy**: Trialed **Pregabalin 150mg BID for 3 months** (neuropathic agent) and **Naproxen 500mg BID for 2 months** (NSAID). This satisfies standard Section 2.2(b).
        3. **Interventional**: Underwent a **right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 28, 2024**, which provided only temporary relief (3 weeks) and no change in UMN signs. This satisfies standard Section 2.2(c).
* **Finding**: **POSITIVE PLAN MISAPPLICATION FINDING**. The member fully met even the plan's standard, non-modified conservative treatment criteria. The plan's assertion that the member failed conservative care requirements is factually incorrect.

### V. CONCLUSION AND ORDER
Based on the binding authority of California State Law and generally accepted clinical standards, the health plan's denial of prior authorization for CPT 22551 is **OVERTURNED**. 

The clinical record establishes that Mr. Santos suffers from cervical myelopathy with objective spinal cord compression and myelomalacia (T2 cord signal change) at C6-C7, presenting an imminent risk of irreversible neurological decline if surgical decompression is delayed. The proposed ACDF at C6-C7 is medically necessary, clinically urgent, and fully supported by national clinical guidelines.

HealthFirst is hereby ordered to **APPROVE** prior authorization for CPT Code 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, as requested by Dr. Lisa Wong, MD, with an effective date matching the date of this determination.
