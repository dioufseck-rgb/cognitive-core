## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

On October 20, 2024, HealthFirst Health Plan denied prior authorization for a C6-C7 ACDF requested by Dr. Karen Liu, MD, on behalf of member Thomas Reed. The plan’s initial reviewer, Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, cited reason code **MED-003**, stating the member did not meet clinical criteria because imaging showed mild cord contact without measurable compression, there were no myelopathic findings, and physical therapy (PT) had "not yet [been] completed to functional plateau."

This independent appeal evaluation has reviewed the complete medical record, the plan’s internal clinical guidelines (**CC-SPINE-2024**), California statutory mandates (**California Insurance Code [CIC] § 10169.5** and **California Health & Safety Code [CHSC] § 1374.32(b)**), and generally accepted clinical guidelines (**AANS/CNS 2023** and **AO Spine 2021**). 

The review demonstrates that:
1. The member's primary clinical presentation is **cervical radiculopathy**, not myelopathy.
2. The member has fully satisfied HealthFirst's standard conservative treatment requirements under **Section 2.2**, including completing 7 weeks of physical therapy, 3–4 months of multi-agent pharmacotherapy, and an interventional epidural steroid injection.
3. The plan's assertion that PT was not completed to a "functional plateau" is factually incorrect and directly contradicted by the physical therapy discharge summary.
4. Under **California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2)**, the plan is legally prohibited from conditioning surgical approval on further physical therapy once a functional plateau has been documented by the treating therapist.
5. The requested procedure is highly indicated as a **Tier 2 Reasonable Indication** under generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS 2023).

Under the governing **Source Hierarchy of Authority** (California State Law $\gg$ Generally Accepted Clinical Standards $\gg$ HealthFirst Plan Criteria), the denial is legally and clinically unsustainable and must be overturned.

## II. CLINICAL RECONSTRUCTION & FINDINGS

### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Disclosure Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
The initial denial letter dated October 20, 2024, cited reason code MED-003 and provided a basic clinical rationale (absence of myelopathy, mild cord contact, and failure to complete PT to a functional plateau). While the letter met the basic procedural requirements of styling a denial under CHSC § 1374.31(b) by referencing clinical concepts, its clinical assertions were factually incorrect. The procedural check is passed, but the substantive clinical findings are legally and factually deficient.

### Step 2: Diagnostic Classification and Clinical Verification
The clinical records were analyzed to determine the correct diagnostic pathway (Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy):
*   **Imaging (MRI dated 2024-08-01):** Shows a C6-C7 disc herniation with mild cord contact but *no* measurable cord compression, *no* T2 cord signal changes (myelomalacia), and mild right C7 foraminal stenosis.
*   **Neurological Exam (dated 2024-08-05):** Reveals right C7 dermatomal distribution numbness, a diminished right tricep reflex, and 7/10 pain at rest. There are *no* bilateral findings, *no* upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (e.g., negative Hoffman sign, no hyperreflexia, no clonus), and no gait instability.
*   **Classification:** The member presents with **classic, unilateral, single-level Cervical Radiculopathy (C6-C7)**. The plan’s initial reviewer correctly noted the absence of myelopathy, meaning the standard radiculopathy pathway under **Section 2** of the plan's criteria applies.

### Step 3: Application of Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)
The plan's denial asserted that the member failed to meet the conservative treatment requirements. A review of the documented conservative trial reveals that the member *fully completed* the standard requirements outlined in **Section 2.2**:

*   **2.2(a) Physical Therapy:** Required: minimum 6 weeks. *Completed:* **7 weeks of supervised physical therapy (21 sessions)**. The plan's denial claimed PT was not completed to a "functional plateau." However, the physical therapy discharge summary explicitly states: *"7 weeks PT completed. PT discharge summary explicitly states functional plateau reached — patient has not improved over final 3 sessions. Radicular symptoms persist unchanged."* The member achieved a clinical functional plateau as defined by standard clinical practice (<10% improvement over 3 consecutive sessions).
*   **2.2(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Required: trial of at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. *Completed:* 
    1.  *Pregabalin 150mg BID (Neuropathic agent)*: taken for **4 months** (partial relief, radicular symptoms persist).
    2.  *Meloxicam 15mg (NSAID)*: taken for **3 months** (inadequate relief).
*   **2.2(c) Interventional Treatment:** Required: minimum of 1 cervical epidural steroid injection. *Completed:* **Right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 20, 2024**, which provided 30% temporary relief for 3 weeks before symptoms returned to rest baseline.

**Finding:** The member successfully completed a comprehensive, multi-modality conservative management trial exceeding all plan duration standards. The plan's initial denial was based on a direct misapplication of its own criteria, failing to recognize that the 6-week PT requirement and the "functional plateau" standard had been fully satisfied.

## III. STATUTORY OVERRIDES & CLINICAL STANDARDS

### Step 4: Statutory Override Evaluation (CIC § 10169.5 & DMHC APL 22-014)
California law strictly regulates prior authorization and restricts health plans from enforcing mechanical conservative treatment requirements.

*   **California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2)** explicitly states:
    > *"A health insurer or disability insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where: ... (2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."*
*   **Section 10169.5(b)** defines a "functional plateau" as a *"documented failure to achieve meaningful functional improvement after a course of therapy, as determined by the treating physical therapist or physician."*

The treating physical therapist documented a formal functional plateau after 21 sessions, noting no improvement over the final 3 sessions with persistent radicular symptoms. By denying the surgical procedure on the basis that PT was "not yet completed to functional plateau," HealthFirst acted in direct violation of **CIC § 10169.5**. The statutory override applies, rendering the plan's PT objection legally invalid.

### Step 5: Clinical Standard Medical Necessity Evaluation (AANS/CNS 2023 & AO Spine 2021)
Under **California Health & Safety Code § 1374.32(b)**, clinical coverage decisions must align with generally accepted standards of medical practice, which supersede restrictive internal plan guidelines.

*   **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023):** Recommend surgical decompression (ACDF) for patients presenting with cervical radiculopathy with matching dermatomal deficits and corresponding imaging findings who have failed to improve after an appropriate conservative trial of 6 to 12 weeks (**Tier 2 Reasonable Indication**).
*   **AO Spine Clinical Practice Guidelines (2021):** Confirm that continuing conservative care beyond 6–8 weeks in a patient with a documented functional plateau and severe persistent pain (7/10 at rest) offers no therapeutic benefit and unnecessarily delays functional recovery.

The member's clinical presentation—severe persistent 7/10 pain, right C7 dermatomal numbness, diminished right tricep reflex, matching C6-C7 disc herniation with right C7 foraminal stenosis, and failure of a rigorous 7-week PT, 4-month pharmacotherapy, and interventional injection trial—fully satisfies the national clinical standards of medical necessity for a C6-C7 ACDF.

## IV. RESOLUTION HIERARCHY & SUMMARY

Applying the strict **Source Hierarchy of Authority** required by the evaluation plan:

$$\text{State of California Law (CIC § 10169.5)} \gg \text{Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023)} \gg \text{HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024)}$$

1.  **California Law** prohibits the plan from denying this surgery based on physical therapy completion, as a functional plateau was formally documented by the treating therapist.
2.  **Generally Accepted Clinical Standards** establish that a single-level ACDF is medically necessary and indicated for this member's persistent, severe C7 radiculopathy.
3.  **Plan Criteria** were actually met, as the member completed 7 weeks of PT, trialed two medications for $\ge$ 4 weeks, and completed an epidural steroid injection.

The initial prior authorization review committed a factual error by claiming the member had not reached a functional plateau, and a regulatory error by attempting to enforce additional physical therapy in violation of California statutory protections.

The denial of coverage for CPT code 22551 (ACDF C6-C7, one level) is hereby **OVERTURNED**.
