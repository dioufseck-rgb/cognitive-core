Dear Mr. Reyes and Dr. Okonkwo,

The HealthFirst Appeals and Grievances Department has completed its review of the first-level appeal filed on November 14, 2024, regarding the prior authorization request for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at the C6-C7 level (CPT 22551) to be performed at UCSF Medical Center. 

After a comprehensive clinical and regulatory review of the complete medical record, HealthFirst has determined that the initial prior authorization denial issued on November 1, 2024, was based on a factual error and is hereby OVERTURNED. The requested surgical procedure is approved.

I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BASIS FOR OVERTURN
The initial denial issued by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, on November 1, 2024, asserted that the member completed only three (3) weeks of physical therapy (PT) and failed to meet the six-week minimum required under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2. The denial also stated that a functional plateau was not documented.

A review of the clinical records submitted with the original prior authorization request reveals this factual claim is incorrect. The member completed a total of nine (9) weeks of supervised physical therapy across two separate providers:
1. Valley Physical Therapy: Three (3) weeks (9 sessions) from June 1, 2024, to June 21, 2024.
2. Capital Physical Therapy: Six (6) weeks (18 sessions) from July 8, 2024, to August 16, 2024.

The discharge summary from Capital Physical Therapy explicitly documents a functional plateau, stating: "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."

Because the member completed nine (9) weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the six-week minimum) and reached a documented functional plateau, and because all other clinical criteria under CC-SPINE-2024 are fully satisfied, the initial prior authorization denial is overturned.

II. CLINICAL PRESENTATION & HISTORY
Mr. Reyes is a 49-year-old male with a history of persistent, right-sided C6-C7 cervical radiculopathy.
- Imaging: A cervical spine MRI dated August 20, 2024, demonstrates a large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression and right foraminal stenosis. No spinal cord contact or myelomalacia is present.
- Neurological Examination: A physical exam dated August 25, 2024, confirms objective neurological deficits correlating with the MRI findings, including right C7 dermatomal numbness, a diminished right tricep reflex, and right-sided grip strength of 4/5. No upper motor neuron (UMN) signs or bilateral findings are present.
- Conservative Treatment History:
  - Physical Therapy: 9 weeks total (27 sessions) completed between June 1, 2024, and August 16, 2024, with a documented functional plateau.
  - Pharmacotherapy: Mr. Reyes completed a four-month trial of Gabapentin (600mg TID) with only partial pain relief, and a three-month trial of Naproxen (500mg BID) which was inadequate.
  - Interventional Treatment: Mr. Reyes underwent a right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 15, 2024, which provided only 35% temporary relief before symptoms returned to baseline within three weeks.

III. PLAN CRITERIA COMPLIANCE ANALYSIS (CC-SPINE-2024)
Under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2, authorization for cervical spinal surgery (CPT 22551) is approved when all of the following criteria are satisfied:

1. Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement): Satisfied. Mr. Reyes has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with correlating nerve root compression and objective radicular symptoms (right C7 numbness, diminished tricep reflex, and 4/5 grip strength). The supporting MRI was performed on August 20, 2024, which is within the required 6-month window.
2. Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement): Satisfied.
   - (a) Physical Therapy: Mr. Reyes completed 9 weeks of supervised physical therapy directed at his cervical condition, exceeding the 6-week minimum.
   - (b) Pharmacotherapy: Mr. Reyes completed trials of two qualifying agents for at least 4 weeks each (Gabapentin for 4 months and Naproxen for 3 months).
   - (c) Interventional Treatment: Mr. Reyes completed a right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 15, 2024.
3. Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement): Satisfied. The MRI dated August 20, 2024, demonstrates structural pathology (large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression) consistent with the clinical presentation and proposed surgical level.
4. Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation): Satisfied. Dr. Sarah Okonkwo, MD, provided the necessary clinical documentation, including conservative treatment outcomes, surgical rationale, functional limitations, and discussion of risks/alternatives.

IV. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE ANALYSIS (CALIFORNIA LAW)
The initial denial also violates California statutory mandates governing prior authorization and utilization review:

1. California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5: This statute prohibits health plans from conditioning surgical approval on the completion of physical therapy if the member has completed a course of therapy and reached a functional plateau. Furthermore, § 10169.5(c) mandates that a plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau. By ignoring the Capital Physical Therapy records and the documented functional plateau, and mechanically denying the request based on an incorrect 3-week count, the plan violated this statutory provision.
2. California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b): This section requires that denial notices specify the clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reasons for the denial. Because the initial denial was based on a critical factual error (asserting only 3 weeks of PT were completed when 9 weeks were documented in the submitted records), the denial notice was procedurally defective.

V. CLINICAL STANDARDS COMPLIANCE ANALYSIS
The requested ACDF at C6-C7 is highly consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice:
1. AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for the Management of Degenerative Cervical Radiculopathy (2023): Mr. Reyes meets the Tier 2 "Reasonable Indication" criteria for surgical intervention, as he has persistent cervical radiculopathy with correlating MRI pathology and objective neurological deficits that have failed to respond to a comprehensive course of conservative management (including 9 weeks of PT, multi-agent pharmacotherapy, and an epidural steroid injection).
2. North American Spine Society (NASS) Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020): These guidelines support surgical decompression and fusion for patients with persistent radicular pain and objective neurological deficits who have reached a functional plateau with conservative care.

VI. CONCLUSION & AUTHORIZATION DETAILS
Based on the clear evidence that Mr. Reyes has completed 9 weeks of physical therapy with a documented functional plateau, trialed two pharmacotherapy agents, and undergone an epidural steroid injection, he meets all clinical and regulatory requirements for the requested procedure. 

The prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED.
