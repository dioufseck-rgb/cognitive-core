Dear Mr. Obi and Dr. Diallo,

The HealthFirst Appeals and Grievances Department has completed its review of the prior authorization appeal submitted on November 22, 2024, regarding the requested single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) at Stanford Medical Center. 

After a comprehensive clinical, regulatory, and contractual review of the complete medical record, HealthFirst has OVERTURNED the initial prior authorization denial issued on November 08, 2024. The requested procedure is hereby APPROVED.

BACKGROUND AND INITIAL DENIAL
On November 08, 2024, HealthFirst Utilization Management issued an initial denial (Reason Code: MED-003) for the requested ACDF C6-C7 procedure. The denial was based on two grounds:
1. An alleged conservative treatment shortfall, specifically claiming that the member had only completed 5 weeks of physical therapy (PT) out of the 6 weeks required under plan criteria.
2. Poorly controlled type 2 diabetes (HbA1c of 8.2%), which the reviewer cited as presenting elevated perioperative and wound healing risks that required optimization prior to surgical authorization.

CLINICAL AND REGULATORY EVALUATION

1. DIAGNOSIS AND CLINICAL PRESENTATION (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1)
The clinical record establishes a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy at C6-C7 with significant functional impairment, satisfying CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a).
- Neurological Examination: A neurological exam dated September 25, 2024, documents objective neurological deficits, including left C7 dermatomal numbness, a diminished left tricep reflex, 4/5 left tricep weakness, and 4+/5 left grip weakness. The member is documented as working reduced hours due to severe pain and hand weakness.
- Imaging Correlation: An MRI of the cervical spine dated September 20, 2024, demonstrates a large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate left C7 nerve root compression and confirmed foraminal stenosis. This objective imaging directly correlates with the member's left-sided C7 radicular symptoms and neurological deficits, satisfying the imaging requirements of CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.3. There is no cord contact or myelomalacia noted.

2. CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENTS (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2)
A detailed audit of the clinical record demonstrates that the member has fully completed all conservative treatment requirements outlined in CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2:

- Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)): The initial denial's assertion that the member completed only 5 weeks of PT is factually incorrect. The physical therapy records document a continuous, supervised course of PT from July 01, 2024, through August 16, 2024, totaling 7 weeks (21 sessions). This exceeds the plan's 6-week minimum requirement. Furthermore, the PT discharge summary documents a functional plateau, stating "no further functional improvement expected."
  Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) and (c), a health plan is prohibited from mechanically applying a minimum duration requirement for physical therapy when a member has completed a course of therapy and reached a documented functional plateau. The mechanical denial of this request based on an incorrect week count violates CIC § 10169.5.
  
- Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)): The member completed trials of two distinct neuropathic agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each: Pregabalin 150mg BID for 5 months (yielding partial pain relief) and Duloxetine 60mg for 3 months (yielding moderate neuropathic benefit). This satisfies the requirement for a trial of at least two agents from the approved classes.

- Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)): The member underwent a left C7 epidural steroid injection on October 10, 2024, which provided 45% temporary relief before symptoms returned to baseline after 4 weeks. This satisfies the requirement for at least one interventional treatment.

3. PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.4)
Dr. Amina Diallo has provided all necessary documentation, including an attestation of completed conservative treatment, clinical rationale for surgical intervention, a description of functional limitations (including reduced working hours), and a statement of surgical risks and alternatives considered, satisfying Section 2.4 in full.

4. RESOLUTION OF THE DIABETES DISTRACTOR (NON-CRITERION DENIAL GROUND)
The initial denial cited the member's poorly controlled type 2 diabetes (HbA1c 8.2% on October 15, 2024) and associated wound healing risks as a basis for withholding prior authorization.
- Plan Criteria Compliance: HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 does not condition prior authorization for ACDF on metabolic control, HbA1c thresholds, or diabetes management.
- Regulatory Compliance: Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b), a denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied. Citing a non-criterion clinical factor (such as diabetes control) to deny a prior authorization request is procedurally defective and invalid.
- Clinical Standards: Generally accepted clinical standards, including the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) and NASS Guidelines (2020), do not utilize diabetes control as a prior authorization gatekeeper. As documented by Dr. Diallo and supported by standard clinical practice, perioperative diabetes optimization is a standard surgical planning and patient safety responsibility managed by the surgical and endocrinology teams preoperatively, not a prior authorization criterion.

CONCLUSION
Because the physical therapy shortfall claim is factually incorrect and the diabetes control concern is a non-criterion perioperative issue, there is no valid basis for denial. The clinical record demonstrates full compliance with all medical necessity criteria under CC-SPINE-2024 (Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, and 2.4) and generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, Tier 2).

Accordingly, the prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED, and the request for ACDF C6-C7 (CPT 22551) is APPROVED. This authorization is valid for 90 days from the date of this letter (through February 22, 2025) per CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.5.
