SUBJECT: Clinical Compliance Analysis under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024

I. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This memorandum provides a formal clinical analysis of the prior authorization request for a one-level ACDF at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) for member Jessica Turner. The analysis is strictly limited to evaluating the member's clinical record against the literal requirements of HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria: Spinal Surgery — Cervical Procedures (Document: CC-SPINE-2024, Effective: January 1, 2024). 

II. CLINICAL PRESENTATION SUMMARY
The member is a 39-year-old female presenting with primary cervical radiculopathy at the C6-C7 level. 
- Clinical Findings (Neurological Exam dated 2024-09-05): Right C7 dermatomal numbness and tingling, diminished tricep reflex, and right hand grip strength of 4+/5.
- Imaging Findings (MRI dated 2024-09-01): C6-C7 disc herniation, right C7 nerve root compression, and moderate foraminal stenosis.

III. LITERAL APPLICATION OF CC-SPINE-2024 CRITERIA
Under CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2, prior authorization for cervical spinal surgery (CPT 22551) requires that ALL medical necessity criteria in Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, and 2.4 are satisfied.

1. Section 2.1 — Diagnosis Requirement: MET
The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (C6-C7) with correlating nerve root compression and objective neurological deficits (right C7 dermatomal numbness, diminished tricep reflex, and 4+/5 grip strength), satisfying Section 2.1(a) and Section 2.1(c). The supporting MRI was performed on 2024-09-01, which is within the required 6-month window.

2. Section 2.2 — Conservative Treatment Requirement: NOT MET
Section 2.2 mandates that the member complete a documented course of conservative treatment prior to surgical authorization, consisting of ALL of the following:

   (a) Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)): NOT MET
       - Requirement: Minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes directed toward the cervical condition.
       - Clinical Record: The member completed 5 weeks of physical therapy (total of 15 sessions).
       - Shortfall: There is a literal 1-week shortfall in the duration of supervised physical therapy.

   (b) Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)): NOT MET
       - Requirement: Trial of at least two specified agents (NSAIDs/COX-2 inhibitors, muscle relaxants, neuropathic agents, or oral corticosteroids) for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
       - Clinical Record: 
         1. Meloxicam (NSAID): 15mg daily for 3 months (compliant).
         2. Prednisone taper (oral corticosteroid): 2 weeks (non-compliant).
       - Shortfall: While the member trialed two appropriate classes of drugs, the second agent (Prednisone) was only trialed for 2 weeks, resulting in a 2-week duration shortfall under the literal 4-week minimum requirement.

   (c) Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)): NOT MET
       - Requirement: At least one interventional treatment, defined as a cervical epidural steroid injection (minimum 1), a cervical medial branch block, or a trigger point injection series.
       - Clinical Record: No interventional treatments have been performed (noted as null).
       - Shortfall: There is a complete shortfall of this requirement, with zero interventional treatments attempted.

3. Section 2.2 — Exception 2.2(A) (Myelopathy Pathway): NOT APPLICABLE
The clinical record was evaluated to determine if the modified conservative treatment requirements under the Myelopathy Pathway apply:
   - Under Exception 2.2(A), the physical therapy requirement is reduced to 4 weeks, and the interventional treatment requirement is waived.
   - However, the "NOTE" in Section 2.2 explicitly states: "The myelopathy exception applies only when myelopathy is the PRIMARY diagnosis. Cases where myelopathy is documented as a finding but radiculopathy is the primary complaint are subject to standard conservative treatment requirements."
   - The member's primary presenting diagnosis is cervical radiculopathy C6-C7. There is no clinical or imaging evidence of cervical myelopathy (no spinal cord compression, no myelomalacia, and no myelopathic symptoms such as gait disturbance, bilateral upper extremity weakness, hyperreflexia, positive Hoffman sign, or bowel/bladder dysfunction).
   - Therefore, the Myelopathy Pathway exception does not apply, and the member remains subject to the standard conservative treatment requirements of Section 2.2.

4. Section 2.3 — Imaging Requirement: MET
The MRI dated 2024-09-01 demonstrates structural pathology (C6-C7 disc herniation and right C7 nerve root compression) consistent with the member's clinical presentation and proposed surgical level. This satisfies Section 2.3.

5. Section 2.4 — Physician Documentation: PARTIALLY MET
The treating physician (Dr. Robert Chen, MD) provided a clinical rationale and declared a functional plateau in the appeal letter. However, under the literal terms of CC-SPINE-2024, a formal plateau is not documented within the physical therapy records themselves, and the literal 6-week duration requirement remains unfulfilled.

IV. SECTION 3 — EXCLUSIONS APPLICABILITY
Under Section 3.2 of CC-SPINE-2024, authorization must be denied if:
"Conservative treatment requirements have not been met as specified in Section 2.2, unless myelopathy exception applies."

Because the member has failed to meet the literal requirements of Section 2.2(a) (5 weeks completed vs. 6 weeks required), Section 2.2(b) (Prednisone trialed for 2 weeks vs. 4 weeks required), and Section 2.2(c) (0 injections vs. 1 required), and because the Myelopathy Pathway exception does not apply, a denial is contractually mandated under Section 3.2.

V. CONCLUSION OF PLAN CRITERIA REVIEW
Under a strict, literal application of HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, the member does not meet the criteria for surgical authorization due to the following specific clinical shortfalls:
1. A 1-week shortfall in supervised physical therapy (Section 2.2(a)).
2. A 2-week duration shortfall in the second pharmacotherapy trial (Section 2.2(b)).
3. A complete absence of required interventional treatment (Section 2.2(c)).

Consequently, under Section 3.2, the prior authorization request for CPT 22551 is non-compliant with the plan's internal medical necessity criteria.
