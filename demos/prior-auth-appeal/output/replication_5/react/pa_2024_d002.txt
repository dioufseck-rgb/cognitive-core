### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The prior authorization denial for an ACDF at C5-C6 for Michael Green is **OVERTURNED**.

This determination is based on:
1.  **Procedural Defects:** The initial denial notice was procedurally defective by failing to include the referenced clinical criteria document and the Independent Medical Review (IMR) notice, in violation of California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b).
2.  **Misapplication of Plan Criteria:** A review of the clinical record against HealthFirst's own Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) demonstrates that the member meets all specified medical necessity criteria for cervical radiculopathy, contrary to the plan's denial reason.
3.  **Adherence to State Law Regarding Physical Therapy:** The member's documented functional plateau after 7 weeks of physical therapy satisfies the plan's requirements and aligns with California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2), which prohibits denying approval based on PT duration when a functional plateau has been reached.
4.  **Consistency with Clinical Standards:** The requested procedure is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for cervical radiculopathy refractory to conservative management.

---

### REVIEW ANALYSIS

This review applies the hierarchy of authority: State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.

**1. Procedural Adequacy of Denial Notice (State Law: CHSC § 1374.31(b))**
The initial denial notice dated 2024-11-08 explicitly referenced an "attached criteria document" which was reportedly not attached, and the "IMR notice not included." California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b) states that a denial notice failing to specify the clinical criteria applied or the specific clinical reason for the denial is "DEFECTIVE." The failure to provide the referenced criteria document, which is fundamental to understanding the denial, renders the denial procedurally inadequate. Furthermore, the omission of the IMR notice is a clear statutory violation. These procedural defects alone are sufficient grounds to overturn the denial.

**2. Clinical Presentation and Diagnosis**
The clinical record describes "Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment."
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-20):** "C5-C6 large disc herniation, moderate left C6 nerve root compression. No cord contact."
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-09-25):** "Left C6 dermatomal numbness, bicep reflex diminished left, bicep weakness 4/5 left."
This presentation (unilateral symptoms, nerve root compression, no spinal cord contact, no listed upper motor neuron signs) is consistent with **cervical radiculopathy**, not cervical myelopathy. This distinction is crucial as conservative treatment requirements and surgical indications differ significantly between these conditions.

**3. Application of Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)**

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:**
    *   The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (2.1(a)) and cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit (2.1(c)), supported by MRI demonstrating C5-C6 large disc herniation and moderate left C6 nerve root compression, consistent with symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion:** This criterion is met.

*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   The plan's "MYELOPATHY EXCEPTION 2.2(A)" does *not* apply here, as the primary diagnosis is radiculopathy, not myelopathy. Therefore, the standard conservative treatment requirements apply.
    *   **(a) Physical therapy:** Requires "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes."
        *   The member completed 7 weeks of PT (21 sessions), exceeding the 6-week minimum.
        *   PT notes document "plateau at week 6. Radicular symptoms persist." This demonstrates that a functional outcome (plateau) was reached, despite persistent symptoms.
        *   **Conclusion:** This criterion is met.
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Requires "trial of at least two of the following for minimum 4 weeks each."
        *   Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (partial relief).
        *   Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months (inadequate).
        *   **Conclusion:** This criterion is met (two agents, duration met).
    *   **(c) Interventional treatment:** Requires "one of the following... Cervical epidural steroid injection (minimum 1)."
        *   The member received a Left C6 epidural on 2024-10-28, which provided 40% relief but returned to baseline in 3 weeks.
        *   **Conclusion:** This criterion is met.

*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:**
    *   Current MRI (2024-09-20, within 6 months) demonstrates structural pathology (C5-C6 large disc herniation with C6 nerve root compression) consistent with the member's symptoms and proposed surgical level.
    *   **Conclusion:** This criterion is met.

**Summary of Plan Criteria Application:** Based on the detailed review of the clinical record against HealthFirst's own criteria, the member **does meet all applicable medical necessity criteria** for the requested ACDF C5-C6. The plan's statement that the "Member does not meet criteria" is unsupported by the evidence and the plan's own policy.

**4. Application of State Law (CIC § 10169.5)**
California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a) explicitly states that a health insurer "shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where... (2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." The clinical record clearly documents that the member completed 7 weeks of PT and reached a functional plateau at week 6. This state law reinforces that the PT requirement has been satisfied.

**5. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021)**
For cervical radiculopathy, generally accepted clinical guidelines support surgical intervention after an adequate trial of conservative treatment.
*   The AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) identify "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" as a TIER 2 "REASONABLE INDICATION" for surgery.
*   Michael Green completed 7 weeks of PT (with documented plateau), two pharmacotherapy trials (4 months Gabapentin, 3 months Naproxen), and one interventional epidural injection which only provided temporary, partial relief. His symptoms (numbness, weakness) persist. This duration and breadth of conservative treatment, combined with persistent symptoms, aligns with clinical standards for surgical consideration.
*   **Conclusion:** The requested procedure is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for cervical radiculopathy refractory to conservative management.

### CONFLICT RESOLUTION AND CONCLUSION

The initial denial is fundamentally flawed due to multiple reasons, following the hierarchy:

1.  **State Law Violation:** The denial is procedurally defective for failing to provide the referenced clinical criteria and the IMR notice, directly violating CHSC § 1374.31(b). This alone is sufficient to overturn.
2.  **Misapplication of Plan Criteria:** Even if the denial were procedurally adequate, the plan's stated reason for denial ("Member does not meet criteria") is factually incorrect. The member's clinical record demonstrates that all criteria outlined in HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 for cervical radiculopathy, including conservative treatment, have been met. Specifically, the member's 7 weeks of PT with a documented plateau meets criterion 2.2(a) and is further supported by CIC § 10169.5(a)(2).
3.  **Clinical Standards:** The requested procedure is medically necessary and appropriate under generally accepted clinical guidelines for cervical radiculopathy after an unsuccessful course of conservative management.

Therefore, the denial is not supported by the plan's own criteria, state law, or clinical standards.

### DISPOSITION

**OVERTURNED**

### WARRANT

The denial for CPT 22551 (ACDF C5-C6) for Michael Green is OVERTURNED. The denial notice was procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b) due to the failure to provide the referenced clinical criteria document and the IMR notice. On the merits, a review of the clinical record against HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 demonstrates that Michael Green meets all medical necessity requirements for cervical radiculopathy, including a confirmed diagnosis (2.1(a), 2.1(c)), current imaging (2.3), and completion of a documented course of conservative treatment (2.2). Specifically, the member completed 7 weeks of physical therapy with a documented functional plateau, satisfying plan criterion 2.2(a) and supported by California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2). Additionally, trials of two pharmacotherapy agents for greater than 4 weeks each (Gabapentin, Naproxen) and one interventional procedure (Left C6 Epidural) were completed, meeting criteria 2.2(b) and 2.2(c). The requested ACDF is consistent with generally accepted clinical standards for cervical radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after an adequate trial of conservative therapy (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023).
