**DISPOSITION: PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**

**WARRANT:**

Upon comprehensive review of the prior authorization appeal for Linda Park, including the submitted clinical record, HealthFirst's clinical criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), relevant California state law, and generally accepted clinical standards, the initial denial for a two-level ACDF at C5-C6 and C6-C7 is **partially overturned**. Surgical authorization is granted for a **single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6**. The request for surgical intervention at C6-C7 remains denied.

**I. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
The clinical record clearly indicates that Linda Park's primary diagnosis is **Cervical Radiculopathy C5-C6**. The neurological examination dated 2024-09-05 documents "Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, bicep reflex diminished right, grip strength 4/5 right." Crucially, it explicitly states "No C7 findings documented. No bilateral findings. No upper motor neuron signs." This confirms the absence of cervical myelopathy as the primary diagnosis, which is a critical distinction for applying appropriate clinical standards and plan criteria (Clinical Standard, Section 1.1).

**II. Application of Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024):**

1.  **Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1):**
    *   **C5-C6:** The member meets criteria 2.1(a) for Cervical Radiculopathy and 2.1(c) for Cervical Disc Herniation with Objective Neurological Deficit. The MRI findings of a "large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, moderate foraminal stenosis" at C5-C6 directly correlate with the documented C6 radicular symptoms and neurological deficits.
    *   **C6-C7:** The member does *not* meet criteria for C6-C7. Imaging shows "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable," and the neurological exam explicitly states "No C7 findings documented." Thus, there is no confirmed diagnosis of radiculopathy or myelopathy attributable to this level.

2.  **Conservative Treatment Requirement (Section 2.2):**
    *   The member has fulfilled the standard conservative treatment requirements. Eight weeks of supervised physical therapy were completed, exceeding the 6-week minimum (2.2(a)). Two pharmacotherapy agents (Gabapentin for 4 months, Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks) were trialed (2.2(b)). One interventional treatment (Right C6 epidural) was performed (2.2(c)). The documentation indicates C6 radicular symptoms persist, and the epidural provided only 50% temporary relief, which fully returned within 4 weeks, demonstrating failure of conservative management for the C5-C6 pathology.
    *   **Myelopathy Exception 2.2(A):** This exception is not applicable as myelopathy is not the primary diagnosis, as explicitly stated in the plan criteria: "The myelopathy exception applies only when myelopathy is the PRIMARY diagnosis."

3.  **Imaging Requirement (Section 2.3):**
    *   **C5-C6:** Current imaging demonstrates structural pathology consistent with symptoms (large disc herniation with nerve root compression correlating to C6 radicular symptoms). This level meets the imaging criteria.
    *   **C6-C7:** Imaging does not demonstrate structural pathology consistent with symptoms (no C7 symptoms, mild degenerative changes without nerve root compression). This level does not meet the imaging criteria.

**III. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**

1.  **CHSC § 1374.31(b) — Denial Notice Adequacy:** The initial denial notice identified a reason code and clinical rationale but failed to specify the *clinical criteria applied* (e.g., specific sections of HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024). This constitutes a procedural defect in the denial notice. While this defect is noted, it does not prevent a medical necessity determination in this appeal.
2.  **CIC § 10169.5 — Physical Therapy as Prerequisite:** The member completed 8 weeks of PT, satisfying the plan's 6-week minimum. Furthermore, given the objective evidence of structural neurological compromise at C5-C6 (large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression and objective neurological deficits), subdivision (a)(3) of this statute would support that further PT would be unlikely to address the condition, thus allowing surgical approval even if the PT duration was an issue.
3.  **DMHC APL 22-014 — Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions:** This All-Plan Letter, which addresses exceptions for time-sensitive neurological conditions like myelopathy with myelomalacia, is not applicable in this case because the member does not have myelopathy with myelomalacia (Clinical Record, Neurological Exam and Imaging findings; Clinical Standard, Section 1.2).
4.  **CHSC § 1374.32(b) — IMR Standard (Clinical Standard Governs):** This provision mandates that if a service meets generally accepted standards of medical practice, it shall be approved regardless of the plan's internal criteria if they are more restrictive. This reinforces the importance of the clinical standard, especially where it might conflict with plan-specific rules.

**IV. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

1.  **C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   The member presents with symptomatic cervical radiculopathy (C6 distribution) with objective neurological deficits and a correlating structural lesion (large disc herniation, moderate-severe nerve root compression) on MRI (Clinical Record).
    *   Conservative treatment has been adequately trialed for 8 weeks of PT, 4 months of neuropathic agents, and an epidural, with persistent symptoms and only temporary relief.
    *   According to the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023 Update), surgical intervention for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment is considered a **TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION** for ACDF (Clinical Standard, Section 3.1). This standard fully supports surgical decompression and fusion at C5-C6.

2.  **C6-C7 Level (Prophylactic Fusion):**
    *   The surgeon's rationale for including C6-C7 is to "prophylactically stabilize C6-C7 to prevent adjacent segment disease" (Clinical Record).
    *   The clinical record indicates only "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable" at C6-C7, and importantly, "No C7 findings documented" on neurological exam. The level is asymptomatic and lacks significant pathology consistent with nerve root or cord compression.
    *   Generally accepted clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS, NASS, AO Spine) do not support prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic, non-compressive vertebral level based solely on mild degenerative changes to prevent adjacent segment disease (Clinical Standard, Section 3.1, 3.3). While adjacent segment disease is a recognized concern, extending fusion to an otherwise asymptomatic and clinically unimplicated level is not considered medically necessary under current evidence-based guidelines for this presentation.

**V. Conclusion and Resolution:**

Based on the application of the hierarchy (State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria):

*   **For C5-C6:** The requested ACDF meets all plan criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3). It is also strongly supported by generally accepted clinical standards as a reasonable indication for persistent radiculopathy after failed conservative therapy (AANS/CNS 2023, Tier 2). State law (CIC 10169.5) further supports that the conservative treatment requirement has been met and exceeded, and that continued conservative therapy would not address the structural neurological compromise.
*   **For C6-C7:** The requested ACDF does not meet HealthFirst's clinical criteria for diagnosis or imaging correlating to symptoms. Furthermore, it is not supported by generally accepted clinical standards for prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic level with only mild degenerative changes. The state regulatory framework does not provide an override for this specific situation.

Therefore, the denial of the two-level ACDF is partially overturned. A single-level ACDF at C5-C6 is medically necessary and meets criteria for approval.

---

**ACTION:**
Authorize CPT code 22551 for a single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6. The component of the request for C6-C7 fusion is denied.
