**Procedure Requested:** Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) C5-C6 and C6-C7, two levels (CPT 22551)

**Facility:** UCLA Medical Center

**Introduction:**
This letter serves as the final determination for the prior authorization appeal request regarding a two-level ACDF for Linda Park. The appeal has been thoroughly reviewed against the HealthFirst clinical coverage criteria, applicable California state laws and regulations, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice.

**Summary of Clinical Information:**
Linda Park, a 45-year-old female, presents with cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6.
*   **Presenting Diagnosis:** Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with adjacent C6-C7 degenerative disease.
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-01):** C5-C6 shows a large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis, clearly correlating with C6 radicular symptoms. C6-C7 shows mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, and no cord contact or identifiable nerve root compression. No myelomalacia (T2 cord signal change) is documented.
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-09-05):** Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, diminished right bicep reflex, and 4/5 right grip strength. No C7 findings, bilateral findings, or upper motor neuron signs (such as Hoffman's sign or hyperreflexia) were documented.
*   **Conservative Treatment:**
    *   Physical Therapy: Completed 8 weeks (24 sessions), with persistent C6 radicular symptoms.
    *   Pharmacotherapy: Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (partial relief) and Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks (minimal benefit).
    *   Interventional: Right C6 epidural steroid injection (2024-10-01) provided 50% temporary relief of C6 symptoms, which returned by week 4.
*   **Surgeon Note:** Dr. Wilson recommends two-level fusion to address C5-C6 pathology and prophylactically stabilize C6-C7 to prevent adjacent segment disease.

**Summary of Denial Reason (Initial Review, 2024-10-28):**
The initial prior authorization request was denied as "Two-level ACDF not medically necessary. Imaging shows pathology at C5-C6 only. C6-C7 shows mild degenerative changes without significant compression or neurological deficit attributable to this level." (Reason Code MED-003, reviewed by Dr. Alan Foster, MD).

**Appeal Basis:**
The member's appeal asserts that criteria are met for at least the C5-C6 level, the surgeon believes both levels should be addressed to prevent adjacent segment disease, and 6 weeks of PT, pharmacotherapy, and injection have been completed.

**Review Analysis:**

This review applies the three-source hierarchy: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.**

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
Based on the neurological examination (unilateral C6 dermatomal symptoms, diminished reflex, weakness, absence of bilateral symptoms or upper motor neuron signs) and imaging findings (nerve root compression at C5-C6, no cord contact at C6-C7, no myelomalacia), Linda Park's primary clinical presentation is **Cervical Radiculopathy at C5-C6**. There is no clinical or imaging evidence to support a diagnosis of cervical myelopathy as defined by HealthFirst Plan Criteria (Section 2.1(b)) or generally accepted clinical standards (Source 3, Section 1.1). Therefore, the myelopathy exception pathways (HealthFirst Plan Criteria Section 2.2(A); DMHC APL 22-014) are not applicable to this case.

**2. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **For C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Member meets criterion 2.1(a) (Cervical radiculopathy with C6 radicular symptoms) and 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit) based on MRI and neurological exam. **MET.**
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:**
        *   (a) Physical therapy: Member completed 8 weeks of PT, exceeding the 6-week minimum. **MET.**
        *   (b) Pharmacotherapy: Member completed trials of Gabapentin (4 months) and Cyclobenzaprine (6 weeks), meeting the requirement for two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. **MET.**
        *   (c) Interventional treatment: Member received a C6 epidural steroid injection. **MET.**
        *   The conservative treatment requirements for C5-C6 radiculopathy are fully met.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Current MRI demonstrates C5-C6 disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, consistent with C6 radicular symptoms. **MET.**
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** The C5-C6 level satisfies all applicable HealthFirst plan criteria for ACDF.

*   **For C6-C7 Level (Prophylactic):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** The imaging shows "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable," and the neurological exam documents "No C7 findings." This does not meet the diagnosis requirement for radiculopathy or myelopathy at C6-C7. **NOT MET.**
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Imaging does not demonstrate structural pathology at C6-C7 consistent with clinical symptoms, as no C7 symptoms are documented. **NOT MET.**
    *   **3.3 Exclusions:** The request for the C6-C7 level falls under Exclusion 3.3, as "Imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with clinical presentation." **APPLIES.**
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** The C6-C7 level does not satisfy HealthFirst plan criteria for ACDF.

**3. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**

*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** The member completed 8 weeks of physical therapy, and symptoms persisted ("C6 radicular symptoms persist"). This satisfies CIC § 10169.5(a)(2) by demonstrating that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms, which would preclude denial based solely on PT completion if it were otherwise unmet.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (Standard of Review for IMR):** This statute mandates that services meeting "generally accepted standards of medical practice" SHALL be approved, regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive. This provision is critical for resolving any conflicts between plan criteria and clinical standards.
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):** This APL is primarily directed at myelopathy cases with objective cord compression and myelomalacia. Since myelopathy is not identified in this case, the specific conservative treatment waivers within this APL do not apply directly. However, the general principle that plans shall not apply internal criteria more restrictive than generally accepted standards (II.3) remains relevant.

**4. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **For C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   The clinical presentation of C6 radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit (diminished reflex, weakness) and clear correlation on MRI (large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression) meets generally accepted indications for ACDF.
    *   Conservative treatment (8 weeks PT, 4 months Gabapentin, 6 weeks Cyclobenzaprine, epidural injection with temporary relief) is consistent with and has exceeded recommended trials (Source 3, Section 2.1, Summary Table for Radiculopathy: "6-12 weeks" PT). Persistent symptoms indicate failure of conservative management.
    *   This falls under TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION for ACDF (Source 3, Section 3.1) for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" or "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care."
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** The C5-C6 level is medically necessary under generally accepted clinical standards.

*   **For C6-C7 Level (Prophylactic):**
    *   The request for fusion at C6-C7 is based on the surgeon's recommendation to prophylactically stabilize the segment to prevent adjacent segment disease.
    *   Generally accepted clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021) **do not support prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic, non-compressive vertebral level** to prevent adjacent segment disease. Surgical indications in these guidelines require correlating symptoms and objective pathology.
    *   The C6-C7 level, with only mild degenerative changes, no nerve root compression, and no associated C7 symptoms, falls under TIER 3 — SURGERY NOT INDICATED for "Imaging findings without correlating symptoms" (Source 3, Section 3.1).
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** The C6-C7 level is NOT medically necessary under generally accepted clinical standards.

**5. Hierarchy Resolution:**

*   For the C5-C6 level, both HealthFirst Plan Criteria and generally accepted Clinical Standards support medical necessity. State laws regarding conservative treatment requirements are also met.
*   For the C6-C7 level, neither HealthFirst Plan Criteria nor generally accepted Clinical Standards support medical necessity for prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic segment. The plan's denial for this level is consistent with both plan criteria and clinical standards.
*   The initial denial stated, "Two-level ACDF not medically necessary," which is an overall denial of the requested procedure. However, the review clearly establishes medical necessity for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6. A denial of the entire two-level request when one level is medically indicated is not appropriate.

**Disposition:**
**OVERTURNED.**

**Warrant:**
The denial for the two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) for Linda Park is OVERTURNED. While the requested second level at C6-C7 is not medically necessary, the primary level at C5-C6 is medically necessary.

1.  **Medical Necessity for C5-C6:** Linda Park meets all HealthFirst Health Plan criteria (Section 2.1(a/c), 2.2, 2.3) for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6 due to symptomatic C6 radiculopathy. Her documented course of conservative treatment (8 weeks of physical therapy with persistent symptoms indicating a functional plateau, two pharmacotherapy agents for appropriate durations, and an interventional injection providing only temporary relief) has failed to resolve her symptoms. This is consistent with and exceeds the requirements of California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2). Furthermore, the clinical presentation and failed conservative treatment meet Tier 2 "Reasonable Indication" for ACDF under generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021 guidelines, Source 3, Section 3.1).

2.  **Lack of Medical Necessity for C6-C7 (Prophylactic):** The requested fusion at C6-C7 for prophylactic prevention of adjacent segment disease is not medically necessary. The clinical record, including imaging, does not demonstrate objective pathology at C6-C7 consistent with clinical symptoms (no C7 symptoms, no nerve root compression, only mild degenerative changes on MRI). This fails HealthFirst Plan Criteria Section 2.1 and 2.3 and falls under Exclusion 3.3. Importantly, generally accepted clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021) **do not support prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic, non-compressive level** to prevent adjacent segment disease. This constitutes a Tier 3 "Surgery Not Indicated" per clinical standards (Source 3, Section 3.1).

Therefore, the plan is directed to authorize a single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6.
