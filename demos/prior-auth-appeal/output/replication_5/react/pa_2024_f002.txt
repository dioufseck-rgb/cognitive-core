**SUBJECT: APPEAL DECISION — Prior Authorization Denial for ACDF C5-C6**

Dear Mr. Clark, Dr. Nguyen, and Cedars-Sinai Medical Center,

This letter serves as the HealthFirst Health Plan appeal determination for the prior authorization request for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 for Mr. Steven Clark. We have thoroughly reviewed the submitted appeal, the original prior authorization request, the denial documentation, and all provided clinical records in accordance with HealthFirst Health Plan's clinical criteria, applicable California state laws and regulations, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice.

**I. Original Denial Summary:**
The initial prior authorization request for CPT 22551 was denied on 2024-10-10 under reason code MED-003, citing "Conflicting physical therapy documentation." The denial stated that records from PT provider 1 (Riverside PT) showed 6 weeks, while records submitted by the member from PT provider 2 (Coastal PT) showed 4 weeks, leading to uncertainty regarding the total verifiable duration of physical therapy. The plan required verification of claimed PT duration before approval.

**II. Appeal Basis and New Information:**
The appeal submitted on behalf of Mr. Clark clarified that:
1.  Both Riverside PT and Coastal PT records are authentic.
2.  The discrepancy was due to an incomplete initial submission of Coastal PT records, where the final 2 weeks were faxed separately.
3.  Full Coastal PT records, documenting 6 weeks of therapy, are now enclosed with the appeal.

This new documentation fully resolves the original denial reason regarding conflicting and unverifiable physical therapy duration.

**III. Clinical Review and Determination:**

Our review proceeded through a hierarchical application of: 1) California State Law, 2) Clinical Standards, and 3) HealthFirst Plan Criteria.

**A. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
Based on the clinical record, Mr. Clark's presenting diagnosis is "Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment."
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-08-15):** C5-C6 large disc herniation, severe right C6 nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis. No mention of spinal cord compression or myelomalacia.
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-08-20):** Right C6 dermatomal numbness, bicep weakness 4/5 right, bicep reflex absent right, positive Spurling right. These are classic signs of *radiculopathy* (nerve root compression with unilateral, dermatomal, lower motor neuron symptoms).
*   The clinical presentation does **not** meet the criteria for cervical myelopathy (Section 2.1(b) of HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, or Clinical Standard Section 1.1) which typically involves spinal cord compression, bilateral symptoms, upper motor neuron signs (e.g., hyperreflexia, Hoffman sign), or gait disturbance. Therefore, the myelopathy exception pathways in plan criteria or state regulations are not the primary basis for this review, though general principles may still apply.

**B. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
With the complete documentation provided in the appeal, we re-evaluated the request against HealthFirst's own criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):
*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** Met. Member has confirmed cervical radiculopathy (2.1(a)) with MRI evidence of structural pathology.
*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **(a) Physical therapy:** The member has now documented 12 weeks of supervised physical therapy (6 weeks at Riverside PT, 6 weeks at Coastal PT), significantly exceeding the plan's minimum 6-week requirement. Both physical therapy providers documented a functional plateau, with no improvement in neurological deficits. This criterion is **met**.
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Member completed Gabapentin (5 months) and Duloxetine (3 months), meeting the requirement for at least two neuropathic agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. This criterion is **met**.
    *   **(c) Interventional treatment:** Member completed a Right C6 epidural steroid injection, meeting the requirement for at least one interventional procedure. This criterion is **met**.
*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** Met. MRI within 6 months demonstrates C5-C6 disc herniation and nerve root compression consistent with symptoms.
*   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** Presumed met as this was not a denial factor.

**Conclusion regarding Plan Criteria:** Based on the complete documentation provided in the appeal, Mr. Clark now fully meets all the conservative treatment requirements outlined in HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 for cervical radiculopathy. The original denial reason related to conflicting PT duration has been rectified.

**C. Application of California Regulatory Framework (State Law):**
*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This statute specifies conditions under which a health insurer cannot condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy.
    *   Subdivision (a)(2) states that a plan cannot require PT completion where "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
    *   Mr. Clark has completed 12 weeks of PT, and both physical therapy providers documented that he reached a functional plateau with persistent neurological symptoms. This provision of state law further supports approval, indicating that even if the plan sought to require more PT, it would be overridden by the documented functional plateau.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** While this is an internal appeal, this section establishes that services meeting "generally accepted standards of medical practice" SHALL be approved regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive. This reinforces the importance of clinical standards in the review process.

**D. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
*   **Conservative Treatment Duration for Radiculopathy:** Clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020) indicate that surgery is a "reasonable option" (AANS/CNS TIER 2) for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment. Mr. Clark has undergone 12 weeks of PT, 5 months of Gabapentin, 3 months of Duloxetine, and one interventional injection, with persistent neurological deficits (right C6 numbness, 4/5 bicep weakness, absent bicep reflex) and only temporary/partial relief. This duration and type of conservative treatment align with, and in some aspects exceed, generally accepted clinical guidelines prior to surgical consideration for radiculopathy.
*   **Functional Plateau:** The documentation of functional plateau by both physical therapists, noting no improvement in neurological deficit despite 12 weeks of therapy, is consistent with the clinical definition of a functional plateau (less than 10% improvement over 3 sessions OR therapist documents maximum therapeutic benefit reached, per Clinical Reference Summary Section 5).
*   **Surgical Indication:** Given the persistent and functionally impairing radiculopathy, objective neurological deficits, imaging correlation, and failure of an extensive conservative treatment trial, Mr. Clark meets the generally accepted clinical indications for ACDF for cervical radiculopathy.

**IV. Resolution of Conflicts:**
There are no conflicts among the sources in this case. The appeal information has clarified the initial denial reason. Upon review of the complete information, Mr. Clark meets all HealthFirst plan criteria for the requested ACDF. Furthermore, California state law (CIC 10169.5) and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice independently support the medical necessity of the requested service given the documented functional plateau after an extensive and failed conservative treatment regimen for his cervical radiculopathy.

**V. Determination:**

Based on the comprehensive review of the submitted appeal, the full clinical record, HealthFirst's medical policy, California state law, and generally accepted clinical guidelines, the denial for prior authorization is hereby:

**OVERTURNED.**

**Warrant:**
The original denial was based on conflicting and unverifiable physical therapy documentation. The appeal successfully resolved this issue by providing complete records from Coastal Physical Therapy, which, when combined with Riverside Physical Therapy records, confirm a total of 12 weeks of documented physical therapy. With this updated information, Mr. Clark has now met or exceeded all conservative treatment requirements as per HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, including the minimum 6 weeks of PT, two pharmacotherapy agents for 4+ weeks, and one interventional procedure. Furthermore, California Insurance Code § 10169.5 prohibits conditioning surgery approval on further physical therapy when a functional plateau has been reached without adequate resolution of symptoms, which is clearly documented in Mr. Clark's case after 12 weeks of therapy. Generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice also support surgical intervention for persistent cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits after a comprehensive 6-12 week trial of conservative treatment. Therefore, the requested ACDF C5-C6 is medically necessary.
