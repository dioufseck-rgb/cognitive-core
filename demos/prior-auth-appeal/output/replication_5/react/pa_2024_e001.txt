**Case ID:** PA-2024-E001
**Member Name:** Victor Patel

**Plan:** HealthFirst PPO Gold — Group Plan 77441
**Requesting Provider:** Dr. Lisa Park, MD — Spine Surgery
**Procedure Requested:** ACDF C5-C6 and C6-C7, two levels (CPT 22551 + 22552)
**Date of Initial Denial:** 2024-11-01
**Reviewer:** Appeals Review Committee

**Disposition:** **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**

**Determination:**
The requested two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) for C5-C6 and C6-C7 (CPT 22552) is **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**. Surgical authorization for a **one-level ACDF at C5-C6 (CPT 22551)** is medically necessary and approved. The addition of the C6-C7 level to the fusion is not supported by the clinical record or generally accepted clinical standards.

**Warrant:**

1.  **Procedural Adequacy of Denial:** The initial denial notice was procedurally adequate, stating the specific reason (two-level fusion not supported, C6-C7 mild changes, one level meets/one does not) and implicitly citing lack of medical necessity per plan criteria, consistent with CHSC § 1374.31(b).

2.  **Assessment of C5-C6 Level:**
    *   **Clinical Record:** The member presents with clear signs and symptoms of C6 radiculopathy, including right bicep weakness (3+/5), absent right bicep reflex, and C6 dermatomal numbness/tingling.
    *   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-15):** Imaging confirms significant structural pathology at C5-C6, demonstrating a "large disc herniation, moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis."
    *   **Conservative Treatment:** The member has completed an 8-week course of physical therapy (24 sessions), a trial of two pharmacotherapy agents (Pregabalin for 4 months, Naproxen for 3 months), and a right C6 epidural steroid injection with good but temporary relief. Despite this, C6 symptoms persist.
    *   **Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024):** The C5-C6 level meets the diagnosis requirement for cervical radiculopathy (2.1a), the conservative treatment requirement (2.2), and the imaging requirement (2.3).
    *   **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):** This presentation meets Tier 2 "Reasonable Indication" for surgery (Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx).
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** Surgical intervention at the C5-C6 level is medically necessary.

3.  **Assessment of C6-C7 Level:**
    *   **Clinical Record:** Neurological exam notes "right tricep reflex slightly diminished" but "No definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms." The physician noted "mild C7 symptoms possibly present but not clearly documented." Physical therapy notes from week 8 indicate "C7 symptoms: minimal and possibly resolving."
    *   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-15):** Imaging findings for C6-C7 describe a "small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, no nerve root compression identifiable, no cord contact."
    *   **Conservative Treatment:** While overall conservative treatment was completed, the PT note suggests C7 symptoms were minimal and possibly resolving, not persistent and debilitating. No C7-specific interventional treatment was performed.
    *   **Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024):** The C6-C7 level does not meet the diagnosis requirements (2.1a or 2.1c) due to lack of objective neurological deficit or clear nerve root compression, nor the imaging requirement (2.3) for structural pathology *consistent with symptoms* that necessitates surgical intervention.
    *   **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):** Surgical intervention for radiculopathy requires "persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" correlated with objective findings (Tier 2). The C6-C7 level, with minimal/resolving symptoms, no definitive dermatomal involvement, and no clear nerve root compression on imaging, does not meet generally accepted clinical standards for surgical indication. This aligns with Tier 3 "Surgery Not Indicated" for "Imaging findings without correlating symptoms" or "Mild radiculopathy not yet trialed on conservative treatment" (or responding to conservative treatment).
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** Surgical intervention at the C6-C7 level is not medically necessary.

4.  **Myelopathy Assessment:**
    *   The clinical record (MRI, neurological exam) does not provide objective evidence of spinal cord compression (such as myelomalacia, T2 signal change, or UMN signs like Hoffman reflex, hyperreflexia, or gait disturbance) to support a diagnosis of cervical myelopathy as the primary condition. Therefore, HealthFirst Plan Criteria's Myelopathy Exception 2.2(A), DMHC APL 22-014 related to urgent myelopathy, and AANS/CNS Tier 1 indications for myelopathy are not applicable in this case.

5.  **California Regulatory Framework (Conflict Resolution):**
    *   **CHSC § 1374.32(b):** This provision mandates approval if a service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice" regardless of plan criteria. While the C5-C6 level clearly meets this standard, the C6-C7 level does not. Therefore, the plan's denial for the *two-level* fusion is consistent with generally accepted standards for the C6-C7 level.
    *   **CIC § 10169.5:** This law waives PT requirements under specific conditions. For C5-C6, the "objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address" (a)(3) is met, and symptoms persisted after 8 weeks of PT. For C6-C7, the PT requirement was met (8 weeks), and given the "minimal and possibly resolving" symptoms, the specific waivers are not triggered in a way that would necessitate surgery at this level. The initial denial was not based on insufficient PT duration.

**Summary of Medical Necessity:**
The clinical evidence strongly supports surgical intervention at the C5-C6 level due to persistent radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits and imaging correlation, having failed conservative treatment. However, the evidence for the C6-C7 level is insufficient to meet medical necessity criteria, lacking definitive nerve root compression, clear objective symptoms, or persistence of symptoms after conservative therapy.

**Decision Rationale:**
The original denial was for a "Two-level fusion." While the denial correctly identified that the C6-C7 level did not meet criteria, the overall request for CPT 22552 (which bundles the two levels) was denied. Since a single-level fusion at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) is medically necessary, the denial is partially overturned to authorize the one-level procedure.

**Action:**
HealthFirst Health Plan is directed to authorize CPT 22551 for Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion at the C5-C6 level. The request for fusion at the C6-C7 level is denied.
