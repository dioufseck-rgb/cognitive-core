**DISPOSITION: OVERTURN**

The denial for prior authorization of an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 for Ms. Theresa Kamau is hereby **OVERTURNED**. The requested procedure is deemed medically necessary based on a comprehensive review of the clinical record, HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), California state law, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice.

---

**WARRANT**

This determination is based on the following findings, applying the hierarchy of authority: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.

**1. Procedurally Flawed Denial (California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b)):**
    * The initial denial (dated 2024-11-20) explicitly states its denial basis as "Prior PA history — NOT a valid criterion under CC-SPINE-2024."
    * **Finding:** A denial based on a criterion that the plan itself acknowledges as "NOT a valid criterion" under its own policy is fundamentally flawed. California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b) specifies that a denial notice failing to specify the clinical criteria applied, or the specific clinical reason, is defective. Basing a denial on an explicitly invalid criterion constitutes a procedural defect. This alone warrants overturning the denial.

**2. Member Meets HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    * Even if the denial were procedurally sound, the member's clinical record demonstrates that all applicable criteria within HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 for cervical spinal surgery (CPT 22551) have been met.
    * **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with objective neurological deficit (right C6 dermatomal numbness, absent bicep reflex right, 3+/5 bicep weakness right). The October 2024 MRI demonstrates a large C5-C6 disc herniation with moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression, consistent with symptoms.
    * **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Met.
        * **Physical Therapy:** The member completed a NEW 8-week course of supervised physical therapy (July-September 2024). This exceeds the plan's minimum 6-week requirement. The therapist explicitly documented a "functional plateau: 'no further improvement expected, neurological deficit persistent and worsening.'"
        * **Pharmacotherapy:** The member completed a trial of Gabapentin 600mg TID for 6 months and two courses of Methylprednisolone dose pack. This meets the requirement of at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
        * **Interventional Treatment:** The member underwent a Right C6 Epidural (November 2024). This meets the requirement for one interventional procedure.
        * **Myelopathy Exception:** While the member's condition is primarily severe radiculopathy, not myelopathy, the standard conservative treatment pathway has been fully satisfied.
    * **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. The October 2024 MRI (within 6 months) demonstrates structural pathology (C5-C6 large disc herniation, moderate-severe nerve root compression, new foraminal stenosis) consistent with symptoms and the proposed surgical level.
    * **2.4 Physician Documentation:** The request from Dr. Osei, a spine surgeon, for an ACDF at C5-C6, along with the detailed clinical record, indicates the necessary documentation and rationale for surgical intervention, including functional limitations ("Unable to continue working full-time").

**3. Material Change in Clinical Status (Contradiction to Denial Reason):**
    * The denial's assertion that the "member's cervical pathology has not materially changed since the prior denial" is directly contradicted by the provided clinical record.
    * **Finding:** A new MRI from October 2024 shows an "interval increase in C5-C6 herniation size" and "new foraminal stenosis at C5-C6" compared to the April 2024 MRI. Furthermore, the neurological exam from October 2024 documents "Bicep weakness 3+/5 right \u2014 interval worsening from prior exam," and the member reports increased pain (8/10 at rest) and inability to work full-time. This unequivocally demonstrates a material and progressive worsening of the member's condition since the prior denial in May 2024.

**4. State Law Protection for Completed Conservative Treatment (California Insurance Code § 10169.5):**
    * California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 prohibits a health insurer from conditioning approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and reached a functional plateau, or where the condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that PT cannot address.
    * **Finding:** The member completed an 8-week PT course and reached a documented functional plateau, satisfying CIC § 10169.5(a)(2). Additionally, the presence of a "large disc herniation with moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression" and "interval worsening" of weakness constitutes "objective evidence of structural neurological compromise" that PT cannot address, satisfying CIC § 10169.5(a)(3). These provisions ensure that further conservative treatment would not be a valid basis for denial, reinforcing that the member has met the threshold for surgical consideration.

**5. Medical Necessity Under Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):**
    * The member's clinical presentation meets established criteria for surgical intervention for severe cervical radiculopathy.
    * **Finding:** According to AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023), the member's presentation of progressive neurological deficit (worsening weakness, increased herniation size) despite an adequate course of conservative treatment (8 weeks PT, pharmacotherapy, injection) qualifies as a TIER 1 Strong Indication or TIER 2 Reasonable Indication for surgery. The NASS Guidelines (2020) also support surgical decompression for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms and functional impairment after a failed conservative trial.
    * The clinical evidence emphasizes that delaying surgery for progressive neurological deficits is not supported by evidence and may result in worse outcomes.

---

**CONCLUSION**

The initial denial by HealthFirst was procedurally defective and factually inaccurate regarding the member's clinical status. The member has fulfilled all requirements of the HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 criteria and meets the established medical necessity standards under California law and generally accepted clinical guidelines for ACDF.

Therefore, the prior authorization for ACDF C5-C6 (CPT 22551) for Ms. Theresa Kamau is **OVERTURNED**, and the procedure is approved.
