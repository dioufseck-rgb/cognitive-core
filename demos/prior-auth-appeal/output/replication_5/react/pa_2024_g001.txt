### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This appeal review concerns the denial of prior authorization for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 for Mr. Marco Reyes. The initial denial was based on the claim that the member had completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy (PT) and had no documented functional plateau, thereby failing to meet conservative treatment requirements under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2.

Upon review of the submitted clinical record and appeal basis, it is evident that the initial denial contained significant factual errors and misapplied the plan's own criteria. ### BACKGROUND AND DENIAL REASON

Mr. Marco Reyes, a 49-year-old HealthFirst PPO Gold member, was denied prior authorization for an ACDF at C6-C7. The presenting diagnosis is persistent right-sided cervical radiculopathy at C6-C7.

The initial denial, issued on 2024-11-01 by Dr. Alan Foster, MD (HealthFirst Utilization Management), cited reason code MED-003, stating: "Member does not meet conservative treatment requirements under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2. Physical therapy: member has completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy. Standard pathway requires 6 weeks minimum. Functional plateau not documented. All other criteria remain unmet."

The member's appeal asserts that the denial miscounts PT duration, that 9 weeks of PT were completed across two providers, that both records were submitted with the original request, that functional plateau was explicitly documented, and that all other conservative treatment criteria were met.

### REVIEW AND ANALYSIS

The appeal was reviewed using the established hierarchy: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
Based on the clinical record, the member's diagnosis is unequivocally **Cervical Radiculopathy**. The MRI explicitly notes "No cord contact. No myelomalacia," and the neurological exam states "No bilateral findings. No UMN signs." This distinction is critical as it determines the applicable treatment pathway and conservative treatment requirements. Therefore, the myelopathy exception in CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(A) and related myelopathy-specific guidance (e.g., DMHC APL 22-014 regarding myelomalacia) are not directly applicable to the diagnosis itself, but the general principles of appropriate conservative care, factual accuracy, and state law apply.

**2. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
The initial denial incorrectly claimed the member completed only 3 weeks of PT and that functional plateau was not documented.
*   **Section 2.2(a) Physical Therapy:** The plan requires a "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes."
    *   The member completed a total of **9 weeks** of physical therapy across two providers, exceeding the 6-week minimum. (Clinical Record: physical_therapy_summary, authoritative_source: structured_fields_in_this_record).
    *   A functional plateau was explicitly documented in the Capital PT discharge summary: "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT." (Clinical Record: physical_therapy_provider_2, physical_therapy_summary).
    *   Therefore, the member **fully met** the physical therapy requirement under the plan's standard pathway.
*   **Section 2.2(b) Pharmacotherapy:** The plan requires "at least two... for minimum 4 weeks each."
    *   Gabapentin was used for 4 months. Naproxen was used for 3 months. (Clinical Record: pharmacotherapy).
    *   This criterion is **met**.
*   **Section 2.2(c) Interventional Treatment:** The plan requires "one of the following."
    *   The member underwent a Right C7 epidural steroid injection. (Clinical Record: interventional).
    *   This criterion is **met**.
*   **Section 2.1 Diagnosis & 2.3 Imaging:** These criteria are also **met**, as confirmed by the MRI findings of C6-C7 disc herniation with C7 nerve root compression correlating with the clinical presentation of cervical radiculopathy.

**Conclusion on Plan Criteria:** The member fully meets HealthFirst's own criteria for surgical authorization, including all conservative treatment requirements. The denial was based on demonstrably false factual claims regarding the duration of PT and documentation of functional plateau.

**3. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**
*   **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This state law explicitly states that a health insurer cannot condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where: "(2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms; OR (3) The condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address."
    *   Mr. Reyes meets provision (2): he completed 9 weeks of PT, and his physical therapist explicitly documented a functional plateau with persistent symptoms. This state law **mandates approval** regardless of any plan-imposed minimum PT duration beyond the documented plateau. (Source: California Regulatory Framework, Part III).
*   **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b) (Denial Notice Defective):** A denial notice that relies on factually incorrect information to determine criteria are not met is substantively flawed. While the denial *cited* criteria, its factual basis for non-compliance was erroneous.
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):** While this APL focuses on myelopathy, it reinforces the principle that plans shall not apply internal criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted standards. In this case, the plan's misapplication of its own criteria, leading to a denial despite criteria being met and a functional plateau documented, is contrary to the spirit of appropriate medical necessity review.

**4. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
*   For cervical radiculopathy, generally accepted clinical guidelines (e.g., AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, Section 3.1 Tier 2) indicate surgery as a "reasonable option" for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx."
*   Mr. Reyes has undergone 9 weeks of PT (within the 6-12 week range), two pharmacotherapy agents for 3-4 months, and one epidural injection with only temporary, partial relief. His symptoms (numbness, diminished reflex, weakness) persist.
*   The clinical definition of a functional plateau includes "Therapist documentation that patient has reached maximum therapeutic benefit." (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 5). This is consistent with the Capital PT discharge summary.
*   Therefore, the requested ACDF for persistent radiculopathy after a comprehensive trial of conservative treatment is **medically necessary and consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice.**

**5. Conflict Resolution:**
The initial denial directly contradicts the facts documented in the clinical record and the provisions of state law (CIC § 10169.5). The plan's criteria for conservative treatment, when correctly applied to the actual clinical evidence, are also met. Furthermore, the requested service aligns with established clinical guidelines for persistent cervical radiculopathy.

---

### CONCLUSION

The initial denial of prior authorization for Mr. Marco Reyes's ACDF C6-C7 was based on demonstrably incorrect factual information regarding the duration of physical therapy and the documentation of a functional plateau. The member has completed 9 weeks of physical therapy and a functional plateau was explicitly documented by his therapist. He has also met all other conservative treatment requirements outlined in the HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 policy.

Furthermore, California law (CIC § 10169.5) prohibits plans from denying surgery based on a minimum PT duration once a functional plateau has been reached. The requested procedure is also consistent with generally accepted clinical standards for persistent cervical radiculopathy after failed conservative management.

Therefore, the criteria for medical necessity have been met under the plan's own policy, state law, and generally accepted clinical standards.

---

### DISPOSITION

**OVERTURN DENIAL.**

The prior authorization for ACDF C6-C7 (CPT 22551) for Mr. Marco Reyes is **APPROVED**.
