**Disposition: OVERTURN DENIAL**

**Warrant:**
The initial denial for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C3-C4 was based on the assertion that "imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4 to support surgical intervention" and that "MRI shows mild degenerative changes." This determination, issued on 2024-10-01, failed to account for the crucial findings of the CT myelogram performed on 2024-09-10, which explicitly clarified the pathology obscured by MRI artifact. Upon review of the complete clinical record, including the CT myelogram, the proposed surgery for cervical radiculopathy at C3-C4 meets both HealthFirst's clinical coverage criteria and generally accepted standards of medical practice, as well as California state regulatory requirements.

**Detailed Analysis:**

1.  **Clinical Presentation: Cervical Radiculopathy (C3-C4)**
    *   The member presents with classic symptoms of left C4 radiculopathy, including shoulder and lateral arm numbness, deltoid weakness (3+/5), and a diminished bicep reflex in the left C4 dermatomal distribution, which are new since 2023. These are unilateral, dermatomal symptoms characteristic of nerve root compression.
    *   The clinical record does not indicate symptoms or signs consistent with cervical myelopathy (e.g., bilateral symptoms, upper motor neuron signs like Hoffman's, hyperreflexia, or gait disturbance), nor is myelomalacia noted on imaging. Therefore, the primary diagnosis is cervical radiculopathy.

2.  **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) Review:**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. The member has cervical radiculopathy (2.1a) and objective neurological deficit (2.1c) consistent with left C4 nerve root compression. The CT myelogram supports the diagnosis, showing "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression confirmed. Significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4.
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement (Standard Pathway):** Met.
        *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** The member completed 10 weeks (30 sessions) of physical therapy, exceeding the 6-week minimum. Documentation states "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms," indicating a functional plateau.
        *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** The member completed trials of Pregabalin (6 months, partial relief) and Duloxetine (3 months, minimal benefit), meeting the requirement for two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
        *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** The member received a left C4 epidural steroid injection, which provided good initial relief (60%) but returned to baseline by week 3, demonstrating its limited long-term efficacy. This meets the requirement for one interventional procedure.
        *   *Conclusion:* All standard conservative treatment requirements under Section 2.2 of HealthFirst's criteria have been demonstrably met and have failed to provide lasting relief. The myelopathy exception (2.2A) is not applicable as the primary diagnosis is radiculopathy.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. The CT myelogram (2024-09-10) explicitly demonstrates "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression confirmed. Significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4, directly correlating with the member's symptoms. This imaging definitively overcomes the limitations and "mild degenerative changes" noted on the prior MRI due to hardware artifact, which was the basis of the initial denial.
    *   *Overall Plan Criteria Conclusion:* Based on the full clinical record, including the critical CT myelogram, the member satisfies all criteria outlined in HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 policy for cervical spinal surgery. The initial denial's reasoning regarding insufficient imaging pathology is directly contradicted by the subsequent (but pre-denial) CT myelogram.

3.  **California Regulatory Framework Review:**
    *   **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) — Physical Therapy as Prerequisite:** This section states that a plan cannot condition approval of surgery on PT completion when "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." In this case, the member completed 10 weeks of PT with "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms," fulfilling the definition of a functional plateau. This state law reinforces that further PT is not required.
    *   **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) — Independent Medical Review (IMR) Standard:** This statute mandates that services meeting "generally accepted standards of medical practice" SHALL be approved, regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive. As demonstrated below, the requested surgery meets generally accepted clinical standards.
    *   **DMHC All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014:** While this APL primarily addresses myelopathy, its general principle that "Plans shall not apply internal criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted standards of medical practice" is relevant. The plan's initial denial, based on an incomplete imaging assessment, was not consistent with generally accepted standards once the full imaging picture was available.

4.  **Clinical Standard Knowledge (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021) Review:**
    *   **Distinction between Radiculopathy and Myelopathy:** The clinical guidelines clearly differentiate these conditions and their treatment algorithms. This case is a radiculopathy.
    *   **Conservative Treatment for Radiculopathy:** While conservative treatment is often effective, for radiculopathy with "persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx," surgery becomes a reasonable option (AANS/CNS 2023, Tier 2 indication). The member has undergone 10 weeks of PT with no improvement, trials of two pharmacotherapy agents, and one interventional procedure with only temporary relief. This constitutes an adequate and failed course of conservative treatment.
    *   **Functional Plateau:** The documented "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms" after 10 weeks of PT aligns with the clinical standard definition of a functional plateau, where the patient has reached maximum therapeutic benefit without adequate symptom resolution, particularly neurological symptoms like weakness.
    *   **Surgical Indication:** Given the objective imaging evidence of moderate-severe nerve root compression at the symptomatic level and the documented failure of comprehensive conservative treatment, an ACDF at C3-C4 is a medically necessary and appropriate intervention under generally accepted clinical guidelines for cervical radiculopathy.

**Conclusion:**
The initial denial was based on an incomplete assessment of the imaging evidence. The CT myelogram directly refutes the claim of insufficient pathology and confirms moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression at C3-C4, consistent with the member's persistent and debilitating radicular symptoms. The member has thoroughly completed and failed an extensive course of conservative treatment, meeting both the plan's criteria and general clinical standards for proceeding with surgical intervention. Furthermore, California state law supports the approval of surgery when a functional plateau has been reached with conservative treatment.

Therefore, the denial of prior authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C3-C4) for Angela Morrison is **OVERTURNED**. The procedure is medically necessary and should be approved.
