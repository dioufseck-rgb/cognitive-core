*   **Cervical Radiculopathy Treatment:**
    *   Clinical guidelines clearly differentiate radiculopathy from myelopathy, noting that conservative treatment is typically effective for radiculopathy but surgery is indicated for persistent symptoms after an adequate conservative trial (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 1.1).

*   **Conclusion:** The clinical standard supports surgical intervention for persistent cervical radiculopathy after a failed conservative trial where a functional plateau has been reached.

Given the direct conflict between the plan's denial reason and the clear documentation and overriding state law, the state law governs.

**V. WARRANT/RATIONALE**

The denial of prior authorization for ACDF C6-C7 for Mr. Thomas Reed is **OVERTURNED**.

The plan's denial reason, "PT not yet completed to functional plateau," is inconsistent with both the member's clinical documentation and California state law.

1.  **Failure to Apply Plan's Own Criteria Correctly:** The member completed 7 weeks of physical therapy. The PT discharge summary explicitly states, "functional plateau reached — patient has not improved over final 3 sessions." This documentation precisely meets HealthFirst's internal definition of a "functional plateau" (<10% improvement over 3 sessions OR therapist documents maximum benefit reached, as per HealthFirst's internal knowledge base). Therefore, the plan's assertion that a functional plateau had not been reached is factually incorrect based on its own criteria and the clinical record.
2.  **Violation of California State Law (CIC § 10169.5):** California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2) explicitly states that a health insurer "shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where... The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." Mr. Reed meets these criteria. He completed 7 weeks of PT, and a functional plateau was documented with persistent radicular symptoms. This state law overrides any plan-specific criteria that would require further PT or deny based on an arbitrary duration after a functional plateau has been reached.
3.  **Consistency with Clinical Standards:** Generally accepted clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS, NASS, AO Spine) support surgical intervention for cervical radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after an adequate course of conservative treatment (typically 6-12 weeks) and documented failure to improve, which is indicated by reaching a functional plateau. The member has undergone 7 weeks of PT, 4 months of pharmacotherapy, and an epidural injection, all of which failed to provide sustained relief for his persistent symptoms (pain 7/10, numbness, diminished reflex). This constitutes an adequate and failed conservative trial, supporting surgical consideration per clinical standards.

Based on the explicit documentation of a functional plateau in physical therapy, which directly meets the plan's own definition and, more importantly, triggers the protective provisions of California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2), the denial is unwarranted. The requested ACDF C6-C7 is medically necessary and should be approved.
