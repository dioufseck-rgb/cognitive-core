**SUMMARY DETERMINATION:** Under the governing hierarchy of authority, applying California State Law, generally accepted clinical guidelines, and plan criteria, a **PARTIAL OVERTURN** is issued. The medical record fully supports the medical necessity of a single-level ACDF at C5–C6 (CPT 22551). However, there is no clinical, radiological, or electrodiagnostic evidence of active pathology, spinal cord compression, or nerve root compression at the C6–C7 level. Prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic level to prevent future adjacent segment disease is not supported by generally accepted clinical standards and is not medically necessary.

### SECTION 2: DIAGNOSTIC CLASSIFICATION AND SEVERITY ANALYSIS

The member’s clinical presentation must be classified to determine the appropriate evidence-based clinical pathway.

* **Clinical Presentation:** The clinical record describes a presenting diagnosis of "cervical radiculopathy C5–C6 with adjacent C6–C7 degenerative disease." 
* **Neurological Examination (September 5, 2024):** Reveals right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, a diminished right bicep reflex, and right grip strength of 4/5. 
* **Myelopathy Screening:** There are no upper motor neuron (UMN) signs documented (e.g., no Hoffman sign, no clonus, no hyperreflexia, no gait or balance disturbances, and no bilateral symptoms). 
* **Imaging Findings (MRI, September 1, 2024):** 
  * **C5–C6:** Large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis. This directly correlates with the right-sided C6 clinical radicular symptoms.
  * **C6–C7:** Mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no spinal cord contact, and no identifiable nerve root compression.
* **Classification:** The member has **cervical radiculopathy only**. There is no clinical or radiological evidence of cervical myelopathy (which requires spinal cord compression on MRI, T2-weighted cord signal changes/myelomalacia, and correlating UMN signs). Consequently, the standard conservative treatment pathways for radiculopathy apply.

### SECTION 3: CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT COMPLIANCE & WAIVER EVALUATION

Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 and HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2), standard radiculopathy pathways require a documented trial of conservative care.

1. **Physical Therapy (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a)):** 
   * *Requirement:* Minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy.
   * *Member's History:* Completed 8 weeks of physical therapy (total of 24 sessions) across two providers.
   * *Status:* **MET**. The member exceeded the 6-week requirement. Right-sided C6 radicular symptoms persist, demonstrating a "functional plateau" under CIC § 10169.5(a)(2).
2. **Pharmacotherapy (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(b)):** 
   * *Requirement:* Trial of at least two agents (NSAIDs, muscle relaxants, neuropathic agents, or oral steroids) for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
   * *Member's History:* Trialed Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (partial relief) and Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks (minimal benefit).
   * *Status:* **MET**. Two appropriate agents were trialed for greater than the 4-week minimum.
3. **Interventional Treatment (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(c)):** 
   * *Requirement:* Minimum of one cervical epidural steroid injection, medial branch block, or trigger point series.
   * *Member's History:* Received a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 1, 2024, resulting in 50% temporary relief which fully recurred by week 4.
   * *Status:* **MET**.

**Conservative Care Conclusion:** The member has completed and failed a comprehensive course of conservative treatment targeted at the C5–C6 level. Under CIC § 10169.5(a)(2), the member has achieved a documented functional plateau, and further conservative care is unlikely to benefit the persistent nerve root compression.

### SECTION 4: CLINICAL STANDARD OVERRIDE & LEVEL-BY-LEVEL ANALYSIS

Using the Governing Hierarchy of Authority, we evaluate each requested level against California Law, Generally Accepted Clinical Standards, and Plan Criteria.

#### A. C5–C6 Level (CPT 22551)
* **Plan Criteria CC-SPINE-2024:** Satisfies Section 2.1(a) (confirmed cervical radiculopathy), Section 2.2 (completed physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and interventional requirements), and Section 2.3 (MRI within 6 months showing structural pathology correlating to clinical deficits).
* **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines):** Meets Tier 2 surgical indications (radiculopathy with persistent, correlating neurological deficits—reflex change and 4/5 motor weakness—after failing 6–12 weeks of comprehensive conservative therapy).
* **Determination:** **Approved**. Decompression and fusion at C5–C6 are medically necessary.

#### B. C6–C7 Level (CPT 22552)
* **Plan Criteria CC-SPINE-2024:** Fails Section 2.3 and Section 3.3. Imaging shows only mild degenerative changes (mild bulge, minimal narrowing) without any nerve root or spinal cord compression. There are no correlating clinical symptoms or neurological deficits documented at the C7 level (biceps reflex is C6, grip strength is primarily C7/C8/T1 but is clinically linked to the severe C5–C6 pathology; no specific C7 dermatomal symptoms or triceps reflex deficits are documented).
* **Surgeon's Rationale:** Dr. Wilson recommends addressing the C6–C7 level "prophylactically" to prevent adjacent segment disease (ASD), citing a 25% 5-year incidence.
* **Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
  * Under AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) Tier 3, surgical intervention is **not indicated** for "imaging findings without correlating symptoms" or for prophylactic stabilization of asymptomatic segments. 
  * There is no high-quality clinical evidence supporting the prophylactic fusion of an adjacent level showing only mild, asymptomatic age-related degenerative changes. Fusing an additional functional segment unnecessarily increases surgical risk (such as pseudoarthrosis, dysphagia, and increased adjacent segment stress at the C7–T1 level) without any documented clinical benefit.
  * California Health & Safety Code § 1374.32(b) mandates that decisions are made in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice. These standards do not support prophylactic multi-level fusion.
* **Determination:** **Denied**. Surgical intervention at C6–C7 is not medically necessary.

### SECTION 5: FINAL DETERMINATION AND ACTIONABLE INSTRUCTIONS

Based on the clinical evidence and regulatory analysis, a **PARTIAL OVERTURN** is ordered:

1. **The Health Plan shall AUTHORIZE CPT Code 22551** (Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion, C5–C6, single level) to be performed by Dr. James Wilson at UCLA Medical Center. All clinical criteria, state law provisions (CIC § 10169.5), and clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023) support the necessity of decompressing the severe, symptomatic nerve root compression at C5–C6.
2. **The Health Plan shall MAINTAIN THE DENIAL of CPT Code 22552** (ACDF, C6–C7, additional level). Prophylactic extension of a fusion construct to an asymptomatic level is a direct violation of clinical standards (AANS/CNS Tier 3; NASS 2020) and fails to meet plan coverage criteria (Section 3.3).

The treating surgeon should modify the surgical plan to a single-level C5–C6 anterior cervical discectomy and fusion.
