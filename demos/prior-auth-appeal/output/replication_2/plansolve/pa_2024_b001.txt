### I. GOVERNING HIERARCHY OF AUTHORITY
In accordance with California regulatory requirements, this appeal review resolves any conflicts between plan policies and clinical standards using the following statutory hierarchy:
$$\text{California State Law (CIC, CHSC)} > \text{Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS, AO Spine)} > \text{Plan Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024)}$$
Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b), the Independent Medical Review (IMR) standard dictates that health services must be approved if they are medically necessary under generally accepted standards of medical practice, regardless of whether a plan's internal criteria are more restrictive.

### II. EVALUATION STEPS & FINDINGS

#### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy & Defect Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
* **Objective:** Evaluate the initial denial notice dated October 28, 2024, issued by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, for procedural compliance with California law.
* **Finding:** The initial denial notice detailed an individualized, level-specific clinical explanation regarding the lack of pathology at C6-C7. However, the notice failed to explicitly cite the plan's internal clinical coverage criteria document (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024). Under CHSC § 1374.31(b), a denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied is technically defective.
* **Action:** Because a procedural defect exists, the denial could be technically overturned on procedural grounds. However, to ensure patient safety and prevent an clinically unindicated and potentially harmful multi-level surgery, this reviewer must execute a de novo clinical evaluation under CHSC § 1374.32 to determine the true medical necessity of each requested level.

#### Step 2: Diagnostic Classification (Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy)
* **Objective:** Analyze clinical records, physical examinations, and MRI findings to distinguish the primary pathology.
* **Finding:** 
  * **Clinical Exam (2024-09-05):** Shows right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, diminished right bicep reflex, and 4/5 right grip strength. There are no bilateral upper extremity deficits, no gait disturbances, and no upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (such as Hoffman's sign, hyperreflexia, or clonus) documented.
  * **Imaging (2024-09-01):** Shows no spinal cord compression or myelomalacia (T2 cord signal changes).
* **Action:** The clinical presentation is classified as **Cervical Radiculopathy** (primarily affecting the right C6 nerve root). There is no evidence of Cervical Myelopathy. Therefore, standard conservative treatment requirements (6 weeks of physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and interventional procedures) are clinically appropriate and applicable.

#### Step 3: Application of Conservative Treatment Exemptions (State Law & Clinical Standard Override)
* **Objective:** Determine if the member is exempt from conservative treatment requirements under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 or DMHC APL 22-014.
* **Finding:** The member does not exhibit objective structural spinal cord injury (myelomalacia) or progressive myelopathy that would waive conservative trials under DMHC APL 22-014. However, the clinical records confirm that the member **has fully completed** the standard plan-required conservative trial under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2:
  1. **Physical Therapy:** Completed 8 weeks of physical therapy across two providers (exceeding the plan's 6-week requirement). Right C6 symptoms persist, indicating a functional plateau (CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)).
  2. **Pharmacotherapy:** Trialed Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (partial relief) and Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks (minimal benefit), satisfying the plan's 2-agent, 4-week requirement.
  3. **Interventional Treatment:** Underwent a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 1, 2024, yielding temporary 50% relief, with symptoms fully returning by week 4.
* **Action:** Conservative treatment requirements have been completely and successfully met. The clinical necessity of surgical decompression may now be evaluated for each proposed level.

#### Step 4: Level-Specific Clinical and Imaging Correlation (C5-C6 vs. C6-C7)
* **Objective:** Evaluate the clinical necessity of each level by correlating MRI findings to the clinical examination and symptoms.

##### A. Level C5-C6 Evaluation:
* **Imaging Correlation:** The MRI dated September 1, 2024, demonstrates a large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis at C5-C6.
* **Clinical Correlation:** The physical exam shows right C6 dermatomal numbness/tingling, a diminished right bicep reflex, and 4/5 grip strength. This perfectly correlates with the C5-C6 nerve root pathology.
* **Medical Necessity Status:** **Met.** Single-level surgery at C5-C6 is medically necessary under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a) and generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Tier 2 indication for persistent radiculopathy failing conservative care).

##### B. Level C6-C7 Evaluation:
* **Imaging Correlation:** The MRI shows only a mild disc bulge and minimal foraminal narrowing. There is no cord contact, no canal stenosis, and no identifiable nerve root compression at this level.
* **Clinical Correlation:** There are no C7 dermatomal symptoms or clinical deficits (e.g., triceps weakness, diminished triceps reflex) documented.
* **Surgeon Rationale:** Dr. Wilson recommends fusing C6-C7 "prophylactically" to prevent adjacent segment disease (ASD), citing a 25% 5-year statistical incidence.
* **Clinical Standard Evaluation:** Under generally accepted medical standards (AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines, AO Spine 2021), prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic adjacent level with mild, non-compressive degenerative changes is **not medically necessary**. Fusing a healthy or minimally degenerated segment without active pathology does not improve clinical radicular outcomes, unnecessarily increases surgical time, blood loss, and rigid spinal segments, and accelerates adjacent segment stress further down at C7-T1. It is explicitly excluded under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.3.
* **Medical Necessity Status:** **Not Met.** The request for fusion at C6-C7 is not medically necessary.
