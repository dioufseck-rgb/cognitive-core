### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

The initial denial issued on October 20, 2024, was based on the clinical assertion that the member did not meet conservative treatment requirements because "physical therapy [was] not yet completed to a functional plateau." 

A comprehensive review of the clinical record, physical therapy discharge summaries, and California statutory provisions reveals:
1. **Procedural Defect:** The denial notice failed to cite the specific clinical coverage criteria applied (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024), violating California Health and Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b).
2. **Factual Error & Plan Criteria Compliance:** The member has fully satisfied the standard radiculopathy pathway under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria **CC-SPINE-2024 (Section 2.2)**. The member completed 7 weeks of supervised physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week requirement) and reached a documented functional plateau.
3. **Statutory Protection:** Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5**, a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning surgical approval on additional physical therapy once a member has reached a documented functional plateau. 

Consequently, the denial cannot be sustained.

### **EVALUATION PLAN WALKTHROUGH**

#### **Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Defect Review (CHSC § 1374.31(b))**
Under California Health and Safety Code **CHSC § 1374.31(b)**, any medical necessity denial notice issued by a health plan must specify the exact clinical coverage criteria applied and provide a clear, factually accurate clinical rationale. 
* *Findings:* The denial notice dated October 20, 2024, cited a generic reason code (MED-003) but failed to reference the specific internal clinical coverage guidelines applied (**HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024**). Furthermore, the clinical assertion that physical therapy was "not yet completed to functional plateau" directly contradicts the documented clinical record.
* *Conclusion:* The denial is procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b). In accordance with the California regulatory framework, a defective notice is treated as if no denial was issued. However, to ensure a robust review, we proceed to evaluate the clinical merits.

#### **Step 2: Clinical and Imaging Diagnosis Classification**
A detailed review of Dr. Karen Liu’s clinical findings and the Stanford Medical Center MRI is required to classify the member's pathology:
* *Imaging (MRI dated 08/01/2024):* Shows a C6-C7 disc herniation with mild cord contact, but **no** measurable compression, **no** T2 cord signal changes, and **no** myelomalacia. There is mild right C7 foraminal stenosis.
* *Neurological Exam (dated 08/05/2024):* Identifies right C7 dermatomal distribution numbness and a diminished right tricep reflex. There are **no** bilateral findings, **no** gait disturbances, and **no** upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (e.g., no Hoffman sign, hyperreflexia, or clonus). Pain is rated at 7/10.
* *Classification:* Because there are no objective upper motor neuron signs or MRI findings of spinal cord compression/myelomalacia, the member's condition is classified as **Cervical Radiculopathy (C6-C7)**, not cervical myelopathy.
* *Action:* Skip to Step 5 (Standard Plan Criteria for Radiculopathy).

#### **Step 3: Statutory Waiver of Conservative Treatment (State Law Override)**
* *Findings:* Because the member's primary diagnosis is cervical radiculopathy without objective MRI evidence of severe structural compromise (no myelomalacia/T2 signal change), the automatic statutory waivers under **CIC § 10169.5(a)(3)** and **DMHC APL 22-014** for urgent neurological conditions do not apply at outset. Conservative treatment remains clinically appropriate.
* *Action:* Proceed to Step 5 to evaluate compliance with the plan's conservative therapy criteria.

#### **Step 4: Clinical Standards and Evidence-Based Guidelines Review**
* *Findings:* Under North American Spine Society (NASS) 2020 and AANS/CNS 2023 guidelines, surgical decompression (ACDF) is a Tier 2 "reasonable indication" for cervical radiculopathy when persistent symptoms fail to resolve after a 6-to-12-week trial of conservative treatment. The member's persistent radicular pain (7/10 at rest) and right C7 sensory/reflex deficits despite 7 weeks of physical therapy, 4 months of neuropathic medication, and an epidural steroid injection satisfy these standards of care.
* *Action:* Proceed to Step 5 to evaluate specific plan policy alignment.

#### **Step 5: Plan Criteria and Myelopathy Exception Analysis**
Because the member has cervical radiculopathy, the Standard Pathway of **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 (Section 2.2)** applies. The member's compliance with these requirements is evaluated below:

1. **Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)):** 
   * *Plan Requirement:* Minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes.
   * *Member's Clinical Record:* Completed **7 weeks** (total of 21 sessions) of supervised physical therapy.
   * *Functional Status:* The PT discharge summary explicitly documents that a **functional plateau was reached**, noting that the patient achieved "no improvement over the final 3 sessions" and "radicular symptoms persist unchanged."
   * *State Law Override:* Under **California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2)**, a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning surgical approval on the completion of further physical therapy if the member has completed a course of PT and reached a functional plateau (defined as <10% improvement over 3 sessions or therapist documentation of max benefit). The initial reviewer's demand for additional physical therapy violates California insurance law.

2. **Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)):** 
   * *Plan Requirement:* Trial of at least two covered agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
   * *Member's Clinical Record:* 
     * *Agent 1:* Pregabalin 150mg BID (neuropathic agent) for **4 months** (partial relief, symptoms persist).
     * *Agent 2:* Meloxicam 15mg (NSAID) for **3 months** (inadequate response).
   * *Status:* Fully Met.

3. **Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)):** 
   * *Plan Requirement:* Minimum of one interventional treatment (e.g., epidural steroid injection).
   * *Member's Clinical Record:* Underwent a **right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 20, 2024**, which yielded a temporary 30% improvement for 3 weeks before symptoms returned to baseline.
   * *Status:* Fully Met.

**Step 5 Conclusion:** The member met 100% of the plan's internal standard conservative treatment criteria. The initial reviewer’s assertion that the clinical criteria were not met constitutes a clear factual error and a misapplication of the health plan's policy.
