### LEGAL & CLINICAL STANDARD OVERRIDES (HIERARCHY OF AUTHORITY)

Under the strict hierarchy of authority governing California appeals:
$$\text{California State Law (CIC/CHSC/DMHC APL)} > \text{Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS, AO Spine)} > \text{Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)}$$

#### 1. California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 Override
Under CIC § 10169.5(a), a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning the approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy if:
*   **Subsection (a)(2):** The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms. 
*   **Subsection (a)(3):** The condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address.

The member has reached a documented functional plateau (zero improvement after 24 sessions) and has an objective structural failed fusion (pseudarthrosis) with progressive motor weakness (4/5 right bicep strength). Physical therapy cannot mechanically reconstruct a failed bony fusion. Therefore, requiring further conservative trials or denying surgery on conservative grounds violates California statutory law.

#### 2. Clinical Standards of Medical Practice (CHSC § 1374.32)
Per CHSC § 1374.32(b), the standard of medical necessity must align with generally accepted standards of medical practice, overriding restrictive internal health plan policies. 
*   **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) & NASS Guidelines (2020):** For patients who present with recurrent and worsening radiculopathy, progressive motor weakness (4/5 bicep strength), and radiographically confirmed pseudarthrosis (failed fusion) that has failed exhaustive conservative measures (PT, NSAIDs, Gabapentin, and Epidural injection), **revision surgical decompression and stabilization is the established clinical standard of care.** Delaying surgical revision in the presence of an active, progressive motor deficit (4/5 weakness) introduces a high risk of permanent, irreversible neurological injury.

The denial of CPT code 22551 is **OVERTURNED**. HealthFirst must authorize this procedure immediately.
