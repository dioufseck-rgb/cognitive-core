### DECISION SUMMARY

**FINAL DETERMINATION: OVERTURN THE DENIAL (APPROVE CLINICAL REQUEST)**

The prior authorization denial issued on November 1, 2024, is hereby **OVERTURNED** in its entirety. The requested Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) C6-C7, one level (CPT 22551) is **APPROVED**. 

This decision is driven by **Condition A (Procedural/Factual)** of the governing Disposition Decision Tree: the health plan's initial clinical denial was based on a demonstrable factual error (erroneously counting only 3 weeks of physical therapy when medical records prove 9 weeks were completed across two separate providers), and the corrected facts fully satisfy the health plan's medical necessity criteria. Additionally, under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2)**, the plan is legally prohibited from conditioning surgery on further physical therapy once a documented functional plateau has been achieved.

---

### EVALUATION REPORT & FINDINGS

#### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Disclosure Audit (CHSC § 1374.31)
*   **Methodology:** The initial denial letter dated November 1, 2024, was audited to ensure compliance with California statutory disclosure standards under **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31**.
*   **Findings:** The denial letter explicitly cited the coverage policy applied (**CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2**), identified the exact clinical deficit (claiming only 3 weeks of physical therapy was completed instead of the required 6 weeks), and noted the absence of a documented functional plateau.
*   **Determination:** **Procedurally Adequate.** Because the denial letter was legally compliant, the review proceeds to a substantive factual and clinical analysis under Step 2.

#### Step 2: Factual Integrity Verification (The Factual Claim Under Test)
*   **Methodology:** A detailed timeline audit of the member's physical therapy billing codes, therapist logs, and clinical records submitted with the original prior authorization request was performed.
*   **Findings:** 
    *   The plan’s initial reviewer, Dr. Alan Foster, asserted that the member completed only **3 weeks** of physical therapy. This count was based solely on the records of **Valley Physical Therapy** (dates of service: June 1, 2024, to June 21, 2024; 3 weeks, 9 sessions).
    *   The member's file contained a second, distinct set of physical therapy records from **Capital Physical Therapy** (dates of service: July 8, 2024, to August 16, 2024; 6 weeks, 18 sessions).
    *   Combining both courses, the member completed a total of **9 weeks and 27 sessions** of supervised cervical physical therapy prior to the surgical request.
    *   The Capital Physical Therapy discharge summary explicitly states: *"Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."* This directly contradicts the plan's claim that a functional plateau was not documented.
*   **Factual Conclusion:** **The Factual Claim Under Test is WRONG.** The member completed 9 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the plan's 6-week threshold), and a clinical functional plateau was explicitly documented.

#### Step 3: Clinical Diagnosis & Pathology Classification
*   **Methodology:** Diagnostic classification was established by reviewing the UCSF Medical Center MRI report (dated August 20, 2024) and the neurological examination notes from Dr. Sarah Okonkwo (dated August 25, 2024).
*   **Findings:**
    *   **Imaging (MRI 2024-08-20):** Large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression and right foraminal stenosis. Crucially, the MRI notes: *"No cord contact. No myelomalacia."*
    *   **Neurological Examination (2024-08-25):** Clinical signs are strictly unilateral and dermatomal, consisting of right C7 dermatomal numbness, a diminished right tricep reflex, and 4/5 grip strength on the right. There are no bilateral findings, no gait instability, and no upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (such as a Hoffman sign or hyperreflexia).
*   **Diagnostic Classification:** **Cervical Radiculopathy (Standard Pathway).** Because there is no spinal cord compression, cord signal change (myelomalacia), or upper motor neuron involvement, the member does not meet the clinical definition of Cervical Myelopathy. Consequently, standard conservative management requirements under **CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2** apply.

#### Step 4: Plan Criteria Compliance Evaluation (CC-SPINE-2024)
Applying the corrected factual record (established in Step 2) to the plan's standard clinical coverage criteria under Section 2.2, compliance is assessed as follows:

1.  **Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement):** **MET.** Confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy supported by a current (August 20, 2024) MRI showing C6-C7 herniation and moderate right C7 nerve root compression matching the clinical presentation.
2.  **Section 2.2(a) (Physical Therapy):** **MET.** The corrected factual record proves the member completed **9 weeks** of physical therapy (Valley PT and Capital PT), exceeding the plan's 6-week minimum.
3.  **Section 2.2(b) (Pharmacotherapy):** **MET.** Dr. Okonkwo's records document trials of two required agents for at least 4 weeks each:
    *   *Gabapentin 600mg TID* for 4 months (neuropathic agent).
    *   *Naproxen 500mg BID* for 3 months (NSAID).
4.  **Section 2.2(c) (Interventional Treatment):** **MET.** The member underwent a right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 15, 2024, which provided only 35% temporary relief, with symptoms returning to baseline within 3 weeks.
5.  **Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation):** **MET.** Dr. Okonkwo submitted the required clinical attestation, descriptive functional limitations, clinical rationale, and documentation of risks and alternatives.

*   **Plan Criteria Conclusion:** **ALL PLAN CRITERIA ARE MET.** Under the corrected facts, the member is fully compliant with CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2.

#### Step 5: Statutory & Regulatory Override Analysis (California Law)
*   **Methodology:** The case was evaluated against California health insurance statutory protections to determine if the plan's denial is legally enforceable.
*   **Findings:** Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2)**, a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy if:
    $$\text{"The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician [or physical therapist] documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."}$$
*   **Application:** The Capital Physical Therapy discharge summary of August 16, 2024, explicitly documented that the member reached a functional plateau ("Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved"). Therefore, the plan's administrative insistence on further conservative treatment violates **CIC § 10169.5**.
*   **Statutory Conclusion:** **Statutory protections require override of the plan's denial.**

#### Step 6: Clinical Standard of Care Evaluation (IMR Standard)
*   **Methodology:** The clinical presentation was evaluated against generally accepted standards of medical practice (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, NASS Guidelines 2020) as mandated by the Independent Medical Review standard (**CHSC § 1374.32(b)**).
*   **Findings:** Under the AANS/CNS 2023 and NASS 2020 guidelines, single-level anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) is a standard, highly effective, and clinically indicated intervention for patients with persistent, severe cervical radiculopathy secondary to a matching MRI-proven disc herniation who have failed an appropriate trial of conservative management (typically defined as 6 to 12 weeks of combined physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and interventional blocks). 
*   **Application:** Having failed 9 weeks of physical therapy, 4 months of Gabapentin, 3 months of Naproxen, and an epidural steroid injection, the member represents a classic Tier 2 "reasonable/strong" surgical indication. Delaying surgery for additional conservative treatment has no evidentiary basis, risks worsening neurological deficits (such as the existing 4/5 grip strength weakness), and is contrary to standard medical practice.
*   **Clinical Standard Conclusion:** **The surgical request is medically necessary under generally accepted clinical standards.**
