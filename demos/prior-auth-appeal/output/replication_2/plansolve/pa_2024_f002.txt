### I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Prior Authorization Appeals Department has conducted a comprehensive review of the medical necessity appeal submitted on behalf of Steven Clark. The requested procedure is a single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) to treat severe, medically refractory cervical radiculopathy. 

The initial denial, issued under Reason Code MED-003, cited conflicting physical therapy (PT) documentation regarding the duration of conservative care. Specifically, the plan claimed an administrative discrepancy between the 6 weeks of therapy completed at Riverside Physical Therapy and an incomplete 4-week record submitted from Coastal Physical Therapy, resulting in an "uncertain" duration of verifiable therapy. 

Upon de novo review of the complete clinical file—including newly submitted records verifying an additional 6 weeks of therapy at Coastal Physical Therapy, comprehensive pharmacotherapy trials, an interventional epidural steroid injection, and objective clinical exams—the initial denial is **OVERTURNED**. The clinical documentation conclusively establishes that the member has satisfied all conservative treatment mandates under both HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) and California regulatory standards (CIC § 10169.5). The requested surgical intervention is medically necessary and is hereby approved.

### III. STEP-BY-STEP EVALUATION AND FINDINGS

#### STEP 1: Procedural Adequacy and Regulatory Compliance Check
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Review the initial denial notice dated October 10, 2024, issued by Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, for compliance with CHSC § 1374.31(b).
*   **Findings:** The initial denial notice focused exclusively on an administrative discrepancy regarding the exact duration of the physical therapy logs. It failed to cite the specific clinical coverage criteria applied (such as CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2) and lacked a substantive clinical rationale. Under CHSC § 1374.31(b), a denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied or the specific clinical reasoning is procedurally **defective**. 
*   **Procedural Action:** The denial is determined to be procedurally defective. To ensure appropriate clinical stewardship, we proceed to a full clinical and regulatory evaluation of the medical record.

#### STEP 2: Diagnostic Classification and Clinical Severity Analysis
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Analyze clinical notes and the cervical MRI to classify the pathology as either Cervical Radiculopathy or Cervical Myelopathy.
*   **Clinical Findings:**
    *   **Diagnostic Classification:** **Cervical Radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment** (confirmed primary diagnosis).
    *   **Objective Imaging (MRI dated August 15, 2024):** Large C5-C6 right-sided disc herniation causing severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. The MRI is current (within 6 months of the request), satisfying CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.3.
    *   **Neurological Examination (August 20, 2024):** Right C6 dermatomal numbness, objective bicep muscle weakness (graded 4/5 right), absent right bicep reflex, and a positive Spurling's maneuver on the right. 
    *   **Pathology Routing:** There is an absence of bilateral upper motor neuron signs (e.g., hyperreflexia, positive Hoffman, gait disturbance) or spinal cord signal changes (myelomalacia). Therefore, this case is routed to the **Radiculopathy/Standard Path (Step 4)**.

#### STEP 3: Myelopathy Statutory and Clinical Standard Evaluation (Urgent Path)
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Assess for myelopathy-related statutory exceptions.
*   **Findings:** Not applicable. The patient exhibits unilateral lower motor neuron deficits consistent with pure C6 radiculopathy.

#### STEP 4: Radiculopathy Conservative Treatment and Functional Plateau Review (Standard Path)
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Evaluate conservative treatment documentation against CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2 and California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5.
*   **Findings:**
    1.  **Physical Therapy (PT) Duration & Verification:**
        *   *Riverside PT (04/01/2024 to 05/10/2024):* 6 weeks completed, consisting of 18 sessions. The plan previously received and verified these records as complete.
        *   *Coastal PT (06/01/2024 to 07/12/2024):* 6 weeks completed, consisting of 18 sessions. The initial submission was partial (weeks 1–4 only), which led to the plan’s administrative denial. The complete 6-week record has now been submitted on appeal.
        *   *Synthesis:* The member has completed a total of **12 weeks** of supervised physical therapy (36 sessions total), far exceeding the 6-week minimum required under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a).
    2.  **Statutory "Functional Plateau" (CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)):**
        *   Both PT providers documented a functional plateau. Riverside PT documented "plateau reached, symptoms persist." Coastal PT documented "no improvement in neurological deficit; therapist documents functional plateau—maximum therapeutic benefit reached."
        *   Under **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)**, a health plan cannot mechanically condition surgical approval on further PT once a functional plateau is documented by the treating therapist or physician. 
    3.  **Pharmacotherapy Requirements (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(b)):**
        *   *Agent 1:* Gabapentin 600mg TID for 5 months (neuropathic agent; achieved only partial relief).
        *   *Agent 2:* Duloxetine 60mg daily for 3 months (neuropathic agent; achieved only partial neuropathic benefit).
        *   *Synthesis:* This satisfies the requirement for a trial of at least two therapeutic agents of different classes for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
    4.  **Interventional Treatment Requirements (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(c)):**
        *   The member underwent a right C6 cervical epidural steroid injection on August 28, 2024. The procedure provided 60% temporary relief, but symptoms returned to baseline within 3 weeks.
        *   *Synthesis:* This satisfies the interventional trial requirement.

*   **Standard Path Action:** Because the member has verified 12 weeks of physical therapy (well beyond the 6-week requirement), has documented a functional plateau at both PT locations, and has failed extensive pharmacotherapy and interventional treatments, the criteria under **Condition D (Standard Path/Functional Plateau)** are fully met.

### IV. CONFLICT RESOLUTION & CLINICAL ALIGNMENT

1.  **Administrative Discrepancy Resolved:** The sole basis of the initial denial was the alleged conflict in PT documentation. The submission of the complete Coastal PT records on appeal proves that the member completed 6 weeks of therapy at Coastal PT in addition to the 6 weeks at Riverside PT. The administrative doubt is resolved; a total of 12 weeks of PT is fully verified.
2.  **Statutory Protection Against Extended PT Mandates:** Under **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)** and **(a)(3)**, because the member completed his PT courses, reached a documented functional plateau, and has objective structural neurological compromise (severe C6 nerve root compression with 4/5 motor weakness and reflex loss) that PT cannot anatomically correct, requiring further conservative delay is illegal and clinically contraindicated.
3.  **Alignment with Clinical Standards:** According to the **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023)** and **NASS (2020)**, surgical decompression via single-level ACDF (CPT 22551) is a Tier 1/2 recommended indication for patients with objective cervical radiculopathy who present with correlating MRI findings, motor weakness (biceps 4/5), reflex loss, and who have failed 6–12 weeks of multi-modal conservative management. Denying surgery in this clinical scenario deviates from generally accepted standards of medical practice.
