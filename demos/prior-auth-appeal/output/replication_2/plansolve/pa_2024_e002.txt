### **3. STEP-BY-STEP CLINICAL EVALUATION & FINDINGS**

#### **Step 1: Procedural Defect and Regulatory Adequacy Check**
*   **Evaluation:** Under CHSC § 1374.31(b), a medical necessity denial notice is legally defective if it fails to specify the clinical criteria applied or the level-by-level clinical reasons for the denial.
*   **Finding:** The initial denial notice issued on November 25, 2024, provided a level-by-level rationale (noting that only C4-C5 was supported and that C3-C4 and C5-C6 lacked sufficient pathology). However, the notice failed to explicitly cite the specific plan clinical coverage criteria applied (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024). While this constitutes a technical omission under California law, a mechanical auto-reversal of the entire request would result in the approval of a multi-level spinal fusion at C3-C4—a level with no objective clinical pathology. This would expose the member to medically inappropriate surgical risks.
*   **Action:** To prevent patient harm and align with CHSC § 1374.32(a)(2) (requiring services to be "clinically appropriate"), we proceed to a full clinical evaluation of the merits for each disputed level.

#### **Step 2: Diagnosis and Clinical Severity Classification**
*   **Evaluation:** Classify the primary pathology as either Cervical Myelopathy or Cervical Radiculopathy based on objective signs and clinical findings.
*   **Finding:** The neurological examination dated October 5, 2024, reveals undeniable objective signs of moderate-to-severe cervical myelopathy: bilateral hyperreflexia, bilateral Hoffman's sign, and a wide-based gait. The patient also exhibits clear, level-specific neurological deficits, including C5 deltoid weakness (4/5) and left C6 bicep weakness (4-/5) with a diminished left bicep reflex.
*   **Action:** The primary clinical driver is classified as **Cervical Myelopathy with Myeloradiculopathy**. This triggers both the plan's Myelopathy Pathway exception (Section 2.2(A)) and the statutory protections under DMHC APL 22-014.

#### **Step 3: Multi-Level Imaging Correlation and Pathology Verification**
*   **Evaluation:** Correlate the MRI dated October 1, 2024, with clinical examination findings for each requested level.
*   **Finding:**
    *   **C3-C4:** MRI demonstrates only a mild disc bulge and minimal foraminal narrowing. There is no cord compression, spinal canal stenosis, or neural foraminal stenosis. This matches the neurological exam ("unclear — neck pain... but no specific radicular pattern") and physical therapy notes ("C3-C4 contribution unclear").
    *   **C4-C5:** MRI reveals a large disc herniation, severe cord compression, and T2-weighted cord signal changes (myelomalacia). This correlates directly with the patient's bilateral deltoid weakness and upper motor neuron myelopathic findings. (Surgical intervention is highly indicated; approved by the plan).
    *   **C5-C6:** MRI demonstrates a moderate disc herniation, moderate left C6 nerve root compression, and foraminal stenosis. This correlates directly with her objective left bicep weakness (4-/5) and diminished bicep reflex (C6 nerve root distribution).
*   **Action:** Objective pathology and clinical correlation are verified for C4-C5 and C5-C6, but are completely absent at C3-C4.

#### **Step 4: Statutory Overrides and Conservative Treatment Exceptions**
*   **Evaluation:** Determine if conservative treatment requirements are waived under CIC § 10169.5 and DMHC APL 22-014, or if they have been met.
*   **Finding:**
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (C4-C5):** The presence of severe cord compression and active myelomalacia (T2 signal change) represents an ongoing spinal cord injury. Under DMHC APL 22-014 and CIC § 10169.5(a)(3), conservative therapy requirements (physical therapy and interventional trials) are statutorily waived because of the risk of irreversible neurological harm. 
    *   **Radiculopathy/Myeloradiculopathy Pathway (C5-C6):** Even if a conservative trial were required for the C5-C6 level, the clinical record demonstrates that the patient has fully completed an exhaustive conservative regimen that exceeds the plan's criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2):
        1.  *Physical Therapy:* Completed 9 weeks (27 sessions) of supervised therapy (plan requires 6 weeks). The physical therapist documented that C5-C6 radicular symptoms persist and myelopathic signs are stable but unimproved, confirming a "functional plateau" under CIC § 10169.5(b).
        2.  *Pharmacotherapy:* Trialed Baclofen 10mg TID for 3 months (muscle relaxant) and Pregabalin 300mg BID for 4 months (neuropathic agent), satisfying the requirement for two agents for at least 4 weeks each.
        3.  *Interventional:* Completed a cervical epidural steroid injection on October 20, 2024, which provided only modest, temporary relief of radicular symptoms and did not affect myelopathic signs.
*   **Action:** The conservative treatment requirements are fully met and clinically exhausted for the C5-C6 level.

#### **Step 5: Clinical Standards Concordance (Generally Accepted Standards)**
*   **Evaluation:** Compare the proposed multi-level surgery against national clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, and AO Spine 2021).
*   **Finding:**
    *   **C5-C6 Decompression:** Strongly supported by AANS/CNS 2023 Tier 1 Guidelines. Performing surgery at C4-C5 while ignoring an adjacent, highly symptomatic, and compressed level (C5-C6) violates clinical standards. Failing to decompress C5-C6 would leave the patient with ongoing radicular weakness (left bicep 4-/5) and increase the risk of adjacent segment disease, necessitating a avoidable future revision surgery.
    *   **C3-C4 Decompression:** Strongly opposed by clinical guidelines. Her C3-C4 findings consist of a mild disc bulge and axial neck pain without radiculopathy or myelopathy. NASS 2020 and AANS/CNS 2023 Tier 3 guidelines explicitly state that surgical fusion is not indicated for axial neck pain alone or for mild imaging findings that lack clinical correlation.

### **4. DETAILED LEVEL-BY-LEVEL DETERMINATION**

#### **C3-C4 Level: DENIAL UPHELD**
*   **Clinical Findings:** MRI reveals only a mild disc bulge and minimal foraminal narrowing. The neurological exam shows no radicular weakness, no dermatomal sensory loss, and no spinal cord compression at this level. The clinical exam notes that the patient's symptoms at C3-C4 are limited to non-specific axial neck pain on palpation.
*   **Clinical Standard Analysis:** Under NASS (2020) and AANS/CNS (2023) Guidelines, surgical decompression and fusion is not indicated for axial neck pain in the absence of matching radiculopathy or myelopathy (Tier 3). Fusing an asymptomatic level with mild degenerative changes exposes the patient to unnecessary surgical risk, including adjacent segment degeneration.
*   **Conclusion:** Medical necessity is not established under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.3 or generally accepted standards of medical practice.

#### **C4-C5 Level: APPROVAL CONFIRMED**
*   **Clinical Findings:** MRI shows severe cord compression with T2 signal changes (myelomalacia), correlating with bilateral hyperreflexia, Hoffman's sign, gait instability, and bilateral deltoid weakness.
*   **Clinical Standard Analysis:** This level meets AANS/CNS 2023 Tier 1 recommendations for urgent surgical decompression due to existing, active spinal cord injury. Conservative therapy is contraindicated as a primary treatment.
*   **Conclusion:** Decompression and fusion at C4-C5 are highly medically necessary.

#### **C5-C6 Level: DENIAL OVERTURNED — APPROVED**
*   **Clinical Findings:** MRI demonstrates a moderate disc herniation with moderate left C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. The neurological exam reveals corresponding left bicep weakness (4-/5) and a diminished left bicep reflex.
*   **Conservative Care Analysis:** The patient has completed 9 weeks (27 sessions) of physical therapy, over 3 months of dual-agent pharmacotherapy (Baclofen and Pregabalin), and a cervical epidural steroid injection on October 20, 2024. Despite this exhaustive trial, C5-C6 radicular symptoms and motor weakness persist.
*   **Plan Criteria & Statutory Alignment:** The patient has met and exceeded the conservative treatment requirements of HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2. Furthermore, because she has reached a documented clinical and functional plateau, demanding further conservative trials is prohibited under CIC § 10169.5(a)(2).
*   **Clinical Standard Analysis:** Decompressing C4-C5 while leaving a highly symptomatic, compressed C5-C6 level untreated is contrary to prudent clinical judgment. AANS/CNS 2023 and AO Spine 2021 clinical guidelines support concurrent decompression of adjacent, structurally compromised, and clinically symptomatic levels during a single surgical session.
*   **Conclusion:** Medical necessity is fully established. Denying this level violates both the plan's own clinical coverage criteria and generally accepted clinical standards.
