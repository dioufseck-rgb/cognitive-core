## Appeal Determination: Prior Authorization for ACDF C5-C6

**Case ID:** PA-2024-D002
**Member:** Michael Green (M-445577), Age 57
**Procedure:** ACDF C5-C6 (CPT 22551)
**Date of Denial:** 2024-11-08
**Reviewer:** Expert Health Plan Appeals Reviewer

---

**DISPOSITION: OVERTURN**

---

**WARRANT:**

The denial for CPT code 22551 (ACDF C5-C6, one level) for member Michael Green should be OVERTURNED for the following reasons, applying the hierarchy of State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria:

**1. Procedural Defects in the Initial Denial Notice (State Law Override):**
The initial denial notice issued by HealthFirst PPO Gold was procedurally defective, rendering it invalid for appeal timelines.
*   **Missing Criteria Document:** The denial notice referenced an attached criteria document, but no such document was provided to the member or the treating provider, as confirmed by both.
*   **Missing IMR Notice:** The denial notice failed to include information regarding the member's Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights.
*   **Violation of CHSC § 1374.31(b):** California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b) explicitly states that "A denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied or the specific clinical reason for the denial is DEFECTIVE and shall be treated as if no denial was issued for purposes of appeal timelines." The failure to provide the referenced criteria document constitutes a violation of this regulation.

**2. Misapplication of Plan Criteria:**
Even if the denial notice were procedurally sound, the plan appears to have misapplied its own criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024) to the member's clinical situation. The denial reason cited "Member does not meet criteria for requested procedure under plan policy," implying a failure to meet medical necessity requirements. However, a review of the clinical record against HealthFirst's policy for cervical spinal surgery reveals that the member *does* meet the conservative treatment requirements for cervical radiculopathy:

*   **Diagnosis (Section 2.1):** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with objective neurological deficit (left C6 dermatomal numbness, diminished bicep reflex, 4/5 bicep weakness) and MRI findings of C5-C6 large disc herniation with moderate left C6 nerve root compression. This satisfies criteria 2.1(a) and 2.1(c).
*   **Physical Therapy (Section 2.2a):** The policy requires "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes." The member completed 7 weeks of PT, with notes documenting a functional plateau reached at week 6. This criterion is met.
*   **Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2b):** The policy requires "trial of at least two...for minimum 4 weeks each." The member underwent trials of Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (partial relief) and Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months (inadequate), satisfying this criterion.
*   **Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2c):** The policy requires "one of the following interventional treatments." The member received a Left C6 Epidural, satisfying this criterion.
*   **Myelopathy Exception (Section 2.2A):** The myelopathy exception is not applicable here, as the member's primary diagnosis and clinical presentation clearly align with cervical radiculopathy (unilateral symptoms, no cord contact on MRI, no upper motor neuron signs), not myelopathy. The plan's criteria explicitly state this exception applies "only when myelopathy is the PRIMARY diagnosis."
*   **Imaging Requirement (Section 2.3):** Current MRI within 6 months demonstrates pathology consistent with symptoms.

Based on the clinical record, all applicable plan criteria appear to be met for the diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy.

**3. State Law Override Regarding Physical Therapy (CIC § 10169.5):**
Even if HealthFirst's policy were interpreted to require a longer or more intensive course of physical therapy, California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) explicitly states that a health insurer cannot condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." The clinical record clearly documents 7 weeks of PT with a plateau reached at week 6, and persistent radicular symptoms. This state law provision prohibits denying coverage based on further PT requirements when a plateau has been documented.

**4. Alignment with Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (CHSC § 1374.32(b)):**
The requested procedure is medically necessary and indicated under generally accepted standards of medical practice for cervical radiculopathy.
*   **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023), Section 3.1 TIER 2(b):** States that surgery is a "reasonable indication" for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx."
*   The member has persistent radicular symptoms (numbness, weakness) and functional impairment after a comprehensive conservative treatment regimen including 7 weeks of physical therapy (documented plateau), 4 months of neuropathic medication, 3 months of NSAIDs, and an epidural injection, all of which exceed or meet the 6-12 week timeframe for conservative treatment trials for radiculopathy.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b):** Mandates that an independent medical reviewer "SHALL recommend approval regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive" if the service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice." In this case, the service meets these generally accepted standards for radiculopathy.

**Conclusion:**
The initial denial was procedurally defective and failed to correctly apply the plan's own criteria. Furthermore, the denial is contrary to California state law regarding physical therapy requirements and generally accepted clinical standards for cervical radiculopathy. Therefore, the prior authorization for ACDF C5-C6 for Michael Green is deemed medically necessary and should be approved.
