**Warrant for Determination:**

This appeal was reviewed by an expert health plan appeals reviewer in accordance with HealthFirst’s appeals process and California regulatory requirements, applying the hierarchy of authority: State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.

**1. Clinical Presentation Analysis:**
The member, Thomas Reed, is a 52-year-old male presenting with cervical radiculopathy at C6-C7, persistent after conservative treatment. The clinical record, including MRI findings (C6-C7 disc herniation, mild right C7 foraminal stenosis, no cord signal change/myelomalacia) and neurological exam (right C7 dermatomal numbness, diminished right tricep reflex, no bilateral findings, no upper motor neuron signs), confirms a primary diagnosis of **cervical radiculopathy**. The original denial correctly noted "No myelopathy findings on exam." Therefore, the myelopathy-specific exceptions or urgent pathway criteria (e.g., HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(A) Exception, DMHC APL 22-014) are not applicable as the primary diagnosis is not myelopathy.

**2. Review Against HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
The original denial, issued by Dr. Patricia Huang, cited "Member does not meet criteria for ACDF. Imaging shows C6-C7 disc herniation with mild cord contact but no measurable compression. No myelopathy findings on exam. PT not yet completed to functional plateau."

Upon review of the clinical record against HealthFirst’s criteria:
*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy C6-C7 with objective neurological deficits and imaging showing C6-C7 disc herniation/foraminal stenosis. This meets criterion 2.1(a) and 2.1(c).
*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** The clinical record clearly states, "7 weeks PT completed. PT discharge summary explicitly states functional plateau reached — patient has not improved over final 3 sessions." HealthFirst's own "Functional plateau" definition states "<10% improvement over 3 sessions OR therapist documents maximum benefit reached." The documentation unequivocally demonstrates that a functional plateau was reached after 7 weeks, exceeding the plan's 6-week minimum. The denial's assertion that "PT not yet completed to functional plateau" is factually incorrect based on the provided clinical documentation and HealthFirst's own definitions.
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** The member completed trials of Pregabalin (4 months) and Meloxicam (3 months), meeting the requirement for two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
    *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** The member underwent a right C7 epidural steroid injection with 30% improvement lasting 3 weeks before symptoms returned to baseline, meeting this requirement.
*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** MRI dated 2024-08-01 (within 6 months) demonstrates pathology consistent with symptoms.
*   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** The appeal basis and clinical record confirm the surgeon's rationale for surgery for persistent radiculopathy after failed conservative treatment.

**Conclusion under Plan Criteria:** The member meets all applicable HealthFirst plan criteria for ACDF, specifically the conservative treatment requirements. The denial's primary stated reason regarding PT completion is inaccurate.

**3. Review Against California State Law and Regulations:**
California law provides explicit protections and standards that override plan criteria where conflicts exist.

*   **CIC § 10169.5 — Physical Therapy as Prerequisite:** This statute explicitly states that a health insurer "shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where... [t]he member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." (CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)).
    *   In this case, the member completed 7 weeks of PT, and the PT discharge summary explicitly states a functional plateau was reached. The treating surgeon's appeal basis further documents this. Therefore, under California law, HealthFirst cannot deny the procedure on the grounds of insufficient PT. The plan's denial directly conflicts with state law.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) — IMR Standard of Review:** This code mandates that if a service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice," it SHALL be approved "regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive." This reinforces that even if plan criteria were interpreted to deny, clinical standards would take precedence if more permissive.

**Conclusion under State Law:** The denial based on PT completion is in direct violation of California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2).

**4. Review Against Clinical Standard of Care:**
Generally accepted clinical guidelines support surgical intervention for persistent cervical radiculopathy after failed conservative treatment.

*   **AANS/CNS Joint Section on Disorders of the Spine: Guidelines for Management of Degenerative Cervical Myelopathy (2023 Update)** and **NASS Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020)**
    *   For **radiculopathy**, surgical intervention (such as ACDF) is a "Reasonable Indication" (TIER 2) for patients with "persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and "significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care" (AANS/CNS 2023, Section 3.1).
    *   The member has completed 7 weeks of PT to a functional plateau, trials of two pharmacotherapy agents for 3-4 months, and an epidural injection with only temporary and partial relief. His persistent pain (7/10 at rest), numbness, and diminished reflex demonstrate significant functional impairment despite an adequate and documented trial of conservative therapies.
*   **Functional Plateau (Clinical Standard):** The clinical standard defines a functional plateau as "<10% improvement... over 3 consecutive therapy sessions, OR Therapist documentation that patient has reached maximum therapeutic benefit." (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 5). The member's PT discharge summary explicitly stating "patient has not improved over final 3 sessions" aligns perfectly with this definition.

**Conclusion under Clinical Standard:** The requested ACDF is medically indicated and consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for persistent cervical radiculopathy that has failed an adequate trial of conservative treatment, as demonstrated by the member reaching a functional plateau in PT and failure of pharmacotherapy and interventional options.

**Summary and Final Determination:**
The original denial was based on the premise that "PT not yet completed to functional plateau." This premise is demonstrably false according to the member's clinical record and HealthFirst's own criteria definition of a functional plateau. Furthermore, California State Law (CIC § 10169.5) explicitly prohibits denying surgical procedures based on PT completion when a functional plateau has been reached. The requested procedure is also consistent with generally accepted clinical standards for persistent cervical radiculopathy after failed conservative management.

Based on the factual inaccuracy of the denial, the direct conflict with California state law, and alignment with generally accepted clinical standards, the denial must be **OVERTURNED**.

**Decision:** The prior authorization denial for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) for member Thomas Reed is **OVERTURNED**. Authorization for the requested procedure is granted.
