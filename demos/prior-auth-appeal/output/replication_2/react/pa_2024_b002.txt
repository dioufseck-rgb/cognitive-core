**DISPOSITION: OVERTURN DENIAL**

---

**WARRANT:**

Upon review of the submitted prior authorization appeal, clinical record, HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, and relevant California regulatory and clinical standard documents, the initial denial for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 for Mr. Thomas Reed is **OVERTURNED**.

The initial denial cited "Member does not meet criteria for ACDF. Imaging shows C6-C7 disc herniation with mild cord contact but no measurable compression. No myelopathy findings on exam. PT not yet completed to functional plateau."

While the clinical record confirms the absence of myelopathy findings (no bilateral symptoms, no upper motor neuron signs, no cord signal change/myelomalacia on MRI), indicating a primary diagnosis of **cervical radiculopathy C6-C7**, the denial's assertion regarding physical therapy completion is factually inaccurate and in violation of California state law.

**Specific findings supporting the overturn decision are as follows:**

1.  **Plan Criteria for Conservative Treatment Met (and Denial Reason Invalid):**
    *   HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(a), requires a "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes." Mr. Reed completed **7 weeks of physical therapy** (21 sessions), thus exceeding the minimum duration requirement.
    *   The denial stated, "PT not yet completed to functional plateau." However, the clinical record, specifically the **Physical Therapy Discharge Summary**, explicitly documents that a "functional plateau reached – patient has not improved over final 3 sessions. Radicular symptoms persist unchanged."
    *   HealthFirst's own definition of "functional plateau" (as per DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE derived from plan criteria) includes "therapist documents maximum benefit reached" or "<10% improvement over 3 sessions." The submitted PT documentation directly fulfills this definition. Therefore, the denial's rationale regarding the functional plateau is **contradicted by the clinical documentation and the plan's own criteria.**
    *   Furthermore, the member completed the pharmacotherapy requirement (Pregabalin for 4 months, Meloxicam for 3 months) and the interventional requirement (Right C7 epidural, which provided only partial and temporary relief), as per HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.2(b) and 2.2(c).

2.  **California State Law Overrides Plan's Mechanical PT Requirement:**
    *   Even if the plan were to dispute the attainment of a functional plateau (which the documentation clearly supports), California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) explicitly states that a health insurer "shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where... The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
    *   This state law takes precedence over any internal plan criteria that would mechanically enforce a minimum PT duration after a functional plateau has been reached (HIERARCHY OF AUTHORITY: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria). The documentation from the physical therapist definitively establishes that a functional plateau was reached.

3.  **Procedure Meets Generally Accepted Clinical Standards:**
    *   The clinical record indicates persistent cervical radiculopathy after a comprehensive trial of conservative treatment including 7 weeks of physical therapy, 4 months of neuropathic medication, 3 months of NSAID, and an epidural injection, all with inadequate or temporary resolution of symptoms.
    *   According to the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for Management of Degenerative Cervical Myelopathy (2023 Update) and NASS Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020), surgery for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment, or significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care, is considered a "REASONABLE INDICATION" (TIER 2 indication) (CLINICAL REFERENCE SUMMARY, Section 3.1).
    *   California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) mandates that if a service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice," it "SHALL be approved regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive." The requested ACDF for persistent radiculopathy after a documented failure of conservative treatment meets these standards.

In summary, the denial's basis regarding physical therapy completion to functional plateau is erroneous given the clinical documentation provided. Furthermore, California state law explicitly prohibits denial on this basis when a functional plateau has been reached. The requested procedure is also consistent with generally accepted clinical standards for cervical radiculopathy after failed conservative management.

Therefore, the denial of prior authorization for CPT code 22551 for Mr. Thomas Reed is **OVERTURNED**, and the service is deemed medically necessary.
