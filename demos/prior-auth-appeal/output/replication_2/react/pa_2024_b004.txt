## APPEAL DETERMINATION: PA-2024-B004

**Member:** Kevin Watkins (M-889900)
**Plan:** HealthFirst PPO Gold — Group Plan 77441
**Procedure:** ACDF C5-C6, one level (CPT 22551)
**Requested By:** Dr. Sarah Martinez, MD
**Initial Denial Date:** 2024-11-10
**Initial Denial Reason:** "Surgical request is premature. Member's symptoms have been present for only 6 weeks. Plan criteria require at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration. Recent onset radiculopathy has a high rate of spontaneous resolution." (MED-003)

---

### **Reviewer's Analysis:**

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
The clinical record describes "Acute cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment." Imaging (MRI 2024-10-01) shows "C5-C6 large disc herniation with moderate right C6 nerve root compression. Foraminal stenosis. No cord contact. No myelomalacia." Neurological exam findings are unilateral to the right C6 dermatome (numbness, diminished bicep reflex, 4/5 grip strength). These findings are consistent with **cervical radiculopathy**, not myelopathy, as per clinical standard definitions (AANS/CNS 2023, Section 1.1). The absence of cord contact or myelomalacia definitively rules out myelopathy. This is critical as myelopathy-specific exceptions in plan criteria and state law do not apply here.

**2. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit and supporting MRI, meeting criteria 2.1(a) and 2.1(c). (Met)
*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** Plan criteria require "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy." The clinical record confirms "6 weeks intensive PT" (18 sessions) was completed. (Met)
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Plan criteria require "trial of at least two... for minimum 4 weeks each." The member completed a 6-week course of Methylprednisolone and a 5-week course of Gabapentin. (Met)
    *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** Plan criteria require "One of the following interventional treatments." The member had a Right C6 epidural steroid injection. (Met)
*   **EXCEPTION 2.2(A) — MYELOPATHY PATHWAY:** This exception does not apply, as the member's diagnosis is radiculopathy, not myelopathy.
*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** MRI dated 2024-10-01 (within 6 months) demonstrates pathology consistent with symptoms. (Met)
*   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** Physician documentation and clinical rationale are implied by the request and appeal basis. (Assumed Met)

**Review of Plan's Denial Reason:**
The denial stated the request was "premature" because "Member's symptoms have been present for only 6 weeks. Plan criteria require at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration." This is a misapplication of the plan's own criteria. HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 (Section 2.2(a)) requires a "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy," which the member demonstrably completed. The criteria does not explicitly state that *symptoms* must be present for 6 weeks *before* surgical consideration *after* a course of conservative treatment has been completed and failed. The denial's reasoning conflates symptom duration with conservative treatment duration and fails to acknowledge the completion and failure of required conservative treatment.

**3. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**

*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):**
    *   Subdivision (a)(2) states that a health insurer cannot condition approval of surgery on completion of physical therapy where "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." The clinical record explicitly states, "Functional plateau documented at 6 weeks. No improvement in fine motor deficits or radicular symptoms. OT confirms inability to perform professional duties." This directly meets the conditions for this waiver.
    *   Subdivision (c) mandates that a plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy "shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau." The plan's denial, citing "only 6 weeks of symptoms" despite completed therapy and documented plateau, constitutes a mechanical application contrary to state law.
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):** This APL primarily addresses myelopathy and myelomalacia and is not directly applicable to this radiculopathy case. However, it reiterates the principle that plans "shall not apply internal criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted standards of medical practice" (DMHC APL 22-014, Section II.3).
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (Standard of Review for IMR):** This section specifies that services meeting "generally accepted standards of medical practice" shall be approved regardless of more restrictive plan criteria. This principle extends to internal appeals where state law or clinical standards override plan criteria.

**4. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **Conservative Treatment Duration:** For radiculopathy, guidelines suggest 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment before surgical consideration (AANS/CNS 2023, Section 3.1, TIER 2(b)). The member has completed 6 weeks of intensive, multi-modality conservative treatment.
*   **Functional Plateau:** Clinical standards define functional plateau as <10% improvement or therapist documentation of maximum benefit (AANS/CNS 2023, Section 5). The member's PT summary explicitly states "Functional plateau documented at 6 weeks. No improvement in fine motor deficits or radicular symptoms. OT confirms inability to perform professional duties."
*   **Surgical Indications:** The member's presentation of persistent radiculopathy symptoms after 6 weeks of failed intensive conservative treatment and significant functional impairment (inability to perform professional duties essential to livelihood) meets Tier 2 "Reasonable Indication" for surgery (AANS/CNS 2023, Section 3.1, TIER 2(b) and (c)). Further delaying surgery for additional conservative treatment when a functional plateau has been reached is not supported by high-quality evidence (AO Spine 2021, Section 2.1).

**5. Conflict Resolution (Hierarchy: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria):**

The plan's denial, based on the premise that the surgical request is "premature" due to only "6 weeks of symptoms" and an implicit requirement for further conservative treatment, is in direct conflict with:

1.  **HealthFirst's own criteria:** The member fulfilled the "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy" (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(a)). The denial misinterprets its own policy.
2.  **California State Law (CIC § 10169.5):** The law explicitly prohibits requiring further physical therapy once a functional plateau has been reached, regardless of the minimum weeks in the plan's policy. The member has reached a documented functional plateau.
3.  **Generally Accepted Clinical Standards:** Surgical consideration is reasonable for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms and significant functional impairment after 6 weeks of failed conservative treatment, especially with a documented functional plateau.

Given the hierarchy, the state law and clinical standards supersede the plan's misapplied or overly restrictive interpretation of its criteria.

---

### **Disposition:**

**OVERTURN**

### **Warrant:**

The initial denial for an ACDF C5-C6 for member Kevin Watkins is hereby **OVERTURNED**. The denial incorrectly asserted that the surgical request was "premature" because "Member's symptoms have been present for only 6 weeks" and cited a misinterpretation of plan criteria requiring "at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration."

1.  **Plan Criteria Met:** The member completed all specified conservative treatment requirements as per HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2, including a minimum of 6 weeks of physical therapy, two pharmacotherapy agents, and one interventional procedure.
2.  **State Law Override:** Crucially, the clinical record documents that the member completed 6 weeks of intensive physical therapy and subsequently reached a "functional plateau" with no improvement in fine motor deficits or radicular symptoms, and documented inability to perform professional duties. Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2), a health plan cannot condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of further physical therapy when a member has completed a course of therapy and reached a functional plateau. Furthermore, CIC § 10169.5(c) prohibits the mechanical application of minimum PT duration requirements without considering a functional plateau.
3.  **Clinical Standards Support Surgery:** Generally accepted clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021) support surgical intervention (Tier 2 indication) for cervical radiculopathy with persistent symptoms and significant functional impairment after 6-12 weeks of failed conservative treatment, particularly when a functional plateau has been reached. The member's occupation as a professional violinist, with right hand weakness preventing violin fingering tasks, constitutes significant, career-ending functional impairment. Delaying surgery further is not supported by evidence in this context.

Therefore, based on the fulfillment of the plan's own conservative treatment duration requirements, the explicit override by California state law regarding functional plateau, and the alignment with generally accepted clinical standards, the denial is unwarranted.
