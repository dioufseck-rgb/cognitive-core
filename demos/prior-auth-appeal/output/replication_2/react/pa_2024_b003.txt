## Appeal Determination: Prior Authorization for ACDF C3-C4

**Case ID:** PA-2024-B003
**Member:** Angela Morrison (M-778899)
**Procedure:** CPT 22551 (ACDF C3-C4, one level)
**Denial Date:** 2024-10-01 (Initial Level)
**Reviewer:** Expert Health Plan Appeals Reviewer

### Background

Angela Morrison, a 67-year-old member of HealthFirst PPO Gold, requested prior authorization for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C3-C4. The request was denied by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, stating "Member meets conservative treatment requirements. However, imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4 to support surgical intervention. MRI shows mild degenerative changes. Procedure not medically necessary."

The member's appeal asserts that conservative treatment requirements were met, the surgeon clinically identifies C3-C4 as the symptomatic level, and a CT myelogram confirms compression not visible on MRI due to hardware artifact.

### Clinical Summary

Ms. Morrison presents with adjacent segment disease at C3-C4 following a successful C4-C5 ACDF in 2019. Her current symptoms include new onset (since 2023) left C4 dermatomal numbness, deltoid weakness (3+/5 left), and a diminished left bicep reflex.

**Imaging Review:**
*   **MRI (2024-08-15):** Reported "SIGNIFICANT ARTIFACT from C4-C5 hardware limiting evaluation. Mild degenerative signal changes visible. Cannot exclude moderate compression due to artifact."
*   **CT Myelogram (2024-09-10):** Performed specifically due to MRI limitations, this study confirmed "C3-C4: moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "Significant foraminal stenosis," concluding that the MRI "underestimated pathology due to hardware artifact from prior fusion."

**Conservative Treatment:**
*   **Physical Therapy:** 10 weeks (30 sessions) with documented "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms."
*   **Pharmacotherapy:** 6 months of Pregabalin (partial relief) and 3 months of Duloxetine (minimal benefit).
*   **Interventional:** Left C4 epidural steroid injection with good initial relief (60%), which returned to baseline by week 3.

### Appeal Review and Analysis

The appeal will be reviewed against the three-source hierarchy: **State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.**

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
The member's symptoms (left C4 dermatomal numbness, deltoid weakness, diminished bicep reflex) and imaging (nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis) are consistent with **cervical radiculopathy**. There are no documented signs of myelopathy (e.g., gait disturbance, bilateral weakness, hyperreflexia, Hoffman sign). Therefore, the myelopathy-specific exceptions in plan criteria and state law (DMHC APL 22-014) are not the primary drivers for this review, though general principles from state law apply.

**2. Application of HealthFirst Plan Clinical Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit (left C4 weakness, numbness) and imaging demonstrating structural pathology. This criterion is met.
*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:** The denial itself states, "Member meets conservative treatment requirements." The clinical record supports this:
    *   **Physical therapy:** 10 weeks of PT exceeds the 6-week minimum (2.2(a)).
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Two agents (Pregabalin for 6 months, Duloxetine for 3 months) meet the minimum 4-week trial for each (2.2(b)).
    *   **Interventional treatment:** One epidural injection was completed (2.2(c)).
    This criterion is clearly met.
*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** The plan's denial was based on "MRI shows mild degenerative changes" and "imaging does not show sufficient pathology." However, the initial reviewer failed to account for the subsequent CT myelogram, which was performed specifically to overcome the limitations of the MRI due to hardware artifact. The CT myelogram clearly demonstrated "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" consistent with the member's symptoms. Plan criteria 2.3 states, "Current imaging (within 6 months) must demonstrate structural pathology consistent with the member's symptoms." The CT myelogram, dated 2024-09-10 (within 6 months), definitively meets this requirement. The denial's reliance solely on the MRI while disregarding the more definitive CT myelogram constitutes an incorrect application of the plan's own imaging criteria and a failure to consider all relevant clinical evidence.
*   **Conclusion on Plan Criteria:** When all clinical information, particularly the CT myelogram, is considered, the member satisfies all of HealthFirst's medical necessity criteria. The denial was based on an incomplete and therefore incorrect assessment of the imaging requirement.

**3. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**

*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) — Standard of Review for IMR:** This critical provision states that the IMR standard does NOT require the service to be consistent with the plan's internal coverage criteria. If the service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice," it SHALL be approved regardless of more restrictive internal criteria. This legal standard mandates approval if the clinical evidence supports the surgery.
*   **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2) — Physical Therapy as Prerequisite:** This state law states that a plan shall not condition approval of surgery on completion of PT where "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." In this case, the member completed 10 weeks of PT, and the note indicates "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms." This clearly demonstrates a functional plateau for the neurological symptoms, which supports surgical intervention.

**4. Application of Clinical Standard Knowledge (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **Diagnosis & Pathology:** The clinical record clearly documents cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits (weakness 3+/5, diminished reflex, numbness) and moderate-severe nerve root compression confirmed by CT myelogram.
*   **Conservative Treatment Efficacy:** The member completed an adequate trial of conservative treatment (10 weeks PT, 2 pharmacotherapies, 1 injection). The lack of improvement in neurological symptoms (weakness) after this trial indicates a failure of conservative management for the specific problem.
*   **Surgical Indications for Radiculopathy:** According to AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) (Source Document 3, Section 3.1), surgery is a **TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION** for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and for "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care." Ms. Morrison's case fits these criteria precisely, with persistent symptoms after 10 weeks of PT and a 3+/5 deltoid weakness representing significant functional impairment. The clinical standard does not require further conservative treatment in this scenario.

**5. Conflict Resolution and Hierarchy:**

The plan's denial was based on an incomplete review of imaging, which directly contradicts the more definitive CT myelogram. When all evidence is considered, the member meets the plan's own criteria.

Furthermore, applying the hierarchy of authority, California state law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)) mandates approval if the service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice." The clinical record and guidelines (AANS/CNS, NASS) clearly establish that ACDF is medically necessary for radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits that have failed an adequate course of conservative treatment. This standard of care overrides any misapplication of the plan's internal criteria.

### Disposition

**OVERTURN** the denial.

### Warrant

The prior authorization denial for CPT 22551 (ACDF C3-C4) for Angela Morrison is **OVERTURNED** for the following reasons:

1.  **Insufficient Basis for Denial:** The initial denial was based on "imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4." This assertion is directly contradicted by the CT myelogram dated 2024-09-10, which explicitly confirmed "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis," overcoming the artifact limitations of the MRI. The initial reviewer failed to consider all relevant and conclusive diagnostic imaging, leading to an incorrect medical necessity determination.
2.  **Compliance with Plan Criteria:** When all available and appropriate imaging is considered, the member meets the imaging requirement (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.3). The denial itself explicitly acknowledged that the member "meets conservative treatment requirements" (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2), and the diagnosis is appropriately documented (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.1). Therefore, the procedure meets HealthFirst's internal medical necessity criteria.
3.  **Adherence to Clinical Standards:** The member's presentation of cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits (left C4 weakness 3+/5, numbness, diminished reflex) and confirmed moderate-severe nerve root compression, refractory to an extensive conservative treatment trial, aligns with generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention. AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) classify this scenario as a "Reasonable Indication" (TIER 2) for surgery for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms and significant functional impairment after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment (Source Document 3, Section 3.1).
4.  **Compliance with California State Law:** Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b), services that meet "generally accepted standards of medical practice" SHALL be approved, irrespective of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive or have been misapplied. Furthermore, the member's completion of physical therapy with "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms" meets the "functional plateau" criteria per California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2), further supporting that the conservative treatment requirement has been satisfied.

Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical record, HealthFirst's plan criteria, California state regulations, and generally accepted clinical standards, the requested ACDF at C3-C4 for Angela Morrison is medically necessary.
