**WARRANT FOR DETERMINATION:**

This appeal was reviewed applying the three-source hierarchy: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.

**I. Clinical Presentation Analysis:**

The clinical record demonstrates that Ms. Linda Park's primary diagnosis is **Cervical Radiculopathy (C6)**, specifically attributable to the C5-C6 level.

*   **Symptoms:** Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, diminished right bicep reflex, and 4/5 grip strength on the right. No C7 symptoms are documented.
*   **Neurological Exam:** Confirms C6 radicular findings. Critically, there are "No C7 findings documented. No bilateral findings. No upper motor neuron signs."
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-01):**
    *   **C5-C6:** "large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, moderate foraminal stenosis. Clear correlation with C6 radicular symptoms."
    *   **C6-C7:** "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable."
    *   There is no evidence of "cord signal change" or "myelomalacia" at any level, nor "spinal cord compression" at C6-C7.

This objective clinical and imaging evidence confirms a diagnosis of **radiculopathy**, not myelopathy. The distinction is critical as treatment algorithms and conservative treatment requirements differ significantly for these two conditions (Clinical Standard, Section 1.1). Therefore, the HealthFirst Plan's Myelopathy Exception 2.2(A) and California regulatory guidance related to myelopathy (DMHC APL 22-014) are not applicable to this case.

**II. Application to C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**

The requested ACDF at the C5-C6 level is medically necessary and meets HealthFirst Health Plan's criteria, generally accepted clinical standards, and California state regulatory requirements.

1.  **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) — MET:**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Member meets criterion 2.1(a) for Cervical Radiculopathy and 2.1(c) for Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit (C6 radiculopathy due to C5-C6 disc herniation).
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:**
        *   **Physical Therapy:** Member completed 8 weeks of supervised PT, exceeding the 6-week minimum (2.2(a)).
        *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Member completed trials of Gabapentin (4 months) and Cyclobenzaprine (6 weeks), meeting the requirement for two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each (2.2(b)).
        *   **Interventional Treatment:** Member completed a right C6 epidural steroid injection, meeting the requirement for one interventional procedure (2.2(c)).
        *   Symptoms persist after conservative treatment, indicating a functional plateau for the C6 radiculopathy.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** MRI demonstrates C5-C6 disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, correlating directly with the member's C6 radicular symptoms.
    *   **2.4 Physician Documentation:** The surgeon's note provides clinical rationale and functional limitations.

2.  **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020) — MET:**
    *   The member's presentation of radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after an adequate conservative trial aligns with a TIER 2 "Reasonable Indication" for surgical intervention per the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023 Update, Section 3.1, TIER 2(b)).

3.  **California Regulatory Framework — Supports Approval:**
    *   **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2):** This provision states that a plan cannot condition approval on completion of PT where the member has completed therapy and reached a functional plateau without adequate symptom resolution. Ms. Park's persistent symptoms after 8 weeks of PT meet this criterion, further supporting the adequacy of her conservative treatment trial.
    *   **CHSC § 1374.32(b):** Mandates approval if a service meets generally accepted standards of medical practice, regardless of more restrictive plan criteria. In this instance, the C5-C6 ACDF meets both plan criteria and clinical standards.

**III. Application to C6-C7 Level (Prophylactic):**

The requested ACDF at the C6-C7 level is **not** medically necessary and does not meet HealthFirst Health Plan's criteria or generally accepted clinical standards.

1.  **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) — NOT MET:**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** There are no documented C7 symptoms, neurological deficits, or imaging evidence of nerve root or spinal cord compression attributable to the C6-C7 level. The member does not have a confirmed diagnosis of radiculopathy or myelopathy at C6-C7.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement / 3.3 Exclusions:** The MRI at C6-C7 shows only "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable." This imaging pathology is *not* consistent with the member's clinical presentation, as no C7 symptoms are documented. Section 3.3 of the plan's criteria explicitly excludes authorization if imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level *consistent with clinical presentation*.
    *   The surgeon's rationale for C6-C7 is "prophylactically stabilize C6-C7 to prevent adjacent segment disease." HealthFirst's Section 2.1 lists specific diagnoses; prophylactic surgery for an asymptomatic level is not an approved indication under these criteria.

2.  **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023) — NOT MET:**
    *   The AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023 Update, Section 3.1) include TIER 3(b): "Surgery NOT INDICATED" for "Imaging findings without correlating symptoms." This directly applies to the C6-C7 level, where imaging shows only mild degenerative changes without correlating C7 symptoms or objective neurological deficit.
    *   While adjacent segment disease (ASD) is a recognized concern, current generally accepted clinical guidelines do not support prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic, non-compressive segment with only mild degenerative changes solely for ASD prevention. Surgical indications are typically tied to symptomatic pathology.

3.  **California Regulatory Framework:**
    *   No California state law or regulatory guidance (e.g., DMHC APL 22-014) mandates approval for an asymptomatic spinal level with mild degenerative changes that lacks correlating neurological symptoms or objective findings.

**IV. Conclusion:**

The initial denial correctly identified that the *two-level* ACDF was not medically necessary due to the lack of pathology and symptoms at the C6-C7 level. However, the available clinical information and the HealthFirst Plan's criteria, supported by clinical standards and state law, demonstrate medical necessity for an ACDF at the C5-C6 level for C6 radiculopathy.

Therefore, the denial is **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**. Authorization for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) is approved. Authorization for the second level at C6-C7 (CPT 22552) is upheld for denial.
