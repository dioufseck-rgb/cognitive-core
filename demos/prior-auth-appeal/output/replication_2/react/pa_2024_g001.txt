### I. Introduction

This determination addresses the appeal submitted by Marco Reyes for the prior authorization denial of an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7. The initial denial by HealthFirst Utilization Management was dated 2024-11-01, citing a failure to meet conservative treatment requirements under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2, specifically alleging only 3 weeks of physical therapy completed and no documented functional plateau.

### II. Procedure and Initial Denial Summary

**Procedure Requested:** CPT 22551, Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) C6-C7, one level.
**Initial Denial Reason (HealthFirst):** Member does not meet conservative treatment requirements under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2. Physical therapy: member has completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy. Standard pathway requires 6 weeks minimum. Functional plateau not documented. All other criteria remain unmet.

### III. Appeal Basis

The member's appeal argues the following:
1.  The denial miscounts the physical therapy (PT) duration; the member completed 3 weeks at Valley PT and 6 weeks at Capital PT, totaling 9 weeks.
2.  Records from both PT providers were submitted with the original authorization request.
3.  The PT discharge from Capital PT explicitly documents a functional plateau.
4.  All other conservative treatment criteria (pharmacotherapy and injection) were also completed.

### IV. Clinical Summary

**Presenting Diagnosis:** Cervical radiculopathy C6-C7, right-sided, persistent.
**Imaging (MRI 2024-08-20):** C6-C7 large disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression. **No cord contact. No myelomalacia.** Right foraminal stenosis confirmed.
**Neurological Exam (2024-08-25):** Right C7 dermatomal numbness. Tricep reflex diminished right. Grip strength 4/5 right. No bilateral findings. No Upper Motor Neuron (UMN) signs.
**Conservative Treatment History:**
*   **Physical Therapy:**
    *   Valley Physical Therapy: 3 weeks (9 sessions), 2024-06-01 to 2024-06-21. Outcome: Partial improvement, symptoms persist.
    *   Capital Physical Therapy: 6 weeks (18 sessions), 2024-07-08 to 2024-08-16. Outcome: Plateau reached. Discharge summary: "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."
    *   **Total PT:** 9 weeks across two providers (submitted with original PA). Functional plateau explicitly documented.
*   **Pharmacotherapy:**
    *   Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months. Response: Partial pain relief, radicular symptoms persist.
    *   Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months. Response: Inadequate.
*   **Interventional:**
    *   Right C7 Epidural Steroid Injection (2024-09-15). Response: 35% temporary relief, returned to baseline in 3 weeks.

### V. Reviewer's Analysis

This review applies the hierarchy of authority: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.**

#### A. Diagnostic Classification

Based on the clinical record (MRI findings of disc herniation and nerve root compression without cord contact or myelomalacia; neurological exam showing unilateral C7 dermatomal symptoms with no bilateral findings or UMN signs), Mr. Reyes's primary diagnosis is **Cervical Radiculopathy**, not Myelopathy. This distinction is critical as treatment algorithms and conservative treatment requirements differ.

#### B. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024)

The initial denial was explicitly based on HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2, "Conservative Treatment Requirement."

1.  **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:**
    *   Mr. Reyes has "Cervical radiculopathy C6-C7," which meets criterion 2.1(a). Imaging (MRI 2024-08-20) confirms structural pathology consistent with symptoms. **This criterion is met.**

2.  **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **2.2(a) Physical therapy:** The plan requires a "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes." The denial stated only 3 weeks were completed and a functional plateau was not documented.
        *   **Factual Correction:** The clinical record, as confirmed by the appeal, clearly documents 3 weeks of PT at Valley Physical Therapy and 6 weeks at Capital Physical Therapy, for a **total of 9 weeks**. Both records were submitted with the original request.
        *   **Functional Plateau:** The discharge summary from Capital Physical Therapy explicitly states, "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT," which satisfies the plan's requirement for "documented functional outcomes" and the definition of functional plateau (Clinical Standard Section 5).
        *   **Conclusion:** With the corrected facts, the member **exceeds** the 6-week minimum PT requirement, and a functional plateau is explicitly documented. **This criterion is met.**
    *   **2.2(b) Pharmacotherapy:** The plan requires a "trial of at least two... for minimum 4 weeks each."
        *   Mr. Reyes completed a 4-month trial of Gabapentin and a 3-month trial of Naproxen.
        *   **Conclusion:** **This criterion is met.**
    *   **2.2(c) Interventional treatment:** The plan requires "one of the following."
        *   Mr. Reyes underwent a right C7 epidural steroid injection (2024-09-15).
        *   **Conclusion:** **This criterion is met.**
    *   **EXCEPTION 2.2(A) — MYELOPATHY PATHWAY:** This exception is not applicable as Mr. Reyes has radiculopathy, not myelopathy. The denial correctly did not apply this exception, but rather failed to correctly apply the standard pathway.

3.  **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:**
    *   Current MRI (within 6 months) demonstrates C6-C7 disc herniation and C7 nerve root compression consistent with symptoms and the proposed surgical level. **This criterion is met.**

4.  **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** The denial did not cite issues with physician documentation, so it is presumed to be adequate.

**Summary of Plan Criteria Application:** The initial denial was based on a factual error regarding the duration of physical therapy and the documentation of a functional plateau. Upon correction of these facts, Mr. Reyes **fully meets all of HealthFirst's medical necessity criteria** outlined in CC-SPINE-2024, including the conservative treatment requirements.

#### C. Application of California Regulatory Framework

1.  **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):**
    *   This state law prohibits plans from conditioning surgical approval on PT completion where "(2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
    *   Mr. Reyes completed 9 weeks of PT, and his physical therapist explicitly documented a "functional plateau" (maximum therapeutic benefit reached).
    *   Furthermore, CIC 10169.5(c) states that "A plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau."
    *   **Conclusion:** The plan's denial based on an alleged failure to meet PT requirements, particularly given the documented functional plateau, directly contravenes California state law.

2.  **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):**
    *   This provision mandates that if a service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice," it shall be approved "regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive."
    *   While Mr. Reyes *does* meet the plan's criteria when correctly applied, this provision reinforces that even if HealthFirst's criteria were somehow more restrictive than what Mr. Reyes completed, the clinical standard would still govern.

#### D. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021)

1.  **Cervical Radiculopathy Treatment:** The clinical guidelines clearly distinguish radiculopathy from myelopathy. For cervical radiculopathy, conservative treatment is typically recommended for 6-12 weeks (Clinical Reference Section 3.1, Tier 2(b)).
2.  **Surgical Indication:** ACDF (CPT 22551) is a "reasonable indication" (TIER 2) for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care" (AANS/CNS Guidelines, Section 3.1).
3.  **Application to Marco Reyes:** Mr. Reyes has persistent right C7 radiculopathy symptoms (numbness, diminished reflex, 4/5 grip strength) despite having completed 9 weeks of physical therapy (with documented functional plateau), a 4-month course of neuropathic pain medication, a 3-month course of NSAIDs, and a cervical epidural steroid injection that provided only temporary, partial relief. This extensive conservative treatment, its documented failure to provide lasting relief, and the documented functional plateau clearly align with the clinical standard for surgical intervention for persistent radiculopathy.

### VI. Conflict Resolution

The HealthFirst denial was based on a fundamental factual error regarding the duration of physical therapy and the documentation of a functional plateau.
1.  **Plan Criteria vs. Clinical Record:** When the factual inaccuracies of the denial are corrected, Mr. Reyes **fully meets** all of HealthFirst's own criteria (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2).
2.  **State Law Override:** Even if HealthFirst were to dispute the adequacy of 9 weeks of PT or the documented functional plateau, California State Law (CIC § 10169.5) explicitly prohibits denials based on PT completion requirements when a functional plateau has been reached. This state law governs over plan criteria.
3.  **Clinical Standard Override:** Mr. Reyes's presentation and history of conservative treatment align with generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS TIER 2 indication) for surgical intervention for persistent cervical radiculopathy. California law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)) mandates approval if the service meets these generally accepted standards, even if plan criteria were more restrictive.

### VII. Disposition

The denial of prior authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) for Marco Reyes is **OVERTURNED**.

### VIII. Warrant

The denial is overturned for the following reasons:

1.  **Factual Error in Initial Denial:** The initial denial asserted that Mr. Reyes completed "only 3 weeks of physical therapy" and that a "functional plateau not documented." This factual claim is demonstrably incorrect. The clinical record clearly shows Mr. Reyes completed a total of **9 weeks of physical therapy** across two providers, and the discharge summary from Capital Physical Therapy explicitly documents that "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT," fulfilling the requirement for a documented functional plateau (Clinical Record; Plan Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a); Clinical Standard Section 5).
2.  **Compliance with Plan Criteria:** Upon correction of the factual errors, Mr. Reyes meets all of HealthFirst's own medical necessity criteria for cervical spinal surgery (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2). He completed more than the minimum 6 weeks of PT, successfully trialed two pharmacotherapy agents for over 4 weeks each, and underwent a cervical epidural steroid injection.
3.  **Violation of California State Law (CIC § 10169.5):** California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2) prohibits health plans from conditioning surgical approval on PT completion where the member has completed a course of PT and reached a functional plateau. Mr. Reyes has met this condition, rendering the plan's denial invalid under state law. Furthermore, CIC § 10169.5(c) prohibits the mechanical application of minimum PT duration requirements without considering whether a functional plateau has been achieved.
4.  **Adherence to Clinical Standards:** Mr. Reyes's persistent cervical radiculopathy, despite a comprehensive and failed course of conservative treatment (9 weeks PT with documented plateau, two pharmacotherapy agents, and an epidural injection) and objective imaging findings, meets the generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, TIER 2 indication) (Clinical Reference Section 3.1). Under CHSC § 1374.32(b), services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice shall be approved.

The HealthFirst denial relied on inaccurate information and failed to comply with both its own criteria, when correctly applied, and relevant California state regulations and generally accepted clinical standards.

### IX. Action Required

HealthFirst Health Plan is directed to approve the prior authorization request for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7, one level) for Marco Reyes without further delay.
