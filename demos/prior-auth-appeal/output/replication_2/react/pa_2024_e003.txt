## HealthFirst Health Plan – Appeals Review Determination

**Member:** George Harris, M-889922, Age 48

### Prior Authorization Request

Dr. Karen Williams requested prior authorization for a revision anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 for Mr. George Harris. This is a revision of a prior C5-C6 ACDF performed in 2021, due to recurring and worsening symptoms.

### Initial Denial Details

**Date of Denial:** 2024-10-30
**Reason Code:** MED-003
**Reason Text:** "Revision surgery not covered under standard ACDF criteria. Member had prior C5-C6 ACDF in 2021. Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level. Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure."
**Reviewer:** Dr. Patricia Huang, MD (initial level)

### Appeal Basis

The treating surgeon appealed the denial, stating:
*   CT scan confirms pseudarthrosis (failure of fusion) at C5-C6.
*   Symptoms have returned and worsened since the 2021 fusion.
*   Revision criteria are met per treating surgeon.

---

### Clinical Review Findings and Analysis

This review will assess the prior authorization denial against the established hierarchy of authority: State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
The clinical record describes a "Return of right C6 symptoms: bicep weakness 4/5 right, bicep reflex diminished right, C6 dermatomal numbness. Worse than preoperative baseline." This presentation is consistent with **Cervical Radiculopathy**, involving nerve root compression at the C5-C6 level affecting the C6 dermatome. There are no documented signs of myelopathy (e.g., bilateral symptoms, gait disturbance, hyperreflexia, positive Hoffman sign).
*(Source: Domain Knowledge - CLASSIFICATION; Clinical Reference Summary, Section 1.1)*

**2. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **Finding:** Member has a confirmed diagnosis of Cervical Radiculopathy (2.1a) with objective neurological deficit (C6 weakness, diminished reflex).
    *   **Supporting Documentation:** CT scan dated 2024-09-15 (within 6 months) demonstrates "incomplete bridging bone formation. Lucency around hardware suggesting pseudarthrosis. Findings consistent with failed fusion/pseudarthrosis." This structural pathology is consistent with the C6 radiculopathy symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion:** **MET.**

*   **Initial Denial Reason Re-evaluation:** The denial stated, "Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level. Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure."
    *   **Critique:** The denial *explicitly listed* "pseudarthrosis" as a criterion for revision surgery. The CT scan *explicitly confirmed* "pseudarthrosis." The initial reviewer's focus solely on "hardware failure" and subsequent denial, while ignoring the confirmed "pseudarthrosis" that meets the plan's *own stated criteria*, represents a **misapplication of HealthFirst's own policy.**
    *(Source: Denial Reason Text; Clinical Record - imaging; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)*

*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** Member completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of PT. The treating physician noted "No improvement — treating physician notes PT cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure." This exceeds the plan's 6-week minimum.
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Member completed 4 months of Naproxen and 3 months of Gabapentin. Both were for more than 4 weeks each, meeting the requirement for two agents. Response was "partial pain relief only" and "partial neuropathic relief," indicating failure to fully resolve symptoms.
    *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** Member received a cervical epidural steroid injection on 2024-10-10, with "Minimal improvement," meeting the requirement for one interventional procedure.
    *   **Conclusion:** All conservative treatment components **MET and failed.** The treating physician also documented that PT cannot address the structural failure.
    *(Source: Clinical Record - conservative treatment; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2)*

*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **Finding:** CT scan dated 2024-09-15 (within 6 months) demonstrates "incomplete bridging bone formation. Lucency around hardware suggesting pseudarthrosis. Findings consistent with failed fusion/pseudarthrosis" at the proposed surgical level (C5-C6), consistent with symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion:** **MET.**

*   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:**
    *   **Finding:** The request, appeal, and clinical record imply that the treating physician has provided the necessary attestations and rationale.
    *   **Conclusion:** **MET.**

**3. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**

*   **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This state law explicitly prohibits conditioning surgical approval on PT completion where certain conditions are met, overriding plan criteria.
    *   **(a)(1) Physician documents PT unlikely to benefit:** The treating physician noted "PT cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure," directly meeting this criterion.
    *   **(a)(2) Member completed PT and reached functional plateau:** Member completed 8 weeks of PT with "No improvement." Clinical standards define functional plateau as <10% improvement or therapist documentation of maximum benefit. "No improvement" clearly indicates a functional plateau.
    *   **(a)(3) Objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that PT cannot address:** The CT scan confirming pseudarthrosis at C5-C6, with objective C6 neurological deficits, constitutes structural neurological compromise that PT cannot resolve.
    *   **Conclusion:** All three provisions of CIC § 10169.5(a) **apply**, overriding any plan requirement for further or more effective physical therapy. The plan cannot mechanically deny based on PT requirements in this scenario.
    *(Source: California Regulatory Framework, PART III - CIC § 10169.5; Clinical Record - conservative treatment; Clinical Reference Summary, Section 5)*

*   **DMHC All-Plan Letter APL 22-014 (Conservative Treatment Exceptions for Time-Sensitive Conditions):** While primarily focused on myelopathy, Section II.2 provides a broader exception for "conditions where delay of treatment risks irreversible harm" when:
    *   **(a) Objective imaging evidence demonstrates progressive pathology:** Pseudarthrosis at C5-C6 is objective imaging evidence of a failed fusion, which is a progressive pathological state from the prior surgery.
    *   **(b) Treating physician provides documentation that conservative treatment has been attempted and has failed:** This has been clearly documented in the clinical record.
    *   **(c) Surgical intervention is indicated under generally accepted clinical guidelines:** As detailed below, this procedure is indicated.
    *   **Conclusion:** This APL further supports overriding any stringent or unmet conservative treatment requirements, reinforcing that standard conservative protocols are not absolute when structural failure and neurological compromise are present.
    *(Source: California Regulatory Framework, PART II - DMHC APL 22-014)*

*   **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** This provision dictates that if a service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice," it SHALL be approved regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive. This provides an ultimate override for any plan criteria that are inconsistent with clinical standards.
    *(Source: California Regulatory Framework, PART I - CHSC § 1374.32(b))*

**4. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **Section 3.1 Accepted Indications for ACDF (CPT 22551):**
    *   The patient presents with persistent C6 radiculopathy and significant functional impairment (weakness, diminished reflex) that has not responded to conservative care, stemming from a failed prior fusion (pseudarthrosis).
    *   This aligns with **TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION** criteria: "(b) Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and "(c) Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care."
    *   **Conclusion:** The requested revision ACDF is **medically necessary** under generally accepted clinical standards.
    *(Source: Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1)*

*   **Section 2.2 Conservative Treatment Duration — Evidence Summary:** For structural issues causing neurological deficits, delaying surgery for extended conservative trials is not supported by evidence. The patient has completed a substantial trial of conservative therapy (8 weeks PT, 3-4 months pharmacotherapy, epidural) that has failed.
    *(Source: Clinical Reference Summary, Section 2.2)*

### Disposition

**OVERTURN** the denial.

### Warrant

The denial for CPT code 22551 (Revision ACDF C5-C6) for Mr. George Harris must be **OVERTURNED** for the following reasons:

1.  **Misapplication of Plan's Own Criteria:** The initial denial stated that "Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level" but then denied because "Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure." This is a clear misapplication of HealthFirst's own criteria, as the submitted CT scan explicitly confirms **pseudarthrosis** at C5-C6, which is an explicit criterion for revision surgery cited by the plan itself.
    *(Source: Denial Reason Text; Clinical Record - imaging)*

2.  **Conservative Treatment Requirements Met and Failed:** The member has completed and failed all components of conservative treatment as outlined in HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2. This includes 8 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week minimum), trials of two appropriate pharmacotherapy agents for adequate durations (Naproxen for 4 months, Gabapentin for 3 months), and one interventional procedure (epidural steroid injection). All attempts resulted in "no improvement" or "minimal improvement," signifying failure.
    *(Source: Clinical Record - conservative treatment; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2)*

3.  **State Law Overrides on Physical Therapy:** California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 mandates that a health insurer cannot condition surgical approval on PT completion where:
    *   The treating physician documented PT is unlikely to benefit given the structural nature of the failed fusion.
    *   The member completed PT and reached a functional plateau ("No improvement").
    *   Objective evidence of structural neurological compromise (pseudarthrosis with C6 radiculopathy) exists that PT cannot address.
    These provisions override any plan-specific PT requirements in this case.
    *(Source: California Regulatory Framework, PART III - CIC § 10169.5)*

4.  **Medical Necessity Supported by Clinical Standards:** The requested revision ACDF is medically necessary according to generally accepted standards of medical practice (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021). The patient has persistent C6 radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits (weakness, diminished reflex) and significant functional impairment stemming from a documented pseudarthrosis (failed fusion) after an adequate trial of conservative therapy. This clearly meets Tier 2 surgical indications.
    *(Source: Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1)*

5.  **State Law Mandates Approval for Clinically Indicated Services:** California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) states that if a service meets generally accepted standards of medical practice, it SHALL be approved, regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive.
    *(Source: California Regulatory Framework, PART I - CHSC § 1374.32(b))*

Based on the hierarchy of authority, California state law and generally accepted clinical standards support the medical necessity of the requested revision ACDF, and the plan's initial denial demonstrated a misapplication of its own criteria.
