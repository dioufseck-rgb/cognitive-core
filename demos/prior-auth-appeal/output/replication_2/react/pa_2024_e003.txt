### **DISPOSITION: OVERTURN DENIAL**

### **WARRANT**

The denial for CPT 22551, Revision ACDF at C5-C6 for Mr. George Harris, is **OVERTURNED**. The initial denial was based on a misapplication of HealthFirst's own criteria, a procedural defect in the denial notice, and the member's clinical presentation meets generally accepted standards of medical practice which supersede plan criteria where more restrictive or incorrectly applied.

**Specific reasons for overturning:**

1.  **Plan Criteria Met and Misapplied by Initial Reviewer:**
    *   The initial denial stated: "Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level. Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure." (Denial, 2024-10-30).
    *   However, the submitted clinical record, specifically the CT scan dated 2024-09-15, explicitly confirms "incomplete bridging bone formation. Lucency around hardware suggesting pseudarthrosis. Findings consistent with failed fusion/pseudarthrosis." (Clinical record, CT scan 1).
    *   **Pseudarthrosis** is one of the explicitly listed criteria for revision surgery in the plan's own denial reason. By focusing solely on "hardware failure" and ignoring the clearly documented pseudarthrosis, the initial reviewer (Dr. Patricia Huang) misapplied HealthFirst's own criteria. Therefore, the member *does* meet the plan's stated criteria for revision surgery. (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, implied by denial reasoning, 2024-10-30).

2.  **Procedural Defect in Denial Notice:**
    *   California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b) mandates that a denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied or the specific clinical reason for the denial is defective. The denial notice in this case, by misrepresenting the submitted clinical facts (focusing only on hardware failure and ignoring confirmed pseudarthrosis), effectively failed to provide an accurate and specific clinical reason for denial. (CHSC § 1374.31(b)).

3.  **Conservative Treatment Requirements Met and Waived by State Law:**
    *   The member has completed 8 weeks of supervised physical therapy (exceeding HealthFirst's 6-week minimum), a trial of two pharmacotherapy agents (Naproxen for 4 months, Gabapentin for 3 months), and an interventional procedure (epidural steroid injection). (Clinical record).
    *   Despite this comprehensive course of treatment, the clinical record notes "No improvement" from PT and "Minimal improvement" from the epidural, with the treating physician documenting that conservative treatment "cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure" and is "expected when structural failure is present." (Clinical record, PT summary, Interventional).
    *   California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 prohibits conditioning approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where:
        *   (a)(1) The physician documents PT is unlikely to benefit given the nature and severity of the condition (met, physician documented PT cannot address structural failure).
        *   (a)(2) The member has completed PT and reached a functional plateau (met, "no improvement" after 8 weeks of PT indicates a functional plateau).
        *   (a)(3) The condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that PT cannot address (met, pseudarthrosis is a structural failure causing radiculopathy that PT cannot resolve).
    *   Therefore, any implicit or explicit requirement for further conservative treatment is preempted by state law. (CIC § 10169.5).

4.  **Consistency with Generally Accepted Clinical Standards:**
    *   Mr. Harris presents with persistent and worsening right C6 radiculopathy (bicep weakness 4/5, diminished bicep reflex, C6 dermatomal numbness) following a failed C5-C6 ACDF, with objective imaging confirming pseudarthrosis. (Clinical record, neurological exam, CT scan).
    *   The AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) and NASS Guidelines (2020) support surgical intervention as a reasonable option (TIER 2 indication) for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care." (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1).
    *   Mr. Harris has clearly surpassed the 6-12 week conservative treatment threshold with documented failure of these interventions to address the underlying structural pseudarthrosis and improve his worsening radicular symptoms. Surgical correction of the failed fusion is indicated per clinical standards. (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1, 5).

---

### **APPEAL DETERMINATION**

Based on the comprehensive review of the submitted clinical documentation, HealthFirst's own clinical coverage criteria, applicable California state laws, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice, the initial denial is **OVERTURNED**.

The requested Revision ACDF (CPT 22551) for Mr. George Harris at C5-C6 is deemed medically necessary and should be authorized.
