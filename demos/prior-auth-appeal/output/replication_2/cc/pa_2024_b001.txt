FINAL APPEAL DETERMINATION: PARTIAL OVERTURN

Dear Ms. Park and Dr. Wilson,

The HealthFirst Health Plan Appeal Resolution Department has completed its review of the prior authorization appeal for the requested two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 and C6-C7. Based on a comprehensive evaluation of the clinical record, HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria, California statutory requirements, and generally accepted standards of medical practice, we have reached a PARTIAL DETERMINATION:

1. C5-C6 LEVEL (CPT 22551): OVERTURNED (APPROVED)
The prior authorization denial for the C5-C6 level is OVERTURNED. Surgical intervention at this level is approved as medically necessary.

2. C6-C7 LEVEL (CPT 22551): UPHELD (DENIED)
The prior authorization denial for the C6-C7 level is UPHELD. Surgical intervention at this level is denied as not medically necessary.

SECTION I: CLINICAL SUMMARY & FINDINGS OF FACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Presenting Diagnosis: The clinical record documents a primary diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6 with adjacent C6-C7 degenerative disc disease (Clinical Record: Presenting Diagnosis).

2. Clinical Presentation & Neurological Exam (Dated September 5, 2024):
   - Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).
   - Diminished right bicep reflex (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).
   - Right grip strength of 4/5 (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).
   - No C7 dermatomal or myotomal findings documented (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).
   - No bilateral findings or upper motor neuron signs documented (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).

3. Diagnostic Imaging (MRI Dated September 1, 2024):
   - C5-C6: Large disc herniation with moderate-to-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis, correlating directly with the patient's right C6 radicular symptoms (Clinical Record: Imaging, mri_1).
   - C6-C7: Mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no spinal cord contact, and no identifiable nerve root compression (Clinical Record: Imaging, mri_1).

4. Conservative Treatment History:
   - Physical Therapy: Completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised physical therapy across two providers, directed at the cervical spine. Right C6 radicular symptoms persisted; no C7 symptoms were ever documented (Clinical Record: Conservative Treatment, PT Summary).
   - Pharmacotherapy: Completed a 4-month trial of Gabapentin 600mg TID (neuropathic agent) with partial relief, and a 6-week trial of Cyclobenzaprine (muscle relaxant) with minimal benefit (Clinical Record: Conservative Treatment, Pharmacotherapy).
   - Interventional Treatment: Received a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 1, 2024, which provided 50% temporary relief of right C6 symptoms, with symptoms fully returning by week 4 (Clinical Record: Conservative Treatment, Interventional).

5. Surgeon's Rationale: Dr. Wilson recommended a two-level fusion to address the active C5-C6 pathology and to prophylactically stabilize the C6-C7 level to prevent adjacent segment disease, citing a literature-based 25% 5-year incidence of adjacent segment disease after single-level ACDF (Clinical Record: Surgeon Note).

SECTION II: LEVEL-BY-LEVEL MEDICAL NECESSITY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ANALYSIS OF THE C5-C6 LEVEL (APPROVED)

Surgical decompression and fusion at the C5-C6 level fully satisfies all applicable clinical, regulatory, and evidence-based standards:

- HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):
  * Section 2.1 (Diagnosis): Satisfied. The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with objective nerve root compression and correlating dermatomal symptoms (right C6 numbness, diminished bicep reflex, 4/5 grip strength) (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).
  * Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment): Satisfied. The member completed 8 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week minimum in 2.2(a)), trialed two qualifying medications (Gabapentin and Cyclobenzaprine) for at least 4 weeks each (satisfying 2.2(b)), and underwent one cervical epidural steroid injection (satisfying 2.2(c)) (Clinical Record: Conservative Treatment).
  * Section 2.3 (Imaging): Satisfied. The MRI dated September 1, 2024, demonstrates a large disc herniation and moderate-to-severe nerve root compression at C5-C6, which directly correlates with the right C6 radicular symptoms (Clinical Record: Imaging, mri_1).

- California Regulatory Framework (CHSC § 1374.32):
  Under California's statutory standard of medical necessity, a service is medically necessary if a prudent physician would provide it to treat an illness or injury in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice. Dr. Wilson's request for the C5-C6 level is clinically appropriate, effective, and aligned with standard practice.

- Clinical Evidence Standard:
  According to the AANS/CNS Joint Section Guidelines (2023) and NASS Guidelines (2020), surgical decompression (ACDF) is a Tier 2 "Reasonable Indication" for patients with cervical radiculopathy who have persistent symptoms and objective neurological deficits after an adequate 6-to-12-week trial of conservative therapy (Clinical Evidence Document: Section 3.1). The member's persistent C6 radiculopathy despite 8 weeks of PT, 4 months of Gabapentin, and an epidural injection meets this standard.

Therefore, the prior authorization denial for the C5-C6 level is overturned and approved.

2. ANALYSIS OF THE C6-C7 LEVEL (DENIED)

Surgical decompression and fusion at the C6-C7 level fails to meet clinical, regulatory, or evidence-based standards of medical necessity:

- HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):
  * Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement): Not Met. Current imaging must demonstrate structural pathology consistent with the member's symptoms and the proposed surgical level. The MRI shows only a "mild disc bulge" and "minimal foraminal narrowing" with "no nerve root compression identifiable" at C6-C7 (Clinical Record: Imaging, mri_1).
  * Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement): Not Met. There is no documented diagnosis of C7 radiculopathy or myelopathy. The neurological exam notes "no C7 findings documented" (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).
  * Section 3.3 (Exclusions): Applicable. The policy explicitly states that authorization will be denied when "imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with clinical presentation" (Plan Criteria Document: Section 3.3).

- California Regulatory Framework (CHSC § 1374.32):
  Under California law, a service must be "clinically appropriate, in terms of type, frequency, extent, site, and duration" and "not primarily for the convenience of the patient, treating physician, or other provider" (Regulatory Document: Part I, § 1374.32(a)). Fusing an asymptomatic spinal level with only mild, non-compressive degenerative changes does not meet this statutory standard.

- Clinical Evidence Standard:
  Under generally accepted standards of medical practice, including the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023), surgical intervention is "Not Indicated" (Tier 3) for imaging findings without correlating clinical symptoms (Clinical Evidence Document: Section 3.1). 
  While Dr. Wilson's note regarding the 25% 5-year incidence of adjacent segment disease is a recognized epidemiological observation, prophylactic fusion of an adjacent, asymptomatic level with mild degenerative changes is not supported by high-quality clinical evidence and does not constitute medical necessity (Clinical Evidence Document: Section 3.1, Tier 3(b)). Prophylactic stabilization in the absence of active nerve root or spinal cord compression is inconsistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice.

Therefore, the prior authorization denial for the C6-C7 level is upheld.

SECTION III: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & PROCEDURAL DEFECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Procedural Defect in Initial Denial Notice:
   Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(a)(2), a health plan's denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reasons for the denial. Furthermore, under § 1374.31(b), a denial notice that fails to provide this level-specific specificity is procedurally defective (Regulatory Document: Part I, § 1374.31).
   The initial denial issued on October 28, 2024, issued a blanket denial for the entire "Two-level ACDF" request. This was procedurally defective because it failed to recognize that the C5-C6 level fully satisfied all clinical criteria and conservative treatment requirements, and it failed to offer a partial approval for the medically necessary level. This appeal determination corrects that defect by separating the levels and issuing a partial overturn.

2. Resolution of Cross-Source Conflicts:
   Under California Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) regulations (APL 22-014 and CHSC § 1374.32(b)), where a conflict exists between a plan's internal coverage criteria and generally accepted standards of medical practice, the clinical standard and state regulatory requirements supersede the plan's internal criteria (Regulatory Document: Part I, § 1374.32; Part II, II.3). 
   In this case, there is no conflict: the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), California statutory medical necessity standards (CHSC § 1374.32), and national clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS, NASS) are in complete alignment. All three sources support surgical intervention at the symptomatic C5-C6 level and exclude surgical intervention at the asymptomatic C6-C7 level.
