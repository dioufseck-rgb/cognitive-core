Dear Mr. Patel and Dr. Park,

The HealthFirst Health Plan Clinical Appeals Department has completed its review of the appeal submitted on November 18, 2024, regarding the prior authorization denial for a two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 and C6-C7. 

Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical record, the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), and the California Regulatory Framework, we have MODIFIED the initial decision. We have OVERTURNED the denial for the C5-C6 level (CPT 22551) and authorized this procedure. We have UPHELD the denial for the C6-C7 level (CPT 22552) because it does not currently meet medical necessity criteria. 

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: PROCEDURAL DEFECT IN INITIAL PLAN DETERMINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31 and CHSC § 1367.01, health plans are required to apply medical necessity criteria reasonably and are prohibited from unnecessarily delaying or denying medically necessary care. 

Our review identifies a significant procedural and clinical defect in the initial denial issued on November 1, 2024. The initial reviewer, Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, issued a blanket denial of the entire two-level request because one of the requested levels (C6-C7) did not meet criteria, stating: "Two-level fusion not approved when one level meets criteria and one does not." 

This blanket denial of a clearly indicated, medically necessary procedure at C5-C6—where the patient exhibits an active, severe neurological deficit (3+/5 right bicep weakness)—constitutes an unlawful delay of care under CHSC § 1367.01. Under California regulatory standards, the plan had an obligation to modify the request at the initial review stage, authorizing the medically necessary C5-C6 level (CPT 22551) while denying the C6-C7 level (CPT 22552). This determination formally corrects that procedural defect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: CLINICAL ANALYSIS & FINDINGS FOR C5-C6 (CPT 22551) — APPROVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The request for ACDF at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) fully satisfies the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) and California's statutory medical necessity standard (CHSC § 1374.32):

1. Diagnosis and Objective Neurological Deficit (Section 2.1): 
   The clinical record dated September 20, 2024, documents a severe, objective neurological deficit in the right C6 dermatomal distribution, including right bicep weakness graded at 3+/5, an absent right bicep reflex, and right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling. This satisfies Section 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit).

2. Imaging Support (Section 2.3):
   The cervical spine MRI dated September 15, 2024, demonstrates a large disc herniation at C5-C6 causing moderate-to-severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. This structural pathology directly correlates with the member's right-sided C6 radiculopathy, satisfying Section 2.3.

3. Conservative Treatment (Section 2.2):
   The member has completed an exhaustive course of conservative therapy that meets and exceeds all plan requirements:
   - Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)): Completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised physical therapy directed at the cervical spine, during which C6 symptoms persisted.
   - Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)): Documented trial of Pregabalin 150mg BID for 4 months (neuropathic agent) and Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months (NSAID), satisfying the requirement for two agents trialed for at least 4 weeks.
   - Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)): Received a right C6 cervical epidural steroid injection on October 15, 2024, which provided 70% temporary relief but symptoms fully returned within 4 weeks.

Because all clinical criteria are met, surgical decompression and fusion at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) is medically necessary and is hereby APPROVED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: CLINICAL ANALYSIS & FINDINGS FOR C6-C7 (CPT 22552) — DENIED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The request for ACDF at C6-C7 (CPT 22552) does not satisfy the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) or generally accepted standards of medical practice, and is therefore UPHELD (DENIED) without prejudice based on the following clinical findings:

1. Lack of Correlative Imaging Pathology (Section 2.3 & Section 3.3):
   The cervical spine MRI dated September 15, 2024, reveals only a small disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing at C6-C7. Crucially, the radiologist noted "no nerve root compression identifiable" and "no cord contact" at this level. Under Section 3.3, surgical authorization must be denied when imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with the clinical presentation.

2. Absence of Definitive Clinical Symptoms (Section 2.1):
   The neurological examination dated September 20, 2024, notes "no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms" and states that mild C7 symptoms are "possibly present but not clearly documented." The only objective finding was a "slightly diminished" right tricep reflex. Furthermore, the physical therapy discharge summary at week 8 explicitly notes that C7 symptoms were "minimal and possibly resolving."

3. Incomplete Conservative Treatment for the C7 Level (Section 2.2):
   While the member failed conservative treatment for the C6 level, there is no documentation of targeted conservative management for a C7 radiculopathy. Specifically, no C7-specific interventional treatment (such as a selective nerve root block) has been performed.

Because the clinical findings and imaging at C6-C7 do not demonstrate significant structural compromise or correlating persistent radicular symptoms, a two-level fusion is not clinically indicated at this time. The denial of CPT 22552 is UPHELD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR C6-C7 RECONSIDERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because the patient's pathology and clinical presentation are strictly right-sided (unilateral), recommending a bilateral injection would expose the patient to unnecessary procedural risks on the asymptomatic left side. 

Therefore, if the patient's right-sided C7 symptoms persist or worsen following the C5-C6 decompression, the following clinical steps are recommended before surgical intervention at C6-C7 is reconsidered:
1. A targeted right-sided (unilateral) selective nerve root block or epidural steroid injection targeting the right C7 nerve root to confirm the C7 level as a symptomatic pain generator.
2. Updated clinical documentation detailing persistent, objective right-sided C7 dermatomal neurological deficits (e.g., progressive tricep weakness, persistent C7 dermatomal sensory loss) that have failed to respond to conservative measures.
3. Updated imaging if there is clinical concern for progressive structural pathology.
