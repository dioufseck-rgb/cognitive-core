FINAL APPEAL DETERMINATION: PARTIAL OVERTURN

Dear Ms. Mills,

HealthFirst Health Plan has completed its review of the appeal submitted on your behalf by Dr. James Torres regarding the prior authorization request for a three-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at the C3-C4, C4-C5, and C5-C6 levels. 

Based on a rigorous, level-by-level clinical and regulatory review of your medical records, the plan's clinical coverage criteria, California regulatory requirements, and generally accepted standards of medical practice, we have issued a PARTIAL OVERTURN of the initial decision. 

The specific determination for each requested spinal level is detailed below:

1. C4-C5 LEVEL: APPROVAL MAINTAINED (No Action Required)
The plan's initial determination on November 25, 2024, supported and approved surgical intervention at the C4-C5 level. This approval remains in full effect. Your clinical record demonstrates severe spinal cord compression and myelomalacia (T2 signal change) on MRI (2024-10-01), correlating with objective myelopathic findings (bilateral hyperreflexia, bilateral Hoffman sign, wide-based gait, and bilateral deltoid weakness of 4/5) on neurological exam (2024-10-05). This satisfies HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(b) (Cervical Myelopathy) and Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement). Because the plan previously approved this level, no appeal action is required to overturn a denial for C4-C5.

2. C5-C6 LEVEL: DENIAL OVERTURNED (Surgical Intervention Approved)
The initial denial of surgical intervention at the C5-C6 level is OVERTURNED. 
* Compliance with Plan Criteria: Under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a), a member must have a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (nerve root compression with radicular symptoms). Your MRI on 2024-10-01 demonstrated a moderate disc herniation with moderate left C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. Your neurological exam on 2024-10-05 documented correlating objective neurological deficits, specifically left bicep weakness (graded 4-/5) and a diminished left bicep reflex. 
* Conservative Treatment: You completed a comprehensive course of conservative treatment that fully satisfies CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2:
  - Physical Therapy: You completed 9 weeks (27 sessions) of supervised physical therapy, exceeding the 6-week minimum required under Section 2.2(a).
  - Pharmacotherapy: You completed trials of Pregabalin (neuropathic agent) for 4 months and Baclofen (muscle relaxant) for 3 months, satisfying the requirement for two agents for at least 4 weeks each under Section 2.2(b).
  - Interventional Treatment: You underwent a cervical epidural steroid injection on 2024-10-20, satisfying Section 2.2(c).
* Clinical Significance of Epidural Response: The plan's initial review noted that the epidural injection on 2024-10-20 resulted in "modest improvement in radicular symptoms." While the plan's Medical Director might argue that this response indicates conservative treatment is effective and surgery is premature, clinical standards (AANS/CNS 2023 Joint Guidelines) dictate otherwise. The physical therapy summary explicitly notes that despite the temporary, modest relief from the epidural, your "C5-C6 radicular symptoms persist" and you continue to exhibit an objective motor deficit (left bicep weakness 4-/5). Under generally accepted standards of medical practice, radiculopathy with persistent motor weakness that is refractory to conservative therapy is a clear indication for surgical decompression (AANS/CNS 2023 Tier 2 Indication). Furthermore, because you have progressive myelopathy at the adjacent C4-C5 level requiring surgery, performing a single-stage two-level decompression and fusion (C4-C5 and C5-C6) is clinically superior to leaving a symptomatic, compressed adjacent level unfused, which would expose you to a high risk of rapid clinical deterioration and subsequent revision surgery.
* Conclusion for C5-C6: Because you met all plan criteria and clinical standards, the denial of the C5-C6 level was incorrect and is hereby overturned.

3. C3-C4 LEVEL: DENIAL UPHELD (Surgical Intervention Not Approved)
The initial denial of surgical intervention at the C3-C4 level is UPHELD.
* Non-Compliance with Plan Criteria: Under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1 and Section 3.1, surgical intervention is not authorized for axial neck pain alone without documented radiculopathy or myelopathy, or where imaging does not demonstrate correlating pathology. Your MRI on 2024-10-01 showed only a "mild disc bulge" and "minimal foraminal narrowing" at C3-C4. Your neurological exam on 2024-10-05 noted "unclear — neck pain with C3-C4 palpation but no specific radicular pattern," and your physical therapy summary confirmed that the "C3-C4 contribution [is] unclear." Because there is no objective neurological deficit or radicular symptom pattern correlating to the C3-C4 level, this level does not meet the plan's medical necessity criteria.
* Evaluation of Surgeon's Rationale and Adjacent Segment Disease (ASD): Dr. Torres has argued that fusing C3-C4 is necessary to prevent adjacent segment disease (ASD), as fusing C4-C5 and C5-C6 will place increased mechanical stress on the C3-C4 level. Under California's Independent Medical Review (IMR) standard (California Health & Safety Code § 1374.32), we must evaluate whether a prudent physician would perform fusion at C3-C4 to prevent ASD. While ASD is a recognized long-term risk of multi-level fusion, generally accepted standards of medical practice (AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines; NASS 2020 Guidelines) do not support prophylactic fusion of a mildly degenerated, asymptomatic level. The added surgical morbidity, increased operative time, higher risk of pseudarthrosis (non-union), and significant loss of cervical range of motion associated with extending the fusion to a third level (C3-C4) outweigh the theoretical benefits of prophylactic fusion when there is no objective neurological compromise or significant structural compression at that level. Prudent clinical judgment dictates limiting the fusion construct to the levels with clear, correlating structural pathology and neurological deficits (C4-C5 and C5-C6). Therefore, surgical intervention at C3-C4 is not medically necessary at this time.

SUMMARY OF AUTHORIZED SERVICES:
* Approved: Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF), Two Levels (C4-C5 and C5-C6) under CPT codes 22551 and 22552 (add-on level).
* Denied: Extension of ACDF to the C3-C4 level.
