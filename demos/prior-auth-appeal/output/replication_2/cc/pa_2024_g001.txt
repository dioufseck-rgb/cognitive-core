Dear Mr. Reyes and Dr. Okonkwo,

HealthFirst Health Plan has completed its clinical and regulatory review of the appeal submitted on November 14, 2024, regarding the prior authorization denial for the requested anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551). 

Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical documentation, the applicable HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), California regulatory requirements, and generally accepted clinical standards, the initial decision to deny coverage has been OVERTURNED. The requested surgical procedure is approved.


I. CLINICAL BACKGROUND & PRESENTING PRESENTATION
Mr. Reyes is a 49-year-old male with a history of persistent, right-sided C6-C7 cervical radiculopathy. 
- Clinical Examination (August 25, 2024): Demonstrates objective neurological deficits, including right C7 dermatomal numbness, a diminished right tricep reflex, and right-sided grip strength of 4/5.
- Diagnostic Imaging (MRI dated August 20, 2024): Confirms a large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression and right foraminal stenosis. No cord contact or myelomalacia is present.


II. RESOLUTION OF FACTUAL DISCREPANCY & PLAN CRITERIA ANALYSIS
The initial denial issued on November 1, 2024, cited Section 2.2 of HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, asserting that the member completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy (PT), failed to meet the 6-week minimum requirement, and did not document a functional plateau. 

A detailed audit of the clinical record submitted with the original prior authorization request reveals a critical factual error in the initial review. The reviewer accounted only for the records from Valley Physical Therapy and entirely overlooked the records from Capital Physical Therapy. 

Under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2, the criteria are fully satisfied as follows:

1. DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT (Section 2.1):
   - Section 2.1(a) requires a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with nerve root compression and radicular symptoms (pain, numbness, weakness in dermatomal distribution). This is satisfied by the clinical exam on August 25, 2024 (C7 numbness, diminished tricep reflex, 4/5 grip strength) and the MRI on August 20, 2024, showing C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression.

2. CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT (Section 2.2):
   - Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)): Requires a minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy directed toward the cervical condition. The clinical record documents that Mr. Reyes completed a total of 9 weeks of physical therapy across two providers:
     * Valley Physical Therapy: June 1, 2024, to June 21, 2024 (3 weeks, 9 sessions) — resulting in partial improvement with persistent symptoms.
     * Capital Physical Therapy: July 8, 2024, to August 16, 2024 (6 weeks, 18 sessions) — resulting in a documented functional plateau.
     * Total PT: 9 weeks and 27 sessions, which exceeds the plan's 6-week minimum.
     * Functional Plateau: The Capital PT discharge summary explicitly documents a functional plateau, stating: "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."
   - Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)): Requires a trial of at least two specified agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. Mr. Reyes completed:
     * Gabapentin (neuropathic agent): 600mg TID for 4 months (partial pain relief, radicular symptoms persist).
     * Naproxen (NSAID): 500mg BID for 3 months (inadequate response).
     This satisfies the requirement for two agents trialed for at least 4 weeks.
   - Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)): Requires at least one interventional treatment. Mr. Reyes underwent a right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 15, 2024, which provided 35% temporary relief before symptoms returned to baseline within 3 weeks. This satisfies the interventional requirement.

3. IMAGING REQUIREMENT (Section 2.3):
   - Satisfied by the MRI dated August 20, 2024 (within 6 months of the request), demonstrating structural pathology consistent with the right C7 radicular symptoms.

4. PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION (Section 2.4):
   - Satisfied by the clinical records and surgical request submitted by Dr. Sarah Okonkwo, MD, which outline the clinical rationale, functional limitations, and failure of conservative therapies.


III. CALIFORNIA REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
Under California law, the mechanical application of physical therapy duration requirements to deny medically necessary surgery is prohibited when a functional plateau has been reached.

- California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) states that a health insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician (or physical therapist) documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms.
- CIC § 10169.5(c) further mandates that a plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau.
- Because Mr. Reyes completed 9 weeks of PT and reached an documented functional plateau (as certified in the Capital PT discharge summary), the plan's initial mechanical denial violates CIC § 10169.5.


IV. CLINICAL EVIDENCE STANDARD
According to generally accepted clinical standards, including the North American Spine Society (NASS) Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020) and the AANS/CNS Joint Section Guidelines (2023), surgical decompression via ACDF is highly indicated (Tier 2 - Reasonable Indication) for patients with cervical radiculopathy who present with correlating MRI findings, objective neurological deficits (sensory loss, reflex changes, and motor weakness), and who have failed to achieve adequate symptom resolution after a comprehensive course of conservative management (including physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and epidural steroid injections).

Delaying surgical intervention in a patient who has already completed 9 weeks of physical therapy, trialed multiple medications, undergone an epidural injection, and reached a documented functional plateau is clinically unsupported and increases the risk of prolonged neurological deficit.


V. CONCLUSION
Because the clinical record demonstrates that Mr. Reyes has fully satisfied all medical necessity criteria under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2, and because the initial denial was based on a factual oversight that violates California Insurance Code § 10169.5, the initial denial is hereby OVERTURNED. 

Authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7, one level) at UCSF Medical Center is APPROVED. This authorization is valid for 90 days from the date of this letter (expiring February 13, 2025) per CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.5.
