FINAL APPEAL DETERMINATION: PARTIAL OVERTURN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dear Mr. Patel and Dr. Park,

HealthFirst Health Plan has completed its clinical and regulatory review of the first-level appeal submitted on November 18, 2024, regarding the prior authorization denial for a two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 and C6-C7. 

Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical records, the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), California statutory and regulatory mandates, and generally accepted clinical guidelines, we have reached the following determination:

1. C5-C6 LEVEL (CPT 22551): The prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED. Surgical intervention at the C5-C6 level is APPROVED.
2. C6-C7 LEVEL (CPT 22552): The prior authorization denial is UPHELD. Surgical intervention at the C6-C7 level is DENIED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLINICAL AND REGULATORY RATIONALE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I. ANALYSIS OF C5-C6 LEVEL (CPT 22551) — OVERTURNED (APPROVED)

The clinical documentation submitted demonstrates that the member meets all plan criteria, regulatory requirements, and clinical standards for surgical decompression and fusion at the C5-C6 level.

1. Clinical Presentation and Diagnosis:
   - The clinical record documents a primary presentation of severe right C6 radiculopathy. 
   - The neurological exam dated September 20, 2024, reveals objective neurological deficits in the C6 distribution: right bicep weakness graded at 3+/5, an absent right bicep reflex, and right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling.
   - This satisfies HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.1(a) (Cervical radiculopathy with nerve root compression and radicular symptoms) and Section 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit).

2. Imaging Findings:
   - The cervical spine MRI dated September 15, 2024, demonstrates a large disc herniation at C5-C6 causing moderate-to-severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis.
   - This satisfies CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement), which requires current imaging demonstrating structural pathology consistent with the member's symptoms and proposed surgical level.

3. Conservative Treatment:
   - The member completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised physical therapy directed at the cervical spine, with persistent C6 symptoms.
   - Pharmacotherapy trials included Pregabalin 150mg BID for 4 months (neuropathic agent) and Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months (NSAID), satisfying the requirement for two agents tried for at least 4 weeks.
   - The member underwent a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 15, 2024, which provided 70% temporary relief but symptoms fully returned within 4 weeks.
   - This satisfies CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement), including physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and interventional treatment.
   - Furthermore, under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(3), a health plan cannot condition surgical approval on the completion of physical therapy when there is objective evidence of structural neurological compromise (such as the 3+/5 bicep weakness and absent reflex documented here) that physical therapy cannot address.

4. Clinical Standards:
   - Under the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) and NASS Guidelines (2020), surgical decompression is a Tier 2 (Strongly Indicated) intervention for patients with cervical radiculopathy, correlating MRI pathology, and objective neurological deficits who have failed conservative management.

Therefore, the denial of CPT 22551 for the C5-C6 level is medically unjustified and is hereby overturned.

II. ANALYSIS OF C6-C7 LEVEL (CPT 22552) — UPHELD (DENIED)

The clinical documentation submitted does not support the medical necessity of extending the fusion to the C6-C7 level at this time.

1. Clinical Presentation and Diagnosis:
   - The neurological exam dated September 20, 2024, notes only a "slightly diminished" right tricep reflex, with "no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms." 
   - The physical therapy discharge summary at week 8 explicitly notes that C7 symptoms were "minimal and possibly resolving."
   - This fails to establish a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy or myelopathy at the C7 level under CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.1.

2. Imaging Findings:
   - The MRI dated September 15, 2024, shows only a "small disc bulge" and "mild foraminal narrowing" at C6-C7, with "no nerve root compression identifiable" and "no cord contact."
   - This fails CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.3 and Section 3.3 (Exclusions), which mandate that imaging must demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with the clinical presentation.

3. Conservative Treatment:
   - While conservative therapy was completed for the cervical spine generally, no C7-specific interventional treatment (such as a C7-specific epidural or selective nerve root block) was performed to confirm the C6-C7 level as a symptomatic pain generator.

4. Clinical Standards:
   - Under the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) and NASS Guidelines (2020), surgical intervention at a level with mild imaging findings, resolving or absent dermatomal symptoms, and no objective nerve root compression is classified as Tier 3 (Surgery Not Indicated).
   - Prophylactic fusion of an adjacent level to "prevent accelerated degeneration" in the absence of correlating clinical and radiographic pathology is not supported by clinical evidence.

Therefore, the denial of CPT 22552 for the C6-C7 level is upheld.

III. RESOLUTION OF PROCEDURAL DEFECT IN INITIAL DENIAL

Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31, a health plan must provide specific clinical criteria and reasons for any denial. The initial denial notice dated November 1, 2024, issued by Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, denied the entire two-level request because "Two-level fusion [is] not approved when one level meets criteria and one does not."

Denying an entire surgical request—including a level (C5-C6) that clearly and unambiguously meets all medical necessity criteria—solely because an adjacent requested level (C6-C7) does not meet criteria is a procedural defect. Under CHSC § 1374.32, health plans must evaluate medical necessity for each requested service. Denying the medically necessary C5-C6 decompression and fusion based on the non-conformance of C6-C7 violates California's medical necessity standard. This appeal determination corrects that defect by issuing a partial approval.
