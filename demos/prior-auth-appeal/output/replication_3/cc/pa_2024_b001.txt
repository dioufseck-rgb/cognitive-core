CLINICAL HISTORY AND PRESENTING EVIDENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mrs. Linda Park is a 45-year-old female presenting with a diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6 with adjacent C6-C7 degenerative disease. 

- Clinical Presentation & Neurological Exam (2024-09-05): The patient exhibits right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, a diminished right bicep reflex, and right grip strength of 4/5. There are no documented C7 dermatomal symptoms, no bilateral findings, and no upper motor neuron signs.
- Imaging (MRI dated 2024-09-01): 
  * C5-C6: Demonstrates a large disc herniation with moderate-to-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis, which clearly correlates with the patient's right C6 radicular symptoms.
  * C6-C7: Demonstrates a mild disc bulge and minimal foraminal narrowing, with no cord contact and no identifiable nerve root compression.
- Conservative Treatment:
  * Physical Therapy: Completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised physical therapy across two providers, with persistent C6 radicular symptoms. No C7 symptoms were documented during therapy.
  * Pharmacotherapy: Completed a 4-month trial of Gabapentin 600mg TID (partial relief) and a 6-week trial of Cyclobenzaprine (minimal benefit).
  * Interventional Treatment: Received a right C6 epidural steroid injection on 2024-10-01, which provided 50% temporary relief of right C6 symptoms, with symptoms fully returning by week 4.
- Surgeon's Rationale: Dr. Wilson recommended a two-level fusion to address the C5-C6 pathology and to prophylactically stabilize the C6-C7 level to prevent adjacent segment disease, noting a 25% 5-year incidence of adjacent segment disease after single-level ACDF.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLINICAL AND REGULATORY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I. ANALYSIS OF C5-C6 LEVEL (OVERTURNED / APPROVED)

The clinical evidence fully satisfies the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (Document: CC-SPINE-2024) for the C5-C6 level:
1. Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1(a)): The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with nerve root compression and correlating radicular symptoms (right C6 numbness, tingling, diminished bicep reflex, and 4/5 grip strength) supported by an MRI within 6 months showing a large disc herniation and moderate-to-severe nerve root compression at C5-C6.
2. Conservative Treatment Requirement (Section 2.2): The member completed 8 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week requirement in Section 2.2(a)), trialed two qualifying medications (Gabapentin for 4 months and Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks, exceeding the 4-week requirement in Section 2.2(b)), and underwent a right C6 epidural steroid injection on 2024-10-01 (satisfying Section 2.2(c)).
3. Imaging Requirement (Section 2.3): The MRI dated 2024-09-01 demonstrates structural pathology at C5-C6 consistent with the C6 radicular symptoms.

Furthermore, under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32 and generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for the Management of Degenerative Cervical Radiculopathy), surgical decompression and fusion at C5-C6 is clinically appropriate, effective, and meets the Tier 2 indication for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after an adequate conservative trial. Therefore, the denial for the C5-C6 level is overturned.

II. ANALYSIS OF C6-C7 LEVEL (UPHELD / DENIED)

The clinical evidence does not satisfy the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria or generally accepted clinical standards for the C6-C7 level:
1. Imaging and Symptom Correlation (Section 2.3 & Section 3.3): HealthFirst policy Section 2.3 requires that current imaging demonstrate structural pathology consistent with the proposed surgical level. Section 3.3 mandates denial when imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with the clinical presentation. The MRI shows only a mild disc bulge and minimal foraminal narrowing at C6-C7, with no nerve root compression or cord contact. There are no documented C7 symptoms or neurological deficits on exam to correlate with this level.
2. Prophylactic Fusion Standard: Dr. Wilson's rationale for fusing C6-C7 is prophylactic stabilization to prevent adjacent segment disease. Under California's statutory standard of medical necessity (CHSC § 1374.32(a)), a service must be clinically appropriate in terms of extent and site, and not primarily for the convenience of the provider. Generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines Tier 3) state that surgery is not indicated for imaging findings without correlating symptoms. Prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic adjacent level with minimal degenerative changes does not meet the threshold of medical necessity. Consequently, the denial for the C6-C7 level is upheld.

III. PROCEDURAL DEFECT NOTE

Under California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b), a plan's denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reasons for the denial. The initial denial notice dated 2024-10-28 failed to cite the specific clinical criteria applied (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024), which constitutes a procedural defect. While this defect is noted for regulatory compliance, it does not alter the substantive medical necessity determination for the C6-C7 level under California's objective clinical standards.
