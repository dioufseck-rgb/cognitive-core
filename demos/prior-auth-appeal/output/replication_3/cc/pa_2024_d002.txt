Dear Mr. Green,

HealthFirst has completed its review of the appeal submitted on your behalf by Dr. Sarah Kim, MD, regarding the prior authorization denial for a single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. 

After a comprehensive clinical and regulatory review of your medical records, the plan's clinical coverage criteria, and applicable California state regulations, HealthFirst has determined that the initial denial issued on November 08, 2024, by Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, is hereby OVERTURNED. Prior authorization for the requested ACDF C5-C6 procedure is APPROVED.

The decision to overturn the denial is based on two independent and sufficient grounds: (1) full clinical conformance with the plan's medical necessity criteria, and (2) critical procedural defects in the initial denial notice under California law. There is no conflict between the plan's internal criteria and the clinical/regulatory standards, as both independently and consistently support the approval of this procedure.

I. CLINICAL ANALYSIS AND CONFORMANCE WITH PLAN CRITERIA
The clinical records demonstrate that you fully satisfy all medical necessity criteria outlined in HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria for Spinal Surgery — Cervical Procedures (Document: CC-SPINE-2024, Effective January 1, 2024):

1. Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1): 
You have a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment. Your neurological examination dated September 25, 2024, documents objective neurological deficits, including left C6 dermatomal numbness, a diminished left bicep reflex, and left bicep weakness (graded 4/5). This clinical presentation is directly supported by an MRI dated September 20, 2024 (within the required 6-month window), which demonstrates a large C5-C6 disc herniation causing moderate left C6 nerve root compression. This satisfies Section 2.1(a) and Section 2.1(c).

2. Conservative Treatment Requirement (Section 2.2):
Your clinical history demonstrates the successful completion of a comprehensive course of conservative treatment that meets and exceeds all plan requirements:
- Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)): You completed 7 weeks of supervised physical therapy (totaling 21 sessions). The physical therapy notes document that you reached a functional plateau at week 6, with persistent radicular symptoms. This satisfies the plan's 6-week minimum requirement.
- Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)): You completed trials of two distinct classes of pharmacotherapy for more than the required 4 weeks each: (1) Gabapentin (a neuropathic agent) at 600mg TID for 4 months, which provided only partial relief, and (2) Naproxen (an NSAID) at 500mg BID for 3 months, which provided inadequate relief. This satisfies Section 2.2(b).
- Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)): You underwent a left C6 epidural steroid injection on October 28, 2024, which provided 40% temporary relief before your symptoms returned to baseline within 3 weeks. This satisfies the requirement for at least one interventional treatment under Section 2.2(c).

3. Imaging Requirement (Section 2.3):
Your MRI dated September 20, 2024, is current (within 6 months of the request) and demonstrates structural pathology (large C5-C6 disc herniation with moderate left C6 nerve root compression) that directly correlates with your clinical symptoms of left C6 radiculopathy and objective neurological deficits. This satisfies Section 2.3.

4. Physician Documentation (Section 2.4):
The clinical documentation submitted by Dr. Sarah Kim, MD, includes the necessary clinical rationale, description of functional limitations, and details of conservative treatments attempted, satisfying Section 2.4.

II. REGULATORY ANALYSIS AND PROCEDURAL DEFECTS
In addition to meeting all clinical criteria, the initial denial notice issued on November 08, 2024, contained severe procedural defects under California law that independently invalidate the denial:

1. Failure to Provide Clinical Criteria (CHSC § 1374.31):
Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(a)(2), a health plan must include the specific criteria applied and the specific clinical reasons in its written denial notice. Furthermore, CHSC § 1374.31(b) states that a denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied is defective and shall be treated as if no denial was issued. The initial denial notice referenced an attached criteria document, but no such document was attached or provided to you or Dr. Kim. This omission constitutes a major procedural violation under CHSC § 1374.31(b).

2. Omission of Independent Medical Review (IMR) Notice (CHSC § 1374.31):
CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2) mandates that all medical necessity denials include a notice of Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights. The initial denial notice failed to include the required IMR notice, violating California statutory requirements.

3. Mechanical Application of Physical Therapy Requirements (CIC § 10169.5):
Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2), a health insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms. CIC § 10169.5(c) explicitly prohibits the mechanical application of a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy. Your physical therapy records clearly document a functional plateau at week 6. Any denial based on a mechanical application of physical therapy duration would violate CIC § 10169.5.

III. CLINICAL EVIDENCE STANDARDS
The clinical evidence base strongly supports surgical intervention for your presentation. According to the North American Spine Society (NASS) Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020) and the AANS/CNS Joint Section Guidelines (2023), surgical decompression via ACDF is a Tier 2 (Reasonable Indication) procedure for patients with cervical radiculopathy who exhibit objective neurological deficits (such as your documented left C6 dermatomal numbness and 4/5 bicep weakness) and persistent symptoms after an adequate trial of conservative therapy (including physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and epidural injections). 

IV. CONCLUSION
Because you meet all clinical criteria under HealthFirst Policy CC-SPINE-2024, and because the initial denial notice was procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b), the prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED. 

An approved prior authorization has been issued for CPT 22551 (ACDF C5-C6, one level) to be performed by Dr. Sarah Kim, MD, at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. This authorization is valid for 90 days from the date of this letter, expiring on February 23, 2025, in accordance with Section 3.5 of the plan policy.
