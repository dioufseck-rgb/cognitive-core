Dear Mr. Obi and Dr. Diallo,

The HealthFirst Health Plan Appeals Department has completed its review of the first-level appeal regarding the prior authorization request for a single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551). After a comprehensive evaluation of the clinical documentation, the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), California regulatory requirements, and generally accepted clinical standards, we have determined that the initial denial issued on November 08, 2024, must be OVERTURNED. Prior authorization for the requested procedure is hereby APPROVED.

I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The initial prior authorization request was denied on November 08, 2024, based on two grounds: (1) an alleged physical therapy (PT) shortfall (claiming only 5 weeks of PT were completed out of 6 required), and (2) poorly controlled type 2 diabetes (HbA1c 8.2%) presenting perioperative and wound healing risks. 

Upon appeal review, both denial grounds are determined to be invalid:
1. The PT shortfall is factually incorrect. Clinical records demonstrate the member completed 7 weeks of continuous physical therapy (21 sessions) from July 01, 2024, through August 16, 2024, with a documented functional plateau. Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5, mechanical application of a duration requirement is prohibited when a functional plateau has been reached.
2. Diabetes control is not a prior authorization criterion under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024. Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b), a denial based on non-plan criteria is procedurally defective. Clinically, perioperative metabolic optimization is the responsibility of the surgical team and does not constitute a prior authorization barrier under generally accepted standards of medical practice.

Because the member satisfies all applicable medical necessity criteria under CC-SPINE-2024, the denial is overturned.

II. CLINICAL HISTORY AND PRESENTING DIAGNOSIS
Mr. Gerald Obi is a 58-year-old male presenting with cervical radiculopathy at C6-C7 with significant functional impairment, including the necessity of working reduced hours due to severe pain and hand weakness (Clinical Record: Presenting Diagnosis). 
- Neurological Examination (dated September 25, 2024): Reveals objective neurological deficits, including left C7 dermatomal numbness, a diminished left tricep reflex, left tricep weakness graded at 4/5, and left grip weakness graded at 4+/5 (Clinical Record: Neurological Exam).
- Imaging (MRI dated September 20, 2024): Demonstrates a large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate left C7 nerve root compression and confirmed foraminal stenosis. No spinal cord contact or myelomalacia is present (Clinical Record: Imaging).
- Comorbidities: The member has a 12-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus, with a recent HbA1c of 8.2% on October 15, 2024, managed with Metformin 1000mg BID and Insulin glargine 20 units QHS (Clinical Record: Comorbidities).

III. DETAILED ANALYSIS OF DENIAL GROUND 1: PHYSICAL THERAPY SHORTFALL
The initial denial asserted that the member completed only 5 weeks of physical therapy, failing to meet the 6-week requirement under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a). This assertion is factually incorrect and legally non-compliant.

1. Factual Correction: Physical therapy records document continuous, supervised treatment from July 01, 2024, through August 16, 2024, representing a total of 7 weeks and 21 completed sessions (Clinical Record: Conservative Treatment - PT Summary). This exceeds the 6-week minimum requirement set forth in CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a).
2. Functional Plateau: The physical therapy discharge summary explicitly notes a functional plateau: "no further functional improvement expected" (Clinical Record: Conservative Treatment - PT Summary).
3. Regulatory Compliance: Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2), a health plan shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician or therapist documents that the member reached a functional plateau. Furthermore, CIC § 10169.5(c) mandates that a plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau. 

Consequently, the physical therapy requirement is fully satisfied both factually (7 weeks completed) and legally (functional plateau documented).

IV. DETAILED ANALYSIS OF DENIAL GROUND 2: DIABETES CONTROL
The initial denial cited the member's poorly controlled type 2 diabetes (HbA1c 8.2%) and associated perioperative risks as a basis for withholding surgical authorization. This ground is contractually and legally invalid.

1. Absence in Plan Criteria: A review of the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria for Spinal Surgery (CC-SPINE-2024) confirms that there are no criteria conditioning surgical authorization for ACDF on metabolic control, HbA1c thresholds, or diabetes management. 
2. Regulatory Violation: Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b), a denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied. Denying coverage based on a clinical parameter that is not part of the plan's published criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) constitutes a procedural defect.
3. Clinical Standard of Care: Under generally accepted standards of medical practice, including the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for Spinal Surgery (2023) and NASS Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020), perioperative diabetes management is a surgical planning and patient safety consideration managed by the surgical and anesthesia teams, not a prior authorization criterion (Clinical Evidence Document: Section 3.1). Dr. Diallo's clinical note on October 20, 2024, confirms that standard perioperative protocols will be utilized to optimize the member's diabetes preoperatively (Clinical Record: Comorbidities). Under CHSC § 1374.32(b), the standard of review for medical necessity must align with these clinical standards rather than restrictive, non-criteria plan barriers.

Therefore, the diabetes-related denial ground is disregarded.

V. COMPREHENSIVE EVALUATION OF MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA (CC-SPINE-2024)
To ensure a reproducible and objective determination, the member's clinical record was evaluated against all medical necessity requirements of CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2:

- Section 2.1: Diagnosis Requirement: MET. The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy C6-C7 with correlating objective neurological deficits (left C7 dermatomal numbness, diminished left tricep reflex, and 4/5 tricep weakness) satisfying Section 2.1(a) and (c).
- Section 2.2: Conservative Treatment Requirement: MET.
  - (a) Physical Therapy: MET. 7 weeks completed (July 01, 2024 – August 16, 2024) with a documented functional plateau, satisfying Section 2.2(a) and CIC § 10169.5.
  - (b) Pharmacotherapy: MET. The member completed trials of two neuropathic agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each: Pregabalin 150mg BID for 5 months and Duloxetine 60mg for 3 months (Clinical Record: Pharmacotherapy), satisfying Section 2.2(b).
  - (c) Interventional Treatment: MET. The member underwent a left C7 epidural steroid injection on October 10, 2024, which provided temporary relief before symptoms returned to baseline (Clinical Record: Interventional), satisfying Section 2.2(c).
- Section 2.3: Imaging Requirement: MET. MRI dated September 20, 2024 (within 6 months of the request) demonstrates structural pathology (large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate left C7 nerve root compression) consistent with the member's symptoms and proposed surgical level (Clinical Record: Imaging).
- Section 2.4: Physician Documentation: MET. Dr. Diallo provided the required clinical rationale, description of functional limitations (reduced working hours), and attestation of conservative treatment failure (Clinical Record: Comorbidities, Neurological Exam, PT Summary), satisfying Section 2.4.

VI. CONCLUSION
Because the member has fully satisfied all clinical criteria under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, and because the initial denial relied on a factual error regarding physical therapy duration and an extra-contractual criterion regarding diabetes control, the prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED. 

Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) is approved. This authorization is valid for 90 days from the date of this letter, through February 23, 2025, per CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.5.
