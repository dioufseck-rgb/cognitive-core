Dear Ms. James and Dr. Adams,

The HealthFirst Health Plan Appeals and Grievances Department has completed its review of the first-level appeal regarding the prior authorization request for a single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) at UCLA Medical Center. 

After a comprehensive review of the clinical documentation, the plan's clinical coverage criteria, California regulatory requirements, and generally accepted clinical standards, HealthFirst has determined that the initial prior authorization denial issued on November 2, 2024, is OVERTURNED. The requested surgical procedure is approved.

I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The initial prior authorization request was denied on November 2, 2024, by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, under Reason Code MED-003, citing a failure to satisfy the plan's physical therapy (PT) requirement. Specifically, the plan had only received records from Valley Physical Therapy documenting 4 weeks of treatment, and could not verify the additional 4 weeks of claimed treatment at Mountain Physical Therapy due to missing records. 

On appeal, the complete records from Mountain Physical Therapy were submitted, confirming an additional 4 weeks (12 sessions) of supervised physical therapy. With these records, the member has documented a total of 8 weeks of physical therapy across two providers, fully satisfying the plan's conservative treatment requirements. Furthermore, under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5, the mechanical application of a rigid duration requirement is prohibited when a functional plateau has been documented. Because the clinical record demonstrates that the member has met all internal plan criteria, state regulatory mandates, and clinical evidence standards, the denial must be overturned.

II. CLINICAL HISTORY AND FINDINGS
Ms. Dorothy James is a 51-year-old female with a presenting diagnosis of bilateral cervical radiculopathy at C6-C7.
- Clinical Presentation & Neurological Exam (September 5, 2024): The member exhibits objective neurological deficits in a bilateral C7 dermatomal distribution, including bilateral tricep weakness graded at 4/5, diminished bilateral tricep reflexes, and bilateral finger tingling.
- Diagnostic Imaging (September 1, 2024): A cervical spine MRI demonstrates a large disc herniation at C6-C7, causing bilateral C7 nerve root compression, bilateral foraminal stenosis, and mild spinal cord contact without compression.
- Conservative Treatment History:
  1. Physical Therapy (Provider 1): Valley Physical Therapy from June 1, 2024, to June 28, 2024 (4 weeks, 12 sessions), resulting in moderate initial improvement followed by a documented plateau.
  2. Physical Therapy (Provider 2): Mountain Physical Therapy from July 15, 2024, to August 9, 2024 (4 weeks, 12 sessions), resulting in no improvement in neurological symptoms and a documented functional plateau at discharge.
  3. Pharmacotherapy: Trial of Pregabalin 150mg BID for 3 months (neuropathic agent) yielding only partial relief, and Naproxen 500mg BID for 2 months (NSAID) yielding inadequate relief.
  4. Interventional Treatment: Bilateral C7 epidural steroid injection performed on September 20, 2024, which provided only temporary relief (50% on the left, 30% on the right) before symptoms returned to baseline.

III. ANALYSIS OF HEALTHFIRST CLINICAL COVERAGE CRITERIA (CC-SPINE-2024)
Under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2, authorization for cervical spinal surgery (CPT 22551) is approved when all of the following criteria are satisfied:
1. Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement): The member must have a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (nerve root compression with radicular symptoms) supported by an MRI within 6 months. Ms. James has a confirmed diagnosis of bilateral C6-C7 cervical radiculopathy, supported by an MRI dated September 1, 2024 (within 6 months of the request), showing a large C6-C7 disc herniation and bilateral C7 nerve root compression, which correlates directly with her bilateral tricep weakness, diminished reflexes, and finger tingling. This criterion is MET.
2. Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement):
   - (a) Physical Therapy: Minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes. The combined records from Valley PT (4 weeks, 12 sessions) and Mountain PT (4 weeks, 12 sessions) document a total of 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised physical therapy directed at her cervical condition. The 17-day gap between the two therapy courses (June 28 to July 15) does not violate the plan's criteria, as CC-SPINE-2024 specifies a cumulative duration of treatment rather than continuous, uninterrupted sessions. This criterion is MET.
   - (b) Pharmacotherapy: Trial of at least two specified agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. Ms. James completed a 3-month trial of Pregabalin (neuropathic agent) and a 2-month trial of Naproxen (NSAID), satisfying the requirement for two agents. This criterion is MET.
   - (c) Interventional Treatment: Minimum of one cervical epidural steroid injection, medial branch block, or trigger point injection series. Ms. James underwent a bilateral C7 epidural steroid injection on September 20, 2024. This criterion is MET.
3. Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement): Current imaging (within 6 months) must demonstrate structural pathology consistent with symptoms. The MRI from September 1, 2024, confirms a large C6-C7 disc herniation and bilateral C7 nerve root compression, which is highly consistent with her bilateral C7 radicular symptoms and the proposed C6-C7 surgical level. This criterion is MET.
4. Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation): Dr. Paul Adams, MD, has provided the necessary clinical documentation, including clinical rationale, functional limitations, and surgical risks. This criterion is MET.

IV. ANALYSIS OF CALIFORNIA REGULATORY FRAMEWORK
Under California law, the plan's initial denial and its rigid application of physical therapy requirements are subject to statutory limitations:
1. California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) & (c): A health insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician or therapist documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms. Furthermore, a plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy shall not be applied mechanically. 
   - Here, the member's records from Valley PT documented a plateau after 4 weeks, and the Mountain PT records documented a functional plateau at discharge on August 9, 2024, with no improvement in neurological symptoms. Mechanically denying the initial request because the plan failed to receive the Mountain PT records—despite having received the Valley PT records documenting a plateau—violates the statutory prohibition against mechanical application of PT duration requirements under CIC § 10169.5.
2. California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b): A denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reason for the denial. The initial denial notice dated November 2, 2024, failed to cite the specific sections of CC-SPINE-2024 being applied, rendering the notice procedurally defective.
3. Administrative vs. Member Failure: The appeal notes that the Mountain PT records were submitted with the initial request but may not have been received by the plan. Under California Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) standards, a plan's internal failure to receive or route submitted medical records constitutes an administrative failure of the plan, which cannot be used to penalize the member or delay medically necessary care.

V. ANALYSIS OF CLINICAL EVIDENCE STANDARDS
Generally accepted standards of medical practice support surgical intervention for Ms. James's clinical presentation:
1. AANS/CNS Joint Section Guidelines for Management of Degenerative Cervical Radiculopathy (2023): Surgery is indicated (Tier 2) for patients with cervical radiculopathy who exhibit persistent radicular pain and objective neurological deficits (such as Ms. James's 4/5 tricep weakness and diminished reflexes) that have failed to respond to an appropriate course of conservative management (typically 6 to 12 weeks). Ms. James has completed 8 weeks of physical therapy, 3 months of neuropathic medication, 2 months of NSAIDs, and an epidural steroid injection, with no lasting relief.
2. AO Spine International Clinical Practice Guidelines (2021) & NASS Guidelines (2020): These guidelines emphasize that physical therapy has limited efficacy in reversing objective neurological deficits (such as motor weakness and reflex changes) caused by structural nerve root compression. Once a patient has reached a functional plateau, continuing conservative therapy is clinically inappropriate and risks prolonged functional impairment. Ms. James's documented plateau at Mountain PT confirms that further conservative treatment is unlikely to benefit her.

VI. RESOLUTION OF CONFLICTS & DISPOSITION
In resolving any potential conflicts between the plan's internal guidelines, state regulations, and clinical evidence:
- The plan's internal guideline (CC-SPINE-2024) requires a 6-week minimum of physical therapy.
- California law (CIC § 10169.5) prohibits the mechanical application of this 6-week requirement when a functional plateau is documented.
- Clinical standards (AANS/CNS 2023) dictate that surgical decompression is indicated when conservative treatment has failed and objective neurological deficits persist.
- Under the regulatory hierarchy, California statutory mandates (CIC § 10169.5) and generally accepted clinical standards supersede any restrictive or mechanical application of the plan's internal criteria. 

However, because the newly submitted Mountain PT records establish that the member has completed 8 weeks of physical therapy, the member fully satisfies the plan's internal criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) as well as all statutory and clinical standards. There is no remaining conflict, and the request is approved on both contractual and clinical grounds.

The prior authorization denial for ACDF C6-C7 (CPT 22551) is hereby OVERTURNED.
