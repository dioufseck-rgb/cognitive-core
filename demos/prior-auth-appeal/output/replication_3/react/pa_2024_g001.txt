### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This appeal involves a prior authorization request for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 for Mr. Marco Reyes. The initial denial was based on the claim that conservative treatment requirements, specifically physical therapy (PT), were not met (only 3 weeks completed, functional plateau not documented).

Upon review of the submitted clinical record and the appeal, it is clear that the denial was based on **material factual errors**. The member, Mr. Reyes, completed a total of **9 weeks of physical therapy** across two providers, exceeding the plan's 6-week minimum requirement. Furthermore, a **functional plateau was explicitly documented** in the discharge summary from Capital Physical Therapy. The member also met all other conservative treatment requirements (pharmacotherapy and interventional procedure) and all other plan criteria (diagnosis, imaging).

Beyond the factual errors, California state law (CIC § 10169.5) explicitly prohibits conditioning surgical approval on further physical therapy once a functional plateau has been reached. Additionally, the requested ACDF is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for persistent cervical radiculopathy after an adequate course of conservative treatment, as outlined in clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023).

Therefore, based on a correct application of HealthFirst's own criteria, California state law, and generally accepted clinical standards, the denial must be **OVERTURNED**.

### REVIEW FINDINGS

1.  **Primary Clinical Presentation:**
    *   Mr. Reyes' presenting diagnosis is **Cervical Radiculopathy C6-C7, right-sided, persistent**.
    *   Imaging (MRI 2024-08-20) confirms a C6-C7 large disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression. Critically, the MRI **does not show cord contact or myelomalacia**.
    *   Neurological exam (2024-08-25) reveals right C7 dermatomal numbness, diminished right tricep reflex, and right grip strength 4/5. **No bilateral findings and no Upper Motor Neuron (UMN) signs are documented.**
    *   **Conclusion:** This is a clear case of cervical radiculopathy, not cervical myelopathy. The plan's "Myelopathy Exception" (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2A) and specific state/clinical guidance related to myelopathy with myelomalacia are not directly applicable to Mr. Reyes' primary diagnosis. (Clinical Record; Clinical Reference Summary, Section 1.1)

2.  **Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** **MET.** Mr. Reyes has confirmed cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits and imaging consistent with right C7 nerve root compression. (Clinical Record; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a))
    *   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:** The denial stated these requirements were not met. This is factually incorrect.
        *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** The denial stated "member has completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy" and "Functional plateau not documented."
            *   **Factual Correction:** Mr. Reyes completed 3 weeks at Valley PT (2024-06-01 to 2024-06-21) and an additional 6 weeks at Capital PT (2024-07-08 to 2024-08-16), totaling **9 weeks (27 sessions)**. Both PT providers' records were submitted with the original PA request. (Clinical Record: physical_therapy_summary)
            *   **Functional Plateau:** The Capital PT discharge summary explicitly documents, "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT." This language directly satisfies HealthFirst's own definition of a functional plateau: "therapist documents maximum benefit reached." (Clinical Record: physical_therapy_summary; Plan Criteria Knowledge; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a) and Plan Criteria Knowledge)
            *   **Conclusion:** The plan's requirement for a "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes" is **MET**.
        *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** **MET.** Mr. Reyes completed trials of Gabapentin (4 months) and Naproxen (3 months), meeting the requirement for "at least two... for minimum 4 weeks each." (Clinical Record; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(b))
        *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** **MET.** Mr. Reyes received a right C7 epidural steroid injection (2024-09-15), which provided only 35% temporary relief, returning to baseline within 3 weeks. This meets the "minimum 1" requirement. (Clinical Record; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(c))
        *   **Myelopathy Exception (2.2A):** Not applicable as Mr. Reyes' primary diagnosis is radiculopathy, not myelopathy. The plan's criteria explicitly state this exception applies "only when myelopathy is the PRIMARY diagnosis." (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2A NOTE)
    *   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** **MET.** Current MRI (2024-08-20) demonstrates structural pathology (C6-C7 disc herniation with C7 nerve root compression) consistent with symptoms and proposed surgical level. (Clinical Record; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.3)
    *   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** Not cited in the denial as unmet, assumed to be provided.
    *   **Conclusion on Plan Criteria:** Mr. Reyes **fully meets all medical necessity criteria** within HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 policy, including the conservative treatment requirements which were the sole basis for the denial. The denial's claim that "All other criteria remain unmet" is also factually incorrect.

3.  **Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**
    *   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This state law explicitly states that a health insurer **shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where "(2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."**
        *   Mr. Reyes completed a 9-week course of PT, and the therapist explicitly documented a functional plateau ("Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected").
        *   **Conclusion:** Under state law, the plan is prohibited from denying this request based on a lack of further PT, regardless of their internal criteria. This provision directly overrides the plan's denial reason. (California Regulatory Framework, Part III; Clinical Record)
    *   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (Standard of Review for IMR):** This section mandates that an IMR (and by extension, the plan during internal appeal for consistency) **shall approve a service if it meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice" regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive.**
        *   **Conclusion:** This reinforces that even if HealthFirst's criteria were *somehow* interpreted to contradict, state-mandated clinical standards would govern.

4.  **Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
    *   The member's presentation of persistent cervical radiculopathy (numbness, weakness, diminished reflex) with objective imaging evidence of nerve root compression (C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression) after an adequate trial of conservative therapy meets generally accepted standards for surgical intervention.
    *   **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023), Section 3.1, TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION for ACDF:**
        *   "(b) Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx"
        *   "(c) Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care"
        *   Mr. Reyes' 9 weeks of PT, 4 months of pharmacotherapy, and a failed epidural injection constitute an adequate conservative trial. His persistent symptoms and grip strength deficit (4/5) indicate significant functional impairment.
    *   **Conclusion:** The requested ACDF for Mr. Reyes' persistent cervical radiculopathy is medically necessary and falls within generally accepted standards of medical practice. (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1)

### CONFLICT RESOLUTION (Hierarchy: State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria)

1.  **Plan Criteria:** The denial was based on a factual error. Mr. Reyes **did meet** HealthFirst's own conservative treatment requirements (9 weeks PT > 6 weeks minimum; documented functional plateau; pharmacotherapy; interventional procedure). The claim that "all other criteria remain unmet" is also factually false based on the clinical record. This alone warrants an overturn.
2.  **State Law (CIC § 10169.5):** Even if the plan's criteria were somehow interpreted otherwise, state law explicitly prohibits denial for PT duration when a functional plateau has been reached. This state law *governs* over any conflicting plan criteria.
3.  **Clinical Standard:** The ACDF is medically necessary per AANS/CNS Tier 2 indications. CHSC § 1374.32(b) mandates approval if the service meets generally accepted medical practice, irrespective of potentially more restrictive plan criteria.

### DISPOSITION

**OVERTURN**

### WARRANT

The denial for prior authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) for Mr. Marco Reyes is OVERTURNED for the following reasons:

1.  **Factual Error in Denial:** The initial denial was based on an erroneous factual premise. The member completed a total of 9 weeks of physical therapy, not 3 weeks, thus exceeding the plan's 6-week minimum requirement. The denial's claim that a functional plateau was not documented is also false, as the Capital PT discharge summary explicitly states, "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT." (Clinical Record: physical_therapy_summary).
2.  **Compliance with Plan Criteria:** Upon correction of the factual errors, Mr. Reyes fully meets all medical necessity criteria outlined in HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 policy, including diagnosis, imaging, and all conservative treatment requirements (physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and interventional therapy). (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3; Clinical Record).
3.  **Superseding State Law:** California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2) prohibits a health insurer from conditioning approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and reached a functional plateau. Mr. Reyes meets this condition, and therefore, the plan's denial based on PT requirements is in violation of state law. (California Regulatory Framework, Part III).
4.  **Consistency with Clinical Standards:** The requested ACDF is medically necessary under generally accepted clinical guidelines. Mr. Reyes presents with persistent cervical radiculopathy and functional impairment (right grip strength 4/5) after an adequate and failed course of conservative treatment, which constitutes a Tier 2 "reasonable indication" for surgery according to AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023). California Health & Safety Code § 1374.32(b) mandates approval for services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice, irrespective of internal plan criteria. (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1; California Regulatory Framework, Part I).

The service is medically necessary and appropriate for the member's condition.
