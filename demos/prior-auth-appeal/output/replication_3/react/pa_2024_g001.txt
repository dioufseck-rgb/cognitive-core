**DISPOSITION: OVERTURN DENIAL**

**WARRANT**

This determination addresses the appeal for prior authorization of an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) for Mr. Marco Reyes. The original denial (Case ID: PA-2024-G001, dated 2024-11-01) cited HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2, specifically stating that the "member does not meet conservative treatment requirements... Physical therapy: member has completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy. Standard pathway requires 6 weeks minimum. Functional plateau not documented. All other criteria remain unmet."

Upon thorough review of the submitted appeal documentation, the member's clinical record, HealthFirst's clinical criteria, California regulatory requirements, and generally accepted clinical standards, the denial is hereby **OVERTURNED**.

**I. Clinical Presentation:**
The clinical record, including the MRI from 2024-08-20 and the neurological exam from 2024-08-25, confirms a diagnosis of **cervical radiculopathy C6-C7, right-sided, persistent**.
*   The MRI findings note "moderate right C7 nerve root compression" with "No cord contact" and "No myelomalacia."
*   The neurological exam details "Right C7 dermatomal numbness," "Tricep reflex diminished right," and "Grip strength 4/5 right," with "No bilateral findings" and "No UMN signs."
This definitively establishes radiculopathy, not myelopathy. Therefore, the myelopathy-specific exceptions in HealthFirst's policy (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(A)) and California regulatory guidance related to myelopathy (DMHC APL 22-014 II.1 & II.2) are not applicable to this case.

**II. Application of HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
The original denial was based on an inaccurate assessment of the member's conservative treatment history, specifically regarding physical therapy completion.
1.  **Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)):** The denial stated "member has completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy" and "Functional plateau not documented."
    *   The appeal and clinical record demonstrate that Mr. Reyes completed **9 weeks of physical therapy** (3 weeks at Valley PT, 6 weeks at Capital PT). Both providers' records were explicitly noted as "SUBMITTED with original PA request." This significantly exceeds the plan's "minimum 6 weeks" requirement.
    *   Furthermore, the Capital PT discharge summary explicitly states, "'Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT.'" This directly documents a **functional plateau**, which also meets HealthFirst's own definition of functional plateau as "therapist documents maximum benefit reached" (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2, Note).
    *   **Conclusion:** The member has demonstrably met the plan's physical therapy requirement, and the denial's factual claims regarding PT duration and functional plateau are incorrect.
2.  **Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)):** The member completed a trial of Gabapentin for 4 months and Naproxen for 3 months. This fulfills the requirement for "at least two... for minimum 4 weeks each" for Gabapentin, and 3 months for Naproxen is a substantial trial, particularly as the denial did not cite this as unmet.
    *   **Conclusion:** This criterion is met.
3.  **Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)):** The member completed a right C7 epidural steroid injection.
    *   **Conclusion:** This criterion is met.
4.  **Other Criteria (2.1 Diagnosis, 2.3 Imaging, 2.4 Physician Documentation):** These criteria appear to be met based on the provided clinical record and the fact that the denial did not specifically cite failure on these points.

**III. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**
California law explicitly addresses physical therapy as a prerequisite for surgery.
1.  **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This statute prohibits a health insurer from conditioning approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where:
    *   "(2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
    *   **Conclusion:** Mr. Reyes completed 9 weeks of PT and reached a functional plateau, as documented by his physical therapist. The denial, therefore, violates CIC § 10169.5(a)(2). The plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks cannot be applied mechanically when a functional plateau has been achieved (CIC § 10169.5(c)).

**IV. Application of Clinical Standard:**
Generally accepted standards of medical practice support surgical intervention in this case.
1.  **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for Management of Degenerative Cervical Myelopathy (2023 Update) / NASS Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020):** These guidelines identify cervical radiculopathy with persistent symptoms and objective neurological deficits after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment as a "Reasonable Indication" (AANS/CNS TIER 2) for surgical intervention like ACDF.
    *   **Conclusion:** Mr. Reyes has persistent right C7 radiculopathy with objective neurological findings despite completing 9 weeks of physical therapy, 4 months of pharmacotherapy, and an epidural injection, with documented functional plateau and temporary relief only. This clinical picture aligns with the accepted indications for ACDF for radiculopathy.

**V. Conflict Resolution (Hierarchy: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria):**
The denial was based on a factual error in applying HealthFirst's own criteria regarding PT duration and functional plateau. When correctly applied, the member *does* meet the plan's criteria. Furthermore, California State Law (CIC § 10169.5) explicitly overrides any plan requirement to continue PT if a functional plateau has been reached. The requested service also meets generally accepted clinical standards.

**Conclusion:**
The denial issued on 2024-11-01 for Case ID PA-2024-G001 is based on an incorrect factual assessment of Mr. Marco Reyes' conservative treatment history. The member has completed more than the required duration of physical therapy and has reached a documented functional plateau, satisfying both HealthFirst's clinical criteria and California state law (CIC § 10169.5). The requested ACDF procedure is medically necessary and consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for persistent cervical radiculopathy after a failed conservative trial.

Therefore, the prior authorization for CPT code 22551 (ACDF C6-C7, one level) for Mr. Marco Reyes is **APPROVED**.
