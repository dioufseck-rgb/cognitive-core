## APPEAL DETERMINATION: Prior Authorization Denial for ACDF C5-C6

**Case ID:** PA-2024-G006
**Member:** Theresa Kamau, M-G006, Age 52
**Plan:** HealthFirst PPO Gold — Group Plan 77441, California (2024 plan year)
**Procedure Requested:** CPT 22551 (ACDF C5-C6, one level)
**Treating Provider:** Dr. Victor Osei, MD — Spine Surgery
**Denial Date:** 2024-11-20
**Reviewer:** Health Plan Appeals Reviewer (Expert)
**Date of Review:** 2024-11-28

---

### I. BACKGROUND OF DENIAL

The initial prior authorization request for an ACDF C5-C6 for Ms. Kamau was denied by HealthFirst Utilization Management on November 20, 2024. The stated reason for denial (Reason Code MED-003) was: "Member has a prior authorization denial history for cervical spine surgery (three-level fusion denied May 2024). Review of current request in context of prior denial pattern suggests the member's cervical pathology has not materially changed since the prior denial. Current request does not demonstrate sufficient change in clinical status to warrant reconsideration."

The denial notice itself included an internal flag: "Prior PA history — NOT a valid criterion under CC-SPINE-2024" and "denial_is_procedurally_flawed": true.

### II. MEMBER'S APPEAL BASIS

The member, through their treating provider, appealed the denial based on the following:
1.  The prior denial was for a different procedure (three-level fusion vs. current single-level).
2.  Clinical status has materially changed since May 2024, evidenced by a new MRI and a new course of conservative treatment.
3.  The current request meets all criteria independently of prior PA history.
4.  Using prior PA history as a denial basis is explicitly not authorized under HealthFirst's own clinical coverage criteria (CC-SPINE-2024).

### III. CLINICAL RECORD SUMMARY

**Presenting Diagnosis:** Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6, progressive, persistent after conservative treatment.

**Clinical Change Since Prior Denial:**
*   **New MRI (October 2024):** Shows interval increase in C5-C6 herniation size, leading to moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression and new foraminal stenosis at C5-C6. Prior MRI (April 2024) showed moderate herniation.
*   **New Conservative Treatment:**
    *   **Physical Therapy (PT):** New 8-week course completed July-September 2024 (total 24 sessions), explicitly documented as reaching a functional plateau ("no further improvement expected, neurological deficit persistent and worsening"). This is separate from any prior PT.
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Gabapentin 600mg TID for 6 months (partial relief), Methylprednisolone dose pack x2 (temporary relief).
    *   **Interventional:** Right C6 epidural on November 5, 2024, providing 50% relief for 2 weeks, then returned to baseline.
*   **Neurological Exam (October 2024):** Right C6 dermatomal numbness, absent bicep reflex right, bicep weakness 3+/5 right. This represents interval worsening from prior exams. Pain 8/10 at rest, unable to continue working full-time.

**Primary Clinical Presentation:** Based on the unilateral dermatomal symptoms, nerve root compression on MRI, and absence of upper motor neuron signs (e.g., Hoffman, hyperreflexia, bilateral symptoms, gait disturbance) or myelomalacia, Ms. Kamau's primary presentation is **severe, progressive cervical radiculopathy** at C5-C6, not cervical myelopathy. This distinction is critical for applying conservative treatment requirements.

### IV. REVIEW AND DETERMINATION

I have reviewed the provided clinical record, the HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), and relevant California regulatory and clinical guidance documents, applying the hierarchy of authority: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria**.

#### A. Procedural Adequacy of Denial (State Law - CHSC § 1374.31(b))

The initial denial is procedurally flawed as explicitly noted within the denial itself and directly challenged by the appeal.
*   **Denial's Self-Admission:** The denial stated, "Prior PA history — NOT a valid criterion under CC-SPINE-2024" and "denial_is_procedurally_flawed": true. This is an internal admission by HealthFirst that the basis for their denial was invalid under their own policy.
*   **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b):** A "denial notice that fails to specify the clinical criteria applied or the specific clinical reason for the denial is DEFECTIVE." By basing a denial on a criterion explicitly acknowledged as *not valid*, the plan has effectively failed to provide a valid "specific clinical reason for the denial" according to its own policy. This renders the denial procedurally defective.

**Conclusion:** The initial denial is procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b). This alone is sufficient grounds to OVERTURN the denial.

#### B. Application of HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 (Plan Criteria)

Even if the denial were not procedurally flawed, the member's request clearly meets HealthFirst's own criteria:

1.  **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** **MET.**
    *   The member has a confirmed diagnosis of "Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6" with "objective neurological deficit" (right C6 numbness, absent bicep reflex, 3+/5 bicep weakness).
    *   Current MRI (October 2024) demonstrates "C5-C6 large disc herniation with moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression" consistent with symptoms, and is within 6 months.

2.  **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:** **MET.**
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (2.2(A))** does **not** apply, as the primary diagnosis is radiculopathy, not myelopathy. Therefore, standard conservative treatment requirements apply.
    *   **(a) Physical therapy:** **MET.** A new 8-week course of supervised PT was completed (July-September 2024). This exceeds the minimum 6 weeks required by the plan. Furthermore, the therapist documented a "functional plateau" with "no further improvement expected, neurological deficit persistent and worsening."
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** **MET.** Gabapentin (6 months) and Methylprednisolone (2 courses) meet the requirement of at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
    *   **(c) Interventional treatment:** **MET.** One right C6 epidural steroid injection was performed (November 2024).

3.  **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** **MET.**
    *   Current MRI (October 2024) is within 6 months and demonstrates "C5-C6 large disc herniation with moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression \u2014 interval increase in size from prior MRI. New foraminal stenosis at C5-C6," which is consistent with the member's symptoms and the proposed surgical level.

4.  **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** **Implicitly MET.**
    *   The request for prior authorization by Dr. Osei, a spine surgeon, along with the detailed clinical record, implicitly confirms the physician's attestation of conservative treatment completion, rationale for surgery, description of functional limitations, and consideration of risks/alternatives.

**Conclusion:** The requested procedure meets all applicable criteria under HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 policy.

#### C. Application of California Regulatory Requirements

1.  **CHSC § 1374.31(b):** As established above, the denial is procedurally defective.
2.  **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** **MET.**
    *   The member completed an 8-week PT course. The treating therapist explicitly documented that the member reached a "functional plateau" with "no further improvement expected" and persistent/worsening neurological deficit. This fully aligns with CIC § 10169.5(a)(2), which states that a plan cannot condition approval on further PT if a functional plateau has been reached.
3.  **DMHC APL 22-014 (Conservative Treatment Exceptions for Urgent Conditions):** While the APL's core focus is myelopathy, it notes that for "conditions where delay of treatment risks irreversible harm, plans SHALL NOT require completion of a full course of conservative treatment... when (a) Objective imaging evidence demonstrates progressive pathology... AND (b) Treating physician provides documentation that conservative treatment has been attempted and has failed to arrest progression."
    *   The clinical record shows "interval increase in C5-C6 herniation size" (progressive pathology) and worsening neurological deficit (3+/5 weakness, absent reflex, 8/10 pain, unable to work full-time) despite conservative treatment. While not a myelopathy, this progressive, severe radiculopathy with objective neurological compromise that has failed conservative treatment demonstrates a level of urgency, further reinforcing that any argument about insufficient conservative treatment would be overridden by state law.
4.  **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard - Generally Accepted Standards):** This provision mandates approval if a service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice," regardless of more restrictive plan criteria.

#### D. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021)

The clinical evidence base strongly supports surgical intervention in this case:
*   **Diagnosis:** The member presents with severe cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6, progressive in nature, with objective neurological deficit (bicep weakness 3+/5, absent reflex), and significant functional impairment (8/10 pain, unable to work full-time).
*   **Conservative Treatment Failure:** The member has undergone an extensive course of conservative treatment, including 8 weeks of PT with documented functional plateau, two pharmacotherapy agents, and an epidural injection that provided only temporary relief.
*   **Surgical Indication:** Per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023, Section 3.1), ACDF is indicated under:
    *   **TIER 1 (Strong Indication):** "(b) Progressive neurological deficit despite conservative treatment." The interval worsening of C6 weakness and absent reflex, combined with increased herniation size, fits this criterion.
    *   **TIER 2 (Reasonable Indication):** "(b) Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and "(c) Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care." Ms. Kamau's symptoms are persistent and severe after 8 weeks of PT and other treatments, and she has significant functional impairment.
*   **Risk of Delay:** Although not myelopathy, progressive neurological deficits with worsening imaging findings suggest that continued delay of appropriate treatment carries risks, as conservative treatment has been thoroughly trialed and failed to arrest progression.

**Conclusion:** The requested ACDF C5-C6 is medically necessary and meets generally accepted standards of medical practice for severe, progressive cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits that have failed extensive conservative management.

### V. CONFLICT RESOLUTION AND FINAL DETERMINATION

Based on the hierarchy of authority (State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria):

1.  **State Law:** The initial denial is procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b) because it explicitly relied on a non-valid criterion ("Prior PA history"). Additionally, the conservative treatment completed, specifically the physical therapy with documented functional plateau, fully complies with and is protected by CIC § 10169.5(a)(2).
2.  **Plan Criteria:** The member's clinical presentation and documented treatment history fully satisfy all criteria outlined in HealthFirst's own CC-SPINE-2024 policy (Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4).
3.  **Clinical Standard:** Surgical intervention is considered medically necessary and meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice" for this presentation of progressive, severe cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit unresponsive to comprehensive conservative treatment (AANS/CNS, NASS, AO Spine guidelines).

Therefore, based on all three levels of review, the prior authorization denial was inappropriate.

### VI. DISPOSITION

**OVERTURNED.**

### VII. WARRANT

The prior authorization denial for CPT 22551 (ACDF C5-C6) for member Theresa Kamau (M-G006) is **OVERTURNED**.

This determination is made for the following reasons:

1.  **Procedural Flaw (State Law):** The initial denial was procedurally defective as it explicitly stated, "Prior PA history — NOT a valid criterion under CC-SPINE-2024," and identified itself as "denial_is_procedurally_flawed." Basing a denial on a non-valid criterion contravenes CHSC § 1374.31(b), which requires a specific and valid clinical reason for denial.
2.  **Compliance with Plan Criteria:** The member fully meets all applicable medical necessity criteria within HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 policy.
    *   **Diagnosis (2.1):** Confirmed progressive cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6 with objective neurological deficit (3+/5 weakness, absent reflex, numbness).
    *   **Conservative Treatment (2.2):** An 8-week course of physical therapy was completed, exceeding the 6-week minimum, with documented functional plateau. Two pharmacotherapy agents (Gabapentin, Methylprednisolone) were trialed for sufficient duration, and one interventional procedure (epidural injection) was performed, providing only temporary relief.
    *   **Imaging (2.3):** A recent MRI (October 2024) within 6 months demonstrates an interval increase in herniation size at C5-C6 with moderate-severe nerve root compression, consistent with symptoms.
3.  **Compliance with State Law (CIC § 10169.5):** The completion of physical therapy with documented functional plateau ensures that further PT cannot be required as a prerequisite for surgery, in accordance with CIC § 10169.5(a)(2). The progressive pathology noted in the MRI and clinical exam aligns with the spirit of DMHC APL 22-014 regarding urgency for conditions with progressive pathology, even for severe radiculopathy.
4.  **Compliance with Clinical Standards (CHSC § 1374.32(b)):** The requested surgery is medically necessary and consistent with "generally accepted standards of medical practice" for severe, progressive cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits that have failed a comprehensive and adequate course of conservative treatment, as outlined in AANS/CNS, NASS, and AO Spine guidelines (AANS/CNS TIER 1(b) and TIER 2(b,c)).

Therefore, the requested ACDF C5-C6 (CPT 22551) for member Theresa Kamau is approved.
