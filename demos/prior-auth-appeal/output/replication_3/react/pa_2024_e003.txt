### **1. OVERVIEW**

This appeal concerns a prior authorization request for a revision anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 for Mr. George Harris, a 48-year-old member with HealthFirst PPO Gold. The initial request for CPT 22551 was denied by Dr. Patricia Huang on 2024-10-30 with Reason Code MED-003, stating: "Revision surgery not covered under standard ACDF criteria. Member had prior C5-C6 ACDF in 2021. Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level. Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure."

The appeal basis provided by the treating surgeon, Dr. Karen Williams, states: "CT scan confirms pseudarthrosis (failure of fusion) at C5-C6," "Symptoms have returned and worsened since 2021 fusion," and "Revision criteria met per treating surgeon."

### **2. CLINICAL FINDINGS AND ASSESSMENT**

**Presenting Diagnosis:** Pseudarthrosis C5-C6 following prior ACDF 2021 — failed fusion, causing symptomatic cervical radiculopathy.

**Key Clinical Information:**
*   **Prior Surgery:** C5-C6 ACDF in 2021, with symptoms recurring at 18 months.
*   **Current Symptoms:** Return of right C6 symptoms including bicep weakness (4/5 right), diminished bicep reflex (right), and C6 dermatomal numbness, noted to be *worse than preoperative baseline* (Neurological Exam, 2024-09-20). These findings are consistent with cervical radiculopathy. No documented myelopathic signs (e.g., bilateral weakness, gait disturbance, hyperreflexia, Hoffman sign).
*   **Imaging:**
    *   MRI (2024-09-01): Limited by hardware artifact; possible non-union, cannot confirm.
    *   CT (2024-09-15): "CT without artifact limitation: C5-C6 — incomplete bridging bone formation. Lucency around hardware suggesting pseudarthrosis. Findings consistent with failed fusion/pseudarthrosis. Hardware appears intact without fracture."
*   **Conservative Treatment:**
    *   Physical Therapy: 8 weeks (24 sessions) completed. Documented "No improvement." Treating physician notes PT "cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure."
    *   Pharmacotherapy: Naproxen 500mg BID for 4 months (partial pain relief), Gabapentin 300mg TID for 3 months (partial neuropathic relief).
    *   Interventional: Epidural (2024-10-10) with "Minimal improvement — treating physician notes this is expected when structural failure is present."

### **3. REVIEW AGAINST APPLICABLE CRITERIA AND STANDARDS**

This review applies the hierarchy of authority: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.**

#### **3.1 HealthFirst Plan Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024)**

*   **Scope (Section 1):** CPT 22551 is explicitly covered.
*   **Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1):** Member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (2.1a) with objective neurological deficit, supported by current CT imaging demonstrating structural pathology (pseudarthrosis at C5-C6) consistent with symptoms. This criterion is met.
*   **Conservative Treatment Requirement (Section 2.2):**
    *   The member completed 8 weeks of supervised PT (minimum 6 weeks required).
    *   The member completed trials of two pharmacotherapy agents (Naproxen for 4 months, Gabapentin for 3 months; minimum 4 weeks each required).
    *   The member received one interventional treatment (epidural).
    *   Based on these facts, the *quantitative* conservative treatment requirements of Section 2.2 appear to have been met.
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (2.2A):** This exception is not applicable as the primary clinical presentation is cervical radiculopathy, not myelopathy, as per the neurological exam provided.
*   **Imaging Requirement (Section 2.3):** Current CT scan (2024-09-15) demonstrates pseudarthrosis at C5-C6, consistent with the member's radicular symptoms. This criterion is met.
*   **Physician Documentation (Section 2.4):** The provided clinical record satisfies the elements of physician documentation.
*   **Exclusions (Section 3):**
    *   **Denial Reason Misapplication:** The initial denial stated: "Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level. Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure." However, the CT scan explicitly confirms **pseudarthrosis** (incomplete bridging bone formation, lucency around hardware), which is one of the *explicitly stated criteria for revision surgery* in the denial reason itself. The initial denial failed to acknowledge or apply its own stated criteria regarding pseudarthrosis. This constitutes a direct misapplication of the plan's criteria.

#### **3.2 California Regulatory Framework**

*   **CHSC § 1374.31(b) (Denial Notice Defect):** The initial denial notice is potentially defective as it failed to specify the "specific clinical reason" for denial by ignoring the documented pseudarthrosis, which was a listed reason for revision in its *own stated criteria*. By solely focusing on "hardware failure" and disregarding "pseudarthrosis," the denial notice did not accurately reflect the clinical situation in light of its stated policy.
*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This state law explicitly overrides plan requirements for PT completion under certain conditions:
    *   **(a)(1) PT unlikely to benefit:** The treating physician documented that PT "cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure," indicating it is unlikely to benefit the member given the nature of the condition.
    *   **(a)(2) Functional Plateau:** The member completed 8 weeks of PT with "No improvement." This constitutes reaching a functional plateau as per the clinical definition (<10% improvement or maximum benefit reached).
    *   **(a)(3) Objective evidence of structural neurological compromise:** The CT scan confirms pseudarthrosis, causing C6 radiculopathy (bicep weakness, numbness, reflex change). This is objective structural neurological compromise that PT cannot address.
    *   **Conclusion:** Even if the plan were to argue insufficient conservative treatment, CIC § 10169.5 would override any mechanical PT duration requirement.
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Conservative Treatment Exceptions):** While primarily focused on myelopathy, Section II.2 outlines conservative treatment exceptions for conditions risking irreversible harm when:
    *   (a) Objective imaging (CT showing pseudarthrosis) demonstrates progressive pathology.
    *   (b) Treating physician documents conservative treatment failure (8 weeks PT, pharmacotherapy, epidural with minimal/no improvement).
    *   (c) Surgical intervention is indicated under generally accepted guidelines.
    *   The current radiculopathy is worsening ("worse than preoperative baseline") due to a structural failure (pseudarthrosis) that conservative measures cannot resolve. This supports waiving further conservative treatment requirements.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard vs. Plan Criteria):** This is critical. California law mandates that the service shall be approved if it meets generally accepted standards of medical practice, regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive.

#### **3.3 Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021)**

*   **Diagnostic Classification (Section 1.1):** The symptoms are clearly classified as cervical radiculopathy.
*   **Conservative Treatment for Radiculopathy:** Clinical guidelines generally recommend a trial of conservative treatment (PT, pharmacotherapy, injections) for radiculopathy. The member has undergone a substantial course of conservative treatment (8 weeks PT, 3-4 months pharmacotherapy, 1 epidural) with minimal to no improvement. Crucially, the underlying pathology is structural pseudarthrosis, which is unlikely to resolve with non-surgical management.
*   **Surgical Indications (Section 3.1):** Per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023):
    *   TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION:
        *   (b) "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx." Mr. Harris has persistent, worsening C6 radiculopathy after a trial of conservative treatment.
        *   (c) "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care." His documented 4/5 bicep weakness and numbness, worsening from baseline, clearly demonstrate significant functional impairment.
    *   **Conclusion:** Surgical intervention (revision ACDF) for symptomatic pseudarthrosis causing persistent radiculopathy after failed conservative treatment is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice.

### **4. CONCLUSION**

The initial denial was based on a misapplication of HealthFirst's own stated criteria. The denial asserted that "revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology," but then denied because "documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure." This selectively ignored the explicit inclusion of "pseudarthrosis" as a valid reason for revision. The CT scan clearly documents pseudarthrosis at C5-C6.

Furthermore, even if conservative treatment completion were a point of contention (which it was not in the original denial), state law (CIC § 10169.5 and DMHC APL 22-014) provides clear exceptions that have been met in this case due to the structural nature of the pathology (pseudarthrosis causing neurological compromise) and documented failure of conservative measures.

Finally, surgical intervention for symptomatic pseudarthrosis causing persistent radiculopathy, particularly with objective neurological deficit and progression, is well-supported by generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS, NASS, AO Spine).

### **5. DISPOSITION**

**OVERTURN**

### **6. WARRANT**

The denial for CPT 22551 (Revision ACDF C5-C6) for Mr. George Harris is hereby OVERTURNED for the following reasons:

1.  **Misapplication of Plan Criteria:** HealthFirst's initial denial incorrectly applied its own criteria by stating that revision surgery criteria include "pseudarthrosis" but then denying the request solely on the absence of "hardware failure," completely disregarding the CT-confirmed pseudarthrosis at C5-C6 (CT 2024-09-15). This represents a direct contradiction and misapplication of the plan's stated policy as articulated in the denial letter itself.
2.  **Compliance with State Law (CIC § 10169.5):** The member has undergone 8 weeks of physical therapy with "No improvement," and the treating physician documented that PT "cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure." This fulfills the conditions of CIC § 10169.5(a)(1), (a)(2), and (a)(3), which mandate that a plan "shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy" when PT is unlikely to benefit, a functional plateau has been reached, or objective structural neurological compromise (pseudarthrosis causing radiculopathy) is present that PT cannot address. Therefore, any requirement for further conservative treatment is overridden by California state law.
3.  **Adherence to Clinical Standards:** Generally accepted clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021) classify persistent radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits (4/5 weakness, diminished reflex, numbness) caused by structural pathology like pseudarthrosis, after a trial of conservative care, as a reasonable indication for surgical intervention (Tier 2 indication per AANS/CNS). The member's symptoms are worsening ("worse than preoperative baseline"), indicating clinical necessity for surgical correction of the failed fusion.
4.  **Overall Medical Necessity:** The requested revision ACDF is medically necessary to address the documented pseudarthrosis causing persistent and worsening C6 radiculopathy, consistent with prudent clinical judgment and generally accepted standards of medical practice in California.

**The requested prior authorization for CPT 22551 is approved.**
