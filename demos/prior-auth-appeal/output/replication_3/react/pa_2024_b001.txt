**DISPOSITION: PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**

The initial denial for a two-level ACDF (C5-C6 and C6-C7) is hereby **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**. Authorization is approved for a **single-level ACDF at C5-C6**. The request for ACDF at C6-C7 remains denied as not medically necessary.

---

**WARRANT AND FINDINGS:**

This determination is based on a comprehensive review of the member's clinical record, HealthFirst's clinical coverage criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), relevant California state laws and regulations, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice. The hierarchy of authority applied is: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
The clinical record clearly indicates a diagnosis of **Cervical Radiculopathy C5-C6**.
*   **Imaging:** MRI findings show "C5-C6: large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, moderate foraminal stenosis. Clear correlation with C6 radicular symptoms." For C6-C7, the MRI shows "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable."
*   **Neurological Exam:** Documented "Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, bicep reflex diminished right, grip strength 4/5 right." Crucially, "No C7 findings documented. No bilateral findings. No upper motor neuron signs."
*   **Conclusion:** Based on these findings and the "DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE — CLASSIFICATION" (Source: Domain Knowledge), the member exhibits classic signs and symptoms of radiculopathy at the C5-C6 level affecting the C6 nerve root, without any evidence of cervical myelopathy (e.g., no upper motor neuron signs, no bilateral symptoms, no cord contact, no myelomalacia). Therefore, the myelopathy-specific exceptions or urgent pathway provisions in HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 or DMHC APL 22-014 are not applicable to this case.

**2. Review of C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**

*   **Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **Section 2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (2.1a) and cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit (2.1c) as supported by MRI showing nerve root compression and neurological exam findings of C6 radiculopathy.
    *   **Section 2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Met.
        *   **Physical Therapy:** 8 weeks of supervised PT completed, exceeding the 6-week minimum (2.2a).
        *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Gabapentin for 4 months and Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks were trialed, meeting the requirement for two agents for 4+ weeks each (2.2b).
        *   **Interventional Treatment:** One right C6 epidural steroid injection was performed (2.2c).
        *   **Functional Plateau:** Even though the plan's 6-week minimum was met, the clinical record notes that "C6 radicular symptoms persist" and epidural relief was only 50% temporary and returned by week 4. This indicates a functional plateau has been reached with conservative therapy, consistent with CIC § 10169.5(a)(2) and the clinical standard definition (Source: Clinical Reference Summary, Section 5).
    *   **Section 2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. The MRI from 2024-09-01 demonstrates "large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, moderate foraminal stenosis" at C5-C6, which is consistent with the member's C6 radicular symptoms.
*   **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
    *   The member's persistent C6 radiculopathy after an adequate trial of conservative treatment meets the TIER 2 "Reasonable Indication" for ACDF, specifically "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" (Source: Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1).
*   **State Law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)):** The service for C5-C6 ACDF meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice" as outlined by leading spine societies. Therefore, approval for ACDF at C5-C6 is warranted.

**3. Review of C6-C7 Level (Adjacent Segment):**

*   **Initial Denial Reason:** The plan's initial denial stated, "C6-C7 shows mild degenerative changes without significant compression or neurological deficit attributable to this level." This aligns with HealthFirst's criteria.
*   **Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **Section 2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Not met for C6-C7. The MRI findings for C6-C7 ("mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable") are not consistent with *symptoms* at this level, as the neurological exam specifically documented "No C7 findings documented."
*   **Appeal Basis (Surgeon Rationale):** Dr. Wilson recommended including C6-C7 prophylactically to prevent adjacent segment disease.
*   **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
    *   The provided clinical guidelines focus on surgical indications for existing, symptomatic pathology (radiculopathy or myelopathy) or progressive neurological deficits. There is no high-quality evidence or generally accepted standard of medical practice within the provided clinical documents that supports prophylactic fusion of an *asymptomatic* level with *mild degenerative changes* (without nerve root compression) solely to prevent adjacent segment disease (Source: Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1, 3.2). The definition of "Medically necessary" per CHSC § 1374.32(a) requires services to be "in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice."
*   **Conclusion:** Both HealthFirst's criteria and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice do not support surgical intervention for the asymptomatic C6-C7 level. The state law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)) instructs that the IMR standard (which includes generally accepted standards) must be met. In this instance, neither the plan criteria nor the clinical standards support the C6-C7 level.

**4. Procedural Adequacy of Denial:**
The initial denial notice provided specific criteria and a clinical reason for the denial of the two-level procedure ("Imaging shows pathology at C5-C6 only. C6-C7 shows mild degenerative changes without significant compression or neurological deficit attributable to this level."). This meets the requirements of CHSC § 1374.31(b) and is therefore procedurally adequate.

---

**Summary of Decision:**

The member's clinical presentation, imaging, and response to conservative treatment fulfill the criteria for ACDF at the C5-C6 level for symptomatic cervical radiculopathy, both under HealthFirst's own criteria and generally accepted clinical guidelines.

However, the medical necessity for ACDF at the C6-C7 level is not supported. There are no documented C7 radicular symptoms, and the imaging findings at C6-C7 demonstrate only mild degenerative changes without nerve root compression. Prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic segment to prevent adjacent segment disease is not considered a generally accepted standard of medical practice under the definition of medical necessity for prior authorization in California.

Therefore, authorization is granted for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6.
