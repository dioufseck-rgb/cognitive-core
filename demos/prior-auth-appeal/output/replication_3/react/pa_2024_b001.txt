**REVIEWER'S DETERMINATION:** **PARTIAL OVERTURN**

The request for Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at **C5-C6** is **OVERTURNED** and deemed medically necessary.
The request for Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at **C6-C7** is **UPHELD** as not medically necessary.

---

**RATIONALE FOR DETERMINATION:**

This appeal was reviewed applying the three-source hierarchy: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
The clinical record confirms a primary diagnosis of **Cervical Radiculopathy** at the C5-C6 level, presenting with objective C6 radicular symptoms (right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, diminished right bicep reflex, 4/5 right grip strength). Imaging (MRI 2024-09-01) reveals a large disc herniation at C5-C6 with moderate-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis, clearly correlating with the documented C6 symptoms. There is no evidence of myelopathy (no spinal cord compression, no bilateral symptoms, no upper motor neuron signs, no myelomalacia). The C6-C7 level shows only mild degenerative changes without nerve root compression or correlation to any documented C7 symptoms.

**2. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **Diagnosis Requirement (2.1):** The member meets criteria 2.1(a) and (c) for cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6 with objective neurological deficit. For C6-C7, no correlating symptoms or nerve compression are identified.
*   **Conservative Treatment Requirement (2.2):** For the C5-C6 level, the member has completed:
    *   **Physical Therapy:** 8 weeks (exceeds 6-week minimum).
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Gabapentin for 4 months and Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks (meets 2 agents, 4-week minimum each).
    *   **Interventional Treatment:** Right C6 epidural steroid injection (meets 1 interventional treatment).
    The conservative treatment requirements for cervical radiculopathy are fully met for the C5-C6 level. The Myelopathy Exception (2.2(A)) does not apply as myelopathy is not the primary diagnosis.
*   **Imaging Requirement (2.3):**
    *   **C5-C6:** Current MRI demonstrates structural pathology (large disc herniation, moderate-severe nerve root compression) consistent with the C6 radicular symptoms. This criterion is met for C5-C6.
    *   **C6-C7:** Imaging shows "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable." This does not demonstrate structural pathology consistent with the *member's symptoms* at C6-C7, as no C7 symptoms are documented. Therefore, criteria 2.3 is *not met* for C6-C7.
*   **Exclusions (3.3):** The plan's exclusion 3.3 ("Imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with clinical presentation") directly applies to the C6-C7 level.

**Based on HealthFirst's plan criteria, the C5-C6 level meets all medical necessity criteria, while the C6-C7 level does not.**

**3. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**

*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** The member completed 8 weeks of PT, and C6 radicular symptoms persist. This indicates that a functional plateau has been reached, and the condition involves objective structural neurological compromise that PT alone cannot fully address (CIC § 10169.5(a)(2) & (3)). Therefore, the conservative treatment requirements for C5-C6 are satisfied by plan criteria and/or state law.
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):** This APL primarily addresses myelopathy with myelomalacia, which is not present in this case. Thus, the specific conservative treatment waivers within this APL for urgent myelopathy cases do not directly apply. However, the general principle that plans shall not apply internal criteria more restrictive than generally accepted standards remains relevant.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** This provision mandates that services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice shall be approved, regardless of more restrictive plan criteria. This will be applied in conjunction with clinical standards.

**4. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **C5-C6 (Radiculopathy):** The member presents with persistent cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6 with objective neurological deficits and correlating imaging, despite an adequate trial of conservative treatment (8 weeks PT, two pharmacotherapy agents for 4+ weeks each, one interventional injection). Per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023), radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment is a TIER 2 "reasonable indication" for surgical intervention (Clinical Evidence Base, Section 3.1). Therefore, surgery at C5-C6 is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice.
*   **C6-C7 (Adjacent Segment Disease Prevention):** The surgeon's rationale for including C6-C7 is "prophylactically stabilize C6-C7 to prevent adjacent segment disease." The clinical record indicates only "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable" at C6-C7, with no documented C7 symptoms. The provided clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021) do not support prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic, non-compressive level for the sole purpose of preventing adjacent segment disease as a "generally accepted standard of medical practice" (CHSC § 1374.32(a)(1)). The guidelines focus on addressing existing symptomatic pathology (myelopathy or radiculopathy) as the indication for fusion (Clinical Evidence Base, Section 3.1).

**5. Conflict Resolution:**

*   **For C5-C6:** The plan's criteria are met, and approval is supported by the clinical standard. There is no conflict.
*   **For C6-C7:** The plan's criteria for medical necessity (2.3, 3.3) are not met for this level. Furthermore, the clinical standards provided do not support prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic level for adjacent segment disease prevention. Therefore, both the plan criteria and the clinical standards indicate that C6-C7 is not medically necessary. There is no conflict.

**Conclusion:** The initial denial correctly identified that medical necessity for C6-C7 was not met. However, it implicitly denied the *entire two-level request* rather than partially approving the medically necessary C5-C6 level.

---

**FINAL DECISION:**

*   **ACDF C5-C6:** **OVERTURNED**. The requested ACDF at C5-C6 is medically necessary as the member meets HealthFirst plan criteria and generally accepted clinical standards for cervical radiculopathy that has failed comprehensive conservative management.
*   **ACDF C6-C7:** **UPHELD**. The requested ACDF at C6-C7 is not medically necessary. The imaging does not demonstrate pathology consistent with symptoms at this level, and prophylactic fusion for adjacent segment disease prevention at an asymptomatic level is not supported by current clinical guidelines as a generally accepted standard of medical practice.

HealthFirst Health Plan is directed to approve prior authorization for CPT code 22551 for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6.
