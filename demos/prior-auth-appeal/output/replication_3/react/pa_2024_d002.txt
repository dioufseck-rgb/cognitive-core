### I. INTRODUCTION

This letter serves as the determination for the first-level appeal of the prior authorization denial for an ACDF C5-C6 for member Michael Green. The initial request was denied on November 8, 2024, citing that the "Member does not meet criteria for requested procedure under plan policy." The appeal specifically raised concerns about procedural defects in the denial notice and the member's inability to evaluate the denial without the referenced criteria document.

This review has been conducted by an expert health plan appeals reviewer, applying the established hierarchy of authority: **State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.**

### II. SUMMARY OF APPEAL BASIS

The appeal was submitted on the following grounds:
1.  No criteria document was attached to the denial notice despite being referenced.
2.  An Independent Medical Review (IMR) notice was not included.
3.  The member and provider could not adequately evaluate the denial without the specific criteria that were applied.

### III. REVIEW FINDINGS

#### A. Procedural Adequacy of Original Denial

The initial denial notice from HealthFirst PPO Gold was procedurally defective for the following reasons:
*   **Missing Criteria Document:** The denial explicitly referenced an attached criteria document (MED-003, "See attached criteria document."), but both the member and provider confirmed no document was attached. This violates **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)**, which states that a denial notice failing to specify the clinical criteria applied is defective.
*   **Missing IMR Notice:** The denial notice failed to include information regarding the member's right to an Independent Medical Review. This is a direct violation of **CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2)**, which mandates that the denial notice "Include... notice of IMR rights."

A procedurally defective denial, as outlined by state law, is to be treated as if no denial was issued. These defects alone are sufficient to warrant overturning the denial.

#### B. Clinical Presentation and Diagnosis

The member's clinical record supports a diagnosis of **cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment**.
*   **Presenting Diagnosis:** Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6.
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-20):** Shows "C5-C6 large disc herniation, moderate left C6 nerve root compression. No cord contact." There is no evidence of spinal cord compression or myelomalacia.
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-09-25):** Reveals "Left C6 dermatomal numbness, bicep reflex diminished left, bicep weakness 4/5 left." These are classic signs of nerve root compression (radiculopathy), not spinal cord compression (myelopathy). The absence of upper motor neuron signs (e.g., Hoffman's, hyperreflexia, gait disturbance, bilateral symptoms) further differentiates this from myelopathy.
*   **Domain Knowledge and Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, Section 1.1):** Clearly distinguish between radiculopathy and myelopathy based on these findings. The member's presentation aligns with cervical radiculopathy. Therefore, the "Myelopathy exception" (Section 2.2(A)) within the HealthFirst plan criteria does not apply.

#### C. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024)

Reviewing the member's clinical record against HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria for Spinal Surgery (CC-SPINE-2024), the member *does* meet the medical necessity requirements for ACDF:

1.  **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:**
    *   **Plan Criterion:** Member must have a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (2.1(a)).
    *   **Member Status:** Confirmed cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 based on MRI findings (nerve root compression) and neurological exam (radicular symptoms).
    *   **Finding:** **MET**.

2.  **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:**
    *   **Plan Criterion (2.2(a) Physical Therapy):** Minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes.
        *   **Member Status:** Member completed 7 weeks of physical therapy (21 sessions). PT notes document a plateau at week 6, with persistent radicular symptoms.
        *   **Finding:** **MET**. The 7 weeks of PT exceeds the 6-week minimum, and the documented plateau at week 6 indicates that maximum benefit from PT was reached, consistent with the definition of "functional plateau" in both the Domain Knowledge and Clinical Standard Knowledge (Section 5, Functional Plateau Definition).
    *   **Plan Criterion (2.2(b) Pharmacotherapy):** Trial of at least two agents for minimum 4 weeks each.
        *   **Member Status:** Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (partial relief) and Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months (inadequate relief).
        *   **Finding:** **MET**. Two agents trialed for sufficient duration.
    *   **Plan Criterion (2.2(c) Interventional Treatment):** One interventional treatment.
        *   **Member Status:** Left C6 epidural steroid injection performed on 2024-10-28, providing 40% relief that returned to baseline in 3 weeks.
        *   **Finding:** **MET**. One interventional procedure completed.

3.  **2.3 Imaging Requirement:**
    *   **Plan Criterion:** Current imaging (within 6 months) demonstrating structural pathology consistent with symptoms and proposed surgical level.
    *   **Member Status:** MRI dated 2024-09-20 (within 6 months of request) showing C5-C6 large disc herniation and moderate left C6 nerve root compression, consistent with the clinical presentation and proposed surgical level.
    *   **Finding:** **MET**.

Based on a thorough review, the member has satisfied all applicable medical necessity criteria outlined in HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 policy for ACDF. The denial reason stating the member "does not meet criteria" is unsubstantiated by the clinical record when evaluated against the plan's own policy.

#### D. Compliance with California State Law

Beyond the procedural defects, the denial also conflicts with California state law regarding physical therapy requirements:
*   **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2):** This law states that a health insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
*   **Member Status:** Michael Green completed 7 weeks of physical therapy, and notes explicitly state a "plateau at week 6."
*   **Finding:** Even if HealthFirst were to argue that more PT was required, state law overrides such a requirement once a functional plateau is reached. The member has fulfilled this legal standard.

#### E. Adherence to Generally Accepted Clinical Standards

The requested ACDF is also supported by generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice:
*   **AANS/CNS Joint Section on Disorders of the Spine Guidelines (2023 Update, Section 3.1):** For cervical radiculopathy, surgical intervention is considered a "Reasonable Indication" (TIER 2) for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care."
*   **Member Status:** Michael Green has persistent C6 radicular symptoms (numbness, weakness 4/5, diminished reflex) and functional impairment despite an adequate trial of conservative care, including 7 weeks of PT, 4 months of Gabapentin, 3 months of Naproxen, and one epidural injection. The 7 weeks of PT falls within the 6-12 week conservative trial timeframe mentioned in the guidelines.
*   **Finding:** The service meets generally accepted standards of medical practice. **CHSC § 1374.32(b)** mandates that services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice SHALL be approved regardless of whether a plan's internal criteria are more restrictive.

### IV. CONCLUSION

The original prior authorization denial is procedurally defective, and a review of the clinical record against HealthFirst's own criteria, relevant state law, and generally accepted clinical standards demonstrates that the requested procedure is medically necessary and should be approved.

### V. RATIONALE FOR DISPOSITION

The denial is **OVERTURNED** for the following compelling reasons:
1.  **Procedural Defects:** The initial denial was procedurally inadequate, failing to provide the referenced criteria document and the mandatory IMR notice, in direct violation of **CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2)** and **(b)**. Such defects render the denial invalid.
2.  **Compliance with Plan Criteria:** The member has demonstrably met all conservative treatment requirements outlined in HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 policy, including the duration of physical therapy (7 weeks, exceeding the 6-week minimum and documenting a functional plateau), pharmacotherapy (two agents for adequate duration), and interventional therapy (one epidural). The diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with objective findings also aligns with the policy.
3.  **Compliance with State Law:** The member's completion of 7 weeks of physical therapy with a documented functional plateau satisfies **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)**, which precludes the plan from requiring further physical therapy.
4.  **Adherence to Clinical Standard:** The clinical presentation of persistent cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits and functional impairment, unresponsive to a comprehensive trial of conservative treatment, meets the "Reasonable Indication" (Tier 2) for ACDF as per **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023)**.

### VI. ACTION

The prior authorization denial for procedure CPT 22551 (ACDF C5-C6, one level) for Michael Green (M-445577) is **OVERTURNED**. HealthFirst PPO Gold is hereby directed to approve the prior authorization for the requested service.
