**Health Plan Appeals Review Determination**

**Case ID:** PA-2024-B003
**Member:** Angela Morrison, M-778899
**Procedure:** ACDF C3-C4 (CPT 22551)
**Requested By:** Dr. Paul Nguyen, MD (Neurosurgery)
**Date of Denial:** 2024-10-01
**Review Level:** First-Level Appeal

**DISPOSITION: OVERTURN**

**WARRANT:**

The initial denial for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C3-C4 for member Angela Morrison was based on the assertion that "imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4 to support surgical intervention. MRI shows mild degenerative changes. Procedure not medically necessary." Upon review of the complete clinical record, plan criteria, California regulatory framework, and generally accepted clinical standards, this denial is found to be incorrect and is therefore OVERTURNED.

The following findings support the overturning of the denial:

1.  **Diagnosis and Imaging Requirements Met:**
    *   **Primary Diagnosis:** The member presents with clear signs and symptoms of cervical radiculopathy at the C4 level, affecting the left C4 dermatomal distribution (shoulder and lateral arm numbness, deltoid weakness 3+/5, diminished bicep reflex). These are consistent with HealthFirst Plan Criteria 2.1(a) for cervical radiculopathy.
    *   **Imaging Findings:** The initial denial referenced only the MRI findings, which were explicitly noted as having "SIGNIFICANT ARTIFACT from C4-C5 hardware limiting evaluation" and stated, "Cannot exclude moderate compression due to artifact." Crucially, a subsequent **CT myelogram (dated 2024-09-10)** was performed specifically to overcome this limitation. The CT myelogram unequivocally confirmed "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4, noting that the "MRI underestimated pathology due to hardware artifact." This directly refutes the denial's assertion of "insufficient pathology" and satisfies HealthFirst Plan Criteria 2.3, which requires current imaging demonstrating structural pathology consistent with symptoms. (Clinical Record - Imaging; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.1a, 2.3, 3.3)

2.  **Conservative Treatment Requirements Met and Exceeded:**
    *   The initial denial acknowledged that the "Member meets conservative treatment requirements." The appeal record confirms this, fulfilling HealthFirst Plan Criteria 2.2:
        *   **Physical Therapy:** The member completed 10 weeks of supervised physical therapy (30 sessions), exceeding the plan's minimum 6-week requirement. Critically, the PT summary states, "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms," indicating a functional plateau.
        *   **Pharmacotherapy:** The member completed trials of Pregabalin (6 months, partial relief) and Duloxetine (3 months, minimal benefit), meeting the requirement for at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
        *   **Interventional Treatment:** The member underwent a left C4 epidural steroid injection, which provided good initial relief but returned to baseline by week 3, satisfying the requirement for one interventional treatment.
    *   **California State Law Override:** Even if the plan were to contest the duration or efficacy of conservative treatment, California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) and (3) stipulate that a plan cannot condition approval on physical therapy completion where the member has reached a functional plateau (as evidenced by "no improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms") or where objective structural neurological compromise (moderate-severe nerve root compression and weakness) exists that PT cannot address. (Clinical Record - Conservative Treatment; HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2; CIC § 10169.5)

3.  **Medical Necessity Under Clinical Standards and State Law:**
    *   **Clinical Standard:** According to generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, Section 3.1), surgical intervention is a "REASONABLE INDICATION" (Tier 2) for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment and significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care. Ms. Morrison's persistent C4 radiculopathy, objective weakness (deltoid 3+/5), and failure to improve after extensive conservative management clearly meet these criteria. (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1)
    *   **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b):** California law mandates that an Independent Medical Review (IMR) standard requires approval of services that are "medically necessary" in accordance with "generally accepted standards of medical practice," regardless of whether the service is consistent with the plan's internal coverage criteria if those criteria are more restrictive. Given that the service meets generally accepted clinical standards and the plan's denial was based on demonstrably incomplete imaging information, the service is medically necessary. (California Regulatory Framework, CHSC § 1374.32(b))

**Conclusion:**

The denial was based on an incomplete assessment of the imaging evidence, failing to account for the definitive CT myelogram findings that clearly demonstrate moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression. Furthermore, the member has completed and failed an extensive course of conservative treatment, with clear objective neurological deficits and a functional plateau. Both HealthFirst's own criteria (when the complete imaging is considered) and prevailing California state law, in conjunction with generally accepted clinical standards, support the medical necessity of the requested ACDF at C3-C4.

For these reasons, the prior authorization denial for CPT 22551 is OVERTURNED, and the procedure is approved.
