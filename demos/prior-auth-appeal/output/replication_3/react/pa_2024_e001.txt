**I. Overview of Appeal and Clinical Record**

This appeal concerns a prior authorization request for a two-level anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 and C6-C7 for member Victor Patel. The initial denial cited a lack of medical necessity for the C6-C7 level. The appealing party argues that both levels are symptomatic, adjacent levels should be treated simultaneously, and both levels meet full criteria.

The clinical record indicates:
*   **Presenting Diagnosis:** Two-level cervical disc disease C5-C6 and C6-C7 with asymmetric severity.
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-15):**
    *   C5-C6: Large disc herniation, moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis.
    *   C6-C7: Small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, no nerve root compression identifiable, no cord contact.
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-09-20):**
    *   C6 level: Right bicep weakness 3+/5, bicep reflex absent right, right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling.
    *   C7 level: Right tricep reflex slightly diminished. No definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms. Physician notes mild C7 symptoms *possibly* present but not clearly documented.
*   **Conservative Treatment:**
    *   Physical Therapy: 8 weeks, 24 sessions. C6 symptoms persist. C7 symptoms: minimal and *possibly resolving* per PT note at week 8.
    *   Pharmacotherapy: Pregabalin (4 months, partial C6 relief), Naproxen (3 months, moderate relief).
    *   Interventional: Right C6 epidural (good C6 relief, returned in 4 weeks). **No C7-specific injection performed.**

**II. Application of Three-Source Hierarchy: State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria**

**A. Procedural Adequacy of Initial Denial:**
The initial denial notice clearly stated the reason for denial (C6-C7 not supported) and the specific problem (mild changes at C6-C7, lack of pathology to support two-level fusion). This meets the requirements of CHSC § 1374.31(a)(2) for specifying the clinical reason for denial.

**B. Assessment for C5-C6 Level:**
1.  **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. MRI shows large disc herniation with moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression, and neurological exam shows objective C6 radiculopathy (weakness 3+/5, absent reflex, dermatomal numbness). This meets 2.1(a) and 2.1(c).
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Met. Member completed 8 weeks of physical therapy (2.2a, exceeding 6-week minimum), trials of Pregabalin and Naproxen for over 4 weeks each (2.2b), and one C6 epidural steroid injection (2.2c).
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. MRI demonstrates structural pathology consistent with symptoms.
2.  **California State Law:**
    *   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy Prerequisite):** The member completed 8 weeks of PT, and C6 symptoms persist. This indicates a functional plateau per CIC § 10169.5(a)(2), meaning even if the plan's 6-week minimum was challenged, the requirement for PT has been satisfied in this case.
    *   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy Exception):** Not applicable, as the member's presentation for C5-C6 is radiculopathy, not myelopathy. No myelomalacia is noted.
3.  **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):**
    *   Surgery for C5-C6 is indicated. The persistent C6 radiculopathy with objective weakness (3+/5) and absent reflex after 8 weeks of conservative treatment, including an epidural, meets Tier 1(b) (Progressive neurological deficit despite conservative treatment) or Tier 2(b) (Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative treatment) surgical indications.

    **Conclusion for C5-C6:** Surgical intervention at C5-C6 is medically necessary and meets all plan criteria, state law, and generally accepted clinical standards. The initial denial reviewer acknowledged this, stating "C5-C6 level shows pathology consistent with surgical criteria."

**C. Assessment for C6-C7 Level:**
1.  **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Not met. The MRI at C6-C7 shows "small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, no nerve root compression identifiable, no cord contact." The neurological exam shows "no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms" and only a "slightly diminished tricep reflex." This does not meet the criteria for cervical radiculopathy (2.1a) or cervical myelopathy (2.1b), nor does it constitute a "disc herniation with objective neurological deficit" (2.1c) at this specific level.
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Not met. While PT and pharmacotherapy were administered, a C7-specific interventional treatment (2.2c) was *not* performed. This would be required if C6-C7 otherwise qualified for surgery.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Not met. The imaging findings ("mild changes only," "no nerve root compression identifiable," "no cord contact") are not consistent with a need for surgical intervention based on the clinical presentation for C6-C7, which lacks definitive symptoms.
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (2.2A):** Not applicable. The clinical record does not indicate myelopathy at C6-C7; there is no spinal cord compression or myelopathic symptoms.
2.  **California State Law:**
    *   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy Prerequisite):** While 8 weeks of PT was completed, the PT note states C7 symptoms were "minimal and possibly resolving." This does not suggest a functional plateau of *significant* symptoms requiring surgery. The lack of definitive C7 pathology means the exemption for "structural neurological compromise" (a)(3) also does not apply.
    *   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):** Not applicable. There is no evidence of myelopathy, myelomalacia, or urgent progressive pathology at C6-C7.
    *   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** This provision would allow approval if clinical standards are met, even if plan criteria are more restrictive. However, as detailed below, clinical standards are *not* met for C6-C7.
3.  **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
    *   **Section 3.1 Accepted Indications for ACDF:** C6-C7 does not meet any Tier 1 or Tier 2 surgical indications. There is no moderate-severe myelopathy, progressive neurological deficit, myelomalacia, or radiculopathy with persistent symptoms. A "small disc bulge" and "mild foraminal narrowing" without objective nerve root compression or definitive clinical symptoms are not considered surgical indications under generally accepted standards of medical practice.
    *   **Adjacent Level Degeneration (Appeal Basis):** While addressing adjacent levels may be considered in some cases of definite, albeit mild, symptomatic pathology, operating on an asymptomatic or minimally symptomatic level lacking objective nerve root compression to prevent future degeneration is not a generally accepted surgical indication.

    **Conclusion for C6-C7:** Surgical intervention at C6-C7 is not medically necessary based on HealthFirst's plan criteria, state law, or generally accepted clinical standards. The appeal basis that "Both levels are symptomatic" and "Member meets full criteria for both levels" is not supported by the clinical record for C6-C7.

**D. Conflict Resolution:**
There is no conflict between the plan's criteria, state law, or clinical standards regarding the C6-C7 level. All sources consistently indicate that a fusion at C6-C7 is not medically necessary given the current clinical presentation and imaging findings.

---

**III. Disposition**

The request was for a two-level ACDF (CPT 22552) at C5-C6 and C6-C7. Based on the comprehensive review, only the C5-C6 level meets the criteria for surgical intervention. The C6-C7 level does not meet medical necessity criteria under plan guidelines, state law, or generally accepted clinical standards. Therefore, the denial of the *two-level* fusion as requested is appropriate.

**Disposition: UPHOLD DENIAL of two-level ACDF (CPT 22552).**

**Warrant:**
The denial for a two-level ACDF is UPHELD because the C6-C7 level does not demonstrate medical necessity for surgical fusion, consistent with HealthFirst Plan Clinical Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.1, 2.2c, 2.3) and generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines, Section 3.1). The MRI findings for C6-C7 show only "small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, no nerve root compression identifiable, no cord contact," which does not constitute a surgical indication. Furthermore, the neurological exam at the C7 level revealed "no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms" and only a "slightly diminished tricep reflex," which is insufficient to warrant surgery per clinical guidelines. Additionally, a C7-specific interventional treatment was not performed, failing a requirement of the plan's conservative treatment criteria (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2c). State laws such as DMHC APL 22-014 and CIC § 10169.5 do not override these findings as they primarily address myelopathy or PT completion for *medically indicated* conditions, neither of which applies to C6-C7 in this case.

While the C5-C6 level clearly meets criteria for surgical intervention (consistent with CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3 and AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines, Section 3.1, Tiers 1b/2b), the request was for a specific two-level procedure (CPT 22552). Since one of the requested levels does not meet medical necessity, the denial of the *two-level* procedure as submitted is upheld. The treating physician may submit a new prior authorization request for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6 (CPT 22551).
