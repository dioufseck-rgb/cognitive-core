## I. FINAL DISPOSITION

**DECISION:** **OVERTURN THE DENIAL IN FULL**  

The Health Plan’s initial decision to deny coverage for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7, one level) is **reversed**. The requested surgical procedure is medically necessary under California law, generally accepted clinical standards, and the Health Plan's own clinical coverage criteria. The plan is directed to issue an immediate authorization for the requested procedure.

---

## II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The initial denial of the requested CCDF/ACDF at C6-C7 was based on the non-receipt of physical therapy records from the member's second provider (Mountain Physical Therapy). The Health Plan asserted it could only verify 4 weeks of the contractually required 6 weeks of physical therapy. 

On appeal, the complete clinical records from Mountain Physical Therapy were submitted, confirming an additional 4 weeks (12 sessions) of physical therapy, bringing the member’s documented conservative treatment to a **total of 8 weeks (24 sessions)** across two providers. Because the missing records have been supplied, and because the member has met and exceeded all of the plan's conservative therapy and diagnostic thresholds, the initial denial is no longer supported and is overturned.

---

## III. STEP-BY-STEP EVALUATION & CLINICAL ANALYSIS

### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Defect Check
The initial denial notice dated November 2, 2024, issued by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, was evaluated against the statutory requirements of **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)**. 
*   **Finding:** The denial notice was procedurally adequate. It specified the precise clinical reason for the denial (non-receipt of Mountain PT records resulting in failure to verify the 6-week conservative trial) and referenced the clinical criteria applied (MED-003). Therefore, the review proceeds to a substantive clinical and statutory evaluation.

### Step 2: Clinical Classification and Diagnostic Verification (Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy)
To apply the correct clinical standard, the member's pathology must be accurately classified.
*   **Imaging Findings (MRI, September 1, 2024):** Demonstrates a large disc herniation at C6-C7, bilateral C7 nerve root compression, and bilateral foraminal stenosis. Crucially, the MRI notes *"mild cord contact without compression"* and no cord signal changes (myelomalacia).
*   **Neurological Examination (September 5, 2024):** Documents bilateral C7 dermatomal symptoms including bilateral triceps weakness (4/5), diminished bilateral triceps reflexes, and bilateral finger tingling. No upper motor neuron signs (such as hyperreflexia, Hoffman's sign, clonus, or gait instability) are documented.
*   **Diagnostic Classification:** Because there is no objective evidence of spinal cord compression, myelomalacia, or upper motor neuron signs, this case is classified as **Bilateral Cervical Radiculopathy at C6-C7**. It does not meet the clinical definition of cervical myelopathy. Therefore, the specialized Myelopathy Exceptions (such as HealthFirst Exception 2.2(a) or DMHC APL 22-014) do not apply. The case must be evaluated under the standard clinical pathways for cervical radiculopathy.

### Step 3: Plan Criteria and Exception Application (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)
Applying the standard criteria under **HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2**, the clinical record demonstrates complete compliance as follows:

1.  **Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement):** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with corresponding bilateral C7 dermatomal weakness, reflex changes, and sensory symptoms. Supported by an MRI within 6 months (September 1, 2024) showing structural pathology (C6-C7 herniation and nerve root compression) matching clinical findings. **(MET)**
2.  **Section 2.2(a) (Physical Therapy):** The plan requires a minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy. 
    *   *Valley Physical Therapy:* June 1, 2024, to June 28, 2024 (4 weeks, 12 sessions) — Documented moderate improvement followed by a plateau. *(Received initially)*
    *   *Mountain Physical Therapy:* July 15, 2024, to August 9, 2024 (4 weeks, 12 sessions) — Documented no improvement in neurological symptoms with a discharge plateau. *(Submitted with Appeal)*
    *   *Total Combined:* **8 weeks (24 sessions)** of supervised physical therapy are now fully documented and verified. This exceeds the 6-week plan requirement. **(MET)**
3.  **Section 2.2(b) (Pharmacotherapy):** The plan requires a trial of at least two designated agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
    *   *Agent 1:* Pregabalin 150mg BID (neuropathic agent) for 3 months (partial relief).
    *   *Agent 2:* Naproxen 500mg BID (NSAID) for 2 months (inadequate response).
    *   Both trials exceeded 4 weeks. **(MET)**
4.  **Section 2.2(c) (Interventional Treatment):** The member underwent a bilateral C7 epidural steroid injection on September 20, 2024, resulting in only temporary relief (50% left-sided, 30% right-sided) before returning to baseline. **(MET)**

**Conclusion under Plan Criteria:** With the submission of the Mountain PT records on appeal, the member has satisfied 100% of the medical necessity criteria outlined in HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024.

### Step 4: Statutory and Regulatory Alignment (California Law Override)
Under the **Hierarchy of Authority**, California state law supersedes any restrictive plan interpretations.
*   **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2):** This statute prohibits a health plan from mechanically conditioning approval of a surgical procedure on physical therapy compliance when the member has completed physical therapy and the treating clinician documents that the member reached a **functional plateau** without adequate symptom resolution.
*   **Application:** Both the Valley PT and Mountain PT records explicitly document a clinical plateau. Specifically, the Mountain PT discharge summary notes *"no improvement in neurological symptoms"* and confirms a discharge plateau. Denying authorization when a functional plateau has been reached across 8 weeks of therapy is a direct violation of CIC § 10169.5.

### Step 5: Clinical Standards Review (AANS/CNS, NASS, AO Spine)
Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)** (the Independent Medical Review standard), a service must be approved if it is in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice, regardless of more restrictive internal plan criteria.
*   According to the **American Association of Neurological Surgeons / Congress of Neurological Surgeons (AANS/CNS) 2023 Guidelines** and the **North American Spine Society (NASS) 2020 Guidelines**, single-level ACDF (CPT 22551) is a strongly supported, Tier 2 indicated procedure for patients with cervical radiculopathy who exhibit objective neurological deficits (such as 4/5 motor weakness and diminished reflexes) and have failed a conservative trial of 6–12 weeks consisting of physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and interventional pain management.
*   Dorothy James exhibits objective bilateral motor weakness (4/5 triceps strength) and reflex loss corresponding directly to C6-C7 compression. She has undergone an exhaustive 8-week course of physical therapy, 3 months of neuropathic medication, and a cervical epidural steroid injection without long-term benefit. Requiring any further delay or conservative measures is contrary to clinical evidence and exposes the member to risks of permanent neurological deficit.

## IV. CONCLUSION AND ORDER

The clinical information submitted on appeal resolves the administrative omission that led to the initial denial. The patient has completed 8 weeks of physical therapy, which has been verified through primary records. Because the patient has bilateral, symptomatic, MRI-confirmed C6-C7 radiculopathy with objective motor deficits that have failed exhaustive conservative therapy, the requested procedure is medically necessary under plan guidelines, California law, and national clinical standards.

The Health Plan is hereby **ORDERED** to:
1.  **Overturn** the denial of prior authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7, one level).
2.  **Approve and authorize** the requested procedure to be performed by Dr. Paul Adams at UCLA Medical Center.
3.  Issue the formal authorization number and notice to the member and provider within the legally mandated timeframes under **CHSC § 1367.01**.
