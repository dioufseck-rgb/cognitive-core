### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This formal appeal review evaluates the medical necessity of a requested single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) for member Thomas Reed. The health plan's initial denial, dated October 20, 2024, was based on the assertion that the member lacked myelopathic findings, lacked measurable spinal cord compression on imaging, and had not completed physical therapy (PT) to a functional plateau. 

Following a comprehensive review of the complete clinical record, relevant state regulations, and medical policy, the initial denial is **OVERTURNED**. The member presents with clinically and anatomically correlated **Cervical Radiculopathy** (not myelopathy), has fully satisfied the standard plan criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2), and has documented a clear physical therapy "functional plateau" after an exhaustive conservative treatment course. Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2)**, the health plan is legally prohibited from enforcing further physical therapy requirements when a functional plateau has been reached and documented.

*   **Finding:** The notice met the basic statutory disclosure requirements of **CHSC § 1374.31(b)** by providing the clinical rationale. However, as detailed below, the clinical rationale applied was substantively flawed due to a medical category error. *Action: Proceed to Step 2.*

#### Step 2: Diagnostic Classification and Objective Pathology Verification
*   **Analysis of Clinical Record:**
    *   **Imaging (MRI dated August 1, 2024):** Demonstrates a C6-C7 disc herniation and mild right C7 foraminal stenosis. Crucially, the MRI shows *no cord signal change*, *no myelomalacia*, and only *mild cord contact without measurable compression*.
    *   **Neurological Exam (dated August 5, 2024):** Documents right C7 dermatomal distribution numbness and a diminished right tricep reflex. There are no bilateral findings, no upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (e.g., negative Hoffman, normal gait, no hyperreflexia), and rest pain is rated severe at 7/10.
*   **Diagnostic Determination:** The clinical signs and imaging findings are entirely unilateral, dermatomal, and consistent with **Cervical Radiculopathy (right C7)**. There is no clinical or radiographic evidence of Cervical Myelopathy. 
*   **Finding:** The member's true presentation is **Cervical Radiculopathy** matching CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a). The initial reviewer’s denial based on the absence of "measurable compression" and "myelopathy findings" represents a clinical category error. The member does not claim to have, nor is the surgeon diagnosing, myelopathy. *Action: Proceed to Step 4 (Radiculopathy Pathway).*

*   **Regulatory Alignment (CIC § 10169.5):** Under California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2), a health plan cannot condition approval of a surgical procedure on further physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating clinician documents that the member has reached a "functional plateau" without adequate resolution of symptoms.

The initial reviewer’s assertion that "PT [was] not yet completed to functional plateau" is factually incorrect and directly contradicted by the clinical record. *Action: Overturn.*

### DISPOSITION AND RATIONALE

**FINAL DISPOSITION: OVERTURNED**

The prior authorization denial for a single-level ACDF at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) is **reversed in full**. 

#### Summary of Rationale:
1.  **Factually Erroneous Denial Basis:** The initial reviewer denied the request citing a lack of myelopathy findings (cord compression, myelomalacia). However, the requested surgery is for **Cervical Radiculopathy** (C7 nerve root compression), which is a separate and distinct diagnosis covered under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a). Requiring myelopathic findings for a radiculopathy diagnosis constitutes an inappropriate clinical standard application.
2.  **Full Compliance with Plan Criteria:** The clinical record demonstrates that the member tried and failed a comprehensive 4-month trial of Pregabalin, a 3-month trial of Meloxicam, and a right C7 epidural injection, meeting all conservative criteria under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2.
3.  **Statutory Violation of PT Requirements:** The member completed 7 weeks (21 sessions) of PT. The physical therapy discharge summary explicitly documents that a "functional plateau" was reached, as the patient's radicular symptoms remained unchanged over the final 3 sessions. Under **California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2)**, a health plan is legally prohibited from denying coverage based on incomplete physical therapy when a documented functional plateau has been achieved.
4.  **Clinical Practice Guidelines:** Per **NASS 2020** and **AANS/CNS 2023** guidelines, surgical decompression (ACDF) is a Tier 2, highly recommended indication for patients with objective cervical radiculopathy who have failed 6 to 12 weeks of comprehensive conservative therapy.
