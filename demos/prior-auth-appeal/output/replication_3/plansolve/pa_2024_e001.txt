## II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Dr. Lisa Park requested a two-level ACDF (C5–C6 and C6–C7). The plan initially issued a blanket denial of the entire request because one level (C6–C7) did not meet clinical criteria. 

This review finds that the initial denial notice was **procedurally defective** under California law for failing to cite the specific clinical coverage criteria applied. Substantively, the medical record demonstrates that the member has severe, symptomatic **right C6 cervical radiculopathy** with objective neurological deficits (3+/5 bicep weakness, absent bicep reflex) and correlating MRI pathology at C5–C6. The member has fully completed the plan’s standard conservative treatment trials (8 weeks of PT, 2 pharmacotherapy agents, and an epidural steroid injection with temporary relief). Fusing C5–C6 is clinically indicated and medically necessary. 

Conversely, the adjacent C6–C7 level exhibits only mild, age-related changes on MRI without nerve root or spinal cord compression, and lacks correlating neurological deficits or symptoms. Multi-level fusion is not indicated solely to prevent "adjacent segment degeneration" in the absence of active, correlating pathology. Therefore, surgical intervention at C6–C7 is denied.

## III. NUMBERED EVALUATION & CLINICAL ANALYSIS

### 1. Procedural Defect Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
*   **Analysis**: Under California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b), a plan’s written denial must explicitly cite the specific clinical coverage criteria applied, specify the clinical reasons for the denial, and provide notice of Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights. The initial denial notice issued on November 1, 2024, failed to cite the applicable plan document (**HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024**) and failed to provide a notice of IMR rights, stating only that a "two-level fusion is not supported" due to mild changes at C6–C7. 
*   **Finding**: **POSITIVE FINDING OF PROCEDURAL DEFECT**. The denial notice is procedurally defective. Under CHSC § 1374.31(b), a defective notice "shall be treated as if no denial was issued" regarding appeal timelines. However, to protect member safety and ensure clinical appropriateness under CHSC § 1374.32(b), this review proceeds to evaluate the clinical merits of each level to avoid authorizing an unnecessary and potentially harmful secondary fusion.

### 2. Clinical Classification and Diagnostic Verification
*   **Analysis**: The clinical record was evaluated to classify the pathology as either cervical radiculopathy or cervical myelopathy:
    *   *Myelopathy Indicators*: The MRI of September 15, 2024, shows no spinal cord compression, no cord contact, and no myelomalacia (T2 cord signal changes). The neurological exam of September 20, 2024, lists no bilateral symptoms, no upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (e.g., positive Hoffman, hyperreflexia, clonus, or gait disturbance), and no bowel/bladder dysfunction.
    *   *Radiculopathy Indicators*: The member presents with unilateral (right) bicep weakness (3+/5), an absent right bicep reflex, and right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling. The C5–C6 MRI shows a large disc herniation, moderate-to-severe right C6 nerve root compression, and foraminal stenosis.
*   **Finding**: **NEGATIVE FINDING FOR MYELOPATHY**. The primary diagnosis is verified as **severe unilateral right C6 cervical radiculopathy** originating from the C5–C6 level. The case is evaluated under standard conservative treatment pathways (Step 4, negative pathway).

### 3. Imaging Correlation and Multi-Level Pathology Analysis
*   **Analysis**: 
    *   **C5–C6 Level**: MRI findings of a large disc herniation and moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression directly correlate with the member's clinical presentation of right bicep weakness (3+/5), absent bicep reflex, and C6 numbness.
    *   **C6–C7 Level**: MRI shows only a small disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing, with no identifiable nerve root compression and no cord contact. The neurological exam reveals a slightly diminished tricep reflex but no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms. Dr. Park noted that C7 symptoms were "possibly present but not clearly documented," and the physical therapy discharge note from week 8 reports that C7-related symptoms were "minimal and possibly resolving."
*   **Finding**: **NEGATIVE FINDING FOR MULTI-LEVEL PATHOLOGY**. While C5–C6 shows severe, clinically correlating pathology, the C6–C7 level shows only mild, age-related changes without structural nerve root compression or correlating clinical symptoms. Under Plan Criteria Section 3.3 and AANS/CNS clinical guidelines, surgery at the C6–C7 level (CPT 22552) is not clinically indicated.

### 4. Statutory Overrides of Conservative Treatment Requirements
*   **Analysis**: 
    *   Because there is no objective spinal cord compression or myelomalacia, the automatic statutory exceptions to conservative treatment under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(3)** and **DMHC APL 22-014** do not apply to waive conservative trials.
    *   Therefore, standard Plan Criteria Section 2.2 applies. We must verify if the member completed the standard conservative trial:
        1.  *Physical Therapy*: Completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised PT with persisting C6 symptoms. (Plan requirement: 6 weeks; **MET**).
        2.  *Pharmacotherapy*: Taken Pregabalin 150mg BID for 4 months and Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months. (Plan requirement: 2 agents for 4+ weeks each; **MET**).
        3.  *Interventional*: Received a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 15, 2024, yielding 70% relief that fully returned after 4 weeks. (Plan requirement: 1 interventional procedure; **MET**).
*   **Finding**: **POSITIVE FINDING FOR PRIMARY LEVEL (C5–C6)**. The member has fully satisfied the conservative treatment requirements under Plan Criteria Section 2.2. The plan’s initial reviewer erred by denying the entire procedure based on C6–C7 pathology deficiencies, ignoring that the primary level (C5–C6) fully met all medical necessity and conservative treatment criteria.

### 5. Clinical Standards Evaluation (CHSC § 1374.32(b))
*   **Analysis**: Under California law, clinical standards of care override restrictive or improperly applied plan criteria. 
    *   **C5–C6 (CPT 22551)**: Under **AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines (Tier 2)** and **NASS 2020 Guidelines**, single-level anterior cervical decompression and fusion (ACDF) is strongly indicated for cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits (3+/5 bicep weakness, absent bicep reflex) that has failed a comprehensive 6-to-12 week conservative trial. Continuing conservative care in the presence of a motor deficit (3+/5 weakness) poses a risk of permanent neurological loss. Surgical decompression is medically necessary.
    *   **C6–C7 (CPT 22552)**: Dr. Park’s appeal basis argues that adjacent levels should be fused simultaneously to prevent accelerated degeneration. However, **AANS/CNS 2023** and **AO Spine 2021** clinical standards do not support prophylactic fusion of adjacent segments. Fusing a level with only mild, stable, or resolving symptoms and no structural nerve root compression (Tier 3) is inappropriate. It unnecessarily increases surgical morbidity, extends recovery times, and accelerates adjacent segment disease at C4–C5 and C7–T1.
*   **Finding**: **PARTIAL CLINICAL INDICATION**. Clinical standards support immediate surgical intervention for C5–C6 (CPT 22551) but contraindicate surgery at C6–C7 (CPT 22552).

---

## IV. CONCLUSION AND ORDER

Applying the **California Hierarchy of Authority** ($\text{State Law} > \text{Clinical Standards} > \text{Plan Criteria}$):

1.  **CPT 22551 (ACDF C5–C6)**: The member met all criteria in **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2**. Furthermore, under **AANS/CNS 2023 Clinical Standards**, surgical decompression is the required standard of care for a persistent motor deficit (3+/5 bicep weakness) post-conservative therapy. The plan's decision to deny the medically necessary C5–C6 level because the secondary level was unindicated is substantively incorrect and is **OVERTURNED**.
2.  **CPT 22552 (ACDF C6–C7)**: This level does not meet medical necessity under **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.3** or **AANS/CNS 2023 Clinical Standards** due to a lack of correlating structural pathology, nerve compression, or clinical deficits. Prophylactic fusion is clinically unsupported. The plan's denial of this level is **UPHELD**.

**Ordered**: HealthFirst shall immediately authorize **CPT code 22551** for ACDF at the C5–C6 level, to be performed by Dr. Lisa Park at UCSF Medical Center. The request for the additional level, **CPT code 22552** (C6–C7), remains denied.
