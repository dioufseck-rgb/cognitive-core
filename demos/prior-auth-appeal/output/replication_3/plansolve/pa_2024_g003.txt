## FINAL APPEAL DETERMINATION

### DISPOSITION: UPHOLD DENIAL
The prior authorization denial for CPT Code 22551 (ACDF C5-C6, one level) is hereby **UPHELD**. Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical record, relevant state regulations, plan criteria, and generally accepted clinical standards, the requested surgical procedure is not determined to be medically necessary at this time. The member must complete the remaining conservative treatment requirements as outlined under the plan’s Myelopathy Exception pathway, as no statutory or clinical overrides are triggered by the current clinical presentation.

---

## DETAILED EVALUATION & FINDINGS

### STEP 1: PROCEDURAL ADEQUACY CHECK (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
Pursuant to **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)**, the initial denial notice issued on November 5, 2024, was audited for statutory communication compliance.
*   **Analysis:** The denial letter explicitly cited the applicable clinical criteria (**HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2**), provided the exact clinical reasons for the denial (completion of only 3 of 6 weeks of physical therapy, use of only one pharmacotherapy agent, and absence of an interventional procedure), and included the mandatory notice of Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights.
*   **Finding:** The denial notice is **procedurally adequate and valid**. It contains no procedural defects. The clinical review proceeds to Step 2.

---

### STEP 2: DIAGNOSTIC ANALYSIS & CLINICAL CATEGORIZATION
The clinical record was evaluated to categorize the primary pathology as either Cervical Radiculopathy or Cervical Myelopathy to establish the correct clinical and regulatory evaluation pathway.
*   **Neurological Exam Findings (10/05/2024):** Bilateral Hoffman signs are present; mild bilateral hyperreflexia is noted; hand grip strength is 4+/5 bilaterally; tandem gait remains intact; Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) are fully intact. The modified Japanese Orthopaedic Association (mJOA) score is **15/18**, indicating **mild myelopathy**.
*   **Imaging Findings (MRI, 10/01/2024):** C5-C6 moderate central cord compression is present. Canal diameter is 8mm. No T2 signal change is present, and **no myelomalacia** is identified.
*   **Finding:** The clinical evidence (objective cord compression on MRI combined with bilateral upper motor neuron signs—specifically Hoffman signs and hyperreflexia) confirms a diagnosis of **Cervical Myelopathy**. The member is correctly classified under the Myelopathy clinical pathway. Proceed to Step 3.

---

### STEP 3: STATE REGULATORY WAIVER COMPLIANCE (CIC § 10169.5 & DMHC APL 22-014)
California law mandates exemptions from standard conservative treatment trials for certain severe or progressive neurological conditions. The medical record was assessed against these statutory standards:

1.  **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(3):** Prohibits conditioning surgery on physical therapy (PT) if there is objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that PT cannot address. Under clinical guidelines, this requires the presence of **myelomalacia** (active spinal cord injury) in conjunction with cord compression. 
    *   *Analysis:* The member’s MRI dated 10/01/2024 explicitly states: *"No T2 signal change. No myelomalacia."* Therefore, this statutory waiver is not triggered.
2.  **DMHC All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014 (Prior Authorization for Time-Sensitive Neurological Conditions):** Prohibits plans from requiring conservative therapy if objective imaging demonstrates *progressive pathology* (such as progressive cord signal change, myelomalacia, or progressive herniation on serial imaging) AND the treating physician documents that conservative treatment has failed to arrest neurological progression.
    *   *Analysis:* The clinical record describes the member's symptoms as **"stable"** over a 6-month duration. There is no serial imaging demonstrating progressive cord herniation, nor is there active myelomalacia. While the treating neurologist, Dr. Osei, documented a letter on 11/01/2024 asserting "severe and progressive" cervical cord compression and "urgency," this assertion is directly contradicted by the objective diagnostic record (mJOA score of 15, intact tandem gait, normal ADLs, stable 6-month course, and an MRI negative for myelomalacia). 
*   **Finding:** Because there is no objective imaging evidence of progressive pathology or myelomalacia, the clinical presentation does not meet the criteria for mandatory statutory waivers under **CIC § 10169.5** or **DMHC APL 22-014**. Procedural and plan-based conservative trials remain legally enforceable. Proceed to Step 5 (via Step 4 clinical guidance).

---

### STEP 4: GENERALLY ACCEPTED CLINICAL STANDARDS ALIGNMENT (AANS/CNS 2023 & AO Spine 2021)
Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)**, the standard of review for medical necessity must align with generally accepted standards of medical practice, which supersede more restrictive plan criteria.
*   **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) & AO Spine Clinical Practice Guidelines (2021):**
    *   **Tier 1 (Strong Surgical Indication):** Indicated for *moderate-to-severe* myelopathy (mJOA < 15), progressive neurological deficits, or MRI-confirmed myelomalacia. In such cases, delaying surgery for conservative management is medically contraindicated.
    *   **Tier 2 (Reasonable Surgical Indication):** Indicated for *mild, stable* myelopathy (mJOA 15-17) after an adequate trial of conservative therapy. Both guideline bodies note that conservative management, including physical therapy, is a safe and appropriate initial strategy for patients with mild, stable symptoms and no myelomalacia.
*   **Finding:** The member presents with mild, stable myelopathy (mJOA 15, intact gait, fully independent ADLs) without myelomalacia. Under generally accepted standards of clinical practice, this does not constitute an urgent Tier 1 indication. Immediate surgical authorization is not mandated by national standards, and completing a structured trial of conservative therapy is clinically appropriate. Proceed to Step 5.

---

### STEP 5: PLAN CLINICAL CRITERIA ALIGNMENT (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)
Because no regulatory waivers or overriding clinical standard mandates apply, the request must be evaluated against the plan’s internal clinical criteria, specifically the **Myelopathy Exception Pathway (Section 2.2(A))**.

Under **CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(A)**, the requirements for cervical myelopathy are modified as follows:
1.  **Physical Therapy:** Reduced to a minimum of **4 weeks** (or a documented functional plateau).
    *   *Member Status:* The member completed only **3 weeks** (9 sessions) of PT. The physical therapy was halted prematurely by Dr. Osei. There is no documentation of a "functional plateau" (defined as <10% improvement over 3 sessions or therapist documentation of maximum benefit). The clinical record states "mild symptoms stable."
    *   *Result:* **Unmet**. (3 weeks completed vs. 4 weeks required).
2.  **Pharmacotherapy:** Minimum trial of **two** agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
    *   *Member Status:* The member trialed only **one** agent: Baclofen 5mg BID for 6 weeks (which resulted in mild spasticity improvement). No second agent (such as an NSAID, gabapentinoid, or oral corticosteroid) was trialed.
    *   *Result:* **Unmet**. (1 agent trialed vs. 2 agents required).
3.  **Interventional Treatment:** Waived if documented as a risk.
    *   *Result:* **Met / Waived**. (Dr. Osei's clinical documentation is sufficient to waive the interventional procedure requirement).

*   **Finding:** The member does not meet the modified clinical coverage criteria under **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(A)**. Specifically, the member did not complete the 4-week physical therapy requirement and failed to trial a second pharmacotherapy agent, with no clinical documentation establishing a functional plateau or contraindication to pharmacotherapy.

---

## CONCLUSION & CLINICAL GUIDANCE

While Dr. Osei’s letter of appeal expresses significant concern regarding the risk of neurological deterioration, his assertions of acute surgical urgency are not supported by the objective clinical findings in the medical record:
*   The member's MRI shows **no myelomalacia** (no T2 signal change), which is the primary objective indicator of urgent structural compromise.
*   The member's neurological exam reveals **mild, stable symptoms** (mJOA score of 15/18), an intact tandem gait, and fully independent functional ADLs over a 6-month period.

Therefore, the initial utilization review decision was clinically and legally correct. To qualify for surgical authorization, the member must satisfy the standard conservative treatment parameters under the **Myelopathy Pathway (Section 2.2(A))**:
1.  **Complete one additional week of physical therapy** (bringing the total to the required 4-week minimum), or provide documentation from the physical therapist demonstrating that a functional plateau has been reached.
2.  **Trial a second pharmacotherapy agent** (such as an NSAID, Gabapentin, Pregabalin, or Duloxetine) for a minimum of 4 weeks, or provide clinical documentation explaining why a second agent is medically contraindicated.

The prior authorization request for CPT Code 22551 is **UPHELD**.
