### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
As an expert health plan appeals reviewer operating under California regulatory jurisdiction, I have conducted a de novo review of the prior authorization denial for an ACDF at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) requested on behalf of member Michael Green. 

The initial denial issued on November 08, 2024, by Dr. Patricia Huang cited reason code MED-003 ("Member does not meet criteria..."). However, the plan failed to attach the referenced clinical criteria, failed to provide specific clinical reasons for the denial, and failed to issue the mandatory Independent Medical Review (IMR) notice. 

Pursuant to the governing **Hierarchy of Authority**, this evaluation applies **California State Law and Regulatory Mandates** (specifically California Health & Safety Code [CHSC] and California Insurance Code [CIC]) as the highest authority, followed by **Generally Accepted Clinical Standards** (AANS/CNS and AO Spine guidelines), and lastly **Plan Clinical Coverage Criteria** (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024).

Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical record, the member **fully satisfies** the plan's medical necessity criteria for cervical radiculopathy. Furthermore, the plan’s initial denial is procedurally defective under state law, and the physical therapy requirements are satisfied via a documented functional plateau under California statutory protections. Therefore, the initial decision is **OVERTURNED** and the surgery is **APPROVED**.

### DETAILED EVALUATION FINDINGS

#### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Regulatory Compliance Check (CHSC § 1374.31)
Under **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(a)**, when a health plan issues a prior authorization denial based on medical necessity, it is legally mandated to provide the specific clinical criteria applied, the specific clinical reasons for the denial, and a notice of the member’s Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights. 

The initial denial notice dated November 08, 2024:
1.  Failed to attach or provide the referenced clinical criteria document.
2.  Did not specify which criteria or conservative treatments were allegedly missing.
3.  Failed to enclose the legally required IMR rights notice.

Pursuant to **CHSC § 1374.31(b)**, a denial notice that fails to specify the applied clinical criteria or the detailed clinical reasons is **defective** and must be treated as if no denial was issued for timeline purposes. Because the clinical record is highly sufficient and contains complete diagnostic, imaging, and conservative treatment details, a de novo clinical review has been executed rather than remanding the case.

#### Step 2: Diagnostic Classification and Clinical Presentation Review
To establish the correct clinical pathway, the clinical record was evaluated to differentiate between cervical myelopathy and cervical radiculopathy:
*   **Imaging Findings (MRI dated 2024-09-20):** Demonstrates a large C5-C6 disc herniation causing moderate left C6 nerve root compression. Crucially, the MRI notes **no spinal cord contact, no cord compression, and no myelomalacia (T2 signal change).**
*   **Neurological Examination (2024-09-25):** Documents left C6 dermatomal numbness, diminished left bicep reflex, and 4/5 left bicep motor weakness. No upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (e.g., positive Hoffman sign, hyperreflexia, clonus, bilateral symptoms, or gait disturbances) are present.
*   **Diagnostic Classification:** The clinical presentation is classified as **Cervical Radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit (C5-C6, left C6 nerve root)**. Because there is no spinal cord compression, standard radiculopathy criteria apply (Section 2.2 of CC-SPINE-2024); the myelopathy exception pathways (Section 2.2(A)) are not active.

#### Step 3: Application of Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)
A de novo application of the plan's own clinical coverage criteria for standard cervical radiculopathy reveals that the member **fully satisfies all medical necessity requirements** outlined in **Section 2.2**:

1.  **Diagnosis (Section 2.1(a)):** Confirmed cervical radiculopathy with dermatomal numbness, diminished reflex, and objective 4/5 motor weakness, supported by an MRI within 6 months showing C5-C6 root compression. (**Met**)
2.  **Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)):** The plan requires a minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy. The clinical record documents that the member completed **7 weeks of supervised physical therapy (total of 21 sessions)**. (**Met**)
3.  **Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)):** The plan requires a trial of at least two covered agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. The member completed:
    *   *Gabapentin 600mg TID* for 4 months (neuropathic agent). (**Met**)
    *   *Naproxen 500mg BID* for 3 months (NSAID). (**Met**)
4.  **Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)):** The plan requires at least one interventional procedure. The member received a **left C6 epidural steroid injection on 2024-10-28**, which provided 40% temporary relief before symptoms returned to baseline after 3 weeks. (**Met**)
5.  **Imaging & Documentation (Sections 2.3 & 2.4):** Current MRI demonstrates concordant pathology at the requested surgical level (C5-C6 herniation compressing the left C6 nerve root, matching the C6 dermatomal deficits and bicep weakness). Dr. Kim documented functional limitations and surgical risks. (**Met**)

The plan's initial medical necessity denial was a clear factual error, as the member exceeded every conservative treatment threshold required under the plan's internal guidelines.

#### Step 4: Statutory Overrides of Conservative Treatment (CIC § 10169.5)
Even if the plan had argued that further conservative management was required, California statutory law overrides such requirements under the current clinical presentation:
*   Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2)**, a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning surgical approval on the completion of additional physical therapy when the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating clinician documents that the member has reached a **functional plateau** without adequate symptom resolution.
*   The clinical record explicitly states that physical therapy notes document a clear **functional plateau at week 6** with persistent, severe radicular symptoms and objective motor weakness (4/5 bicep). Mechanically requiring additional conservative trials violates **CIC § 10169.5(c)**.

#### Step 5: Clinical Standards and Medical Necessity Alignment (AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines)
Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)**, the standard for medical necessity during an appeal must align with generally accepted standards of medical practice rather than solely relying on a plan's internal policies.
*   According to the **AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines** and the **North American Spine Society (NASS) 2020 Guidelines**, single-level anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) is classified as a **Tier 2 Reasonable and Highly Indicated** procedure for patients presenting with cervical radiculopathy, persistent functional limitations, and objective neurological deficits (motor weakness and reflex changes) that have failed to resolve after an appropriate conservative trial of 6 to 12 weeks.
*   Delaying surgical decompression in a patient with a documented progressive motor deficit (4/5 bicep weakness) and a functional plateau risks permanent, irreversible nerve root injury. Proceeding with CPT 22551 is fully aligned with generally accepted clinical standards.

### DISPOSITION & DETERMINATION

Based on the governing hierarchy of authority, the initial decision to deny prior authorization for CPT 22551 is **OVERTURNED**. 

1.  **Procedural Ruling:** The denial issued on 2024-11-08 was defective under **CHSC § 1374.31(b)**.
2.  **Clinical Ruling:** A de novo clinical review demonstrates that the member suffers from severe cervical radiculopathy and has successfully completed 7 weeks of physical therapy (reaching a documented functional plateau under **CIC § 10169.5**), 4 months of dual-agent pharmacotherapy, and a cervical epidural steroid injection.
3.  **Authorization:** Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) C5-C6 (CPT 22551) is **APPROVED** as medically necessary.

The plan is directed to issue an immediate, unconditional authorization for the requested procedure at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, to be performed by Dr. Sarah Kim, MD, valid for 90 days from the date of this determination.
