### II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY OF CLINICAL FINDINGS

This case involves a 60-year-old male with a presenting diagnosis of asymmetric two-level cervical disc disease at C5–C6 and C6–C7. The treating surgeon requested a two-level ACDF. The health plan denied the entire request because C6–C7 did not meet the objective criteria, invoking a policy rule that two-level fusions are not approved when only one level meets criteria.

Upon clinical and regulatory review under California law:
1.  **C5–C6** exhibits severe, symptomatic, clinically correlated radiculopathy (right bicep weakness 3+/5, absent bicep reflex, C6 dermatomal numbness) that has failed a comprehensive 8-week physical therapy trial, a 4-month neuropathic/NSAID pharmacotherapy trial, and a targeted epidural steroid injection. It fully satisfies **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a) and 2.2**.
2.  **C6–C7** exhibits only a small disc bulge with mild foraminal narrowing, no nerve root compression, and no cord contact. The neurological exam shows no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms, and the physical therapist documented that any potential C7 symptoms were minimal and resolving by week 8. 
3.  **No Myelopathy** is present. Unilateral symptoms and the lack of upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (e.g., hyperreflexia, positive Hoffman's sign, gait disturbances) classify this case strictly as **cervical radiculopathy**.
4.  Therefore, state regulatory waivers for urgent neurological compromise (e.g., myelomalacia) do not apply to bypass plan criteria at C6–C7. Clinical standards of care do not support surgery at C6–C7, overriding the provider's argument regarding prophylactic adjacent-level fusion.

### III. STEP-BY-STEP EVALUATION ANALYSIS

#### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Denial Notice Compliance Check
The health plan’s initial denial notice dated November 1, 2024, was evaluated against California statutory requirements (**CHSC § 1374.31**).
*   **Finding:** **Compliant (Positive Finding).** 
*   **Analysis:** The denial notice complies with **CHSC § 1374.31(a)**. It explicitly cited the internal clinical coverage criteria applied (*HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024*), provided a clear and specific clinical rationale for the level-by-level determination (detailing that C5–C6 met the criteria while C6–C7 showed only mild degenerative changes, and explaining the multi-level policy rule), and included the mandated Independent Medical Review (IMR) notification. No procedural defects under **CHSC § 1374.31(b)** are present.

#### Step 2: Diagnostic Classification (Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy)
A review of the clinical record was conducted to determine the primary diagnosis and differentiate between radiculopathy and myelopathy, as these conditions utilize distinct clinical and regulatory pathways.
*   **Finding:** **Cervical Radiculopathy (Negative Finding).**
*   **Analysis:** Clinical documentation dated September 20, 2024, reveals strictly unilateral, dermatomal signs: right bicep weakness (3+/5), absent right bicep reflex, and right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling. Crucially, there are no upper motor neuron signs (such as hyperreflexia, a positive Hoffman’s sign, clonus, or gait instability) and no bilateral upper extremity symptoms. The primary clinical diagnosis is **C6 cervical radiculopathy**. There is no objective clinical or radiographic evidence of cervical myelopathy.

#### Step 3: Segmental Imaging and Clinical Correlation (C5–C6 & C6–C7)
The radiologist's MRI report dated September 15, 2024, and neurosurgical clinical notes were evaluated to assess structural pathology and correlate it with the physical examination.
*   **Finding:** **Isolated C5–C6 Pathology (Negative Finding).**
*   **Analysis:** 
    *   **C5–C6:** The MRI demonstrates a large disc herniation, moderate-to-severe right C6 nerve root compression, and foraminal stenosis. This directly correlates with the physical exam findings of right bicep weakness (3+/5), absent right bicep reflex, and right C6 dermatomal numbness. 
    *   **C6–C7:** The MRI demonstrates only a small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, no identifiable nerve root compression, and no cord contact. The physical exam reveals no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms (only a "slightly diminished" right tricep reflex and "minimal and possibly resolving" symptoms per the week 8 PT note). 
    *   **Conclusion:** Severe pathology is isolated to C5–C6. The C6–C7 level exhibits mild degenerative changes without objective compression or clinical correlation.

#### Step 4: Application of State Regulatory Waivers vs. Plan Criteria
We evaluated whether California state laws override the plan's conservative therapy requirements for either level.
*   **Finding:** **Standard Plan Criteria Active; C5–C6 Satisfies / C6–C7 Fails (Negative Finding).**
*   **Analysis:** Because there is no objective structural cord compromise, spinal cord compression, or myelomalacia on MRI, standard state-level myelopathy waivers under **CIC § 10169.5(a)(3)** and **DMHC APL 22-014** do not apply. Standard conservative therapy requirements under *HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2* remain active.
    *   **C5–C6 Conservative Therapy Audit:**
        *   *Physical Therapy:* Member completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised PT targeting this level, exceeding the 6-week requirement under Section 2.2(a). Persistent C6 symptoms are documented.
        *   *Pharmacotherapy:* Member completed a 4-month trial of Pregabalin 150mg BID (neuropathic) and a 3-month trial of Naproxen 500mg BID (NSAID), satisfying the requirement for two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each under Section 2.2(b).
        *   *Interventional:* Member underwent a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 15, 2024, yielding temporary 70% relief which fully returned after 4 weeks, satisfying Section 2.2(c).
        *   *Result:* C5–C6 meets all conservative therapy requirements.
    *   **C6–C7 Conservative Therapy Audit:**
        *   No C7-specific interventional blocks were performed. Pharmacotherapy response was documented as "uncertain C7 contribution." Physical therapy notes at week 8 indicated C7 symptoms were "minimal and possibly resolving," showing a functional resolution rather than a failure of conservative therapy.
        *   *Result:* C6–C7 fails standard conservative therapy requirements.

#### Step 5: Clinical Standard of Care Overrides
The plan’s multi-level denial was compared against generally accepted medical standards (**CHSC § 1374.32(b)**), including **AANS/CNS 2023**, **AO Spine 2021**, and **NASS 2020** guidelines.
*   **Finding:** **No Standard of Care Override for C6–C7 (Negative Finding).**
*   **Analysis:** Generally accepted clinical standards do not support surgical decompression at a spinal level that lacks clinical symptoms and correlating radiographic compression. The appeal argument that the C6–C7 level must be addressed simultaneously to "prevent accelerated degeneration" at adjacent levels is not supported by spine surgery guidelines. Prophylactic fusion of mildly degenerative, non-compressive segments is contraindicated under **AANS/CNS Tier 3 criteria** (surgery not indicated for imaging findings without correlating symptoms or mild radiculopathy resolving on conservative treatment).

### IV. DISPOSITION DIRECTIVE

Based on the **Hierarchy of Authority** and the **California Independent Medical Review Standard (CHSC § 1374.32)**:

1.  **CPT Code 22551 (ACDF C5–C6):** The denial is **OVERTURNED**. HealthFirst is directed to immediately authorize anterior cervical discectomy and fusion at the C5–C6 level. The clinical records demonstrate severe, correlated radiculopathy and the complete exhaustion of conservative therapy in full compliance with both *HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024* guidelines and national clinical standards (*AANS/CNS 2023*).
2.  **CPT Code 22552 (ACDF C6–C7, additional level):** The denial is **UPHELD**. The medical records do not support the necessity of addressing the C6–C7 level. The imaging shows only mild degenerative changes without nerve root or spinal cord compression, and physical therapy records demonstrate that potential C7 symptoms were resolving. Under *HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.3* and *AANS/CNS Tier 3 standards*, surgery is not indicated at this level.

**Instructions to the Health Plan:** The plan must issue an authorization for the single-level procedure (CPT 22551) at C5–C6 within the standard regulatory timeframe.
