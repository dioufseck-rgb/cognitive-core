### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CLINICAL FINDINGS

This formal appeal review has been conducted in accordance with **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b)**, **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5**, and **HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024)**. 

The member, a 45-year-old female, presents with a diagnosis of symptomatic, persistent right C6 cervical radiculopathy. She has undergone extensive, well-documented conservative management that has failed to provide sustained functional relief. 

A comprehensive clinical and regulatory review demonstrates that surgical intervention at the **C5-C6 level** is medically necessary, as the member meets all standard plan criteria and generally accepted clinical standards. However, the request to perform a second level of fusion at **C6-C7** for "prophylactic" stabilization to prevent adjacent segment disease is not medically necessary, lacks objective radiographic and clinical correlation, and is unsupported by national clinical guidelines. 

---

### EVALUATION STEPS & CLINICAL ANALYSIS

#### Step 1: Procedural Defect and Notice Adequacy Check
*   **Authority:** California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)
*   **Finding:** The initial denial notice issued on October 28, 2024, by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, cited Reason Code **MED-003** and provided a clear, specific clinical reason for the partial denial (imaging showing pathology at C5-C6 only, with mild, non-compressive degenerative changes at C6-C7). The notice cited the clinical coverage criteria applied and enclosed standard California Independent Medical Review (IMR) notification documents. 
*   **Action:** No procedural defects are identified. The denial notice is legally sufficient under CHSC § 1374.31(b). The review proceeds to clinical and statutory evaluation.

#### Step 2: Diagnostic Classification (Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy)
*   **Authority:** AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023); HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1
*   **Clinical Record Review:** The neurological examination dated September 5, 2024, reveals right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, a diminished right biceps reflex, and right grip strength of 4/5. Crucially, the examiner documented: **no bilateral findings, no C7 dermatomal/myotomal deficits, and no upper motor neuron (UMN) signs** (such as positive Hoffman sign, hyperreflexia, clonus, or gait instability). The cervical MRI dated September 1, 2024, confirms a large disc herniation at C5-C6, but shows **no central canal stenosis, no spinal cord compression, and no cord signal change (myelomalacia)** at any level.
*   **Action:** The member's primary diagnosis is classified as **unilateral C5-C6 cervical radiculopathy without myelopathy**. Therefore, the standard radiculopathy conservative treatment pathway under **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2** applies. The modified myelopathy pathway (Exception 2.2(A)) and associated myelopathy regulatory waivers (DMHC APL 22-014) are not triggered.

#### Step 3: Level-Specific Imaging and Clinical Correlation
*   **Authority:** HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.3 & Section 3.3; AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines
*   **C5-C6 Level Analysis:** 
    *   *Imaging Findings:* MRI (September 1, 2024) reveals a large disc herniation with moderate-to-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis.
    *   *Clinical Correlation:* Highly correlated with the patient's right C6 radicular pain, C6 dermatomal numbness, diminished right biceps reflex, and 4/5 right grip strength.
    *   *Surgical Standard:* Met.
*   **C6-C7 Level Analysis:**
    *   *Imaging Findings:* MRI (September 1, 2024) reveals only a mild disc bulge with minimal foraminal narrowing, **no cord contact, and no identifiable nerve root compression**.
    *   *Clinical Correlation:* No correlating symptoms or physical exam deficits. No C7 dermatomal sensory loss, no triceps weakness, and no diminished triceps reflex are documented in the clinical record.
    *   *Surgical Standard:* Not met. The surgeon, Dr. James Wilson, MD, explicitly states the justification for fusing C6-C7 is "prophylactic" to stabilize the level and prevent adjacent segment disease (noting a 25% 5-year incidence).
*   **Action:** Under **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.3**, surgical authorization must be denied when imaging does not demonstrate correlating pathology at the proposed level. Prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic, non-compressive level is clinically unsupported. The C5-C6 level shows clear pathological and clinical correlation, while the C6-C7 level does not.

#### Step 4: Statutory and Regulatory Conservative Treatment Override Analysis
*   **Authority:** California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5; DMHC All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014
*   **Clinical Record Review (Conservative Trials completed for C5-C6):**
    1.  *Physical Therapy:* The patient completed **8 weeks of supervised physical therapy** across 24 total sessions, exceeding the plan's 6-week requirement (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a)).
    2.  *Pharmacotherapy:* The patient completed a **4-month course of Gabapentin 600mg TID** (partial relief) and a **6-week course of Cyclobenzaprine** (minimal benefit), satisfying the plan's requirement of trying at least two neuropathic/muscle relaxant agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(b)).
    3.  *Interventional Treatment:* The patient underwent a **right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 1, 2024**, which provided 50% temporary relief before symptoms fully returned by week 4. This satisfies the interventional requirement (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(c)).
*   **Action:** The member has fully satisfied the conservative treatment prerequisites under standard plan criteria for the **C5-C6 level**. No statutory/regulatory waiver is required to approve C5-C6 because compliance is complete. 

For the **C6-C7 level**, because there is no clinical radiculopathy or myelopathy localized to this level, no conservative therapy could be targeted to it, and no statutory exception (such as structural compromise under CIC § 10169.5(a)(3)) is active to justify bypassing clinical criteria for an asymptomatic level.

#### Step 5: Generally Accepted Clinical Standards Evaluation
*   **Authority:** California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard); AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines; NASS 2020 Guidelines
*   **Analysis:**
    *   *C5-C6 Level:* The patient's clinical presentation represents a **Tier 2 Surgical Indication** under the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) and NASS Guidelines (2020) (symptomatic radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits [4/5 grip strength, diminished reflex] that has failed to resolve after >6 weeks of active physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and epidural steroid injection). Denying surgery at C5-C6 is inconsistent with generally accepted clinical standards.
    *   *C6-C7 Level:* Fusing an adjacent, asymptomatic level solely to prevent adjacent segment disease (ASD) is considered **Tier 3 (Surgery Not Indicated)** under AANS/CNS (2023) and NASS (2020) guidelines. There is no high-quality clinical evidence showing that prophylactic fusion of mild, non-compressive degenerative discs reduces long-term disability or improves outcomes; rather, it unnecessarily increases surgical morbidity, blood loss, and operative time. Under the standard of clinical practice, adjacent segment disease risk does not justify multi-level fusion in the absence of corresponding focal pathology and clinical symptoms at the adjacent level.
*   **Action:** Pursuant to CHSC § 1374.32(b), generally accepted clinical standards mandate the approval of the clinically indicated **C5-C6 level** and the denial of the prophylactic **C6-C7 level**.

---

### FINAL CONCLUSION & RESOLUTION

Applying the established **Source Hierarchy of Authority**, the final determination is a **Partial Approval**:

1.  **C5-C6 ACDF (CPT 22551): APPROVED.** The member meets all diagnostic, imaging, and conservative treatment criteria outlined in **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2**. The clinical necessity is strongly supported by the **AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines** for persistent radiculopathy with correlating motor deficit (4/5 grip strength) failing conservative trials.
2.  **C6-C7 ACDF (CPT 22551 / 22552): DENIED.** The imaging shows only mild degenerative changes without nerve root or cord compression, and there is no correlating clinical symptom or neurological sign attributable to the C7 level. Prophylactic fusion to prevent adjacent segment disease is excluded under **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.3** and is unsupported by generally accepted standards of medical practice (**AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines**).

The plan's initial absolute denial of the entire two-level procedure is **OVERTURNED in part** to allow the clinically indicated single-level C5-C6 ACDF, and **UPHELD in part** to deny the C6-C7 level.
