## FINAL DISPOSITION

**The initial denial of authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) is OVERTURNED. This clinical service is hereby APPROVED.**

## EVALUATION & FINDINGS

### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Defect Check
Under **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)**, a denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied and provide the specific clinical reasons for the determination. 
*   **Finding:** The initial denial notice dated November 1, 2024, explicitly cited *CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2*, stated the clinical reason (insufficient physical therapy duration), and included standard Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights. 
*   **Conclusion:** The denial notice was procedurally adequate. The review must therefore proceed to a substantive clinical and factual audit.

### Step 2: Factual Audit of Conservative Treatment (Physical Therapy)
The initial denial claimed that the member completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy, failing to meet the plan's 6-week minimum requirement.
*   **Factual Audit:** 
    *   **Valley Physical Therapy:** Completed 3 weeks of supervised PT (9 sessions) from June 1, 2024, to June 21, 2024. Outcome: Partial improvement; symptoms persisted.
    *   **Capital Physical Therapy:** Completed 6 weeks of supervised PT (18 sessions) from July 8, 2024, to August 16, 2024. Outcome: Functional plateau.
    *   **Aggregate PT History:** **9 weeks total** across **27 sessions**.
*   **Conclusion:** The plan's factual claim under test ("3 weeks PT completed") is **demonstrably wrong**. The clinical records for both physical therapy providers were submitted in full with the original prior authorization request. The member completed 9 weeks of physical therapy, which exceeds the plan's 6-week standard conservative treatment requirement under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a).

### Step 3: Diagnostic Classification (Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy)
To determine the correct clinical pathway and evaluate potential medical urgency, the member's clinical presentation and imaging must be classified.
*   **Clinical Review:**
    *   **MRI (August 20, 2024):** Large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression and right foraminal stenosis. Crucially, the MRI report notes: *"No cord contact. No myelomalacia."*
    *   **Neurological Examination (August 25, 2024):** Unilateral findings consistent with C7 nerve root pathology, including right C7 dermatomal numbness, diminished right tricep reflex, and 4/5 right grip strength. There are *no bilateral findings* and *no upper motor neuron (UMN) signs* (e.g., no Hoffman sign, hyperreflexia, gait disturbance, or clonus).
*   **Conclusion:** The primary clinical diagnosis is **Cervical Radiculopathy** (C6-C7, right-sided, persistent). Because there is no spinal cord compression, cord signal change, or UMN findings, the diagnostic criteria for cervical myelopathy are not met. The review must proceed under the **Standard Radiculopathy Pathway**.

### Step 4: Plan Criteria Compliance Check (CC-SPINE-2024)
Applying the Standard Radiculopathy Pathway rules to the audited clinical record:
1.  **Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1(a)):** Confirmed right-sided C6-C7 cervical radiculopathy. Supported by MRI (August 20, 2024) showing structural pathology correlating directly with symptoms and deficits. **(MET)**
2.  **Conservative Treatment — Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)):** Minimum 6 weeks of supervised PT required. Audited records show **9 weeks completed**. **(MET)**
3.  **Conservative Treatment — Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)):** Trial of at least two agents for $\ge$ 4 weeks each required.
    *   *Agent 1:* Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (neuropathic). **(MET)**
    *   *Agent 2:* Naproxen 500mg BID for 3 months (NSAID). **(MET)**
4.  **Conservative Treatment — Interventional (Section 2.2(c)):** Minimum of one cervical epidural steroid injection or block required. 
    *   *Procedure:* Right C7 epidural steroid injection completed on September 15, 2024 (provided 35% temporary relief, returning to baseline within 3 weeks). **(MET)**
5.  **Imaging and Documentation (Sections 2.3 & 2.4):** Current MRI (within 6 months) demonstrates matching pathology at C6-C7. Dr. Okonkwo's surgical request included clinical rationale, functional limitations, and risk discussions. **(MET)**

*   **Conclusion:** The member meets 100% of the plan's standard clinical coverage criteria under Section 2.2. The initial reviewer's assertion that *"all other criteria remain unmet"* is medically and factually incorrect.

### Step 5: Application of State Regulatory Overrides
Under the **Hierarchy of Authority**, California state law overrides any more restrictive internal plan criteria:
$$\text{California State Law \& Regulations} > \text{Generally Accepted Clinical Standards} > \text{HealthFirst Plan Criteria}$$

*   **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2):** A health plan shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on further physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician/therapist documents that the member reached a **functional plateau**.
    *   *Clinical Evidence:* The Capital PT discharge summary explicitly documents: *"Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."*
    *   *Application:* This constitutes an unambiguous functional plateau as defined by **CIC § 10169.5(b)**. Mechanically denying surgery to demand further physical therapy trials is an explicit violation of California law.
*   *Note on Myelopathy Overrides:* Because the patient does not have myelomalacia or cord compression, the urgent overrides of **CIC § 10169.5(a)(3)** and **DMHC APL 22-014** do not apply in this specific case. However, the functional plateau statutory protection under § 10169.5(a)(2) is fully triggered.

### Step 6: Generally Accepted Clinical Standards Evaluation
Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)** (the statutory standard applied in Independent Medical Reviews), medical necessity must be evaluated using generally accepted standards of medical practice, which override restrictive internal plan policies.
*   **Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):** Surgical decompression and fusion (ACDF) is classified as a clinically appropriate and indicated intervention (Tier 2/Reasonable Indication) for patients with persistent, severe cervical radiculopathy who demonstrate objective neurological deficits (diminished reflexes, 4/5 motor weakness) and who have failed an exhaustive course of conservative therapy (minimum 6–12 weeks).
*   **Clinical Evidence:** Mr. Reyes has undergone **9 weeks of PT, 4 months of Gabapentin, 3 months of Naproxen, and a targeted epidural steroid injection** without sustained functional improvement. He has objective dermatomal sensory deficits and motor weakness (grip strength 4/5). Continuing conservative therapy in the presence of motor deficits and documented functional plateau is clinically contraindicated.
*   **Conclusion:** The requested C6-C7 ACDF is medically necessary and standard-of-care under national clinical guidelines.

### Step 7: Synthesis and Conflict Resolution
Applying the **Hierarchy of Authority**:
1.  **Plan Criteria:** Fully satisfied. The member met the 6-week PT requirement (9 weeks completed), the multi-agent pharmacotherapy trial, and the interventional injection requirement.
2.  **State Law:** Under **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)**, further conservative physical therapy requirements are legally barred due to the documented functional plateau at Capital PT. 
3.  **Clinical Standards:** Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)**, the clinical presentation and failure of comprehensive conservative care render the surgical procedure medically necessary.

There are no conflicts. All levels of authority—state law, generally accepted clinical guidelines, and the plan's own clinical coverage criteria—unanimously support the medical necessity of the requested C6-C7 ACDF.

---

## DECISION SUMMARY
The prior authorization denial was the result of an administrative and clinical oversight, specifically a failure to review the complete submitted medical record, which led to a miscalculation of the physical therapy weeks completed. 

Because the member has completed **9 weeks of physical therapy** (with a documented functional plateau), **multiple pharmacotherapy trials**, and a **cervical epidural steroid injection**, and presents with **correlating right C7 radicular signs and motor weakness**, the criteria for C6-C7 ACDF (CPT 22551) are fully satisfied.

**The initial denial is OVERTURNED. The requested surgery is APPROVED.**
