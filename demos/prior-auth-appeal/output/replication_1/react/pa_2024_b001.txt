**I. BACKGROUND OF APPEAL**

Linda Park's request for a two-level ACDF (C5-C6 and C6-C7) was initially denied by HealthFirst PPO Gold on 2024-10-28, citing lack of medical necessity for the two-level procedure, specifically for the C6-C7 level. The member appealed this decision, asserting that she meets plan criteria for at least the C5-C6 level, that the surgeon recommends both levels to prevent adjacent segment disease, and that conservative treatment (PT, pharmacotherapy, injection) has been completed.

**II. CLINICAL SUMMARY**

Linda Park is a 45-year-old female presenting with cervical radiculopathy.
*   **Presenting Diagnosis:** Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with adjacent C6-C7 degenerative disease.
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-01):**
    *   C5-C6: Large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis, correlating with C6 radicular symptoms.
    *   C6-C7: Mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no identifiable nerve root compression. Critically, no myelomalacia (cord signal change) was noted.
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-09-05):** Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, diminished right bicep reflex, and 4/5 grip strength on the right. No C7 findings, no bilateral symptoms, and no upper motor neuron signs were documented. This confirms C6 radiculopathy and rules out myelopathy.
*   **Conservative Treatment:**
    *   Physical Therapy: 8 weeks completed, with C6 radicular symptoms persisting. No C7 symptoms were ever documented.
    *   Pharmacotherapy: Gabapentin 600mg TID for 4 months (partial relief); Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks (minimal benefit).
    *   Interventional: Right C6 epidural steroid injection (2024-10-01) provided 50% temporary relief, fully returning by week 4.
*   **Surgeon's Rationale:** Dr. Wilson recommends two-level fusion to address the C5-C6 pathology and prophylactically stabilize C6-C7 to prevent adjacent segment disease.

**III. REGULATORY AND CLINICAL GUIDELINE REVIEW**

This review applies the three-source hierarchy: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.**

**A. Assessment of Primary Clinical Presentation:**
The clinical record unequivocally supports a diagnosis of **Cervical Radiculopathy at C5-C6**, specifically impacting the C6 nerve root. The neurological exam and MRI findings are consistent with nerve root compression at C5-C6. There is no evidence in the clinical record (e.g., upper motor neuron signs, bilateral symptoms, myelomalacia) to support a diagnosis of cervical myelopathy. Therefore, any plan criteria or state regulations specific to myelopathy (e.g., HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Exception 2.2(A); DMHC APL 22-014; CIC § 10169.5(a)(3) for myelomalacia) are not applicable to this case.

**B. Application of HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

1.  **C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. Member has confirmed cervical radiculopathy (2.1(a)) and cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit (2.1(c)) at C5-C6. (Clinical Record: MRI C5-C6 findings; Neurological Exam C6 findings).
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Met. The member completed 8 weeks of PT (exceeding the 6-week minimum, 2.2(a)), two pharmacotherapy agents for adequate duration (Gabapentin 4 months, Cyclobenzaprine 6 weeks, 2.2(b)), and one interventional treatment (C6 epidural, 2.2(c)). The persistence of symptoms after this comprehensive course indicates a functional plateau.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. Current MRI demonstrates structural pathology (large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression) at C5-C6 consistent with the member's C6 radicular symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** All plan criteria for ACDF at the C5-C6 level are met.

2.  **C6-C7 Level (Prophylactic):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Not met. The clinical record documents "No C7 findings documented" and "no nerve root compression identifiable" at C6-C7. There are no radicular or myelopathic symptoms attributable to this level.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Not met. Imaging shows only "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing" without correlation to symptoms. This falls under Exclusion 3.3.
    *   **Surgeon's Rationale:** The surgeon's rationale for addressing C6-C7 is "to prevent adjacent segment disease." This is a prophylactic indication and does not meet the plan's criteria for a diagnosis of active pathology or neurological deficit at that level.
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** The C6-C7 level does not meet HealthFirst's medical necessity criteria.

**C. Application of California Regulatory Framework:**

1.  **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):**
    *   For the C5-C6 level, the member completed 8 weeks of PT, and symptoms persisted. This satisfies subdivision (a)(2) "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms." Therefore, the PT requirement should not be a barrier to approval for C5-C6.

2.  **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):**
    *   This All-Plan Letter is specific to myelopathy and urgent neurological conditions (e.g., myelomalacia). As Linda Park does not have myelopathy, this APL does not apply to this case.

3.  **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard vs. Plan Criteria):**
    *   This section mandates that services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice SHALL be approved, regardless of more restrictive plan criteria.

**D. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

1.  **C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   The clinical record demonstrates persistent radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits and correlating imaging after a documented, adequate course of conservative treatment. This aligns with a TIER 2 "Reasonable Indication" for surgery per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023, Section 3.1, TIER 2(b): "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx").
    *   The duration of conservative treatment completed (8 weeks PT, pharmacotherapy, injection) is consistent with the evidence base for radiculopathy.

2.  **C6-C7 Level (Prophylactic):**
    *   For the C6-C7 level, the imaging findings show mild degenerative changes without neurological correlation or symptoms. The surgeon's rationale for preventing adjacent segment disease is not considered a primary, generally accepted indication for surgical intervention on an asymptomatic, non-pathological level.
    *   AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023, Section 3.1) lists "Imaging findings without correlating symptoms" under TIER 3 "Surgery Not Indicated." The IMR standard (CHSC 1374.32(a)) requires services to be for "preventing, evaluating, diagnosing, or treating an illness, injury, disease, or its symptoms." As the C6-C7 level has no current symptoms or active pathology requiring treatment, prophylactic fusion for adjacent segment disease prevention is not supported by generally accepted standards of medical practice.

**IV. CONFLICT RESOLUTION (State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria)**

*   **For the C5-C6 level:** All three sources (Plan Criteria, State Law, Clinical Standard) support the medical necessity of ACDF for this level. The conservative treatment requirements have been met, and the clinical presentation constitutes a reasonable surgical indication.
*   **For the C6-C7 level:** Both Plan Criteria (Section 3.3 Exclusion) and Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS TIER 3) indicate that surgery is not medically necessary for an asymptomatic level with mild degenerative changes, even for prophylactic reasons. State law provisions related to myelopathy do not apply, and there are no state laws overriding the determination for an asymptomatic, non-pathological level.

**V. DISPOSITION**

The original denial for a "Two-level ACDF" is **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED** and **PARTIALLY UPHELD**.

**Determination:**
*   **OVERTURNED for C5-C6:** The Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at level C5-C6 (single level) is determined to be medically necessary and is **APPROVED**.
*   **UPHELD for C6-C7:** The addition of the C6-C7 level to the ACDF procedure is determined to be not medically necessary and is **DENIED**.

**VI. WARRANT AND RATIONALE**

The denial of the two-level ACDF is partially overturned for the C5-C6 level and upheld for the C6-C7 level based on the following:

1.  **Medical Necessity for C5-C6 ACDF:** Linda Park presents with documented C5-C6 cervical radiculopathy, supported by objective neurological findings (right C6 numbness/tingling, diminished bicep reflex, 4/5 grip strength) and correlating MRI evidence of a large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression at C5-C6. She has completed an extensive course of conservative treatment, including 8 weeks of physical therapy, two pharmacotherapy agents for adequate durations (4+ months Gabapentin, 6 weeks Cyclobenzaprine), and an epidural steroid injection, all with persistent C6 radicular symptoms. This meets all specified criteria in HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.1(a), 2.2, and 2.3. Furthermore, the completion of conservative therapy with persistent symptoms satisfies California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2), which precludes denial based on conservative treatment requirements when a functional plateau has been reached. This presentation is also consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for surgical intervention in cases of radiculopathy refractory to conservative management (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, Section 3.1, TIER 2(b)).

2.  **Lack of Medical Necessity for C6-C7 ACDF:** The clinical record provides no objective evidence of C7 radiculopathy, myelopathy, or other active pathology at the C6-C7 level that correlates with symptoms. The neurological exam explicitly states "No C7 findings documented," and the MRI describes only a "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable" at C6-C7. This fails to meet HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.1 (diagnosis requirement) and 2.3 (imaging correlation with symptoms), triggering Exclusion 3.3. The surgeon's rationale for prophylactic stabilization of C6-C7 to prevent adjacent segment disease is not a generally accepted indication for surgical intervention on an asymptomatic and non-pathological spinal level. Under generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, Section 3.1, TIER 3(b)), surgery is not indicated for "Imaging findings without correlating symptoms." Furthermore, the IMR standard in California Health & Safety Code § 1374.32(a) requires services to treat an existing illness or symptoms, not solely for prophylactic stabilization of an asymptomatic level.

Therefore, the request for a two-level ACDF is approved for the medically necessary C5-C6 level only, and denied for the C6-C7 level.
