**REVIEWER FINDINGS AND DETERMINATION:**

This appeal for a two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 and C6-C7 for member Linda Park has been thoroughly reviewed against HealthFirst's clinical criteria, California state regulations, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice. The three-source hierarchy (State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria) was applied to resolve any conflicts.

**1. Clinical Presentation and Diagnosis:**
The clinical record indicates a primary diagnosis of **Cervical Radiculopathy affecting the C6 nerve root**, originating from the C5-C6 level. This is supported by:
*   **Symptoms:** Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling.
*   **Neurological Exam:** Diminished right bicep reflex, grip strength 4/5 right. **No C7 findings, no bilateral findings, and no upper motor neuron signs** were documented, definitively ruling out cervical myelopathy.
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-01):** C5-C6 shows "large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, moderate foraminal stenosis. Clear correlation with C6 radicular symptoms."
*   **C6-C7 Level:** Imaging shows "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable." No C7-related symptoms or neurological deficits are present in the record.

**2. Review Against HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. Member has confirmed cervical radiculopathy (2.1a) and cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit (2.1c) at C5-C6, consistent with C6 symptoms.
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Met. The member completed 8 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week minimum), underwent a trial of two pharmacotherapy agents (Gabapentin for 4 months, Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks), and received one interventional epidural steroid injection (right C6 epidural). Despite this, C6 radicular symptoms persist.
    *   **2.2(A) Myelopathy Exception:** Not applicable. The clinical record confirms radiculopathy, not myelopathy, as the primary diagnosis.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. MRI demonstrates structural pathology at C5-C6 (large disc herniation, moderate-severe nerve root compression) consistent with the member's C6 radicular symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** All HealthFirst plan criteria for cervical surgery are met for the C5-C6 level.

*   **C6-C7 Level (Asymptomatic Degenerative Disease):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Not met. There are no documented C7 radicular or myelopathic symptoms, nor objective neurological deficits attributable to C6-C7.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Not met. Imaging for C6-C7 shows "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable," which is not consistent with any clinical symptoms or deficits at this level.
    *   **3.3 Exclusions:** This level falls under exclusion 3.3 ("Imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with clinical presentation").
    *   **Surgeon's Rationale:** The surgeon's recommendation to address C6-C7 "prophylactically... to prevent adjacent segment disease" is not a criterion for medical necessity under HealthFirst's policy.
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** The C6-C7 level does not meet HealthFirst's medical necessity criteria.

**3. Review Against California Regulatory Framework:**

*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy Prerequisite):** The member completed 8 weeks of PT, and C6 radicular symptoms persist despite this and other conservative measures. This satisfies the intent of 10169.5(a)(2) that the member has reached a functional plateau without adequate symptom resolution, especially for radiculopathy where 6-12 weeks is a typical trial.
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy/Urgent Conditions):** This All-Plan Letter specifically addresses myelopathy with cord compression and myelomalacia, providing exceptions to conservative treatment requirements for such urgent conditions. As the member's diagnosis is radiculopathy and not myelopathy, this APL does not apply to this case.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** This states that services meeting "generally accepted standards of medical practice" SHALL be approved regardless of more restrictive plan criteria. We must assess if the requested two-level surgery aligns with these standards.

**4. Review Against Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   The member's presentation of cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits and persistent symptoms after an adequate trial of conservative treatment (8 weeks PT, pharmacotherapy, epidural) meets **TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION** for ACDF per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023 Update) and NASS Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020). These guidelines recommend surgery for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative therapy. The 8 weeks of PT, coupled with other modalities, demonstrates failure of conservative management.
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** Surgical intervention at C5-C6 is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice.

*   **C6-C7 Level (Asymptomatic Degenerative Disease):**
    *   The clinical record shows no correlation between the mild degenerative changes at C6-C7 and any documented symptoms or neurological deficits. The surgeon's rationale for prophylactic fusion to prevent adjacent segment disease is **not supported** by generally accepted clinical standards. AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023 Update) categorize "imaging findings without correlating symptoms" as **TIER 3 — SURGERY NOT INDICATED**. AO Spine Guidelines (2021) and NASS Guidelines (2020) do not support prophylactic fusion of asymptomatic levels.
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** Prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic level for prevention of adjacent segment disease is not considered medically necessary under generally accepted standards of medical practice.

**5. Conflict Resolution and Final Determination:**

*   **For the C5-C6 level:** All three sources (HealthFirst Plan Criteria, California State Law, and Clinical Standard) support the medical necessity of ACDF at C5-C6. The member has failed an adequate course of conservative treatment for symptomatic C6 radiculopathy clearly correlated to pathology at C5-C6.
*   **For the C6-C7 level:** All three sources (HealthFirst Plan Criteria, California State Law, and Clinical Standard) oppose the medical necessity of ACDF at C6-C7. There are no correlating symptoms or objective neurological findings, and prophylactic fusion for adjacent segment disease is not a generally accepted indication for surgery at an asymptomatic level.

Therefore, the original denial of a "two-level ACDF" should be partially overturned to approve the medically necessary level, and partially upheld for the non-medically necessary level.

**DISPOSITION: PARTIAL OVERTURN / PARTIAL UPHOLD**

**WARRANT:**
The denial for a **two-level ACDF (C5-C6 and C6-C7)** is **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED** for the C5-C6 level and **UPHELD** for the C6-C7 level.

1.  **Approval for ACDF C5-C6:** The member, Linda Park, presents with symptomatic right C6 cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological findings (right C6 dermatomal numbness/tingling, diminished right bicep reflex, 4/5 grip strength) clearly correlating to a large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression and moderate foraminal stenosis at C5-C6 on MRI. She has completed an extensive course of conservative treatment (8 weeks of physical therapy, 4 months of Gabapentin, 6 weeks of Cyclobenzaprine, and one C6 epidural injection) with persistent symptoms and only temporary relief from intervention. This presentation meets all of HealthFirst's own clinical criteria for cervical surgery (CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.1a, 2.1c, 2.2, 2.3) and aligns with generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice for ACDF for radiculopathy resistant to conservative treatment (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, NASS Guidelines 2020, Tier 2 indications). California Insurance Code § 10169.5 supports that conservative treatment requirements are met when a functional plateau has been reached with persistent symptoms.

2.  **Denial for ACDF C6-C7:** The clinical record provides no objective evidence of symptomatic nerve root compression or neurological deficit attributable to the C6-C7 level. The MRI shows only "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable," and the neurological exam documented no C7 findings. The surgeon's rationale for prophylactic fusion to prevent adjacent segment disease at an asymptomatic level is not considered medically necessary under HealthFirst's criteria (fails CC-SPINE-2024 Sections 2.1, 2.3, and falls under Exclusion 3.3). Furthermore, generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice do not support prophylactic surgical intervention for asymptomatic degenerative changes or to prevent adjacent segment disease (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, Tier 3; NASS Guidelines 2020; AO Spine Guidelines 2021). Thus, this portion of the requested procedure does not meet the standard for medical necessity per CHSC § 1374.32(b).

**ACTION:**
HealthFirst Health Plan is directed to authorize a **single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 (CPT 22551 for one level)** for member Linda Park. The request for ACDF at C6-C7 remains denied.
