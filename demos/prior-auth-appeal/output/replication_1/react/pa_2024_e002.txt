**Subject:** Appeal Determination for Prior Authorization Denial of CPT 22551 (ACDF C3-C4, C4-C5, C5-C6)

Dear Dr. Torres and Catherine Mills,

This letter serves as the HealthFirst Health Plan appeal determination regarding the prior authorization request for a three-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C3-C4, C4-C5, and C5-C6 (CPT 22551) for Catherine Mills. The initial prior authorization request was denied on November 25, 2024.

**Initial Denial Reason (Dr. Alan Foster, MD):**
"Three-level ACDF not approved. Review of imaging and clinical record supports surgical intervention at one level (C4-C5) only. C3-C4 and C5-C6 do not demonstrate pathology of sufficient severity to warrant surgical intervention at this time."

**Appeal Basis (Treating Surgeon):**
1.  All three levels are symptomatic.
2.  Treating surgeon has documented reasoning for three-level fusion.
3.  Conservative treatment completed for all levels.

**Clinical Summary:**
Catherine Mills, a 53-year-old female, presents with multi-level cervical spondylosis with myeloradiculopathy.

*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-10-01):**
    *   **C4-C5:** Large disc herniation, severe cord compression, T2 signal change (myelomalacia).
    *   **C5-C6:** Moderate disc herniation, moderate left C6 nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis.
    *   **C3-C4:** Mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing.
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-10-05):**
    *   **Myelopathy:** Bilateral hyperreflexia, bilateral Hoffman sign, wide-based gait.
    *   **Radiculopathy:** Bilateral C5 deltoid weakness (4/5), left C6 bicep weakness (4-/5), diminished left bicep reflex.
    *   **C3-C4:** Neck pain with C3-C4 palpation, but no specific radicular pattern documented.
*   **Conservative Treatment:**
    *   **Physical Therapy:** 9 weeks (27 sessions) completed. Myelopathic symptoms stable but not improved; C5-C6 radicular symptoms persist.
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Baclofen 10mg TID (3 months, minimal spasticity relief) and Pregabalin 300mg BID (4 months, partial pain relief).
    *   **Interventional:** Cervical epidural steroid injection (2024-10-20), with modest improvement in radicular symptoms but no change in myelopathy signs.

**Review Standards Applied:**
This appeal was reviewed applying the following hierarchy of authority:
1.  California State Law and Regulations (e.g., California Insurance Code (CIC), California Health & Safety Code (CHSC), DMHC All-Plan Letters).
2.  Generally Accepted Clinical Standards of Medical Practice (e.g., AANS/CNS Guidelines 2023, NASS Guidelines 2020, AO Spine Guidelines 2021).
3.  HealthFirst Health Plan Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024).

**Analysis and Findings:**

**I. Overall Clinical Presentation and Primary Diagnosis:**
The clinical record clearly indicates that Ms. Mills suffers from **cervical myelopathy** due to severe cord compression and myelomalacia at C4-C5, evidenced by significant upper motor neuron signs and gait disturbance. Concurrently, she also presents with **cervical radiculopathy** at C5-C6 with objective neurological deficit. This distinction is critical as myelopathy and radiculopathy have different treatment algorithms and urgency. Per HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(b), Ms. Mills meets the diagnostic criteria for cervical myelopathy, and it is the primary diagnosis based on the severity of findings.

**II. Conservative Treatment Requirements:**
*   **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2):** Ms. Mills completed 9 weeks of physical therapy, 3-4 months of two pharmacotherapy agents, and one interventional procedure.
    *   The plan's "Myelopathy Exception" (Section 2.2(A)) reduces PT to 4 weeks minimum. Ms. Mills' 9 weeks of PT clearly exceeds this.
    *   Pharmacotherapy requirement (4 weeks for 2 agents) was met.
    *   Interventional requirement was met, although it could have been waived if the physician documented risk.
    *   Therefore, the member technically meets the plan's conservative treatment requirements, including the myelopathy exception, for all components.
*   **California State Law and Clinical Standards Override:**
    *   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Section II.2):** For conditions with objective myelomalacia (as seen at C4-C5), plans SHALL NOT require full conservative treatment if conservative treatment has been attempted and failed, and surgery is indicated. Myelomalacia specifically supports waiver of standard conservative requirements.
    *   **CIC § 10169.5(a)(3):** A plan cannot condition approval on PT where "The condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address." Myelomalacia with severe cord compression at C4-C5 meets this criterion.
    *   **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2) & Clinical Standards (Section 5):** The physical therapy note indicates myelopathic symptoms are "stable but not improved" and radicular symptoms "persist." This demonstrates a functional plateau has been reached. Clinical guidelines (AO Spine 2021, AANS/CNS 2023) state that physical therapy does not produce meaningful functional improvement for moderate-to-severe myelopathy and delaying surgery to complete PT is not supported by evidence.
    *   **Conclusion:** Based on state law and clinical standards, the completion of standard conservative treatment requirements cannot be used as a basis to deny surgery for Ms. Mills' myelopathy and associated radiculopathy.

**III. Medical Necessity Assessment by Level:**

**A. C4-C5 Level:**
*   **Clinical Findings:** Severe cord compression, T2 signal change (myelomalacia), bilateral myelopathic signs (Hoffman, hyperreflexia, gait disturbance, bilateral C5 weakness).
*   **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):** Meets diagnosis (2.1b), imaging (2.3), and conservative treatment (2.2) criteria.
*   **Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):** Myelopathy with objective cord compression and myelomalacia is a TIER 1 STRONG surgical indication (Section 3.1).
*   **Initial Denial:** The plan acknowledged that "surgical intervention at one level (C4-C5) only" was supported.
*   **Conclusion:** Surgical intervention at C4-C5 is medically necessary and meets all applicable criteria, including the plan's own.

**B. C5-C6 Level:**
*   **Clinical Findings:** Moderate disc herniation, moderate left C6 nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis, left C6 bicep weakness (4-/5), diminished left bicep reflex. Radicular symptoms persist despite 9 weeks PT, 4 months pharmacotherapy, and an epidural.
*   **Initial Denial:** Denied for "not demonstrat[ing] pathology of sufficient severity."
*   **Conflict with Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):** For radiculopathy, surgery is a TIER 2 REASONABLE indication when there are persistent symptoms and significant functional impairment after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment (Section 3.1). Ms. Mills has objective weakness and persistent symptoms after 9 weeks of PT, extensive pharmacotherapy, and an injection, meeting these criteria. The plan's assessment of "insufficient severity" for this symptomatic level, especially in the context of a primary myelopathy and failed conservative care, is contrary to generally accepted standards of medical practice.
*   **Conflict with State Law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)):** The IMR standard requires approval if the service is in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice, regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive (Section II.3, Part I).
*   **Conclusion:** The denial for surgical intervention at C5-C6 is contrary to generally accepted clinical standards and California state law. This denial should be OVERTURNED.

**C. C3-C4 Level:**
*   **Clinical Findings:** Mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing. The neurological exam noted "unclear — neck pain with C3-C4 palpation but no specific radicular pattern."
*   **Initial Denial:** Denied for "not demonstrat[ing] pathology of sufficient severity."
*   **Consistency with Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023):** "Imaging findings without correlating symptoms" (Section 3.1, Tier 3) and "Axial neck pain without documented radiculopathy or myelopathy" (Section 3.1, Tier 3) are generally not indications for surgical intervention. The objective clinical record does not provide sufficient evidence of structural neurological compromise or radicular symptoms at C3-C4 that would warrant surgical intervention, especially compared to the clear pathology at other levels.
*   **Conclusion:** The denial for surgical intervention at C3-C4 is consistent with generally accepted clinical standards based on the provided objective medical record. This denial should be UPHELD.

**Disposition:**

Based on a comprehensive review of the submitted clinical documentation, HealthFirst Health Plan Clinical Coverage Criteria, California State Law, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice, the initial prior authorization denial for a three-level ACDF (C3-C4, C4-C5, C5-C6) is **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**.

**Warrant:**

1.  **C4-C5 (OVERTURNED in effect, as it was already conceded by the plan):** The request for surgery at C4-C5 is medically necessary due to severe cervical myelopathy with objective cord compression and myelomalacia, upper motor neuron signs, and bilateral C5 weakness. This meets HealthFirst's diagnostic criteria (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.1b), is a Tier 1 strong surgical indication under AANS/CNS 2023 guidelines (Section 3.1), and is supported by state mandates (DMHC APL 22-014, CIC § 10169.5) which waive or modify conservative treatment requirements for this urgent condition.
2.  **C5-C6 (OVERTURNED):** The denial for C5-C6 is overturned. Ms. Mills presents with moderate disc herniation, nerve root compression, and objective left C6 weakness, with symptoms persisting after a prolonged and failed course of conservative treatment (9 weeks PT, 4 months pharmacotherapy, epidural). This meets Tier 2 surgical indications for radiculopathy under AANS/CNS 2023 guidelines (Section 3.1). The plan's assessment of "insufficient severity" is contrary to generally accepted standards of medical practice, particularly when considering the patient's primary myelopathy and the overriding nature of state law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)) and clinical standards (DMHC APL 22-014, CIC § 10169.5) regarding conservative treatment for neurological compromise.
3.  **C3-C4 (UPHELD):** The denial for C3-C4 is upheld. The objective imaging findings (mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing) and neurological exam (unclear radicular pattern) for this level do not demonstrate sufficient severity or direct correlation with the patient's primary myelopathy or radicular symptoms to meet generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention (AANS/CNS 2023, Section 3.1, Tier 3).

**Decision:**

HealthFirst Health Plan will **approve an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) for two levels: C4-C5 and C5-C6 (CPT 22551 for two levels).** The requested C3-C4 level is not approved at this time.
