## PRIOR AUTHORIZATION APPEAL DETERMINATION LETTER

**Case ID:** PA-2024-E003
**Member:** George Harris (M-889922)
**Plan:** HealthFirst PPO Gold — Group Plan 77441
**Procedure:** CPT 22551 (Revision ACDF C5-C6)
**Date of Appeal Review:** 2024-11-06

**Appeal Disposition:** **OVERTURN**

**Warrant:**

This appeal concerns the denial of prior authorization for a revision Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 for Mr. George Harris. The initial denial stated that "Revision surgery not covered under standard ACDF criteria. Member had prior C5-C6 ACDF in 2021. Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level. Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure."

Upon a comprehensive review of the submitted clinical record, HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, California State Law, and generally accepted clinical standards, the denial is **OVERTURNED** for the following reasons:

1.  **Denial's Flawed Rationale and Misapplication of Plan Criteria:**
    *   The denial explicitly states that revision criteria include "hardware failure, **pseudarthrosis**, or new pathology."
    *   However, the denial then *only* cites a lack of "hardware failure" and fails to address the documented evidence of pseudarthrosis.
    *   The clinical record, specifically the **CT scan dated 2024-09-15**, unequivocally confirms "incomplete bridging bone formation. Lucency around hardware suggesting **pseudarthrosis**. Findings consistent with failed fusion/pseudarthrosis." (Clinical Record, Imaging: ct_1).
    *   Therefore, the member *does* meet one of the plan's *own stated criteria* for revision surgery (pseudarthrosis), rendering the initial denial's reasoning flawed and incomplete. This also raises concerns about the procedural adequacy of the denial notice under **CHSC § 1374.31(b)**, which requires the denial to specify the clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reason, and implies a full review of submitted documentation against those criteria.

2.  **Fulfillment of HealthFirst's Conservative Treatment Requirements (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   The member's primary clinical presentation is **cervical radiculopathy** (return of right C6 symptoms: bicep weakness 4/5 right, bicep reflex diminished right, C6 dermatomal numbness), not myelopathy (Clinical Record, Neurological Exam). Therefore, the standard conservative treatment pathway applies, not the myelopathy exception.
    *   **Physical Therapy:** The plan requires a minimum of 6 weeks. The member completed **8 weeks** of supervised physical therapy (Clinical Record, Conservative Treatment: physical_therapy_summary). This requirement is met.
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** The plan requires at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. The member completed **Naproxen 500mg BID for 4 months** and **Gabapentin 300mg TID for 3 months** (Clinical Record, Conservative Treatment: pharmacotherapy). This requirement is met.
    *   **Interventional Treatment:** The plan requires one interventional treatment. The member received a **cervical epidural steroid injection** (Clinical Record, Conservative Treatment: interventional). This requirement is met.
    *   All components of the plan's conservative treatment requirements (Section 2.2 of HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024) have been satisfied for a radiculopathy diagnosis.

3.  **Consistency with California State Law Regarding Physical Therapy:**
    *   Even if the duration of physical therapy were deemed insufficient by the plan (which it was not), **California Insurance Code § 10169.5** would override such a requirement. The treating physician documented that "PT cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure" (Clinical Record, Conservative Treatment: physical_therapy_summary) and noted "No improvement," indicating the member reached a functional plateau. This aligns with **CIC § 10169.5(a)(1) and (2)**, which states a plan cannot require PT completion where the physician documents it's unlikely to benefit or the member reached a functional plateau.

4.  **Adherence to Generally Accepted Clinical Standards:**
    *   The member presents with persistent cervical radiculopathy following a failed prior fusion, confirmed by imaging as pseudarthrosis.
    *   The member has undergone an adequate course of conservative treatment without meaningful relief, with symptoms described as "worse than preoperative baseline" (Clinical Record, Neurological Exam).
    *   Per **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) and NASS Guidelines (2020)**, surgical intervention is a **TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION** for "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" and "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care" (Clinical Evidence Base, Section 3.1).
    *   The proposed revision ACDF is medically necessary and consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for managing persistent radiculopathy secondary to pseudarthrosis following a failed fusion. **CHSC § 1374.32(b)** mandates approval if the service meets generally accepted standards, regardless of potentially more restrictive plan criteria.

**Conclusion:**

The initial denial failed to properly evaluate the submitted clinical evidence against its own criteria, specifically overlooking the definitive imaging confirmation of pseudarthrosis, which is an accepted indication for revision surgery under HealthFirst's implicit criteria. Furthermore, Mr. Harris has fully met all conservative treatment requirements as outlined in HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 for a radiculopathy diagnosis. His condition, characterized by persistent radiculopathy due to pseudarthrosis after a failed prior fusion and inadequate response to conservative care, meets the generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention.

Therefore, the prior authorization for CPT 22551 (Revision ACDF C5-C6) for Mr. George Harris is deemed medically necessary and the denial is **OVERTURNED**. Authorization for the requested procedure is granted.
