Dear Mr. Patel,

This letter is to inform you of the decision regarding your appeal for prior authorization of an anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at levels C5-C6 and C6-C7. Your request was initially denied on November 1, 2024, for MED-003, stating that the two-level fusion was not supported as the C6-C7 level showed only mild changes and did not meet surgical criteria.

We have conducted a comprehensive review of your appeal, considering the submitted clinical documentation, HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), relevant California state laws (California Insurance Code, California Health & Safety Code, DMHC All-Plan Letters), and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice (AANS/CNS, NASS, AO Spine guidelines). Our review applies the mandated hierarchy of authority: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.**

---

**1. Summary of Clinical Presentation and Original Denial:**

*   **Presenting Diagnosis:** Two-level cervical disc disease C5-C6 and C6-C7 with asymmetric severity.
*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-15):**
    *   **C5-C6:** Large disc herniation, moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis.
    *   **C6-C7:** Small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, *no nerve root compression identifiable, no cord contact*.
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-09-20):**
    *   **C6 Level:** Right bicep weakness 3+/5, bicep reflex absent right, right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling.
    *   **C7 Level:** Right tricep reflex slightly diminished. *No definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms*. Physician notes "mild C7 symptoms possibly present but not clearly documented."
*   **Conservative Treatment:**
    *   **Physical Therapy:** 8 weeks, 24 sessions. C6 symptoms persist; C7 symptoms noted as minimal and possibly resolving.
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Pregabalin (4 months), Naproxen (3 months).
    *   **Interventional:** Right C6 epidural (good C6 relief, returned in 4 weeks). *No C7-specific injection performed.*
*   **Original Denial Reason:** Two-level fusion not supported; C5-C6 meets criteria, but C6-C7 shows only mild changes and does not.

---

**2. Application of HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **C5-C6 Level (Radiculopathy):**
    *   **Diagnosis (Section 2.1):** The MRI findings of moderate-severe C6 nerve root compression and neurological exam findings of weakness, absent reflex, and dermatomal symptoms clearly meet criteria 2.1(a) (Cervical radiculopathy) and 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit).
    *   **Conservative Treatment (Section 2.2):** The member completed 8 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding 6-week minimum), trialed two pharmacotherapy agents for over 4 weeks each, and received a C6-specific epidural injection. These requirements are met for the C5-C6 level.
    *   **Imaging (Section 2.3):** Current MRI demonstrates pathology consistent with symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** This level *meets* HealthFirst's plan criteria for surgical intervention.

*   **C6-C7 Level (Mild Changes):**
    *   **Diagnosis (Section 2.1):** The MRI shows a "small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing," but explicitly states "no nerve root compression identifiable" and "no cord contact." The neurological exam reports "no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms" with only a "slightly diminished" right tricep reflex. These findings *do not* meet criteria 2.1(a) (Cervical radiculopathy requiring nerve root compression with radicular symptoms) or 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit), as there is no clear nerve root compression or definitive neurological deficit.
    *   **Conservative Treatment (Section 2.2):** While physical therapy and pharmacotherapy were completed, *no C7-specific interventional treatment* (e.g., C7 epidural or branch block) was performed. This fails to meet criterion 2.2(c) for the C6-C7 level.
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (Section 2.2(A)):** The clinical record does not indicate cervical myelopathy (no cord compression, myelomalacia, or upper motor neuron signs such as Hoffman's reflex or gait disturbance). Therefore, the myelopathy exception to conservative treatment requirements does not apply.
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** This level *does not meet* HealthFirst's plan criteria for surgical intervention.

---

**3. Application of California Regulatory Framework:**

*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** This law mandates approval if a service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice" regardless of more restrictive plan criteria. This is critical for evaluating clinical standard conflicts.
*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy Prerequisite):** The member completed 8 weeks of PT, and C6 symptoms persisted, indicating a functional plateau. The plan's PT requirement was met for C5-C6 and the law ensures PT completion is not a barrier when ineffective. This law does not apply to the C6-C7 denial, which was based on lack of pathology and specific conservative treatment for that level.
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):** This All-Plan Letter specifically addresses myelopathy with objective cord compression and myelomalacia. As the clinical record confirms *no cord contact* and no myelopathic signs, this APL's provisions for waiving conservative treatment requirements for urgent myelopathy cases *do not apply* to this member's presentation.
*   **CHSC § 1374.31(b) (Denial Notice Adequacy):** The denial notice specified the clinical reason for the denial (lack of support for C6-C7 fusion due to mild changes), which is procedurally adequate.

---

**4. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **Distinction: Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy (Section 1.1):** The member's presentation for C5-C6 is consistent with cervical radiculopathy. There is no evidence of cervical myelopathy. Therefore, guidelines specific to myelopathy (e.g., concerning surgical urgency and waiver of PT) are not applicable.
*   **Surgical Indications for ACDF (Section 3.1):**
    *   **C5-C6 Level:** The member's C6 radiculopathy with persistent symptoms (weakness 3+/5, absent reflex, numbness) after an adequate trial of conservative treatment (8 weeks PT, 2 pharmacotherapies, targeted epidural) meets **TIER 2 — Reasonable Indication (b) and (c)** for ACDF under AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023).
    *   **C6-C7 Level:** The MRI shows "small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, no nerve root compression identifiable, no cord contact." The neurological exam notes "no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms" and only a "slightly diminished" tricep reflex. These findings *do not* constitute a surgical indication under **TIER 1 or TIER 2** of the AANS/CNS guidelines. A "small disc bulge" and "mild foraminal narrowing" without objective nerve root compression or definitive, persistent radicular symptoms typically fall under **TIER 3 — Surgery Not Indicated** (imaging findings without correlating symptoms, or mild symptoms not meeting objective criteria). The argument for addressing adjacent levels simultaneously, while a general consideration, does not override the requirement for each proposed surgical level to meet objective clinical and imaging criteria for intervention.
*   **Conservative Treatment Duration (Section 2.2):** For radiculopathy, generally accepted guidelines support conservative treatment for 6-12 weeks. The member completed 8 weeks of PT and other conservative measures, which is consistent with clinical standards prior to surgical consideration.

---

**5. Conflict Resolution and Final Determination:**

The review indicates a clear disparity between the C5-C6 and C6-C7 levels.

*   **For the C5-C6 Level:** Both HealthFirst's plan criteria and generally accepted clinical standards support the medical necessity for surgical intervention due to documented radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits and failed conservative treatment. State law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)) would mandate approval if the clinical standard is met, which it is for this level.
*   **For the C6-C7 Level:** HealthFirst's plan criteria were not met due to lack of definitive diagnostic findings and incomplete conservative treatment (no C7-specific injection). More importantly, the objective clinical evidence (MRI findings of no nerve root compression or cord contact, and no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms) *does not meet generally accepted clinical standards* for surgical intervention. Since the service for the C6-C7 level does not meet generally accepted standards, CHSC § 1374.32(b) does not compel approval of this level.

Therefore, the request for a *two-level* fusion as submitted cannot be approved. However, the C5-C6 level clearly meets medical necessity.

**Disposition: PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**

The prior authorization denial for CPT code 22552 (ACDF C5-C6 AND C6-C7, two levels) is **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**.

*   Authorization for a **single-level ACDF at C5-C6 (CPT 22551)** is **APPROVED**.
*   The denial for the **C6-C7 level** is **UPHELD**.

**Warrant:**
The C5-C6 level meets HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 (Sections 2.1(a), 2.1(c), 2.2, 2.3) and is consistent with Tier 2 surgical indications for radiculopathy under AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, following an adequate trial of conservative treatment.

The C6-C7 level, however, does not meet HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria (Sections 2.1(a), 2.1(c) due to lack of identifiable nerve root compression or definitive neurological deficits, and Section 2.2(c) due to absence of C7-specific interventional treatment). Furthermore, the clinical evidence (MRI showing no identifiable nerve root compression/cord contact, and neurological exam noting no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms) does not support surgical intervention under generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS 2023 Tier 1 or 2 indications). The argument for addressing adjacent levels for prevention does not supersede the requirement for pathology and symptoms warranting surgery at the specific level. California state law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)) mandates approval if a service meets generally accepted standards, but in this instance, the C6-C7 level does not meet such standards, thus the denial for this level is consistent with both plan criteria and clinical guidelines. The DMHC APL 22-014 for myelopathy is not applicable.

A new authorization for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6 will be issued. Please contact your treating physician to discuss this determination and next steps.
