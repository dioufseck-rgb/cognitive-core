**I. BACKGROUND**

Mr. Victor Patel, a 60-year-old member, requested prior authorization for a two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 and C6-C7. The request was denied by HealthFirst PPO Gold, stating that while the C5-C6 level met surgical criteria, the C6-C7 level showed only mild changes and therefore a two-level fusion was not supported. The member's appeal asserts that both levels are symptomatic, adjacent levels should be addressed simultaneously, and the member meets full criteria for both levels.

---

**II. REVIEW FRAMEWORK**

This appeal determination is conducted in accordance with California regulatory requirements, HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice. The hierarchy of authority is: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.**

---

**III. FINDINGS OF REVIEW**

**A. Procedural Adequacy of Denial Notice (State Law Review)**

California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b) mandates that a denial notice must specify the *clinical criteria applied* and the *specific clinical reason* for the denial. The initial denial notice stated, "Two-level fusion not supported. C6-C7 shows mild changes only." While a clinical reason is provided, the notice **fails to specify which exact clinical criteria from HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 (e.g., Section 2.1 Diagnosis or 2.3 Imaging) were not met for the C6-C7 level.** This omission renders the denial notice procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b). Per DMHC APL 22-014, Part V, procedural defects are a common reason for overturning denials in California.

**B. Medical Necessity Review – C5-C6 Level**

1.  **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** MRI (2024-09-15) shows a large disc herniation with moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. Neurological exam (2024-09-20) reveals right bicep weakness (3+/5), absent right bicep reflex, and right C6 dermatomal numbness/tingling. This clearly meets criteria 2.1(a) (Cervical radiculopathy) and 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit).
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** The member completed 8 weeks of PT (exceeds 6-week minimum), trialed Pregabalin (4 months) and Naproxen (3 months) (exceeds 2 agents, 4 weeks each), and received a right C6 epidural steroid injection (meets 1 interventional). C6 symptoms persisted after PT and returned after injection. All conservative treatment requirements are met for C5-C6.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Current MRI (within 6 months) demonstrates structural pathology consistent with symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** The C5-C6 level meets all applicable HealthFirst medical necessity criteria. This is consistent with the initial denial's implicit acknowledgment that C5-C6 pathology supports surgical criteria.

2.  **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
    *   The member's presentation for C5-C6 (large disc herniation, moderate-severe nerve root compression, objective C6 neurological deficit, and failure of comprehensive conservative treatment) meets Tier 1 or Tier 2 surgical indications for ACDF, specifically "Progressive neurological deficit despite conservative treatment" or "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" (AANS/CNS Section 3.1).
    *   **Conclusion for C5-C6:** Surgical intervention at C5-C6 is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice.

**C. Medical Necessity Review – C6-C7 Level**

1.  **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** MRI (2024-09-15) shows a small disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing, but **no nerve root compression identifiable and no cord contact.** Neurological exam (2024-09-20) notes "right tricep reflex slightly diminished" but "No definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms." The physician notes "mild C7 symptoms possibly present but not clearly documented." These findings do not meet 2.1(a) (Cervical radiculopathy with nerve root compression and radicular symptoms) or 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit) for the C6-C7 level. There is no evidence of myelopathy (2.1(b)).
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** No C7-specific interventional injection was performed. PT notes "C7 symptoms: minimal and possibly resolving." This suggests conservative treatment was not fully directed at or demonstrably failed for the C6-C7 level, particularly 2.2(c).
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** The C6-C7 level does not meet HealthFirst medical necessity criteria, specifically Diagnosis Requirement 2.1 and Conservative Treatment Requirement 2.2(c).

2.  **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
    *   Clinical standards emphasize objective evidence of nerve root or cord compression *with correlating symptoms and deficits* for surgical indications (AANS/CNS Section 3.1). For C6-C7, the MRI shows only mild changes without identifiable nerve root compression or cord contact. The neurological exam lacks definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms, and a slightly diminished reflex alone is insufficient for a surgical indication.
    *   The appeal's argument for addressing adjacent levels simultaneously to prevent accelerated degeneration is not supported by clinical standards for *prophylactic* fusion of asymptomatic or mildly degenerative levels without objective pathology or correlating symptoms (AANS/CNS Section 3.1, Tier 3).
    *   **Conclusion for C6-C7:** Surgical intervention at C6-C7 is not supported by generally accepted standards of medical practice.

**D. Application of State Laws Regarding Conservative Treatment Waivers (CIC §10169.5, DMHC APL 22-014)**

Neither CIC §10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite) nor DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions) applies to override the plan's criteria for the C6-C7 level:
*   Mr. Patel does not have myelopathy or myelomalacia; therefore, APL 22-014 is not applicable.
*   The conditions for waiving PT under CIC §10169.5 (contraindicated, functional plateau for the condition, structural neurological compromise that PT cannot address) are not met for C6-C7, given the minimal and resolving nature of any C7 symptoms and lack of neurological compromise at that level.

---

**IV. CONFLICT RESOLUTION (State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria)**

1.  **Procedural Defect:** The initial denial notice is procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b) for failing to specify the clinical criteria applied. This alone provides grounds for overturn or remand.
2.  **C5-C6 Medical Necessity:** This level meets both HealthFirst's plan criteria and generally accepted clinical standards. There is no conflict; surgery at C5-C6 is medically necessary.
3.  **C6-C7 Medical Necessity:** This level fails to meet HealthFirst's plan criteria (2.1, 2.2c) and also fails to meet generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention. There is no conflict that would benefit the member for C6-C7; both sources indicate it is not medically necessary.

**V. DISPOSITION**

Based on the comprehensive review of the clinical record, HealthFirst's plan criteria, and relevant California state law and clinical standards, the denial of prior authorization for CPT 22552 (two-level ACDF) is **OVERTURNED with modification**.

**Warrant:**
The initial denial of the two-level ACDF (CPT 22552) is overturned primarily due to a **procedural defect** in the denial notice, which failed to specify the clinical criteria applied for the C6-C7 level, as required by California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b). This procedural defect alone is grounds for overturning the denial action.

Furthermore, a substantive review confirms that surgical intervention at the **C5-C6 level is medically necessary.** The clinical documentation demonstrates a large disc herniation with moderate-severe C6 nerve root compression and objective neurological deficits (right bicep weakness 3+/5, absent reflex, numbness/tingling), which meets HealthFirst's plan criteria (CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.1(a)/(c), 2.2, 2.3) and aligns with Tier 1/2 surgical indications for radiculopathy per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023). Comprehensive conservative treatment for C5-C6 symptoms has been completed and failed.

However, surgical intervention at the **C6-C7 level is not medically necessary.** The imaging findings (small disc bulge, mild foraminal narrowing, no identifiable nerve root compression, no cord contact) and clinical findings (slightly diminished tricep reflex, no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms, "minimal and possibly resolving" C7 symptoms per PT) do not meet HealthFirst's plan criteria (CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.1, 2.2c) nor generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention (AANS/CNS Section 3.1, Tier 3). Prophylactic fusion of an adjacent, non-symptomatic or mildly symptomatic level without clear pathology is not supported by clinical evidence.

Therefore, while the denial of the full two-level procedure (CPT 22552) is overturned due to procedural defect and the medical necessity of one level, the evidence supports authorization for a **single-level ACDF at C5-C6 (CPT 22551)**.
