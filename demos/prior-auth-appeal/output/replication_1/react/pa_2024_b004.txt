### **Initial Denial Summary**

The initial prior authorization request for an ACDF at C5-C6 was denied on 2024-11-10 by Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, with reason code MED-003. The stated reason for denial was: "Member meets conservative treatment requirements. However, surgical request is premature. Member's symptoms have been present for only 6 weeks. Plan criteria require at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration. Recent onset radiculopathy has a high rate of spontaneous resolution."

### **Appeal Basis**

The member's appeal highlights the following points:
*   Member is a professional musician, and right hand weakness is career-ending.
*   All conservative treatment was completed and failed within the 6-week period.
*   A functional plateau was reached at 6 weeks.
*   The physician recommends surgery given the documented functional impairment.

### **Clinical Presentation & Relevant Findings**

Mr. Kevin Watkins, a 41-year-old professional violinist, presents with acute right-sided cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6, manifesting as right C6 dermatomal numbness, diminished right bicep reflex, and 4/5 right grip strength. Crucially, fine motor testing confirms an inability to perform violin fingering tasks, with functional impairment documented by occupational therapy as career-ending.

**Imaging (MRI 2024-10-01):** Reveals a large disc herniation at C5-C6 with moderate right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. Importantly, there is **no cord contact and no myelomalacia** (T2 cord signal change). This clarifies the diagnosis as **radiculopathy**, not myelopathy.

**Conservative Treatment:** Over the past 6 weeks, the member has completed:
*   **Physical Therapy:** 6 weeks of intensive PT (18 sessions), with documented functional plateau and no improvement in fine motor deficits or radicular symptoms. OT confirms inability to perform professional duties.
*   **Pharmacotherapy:** 6 weeks of Methylprednisolone dose packs (x3) with temporary partial relief, and 5 weeks of Gabapentin (600mg TID) with partial pain relief but no motor improvement.
*   **Interventional:** One right C6 epidural steroid injection (2024-10-28), providing only 25% temporary relief with no motor improvement and a return to baseline within 2 weeks.

### **Appeal Determination: OVERTURN**

The denial for CPT 22551 (ACDF C5-C6) for Kevin Watkins is **OVERTURNED**.

---

### **Warrant and Rationale**

This determination is made based on the hierarchy of authority: **State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria**.

1.  **Clarification of Diagnosis: Radiculopathy vs. Myelopathy**
    *   The clinical record, specifically the MRI findings (no cord contact, no myelomalacia) and neurological exam (unilateral dermatomal symptoms, LMN signs only), definitively establishes the diagnosis as **cervical radiculopathy**, not cervical myelopathy. This is critical because the myelopathy-specific exceptions in plan criteria and state regulations (e.g., DMHC APL 22-014) regarding conservative treatment waivers are not directly applicable here.

2.  **Misapplication or Misstatement of Plan Criteria by Initial Reviewer (Plan Criteria Level)**
    *   The initial denial stated, "Plan criteria require at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration."
    *   Review of **HealthFirst Health Plan Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 (Section 2.2(a))** shows a requirement for "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy." It **does not explicitly state a 6-week *symptom duration* requirement**.
    *   The member *has* completed 6 weeks of intensive physical therapy (18 sessions) and also undergone trials of two pharmacotherapy agents for 5-6 weeks and one interventional procedure. These meet the plan's conservative treatment requirements as outlined in Section 2.2 of CC-SPINE-2024.
    *   Therefore, the initial denial reason appears to be based on a misstatement or misinterpretation of the plan's own published criteria.

3.  **Override by State Law (California Insurance Code § 10169.5) — Functional Plateau**
    *   California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) states that a health insurer cannot condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
    *   In this case, the member completed a 6-week course of intensive physical therapy, and the clinical record explicitly documents a "functional plateau" with "no improvement in fine motor deficits or radicular symptoms." Occupational therapy further confirms the inability to perform professional duties, signifying maximum benefit from conservative care for the primary functional deficit.
    *   Per **CIC § 10169.5(c)**, a plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy "shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau."
    *   Since a functional plateau has been clearly documented after a completed course of therapy, **state law overrides any mechanical application of a duration-based conservative treatment requirement** that the plan might impose, including the implied "6 weeks of symptoms" mentioned in the denial.

4.  **Override by Clinical Standard (CHSC 1374.32(b) and AANS/CNS Guidelines) — Significant Functional Impairment**
    *   California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) mandates that if a health care service meets "generally accepted standards of medical practice," it shall be approved regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive.
    *   The AANS/CNS Joint Section on Disorders of the Spine: Guidelines for Management of Degenerative Cervical Myelopathy (2023 Update) (which also covers radiculopathy) identifies **TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION** for surgery as:
        *   (b) Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative treatment.
        *   (c) Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care.
    *   Mr. Watkins' presentation squarely meets criterion (c). He has a documented "career-ending" right-hand weakness and inability to perform professional duties (violinist). This constitutes "significant functional impairment." Despite an intensive 6-week course of conservative treatment (PT, pharmacotherapy, epidural), he has reached a functional plateau with no improvement in his critical fine motor deficits. While 6 weeks is at the lower end of the 6-12 week general recommendation for radiculopathy (Tier 2(b)), the severity of the functional impairment (Tier 2(c)) and documented failure of comprehensive conservative treatment override the need for further delay in this specific case. The "high rate of spontaneous resolution" cited in the denial is a general statistical observation for *recent onset radiculopathy*, but in this individual case, spontaneous resolution did not occur despite a robust trial of conservative measures, and the condition remains career-ending.

### **Conclusion**

The initial denial by HealthFirst was based on a misapplication/misstatement of its own criteria regarding symptom duration. More importantly, it conflicts with California state law regarding functional plateau after conservative treatment (CIC § 10169.5) and fails to adequately consider the generally accepted clinical standards for surgery in cases of significant, career-ending functional impairment from radiculopathy that has failed comprehensive conservative care (AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines).

Given that the member has completed an intensive 6-week course of conservative treatment, has documented objective neurological deficits and severe functional impairment (career-ending for his profession), and has reached a functional plateau, further delaying surgical intervention is not supported by state law or generally accepted clinical practice.

**Action:** HealthFirst Health Plan is directed to **OVERTURN** the denial and authorize the requested ACDF C5-C6 (CPT 22551).
