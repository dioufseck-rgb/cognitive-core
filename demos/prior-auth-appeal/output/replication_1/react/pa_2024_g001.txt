**WARRANT**

This appeal reviews the denial of prior authorization for an ACDF at C6-C7 for member Marco Reyes, initially denied due to unmet conservative treatment requirements, specifically the duration of physical therapy (PT) and lack of documented functional plateau. Upon review of the submitted appeal basis, clinical record, HealthFirst's clinical coverage criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), California regulatory framework, and generally accepted clinical standards, the denial is **OVERTURNED**.

**1. Factual Error in Initial Denial:**
The initial denial stated, "Member has completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy. Functional plateau not documented." This claim is factually incorrect based on the submitted clinical record.
*   The clinical record explicitly documents **9 weeks of physical therapy** across two providers (3 weeks at Valley Physical Therapy from 2024-06-01 to 2024-06-21 and 6 weeks at Capital Physical Therapy from 2024-07-08 to 2024-08-16). Both sets of records were submitted with the original prior authorization request.
*   The discharge summary from Capital Physical Therapy explicitly documents a functional plateau: "'Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT.'" This directly contradicts the denial's claim that functional plateau was not documented.

**2. Compliance with HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
Upon correcting the factual errors, the member demonstrably meets all applicable criteria under HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement) for cervical radiculopathy:

*   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. Member has confirmed cervical radiculopathy C6-C7 with objective neurological deficit (right C7 dermatomal numbness, diminished tricep reflex, 4/5 grip strength) and MRI findings of C6-C7 large disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression. (Clinical Record; CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(a)).
*   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Met.
    *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** The member completed 9 weeks of supervised PT, exceeding the minimum 6-week requirement. A functional plateau was explicitly documented by the treating physical therapist, satisfying the plan's definition ("therapist documents maximum benefit reached"). (Clinical Record; CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a), Functional Plateau Note).
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** The member completed trials of Gabapentin for 4 months and Naproxen for 3 months, meeting the requirement of at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. (Clinical Record; CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(b)).
    *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** The member underwent a Right C7 epidural steroid injection, satisfying the requirement for one interventional treatment. (Clinical Record; CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(c)).
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (2.2A):** This exception is not applicable, as the clinical presentation (unilateral symptoms, no bilateral findings, no UMN signs, no myelomalacia, no cord contact) is consistent with cervical radiculopathy, not myelopathy. The plan's criteria explicitly state this exception applies only when myelopathy is the primary diagnosis. (Clinical Record; CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(A) NOTE).
*   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. MRI dated 2024-08-20 (within 6 months) demonstrates C6-C7 disc herniation and nerve root compression consistent with symptoms. (Clinical Record; CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.3).
*   **2.4 Physician Documentation:** The appeal basis indicates all criteria are met, implying this documentation has been provided.

**3. Compliance with California Regulatory Framework:**
*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This state law explicitly prohibits a health insurer from conditioning approval of surgery on PT completion when the member has completed PT and reached a functional plateau (CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)). The member completed 9 weeks of PT, and the therapist documented a functional plateau, directly meeting this state law provision. Furthermore, the plan's mechanical application of a minimum PT duration without reference to a documented functional plateau, as occurred in the initial denial, is contrary to CIC § 10169.5(c).
*   **DMHC APL 22-014:** While this APL primarily addresses myelopathy and myelomalacia, which are not present here, it generally reinforces that plans shall not apply internal criteria more restrictive than generally accepted standards. In this case, correcting the factual errors shows the plan's own criteria are met, which aligns with generally accepted standards.

**4. Compliance with Clinical Standard of Care (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
*   The clinical presentation is consistent with cervical radiculopathy. According to AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023), surgical intervention for radiculopathy is a "TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION" when symptoms persist after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment or when there is significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care (Source Document 3, Section 3.1). Marco Reyes has completed 9 weeks of conservative treatment, including PT, pharmacotherapy, and an epidural, with persistent symptoms and documented functional limitations (Grip strength 4/5). This aligns with accepted clinical standards.
*   The clinical standard defines a functional plateau as "Therapist documentation that patient has reached maximum therapeutic benefit," which was provided by Capital PT (Source Document 3, Section 5).

**Conclusion:**
The denial was based on a clear factual error regarding the duration and outcome of the member's physical therapy. Upon accurate assessment of the clinical record, the member has completed a comprehensive course of conservative treatment, including 9 weeks of physical therapy with a documented functional plateau, two appropriate pharmacotherapy agents, and an interventional procedure. All criteria within the HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 policy are met, and the denial of the myelopathy exception was appropriate as the member has radiculopathy, not myelopathy. Furthermore, the denial as issued is contrary to California Insurance Code § 10169.5, which mandates approval when a functional plateau has been reached. The requested ACDF procedure is medically necessary and meets generally accepted standards of medical practice for persistent cervical radiculopathy refractory to conservative management.
