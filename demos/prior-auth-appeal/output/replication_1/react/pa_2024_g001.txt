**I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

This appeal reviews the denial of prior authorization for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 for member Marco Reyes. The initial denial cited a failure to meet conservative treatment requirements, specifically alleging only 3 weeks of physical therapy (PT) completed and no documented functional plateau.

Upon review of the submitted clinical documentation and the appeal basis, it has been determined that the original denial was based on significant factual errors. The member has, in fact, completed 9 weeks of physical therapy across two providers, and a functional plateau was explicitly documented. All other conservative treatment requirements under HealthFirst's CC-SPINE-2024 criteria were also met.

Furthermore, California state law (CIC § 10169.5) explicitly prohibits conditioning surgical approval on PT completion when a functional plateau has been reached. The member's condition also meets generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention after an adequate trial of conservative therapy.

Therefore, the denial is **OVERTURNED**.

**V. APPLICATION OF CALIFORNIA REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS**

1.  **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 — Physical Therapy as Prerequisite:**
    *   This state law prohibits plans from conditioning surgical approval on PT completion where: "(2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
    *   **Finding:** The member completed 9 weeks of PT, and the Capital PT discharge summary explicitly documented "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT." This directly satisfies the definition of a functional plateau under CIC § 10169.5(b).
    *   **Conclusion:** Even if the 6-week minimum had not been met (which it was, by 3 weeks), the plan could not deny based on PT duration due to the documented functional plateau as per state law. This regulation further validates that the PT requirement is satisfied.
    *   **Citation:** CIC § 10169.5 (a)(2) and (c), Clinical record (Capital PT discharge summary).

2.  **DMHC All-Plan Letter APL 22-014 — Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions:**
    *   This APL primarily addresses myelopathy and conditions with objective imaging showing progressive pathology (e.g., myelomalacia) where standard conservative treatment requirements may be waived.
    *   **Finding:** Marco Reyes has radiculopathy, not myelopathy, and his MRI explicitly states no myelomalacia.
    *   **Conclusion:** This APL is not directly applicable to the specific urgency criteria, however, the principle that "Plans shall not apply internal criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted standards of medical practice" (II.3) remains relevant.
    *   **Citation:** DMHC APL 22-014.

3.  **CHSC § 1374.32(b) — Standard of Review for IMR:**
    *   This statute mandates that services meeting "generally accepted standards of medical practice" SHALL be approved, regardless of more restrictive plan internal criteria.
    *   **Conclusion:** This emphasizes the importance of evaluating against clinical standards, which is done below.
    *   **Citation:** CHSC § 1374.32(b).

**VI. APPLICATION OF CLINICAL STANDARD (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021)**

1.  **Diagnosis:** Confirmed cervical radiculopathy (Section 1.1 Clinical Reference Summary). The absence of myelomalacia and UMN signs confirms this as radiculopathy, distinguishing it from myelopathy (Section 1.2, 3.2).

2.  **Conservative Treatment Efficacy:** For radiculopathy, conservative treatment is typically effective (70-80% resolution). However, for *persistent* radiculopathy after a comprehensive conservative trial, surgery becomes indicated (Section 2.1, 3.1 Tier 2(b)). The clinical standard for functional plateau (less than 10% improvement over 3 sessions OR therapist documents maximum benefit reached, Section 5) has been met.

3.  **Surgical Indication:** Per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) Section 3.1, ACDF (CPT 22551) is indicated under TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION for:
    *   (b) Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx.
    *   (c) Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care.
    *   **Finding:** Marco Reyes has persistent right-sided C7 radiculopathy after 9 weeks of PT, 4 months of Gabapentin, 3 months of Naproxen, and a C7 epidural. His symptoms include numbness, diminished tricep reflex, and 4/5 grip strength on the right. This constitutes persistent symptoms and functional impairment not responding to an adequate conservative trial.
    *   **Conclusion:** Surgical intervention is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for this clinical presentation.
    *   **Citation:** AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) Section 3.1 TIER 2(b) and (c).

---

**VII. CONFLICT RESOLUTION AND DISPOSITION**

The hierarchy of authority is State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.

The plan's denial was based on a **fundamental factual error** regarding the duration of physical therapy and the documentation of a functional plateau.

*   **Factual Correction:** The member completed 9 weeks of PT and achieved a documented functional plateau, satisfying and exceeding HealthFirst's 6-week minimum PT requirement and the requirement for documented functional outcomes (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2a).
*   **State Law Override:** Even if the PT duration was disputed, California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2) explicitly states that plans cannot require PT completion when a functional plateau has been reached. This law supersedes any internal plan criteria regarding a minimum PT duration in such circumstances.
*   **Clinical Standard:** For persistent radiculopathy failing a comprehensive conservative trial (which Marco Reyes completed), surgery is a reasonable and medically accepted treatment option per AANS/CNS guidelines.

There is no conflict between state law, clinical standards, and the plan's criteria once the factual inaccuracies of the denial are corrected. All three sources support approval.

**DISPOSITION: OVERTURN DENIAL.**
