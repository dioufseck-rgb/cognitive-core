1. C5-C6 LEVEL (CPT 22551) — OVERTURNED (APPROVED)
The prior authorization denial for the C5-C6 level is OVERTURNED because the clinical record fully satisfies all medical necessity criteria under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024:
- Section 2.1 (Diagnosis): Confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits (3+/5 bicep weakness, absent bicep reflex, and C6 dermatomal numbness/tingling).
- Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment): 
  - Section 2.2(a): Completed 8 weeks of supervised physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week minimum).
  - Section 2.2(b): Completed trials of two compliant medications (Pregabalin for 4 months, Naproxen for 3 months), exceeding the 4-week minimum.
  - Section 2.2(c): Completed a right C6 epidural steroid injection on 2024-10-15.
- Section 2.3 (Imaging): MRI dated 2024-09-15 (within 6 months) demonstrates structural pathology (large disc herniation, moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression) directly correlating with the C6 radicular symptoms.
- Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation): Dr. Park provided the required clinical rationale and functional limitations.

Furthermore, under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32, surgical decompression at C5-C6 is clinically appropriate and in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice. The AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) classify this presentation as a Tier 2 indication (surgery reasonable/recommended for persistent radiculopathy with matching MRI pathology and failed conservative treatment). Therefore, the C5-C6 level is medically necessary and approved.

2. C6-C7 LEVEL (CPT 22552) — UPHELD / REMANDED (DENIED WITHOUT PREJUDICE)
The prior authorization denial for the C6-C7 level is UPHELD and REMANDED for further clinical correlation because the clinical record does not support the medical necessity of surgery at this level under CC-SPINE-2024:
- Section 2.1 (Diagnosis): There is no documented objective neurological deficit or definitive radicular symptoms in the C7 dermatomal distribution. The right tricep reflex is only slightly diminished, and the physical therapy note at week 8 indicates C7 symptoms are minimal and possibly resolving.
- Section 2.2(c) (Conservative Treatment): No interventional treatment (such as a C7-specific epidural or selective nerve root block) has been performed to target or diagnostically isolate the C7 level.
- Section 2.3 (Imaging): The MRI shows only a small disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing at C6-C7, with no identifiable nerve root compression or spinal cord contact. This does not demonstrate structural pathology consistent with active radiculopathy at this level.

Under generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines, 2023), surgical intervention at a level with mild imaging findings and equivocal, resolving clinical symptoms is classified as Tier 3 (surgery not indicated). Prophylactic fusion of adjacent levels to prevent accelerated degeneration is not supported by high-quality clinical evidence (NASS Guidelines, 2020). 

To establish medical necessity for the C6-C7 level, the treating physician should document clear, persistent C7 dermatomal symptoms and consider a diagnostic bilateral or selective epidural injection targeting both levels to confirm C7 symptomatic contribution before surgical intervention at this level is reconsidered.

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & PROCEDURAL DEFECTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(a)(2), health plans are required to provide the specific clinical criteria and clinical reasons for denying any requested service. The initial denial issued on November 1, 2024, by Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, applied a binary "all-or-nothing" denial to the entire two-level request because one level did not meet criteria ("Two-level fusion not approved when one level meets criteria and one does not").

This binary denial of a multi-level procedure where one level is clearly medically necessary constitutes a procedural defect under CHSC § 1374.31(b). Under California law, the plan is obligated to modify the request to approve the medically necessary portion (C5-C6) rather than denying the entire request. Under CHSC § 1374.32, the clinical standard of medical necessity governs, and the plan's internal criteria cannot be applied in a manner that restricts access to clearly indicated, medically necessary care. Accordingly, we have corrected this defect by approving the C5-C6 level and upholding/remanding the C6-C7 level.

Because a portion of your requested service (the C6-C7 level, CPT 22552) was not approved, you have the right to request an Independent Medical Review (IMR) from the California Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) under CHSC § 1374.30.

An IMR is an objective review conducted by independent, board-certified specialists who are not affiliated with HealthFirst. If the IMR is decided in your favor, HealthFirst is legally bound to accept the decision and authorize the service. There is no cost to you for an IMR.

You may apply for an IMR if:
1. Your provider has recommended the service as medically necessary, and
2. The service has been denied, modified, or delayed by the plan based on medical necessity, and
3. You have filed an appeal with the plan and the decision was not resolved to your satisfaction within 30 days (or you qualify for an expedited review).

To apply, please complete the IMR Application Form enclosed with this letter or apply online at the DMHC website (www.dmhc.ca.gov). For questions, you may contact the DMHC Help Center at 1-888-466-2219.
