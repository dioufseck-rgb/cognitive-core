Dear Ms. Morrison and Dr. Nguyen,

The HealthFirst Health Plan Appeals and Grievances Department has completed its review of the prior authorization appeal for the requested anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at the C3-C4 level (CPT 22551). Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical records, plan coverage criteria, California regulatory standards, and generally accepted clinical guidelines, the initial denial is OVERTURNED.

### I. CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE AND CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT ANALYSIS
Under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2, a member must complete a documented course of conservative treatment, including physical therapy (minimum 6 weeks), pharmacotherapy (trial of at least two agents for 4 weeks each), and at least one interventional treatment (such as an epidural steroid injection).

The chronological timeline of the member's interventional treatment is critical to this determination:
1. September 25, 2024: The member underwent a left C4 epidural steroid injection.
2. October 1, 2024: HealthFirst issued an initial denial of the ACDF request. At that time, the member was only six days post-injection and experiencing 60% temporary relief.
3. October 15, 2024: The member submitted this appeal, supported by clinical documentation from Dr. Nguyen showing that by week 3 post-injection (approximately October 16, 2024), the member's symptoms had fully returned to baseline.

Acknowledgment of Initial Decision Validity:
We explicitly acknowledge that the initial denial issued on October 1, 2024, was correct when made. On that date, the member was actively experiencing 60% relief from a very recent epidural injection, meaning conservative interventional treatment was actively succeeding. An appeal cannot retroactively invalidate a decision that was correct based on the clinical facts available on October 1, 2024.

However, as of the date of this appeal review (October 15, 2024), subsequent clinical developments have occurred. The documented return of symptoms to baseline by week 3 establishes that the interventional treatment has failed to provide sustained relief. Because the complete clinical picture as of the appeal date now demonstrates the definitive failure of conservative interventional treatment, the requirements of Section 2.2(c) are fully satisfied.

### II. SUBSTANTIVE CLINICAL CORRECTNESS AND IMAGING ANALYSIS (CHSC § 1374.32)
The initial denial stated that imaging did not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4, citing "mild degenerative changes" on the MRI dated August 15, 2024.

While the plan's denial notice was not procedurally defective under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)—as it did provide the specific criteria and clinical reasons applied—the denial is substantively incorrect under CHSC § 1374.32.

CHSC § 1374.32 mandates that medical necessity determinations must align with generally accepted standards of medical practice and prudent clinical judgment. The plan's internal criteria cannot be applied restrictively to deny care when clinical standards dictate otherwise:
1. MRI Limitations: The MRI report from August 15, 2024, explicitly noted "SIGNIFICANT ARTIFACT from C4-C5 hardware limiting evaluation" and stated that "moderate compression" could not be excluded. Relying solely on this compromised scan violates prudent clinical standards.
2. CT Myelogram Findings: To resolve the MRI hardware artifact, a CT myelogram was performed on September 10, 2024. This study confirmed "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4.
3. Clinical Correlation: The CT myelogram findings perfectly correlate with the member's neurological exam from August 20, 2024, which documented left C4 dermatomal numbness, deltoid weakness (3+/5), and a diminished left bicep reflex.

Under CHSC § 1374.32, the definitive CT myelogram must supersede the artifact-limited MRI. The objective imaging confirms severe pathology at the proposed surgical level, satisfying Section 2.3 of CC-SPINE-2024 and generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023).

### III. RESOLUTION OF SECTION 2.4 DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
Section 2.4 of CC-SPINE-2024 requires the treating physician to provide an attestation of conservative treatment completion, clinical rationale, description of functional limitations, and a statement of surgical risks/alternatives.

We have reviewed the clinical records submitted by Dr. Nguyen and find that they contain the necessary clinical rationale (adjacent segment disease at C3-C4 following a 2019 C4-C5 fusion, progressive left C4 radiculopathy, and objective motor weakness) and detailed functional limitations. Under CHSC § 1374.32, minor administrative omissions or the absence of a specific, standalone "attestation form" cannot be used to deny medically necessary care that otherwise meets all clinical and objective standards. The comprehensive clinical notes submitted serve as a substantive equivalent to the documentation requirements of Section 2.4.

### IV. FINAL DISPOSITION
The prior authorization denial for ACDF C3-C4 (CPT 22551) is OVERTURNED.
