Dear Ms. Dorothy James,

HealthFirst Health Plan has completed its review of the first-level appeal regarding the prior authorization request for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at the C6-C7 level (CPT code 22551) to be performed at UCLA Medical Center by Dr. Paul Adams, MD. 

After a comprehensive clinical and regulatory evaluation of the newly submitted medical records, HealthFirst has determined that the initial prior authorization denial issued on November 02, 2024, is OVERTURNED. The requested surgical procedure is approved.

I. BACKGROUND AND INITIAL DENIAL REASON
On November 02, 2024, HealthFirst initially denied prior authorization for CPT 22551 under denial code MED-003. The denial was based on the plan's determination that the conservative treatment requirement for physical therapy had not been fully verified. While the plan had received records from Valley Physical Therapy documenting 4 weeks of treatment, the records from a second provider, Mountain Physical Therapy, had not been received at the time of the initial review. Consequently, the plan could not verify the claimed 8-week total duration of physical therapy.

On November 16, 2024, an appeal was submitted on your behalf, which enclosed the complete treatment records from Mountain Physical Therapy. These records have now been integrated into your clinical file and reviewed in detail.

II. CLINICAL PRESENTATION AND FINDINGS
The clinical record establishes the following medical facts:
1. Presenting Diagnosis: Bilateral cervical radiculopathy at C6-C7.
2. Objective Imaging: A cervical spine MRI dated September 01, 2024, demonstrates a large disc herniation at C6-C7, resulting in bilateral C7 nerve root compression, bilateral foraminal stenosis, and mild spinal cord contact without compression.
3. Neurological Examination: A physical examination dated September 05, 2024, documents objective neurological deficits consistent with bilateral C7 dermatomal distribution, including bilateral tricep weakness graded at 4/5, diminished bilateral tricep reflexes, and bilateral finger tingling.
4. Conservative Treatment History:
   - Physical Therapy (Provider 1): Valley Physical Therapy from June 01, 2024, to June 28, 2024 (4 weeks, 12 sessions), resulting in moderate initial improvement followed by a documented functional plateau.
   - Physical Therapy (Provider 2): Mountain Physical Therapy from July 15, 2024, to August 09, 2024 (4 weeks, 12 sessions), resulting in no improvement in neurological symptoms and a documented functional plateau at discharge.
   - Pharmacotherapy: A 3-month trial of Pregabalin (150mg BID) yielding only partial relief, and a 2-month trial of Naproxen (500mg BID) yielding inadequate relief.
   - Interventional Treatment: A bilateral C7 epidural steroid injection performed on September 20, 2024, which provided temporary relief (50% on the left, 30% on the right) before symptoms returned to baseline.

III. ANALYSIS OF PLAN COVERAGE CRITERIA (CC-SPINE-2024)
Under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria Document CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2, authorization for cervical spinal surgery is approved when all of the following criteria are satisfied:

1. Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement): The member must have a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (nerve root compression with radicular symptoms) supported by an MRI within 6 months. This criterion is MET. Your MRI dated September 01, 2024, confirms a large C6-C7 disc herniation with bilateral C7 nerve root compression, and your neurological exam on September 05, 2024, confirms corresponding bilateral tricep weakness (4/5) and diminished reflexes.

2. Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement): The member must complete a documented course of conservative treatment consisting of:
   - (a) Physical Therapy: Minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes. This criterion is MET. With the submission of the Mountain Physical Therapy records, you have completed a total of 8 weeks of supervised physical therapy (24 total sessions) directed at the cervical spine. Although there was a 17-day gap in treatment between the two providers (June 28 to July 15), CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a) specifies a cumulative duration of 6 weeks and does not mandate uninterrupted continuity. 
   - (b) Pharmacotherapy: Trial of at least two specified agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. This criterion is MET. You completed a 3-month trial of Pregabalin (neuropathic agent) and a 2-month trial of Naproxen (NSAID).
   - (c) Interventional Treatment: Minimum of one cervical epidural steroid injection, medial branch block, or trigger point injection series. This criterion is MET. You underwent a bilateral C7 epidural steroid injection on September 20, 2024.

3. Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement): Current imaging within 6 months must demonstrate structural pathology consistent with symptoms. This criterion is MET. The MRI dated September 01, 2024, is within the 6-month window and demonstrates C6-C7 pathology correlating with your bilateral C7 radiculopathy.

4. Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation): Dr. Paul Adams, MD, has provided the necessary clinical rationale, description of functional limitations, and statement of surgical risks.

IV. ANALYSIS OF CALIFORNIA REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS
Your plan is governed by California law, which provides strong protections regarding prior authorization and conservative treatment requirements:

1. California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) & (c): Under California law, a health insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician or therapist documents that the member reached a "functional plateau" without adequate resolution of symptoms. Furthermore, a plan's internal requirement for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau. Both Valley Physical Therapy and Mountain Physical Therapy documented that you reached a functional plateau with persistent neurological deficits. Therefore, mechanically denying coverage based on a documentation gap or a minor gap in therapy continuity violates CIC § 10169.5.

2. California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32: Under the standard of review for Independent Medical Review (IMR), a service is considered medically necessary if it is in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice and is clinically appropriate in terms of type, frequency, and duration. The IMR standard explicitly states that plan criteria shall not be the sole or determinative basis for a decision if the service meets generally accepted clinical standards.

V. ANALYSIS OF CLINICAL EVIDENCE STANDARDS
According to the AANS/CNS Joint Section on Disorders of the Spine Guidelines for Management of Degenerative Cervical Conditions (2023) and the North American Spine Society (NASS) Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020), surgical decompression via ACDF is highly indicated (Tier 2 - Reasonable Indication) for patients presenting with cervical radiculopathy, objective neurological deficits (such as 4/5 motor weakness and diminished reflexes), and corresponding MRI evidence of nerve root compression who have failed to improve after 6 to 12 weeks of conservative management. 

Your clinical course demonstrates 8 weeks of physical therapy, multiple pharmacotherapy trials, and an epidural steroid injection without lasting relief. Forcing additional conservative treatment in the presence of objective, progressive neurological deficits (tricep weakness) is not supported by clinical evidence and poses a risk of prolonged neurological impairment.

VI. CONCLUSION AND AUTHORIZATION DETAILS
Because the newly submitted Mountain Physical Therapy records resolve the documentation gap from the initial review, you have fully satisfied the clinical criteria under HealthFirst Policy CC-SPINE-2024, California Insurance Code § 10169.5, and generally accepted clinical guidelines. 

Accordingly, the prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED.
