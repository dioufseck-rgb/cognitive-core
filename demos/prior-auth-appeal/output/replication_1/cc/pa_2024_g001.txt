Dear Mr. Reyes and Dr. Okonkwo,

HealthFirst Health Plan has completed its clinical and regulatory review of the first-level appeal submitted on November 14, 2024, regarding the prior authorization request for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551). 

After a comprehensive review of the complete clinical record, the plan's clinical coverage criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), California statutory requirements, and generally accepted clinical standards, HealthFirst has determined that the initial prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED. The requested surgical procedure is approved.

I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BASIS FOR OVERTURN
The initial denial issued on November 1, 2024, by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, was based on a factual error regarding the member's conservative treatment history. The denial asserted that the member completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy (PT) and failed to document a functional plateau, thereby failing to meet the 6-week minimum requirement under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2.

A review of the full clinical record submitted with the original prior authorization request reveals that the member completed a total of 9 weeks of supervised physical therapy across two separate providers:
1. Valley Physical Therapy: 3 weeks (June 1, 2024, to June 21, 2024; 9 sessions)
2. Capital Physical Therapy: 6 weeks (July 8, 2024, to August 16, 2024; 18 sessions)

The discharge summary from Capital Physical Therapy explicitly documents that a functional plateau was reached, stating: "Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."

Because the member completed 9 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week requirement) with a documented functional plateau, and has satisfied all other clinical, imaging, and conservative treatment criteria, the initial denial was factually incorrect and is hereby overturned.

II. CLINICAL PRESENTATION & HISTORY
Mr. Reyes is a 49-year-old male with a history of persistent, right-sided cervical radiculopathy at C6-C7. 
- Clinical Exam (August 25, 2024): Demonstrates objective neurological deficits, including right C7 dermatomal numbness, a diminished right tricep reflex, and right-sided grip strength of 4/5. No upper motor neuron (UMN) signs or bilateral findings are present.
- Imaging (MRI, August 20, 2024): Confirms a large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression and right foraminal stenosis.
- Conservative Treatment:
  - Physical Therapy: 9 weeks total (27 sessions) completed between June 1, 2024, and August 16, 2024, with a documented functional plateau.
  - Pharmacotherapy: Completed a 4-month trial of Gabapentin (600mg TID) and a 3-month trial of Naproxen (500mg BID) with persistent radicular symptoms.
  - Interventional Treatment: Received a right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 15, 2024, which provided only 35% temporary relief before symptoms returned to baseline within 3 weeks.

III. EVALUATION OF PLAN CRITERIA (CC-SPINE-2024)
Under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2, authorization for cervical spinal surgery is approved when all of the following criteria are satisfied:

1. Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement): SATISFIED. The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with nerve root compression and corresponding radicular symptoms (pain, numbness, and weakness in a dermatomal distribution), supported by an MRI within 6 months demonstrating structural pathology consistent with symptoms.
2. Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement): SATISFIED.
   - (a) Physical Therapy: The member completed 9 weeks of supervised physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week minimum) directed at the cervical spine, with documented functional outcomes and a documented functional plateau.
   - (b) Pharmacotherapy: The member completed trials of two qualifying agents (Gabapentin, a neuropathic agent, for 4 months; Naproxen, an NSAID, for 3 months), satisfying the requirement of at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
   - (c) Interventional Treatment: The member completed a right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 15, 2024, satisfying the requirement of at least one interventional treatment.
3. Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement): SATISFIED. The MRI dated August 20, 2024 (within 6 months of the request) demonstrates a large C6-C7 disc herniation with moderate right C7 nerve root compression, directly correlating with the member's right-sided C7 radicular symptoms and neurological exam findings.
4. Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation): SATISFIED. Dr. Sarah Okonkwo provided the necessary clinical documentation, including an attestation of completed conservative treatment, clinical rationale for surgery, description of functional limitations, and discussion of risks/alternatives.

IV. CALIFORNIA REGULATORY COMPLIANCE ANALYSIS
The initial denial also violated key provisions of the California regulatory framework:
1. California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5: Under subdivision (a)(2), a health insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician or therapist documents that the member reached a functional plateau. Furthermore, subdivision (c) prohibits the mechanical application of a minimum week count without reference to whether a functional plateau has been achieved. By ignoring the Capital Physical Therapy records and the documented plateau, and mechanically applying a 6-week rule to deny coverage, the plan violated CIC § 10169.5.
2. California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b): A denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reasons for the denial. Because the initial denial notice relied on a false factual claim (asserting only 3 weeks of PT were completed when 9 weeks were documented in records submitted with the original request), the denial notice was factually defective.

V. CLINICAL EVIDENCE AND GUIDELINES ALIGNMENT
The requested ACDF at C6-C7 aligns fully with generally accepted standards of medical practice:
1. AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) & NASS Guidelines (2020): These guidelines establish that surgical decompression (ACDF) is a Tier 2 (Reasonable Indication) intervention for patients with cervical radiculopathy who have persistent, objective neurological deficits and radicular symptoms that have failed to respond to an adequate course of conservative management (typically 6 to 12 weeks of physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, and interventional injections). 
2. Functional Plateau Definition: Under clinical standards, a functional plateau is reached when a patient achieves maximum therapeutic benefit and no further improvement is expected. The Capital Physical Therapy discharge summary explicitly documented this status. Continuing to mandate physical therapy beyond a documented functional plateau is clinically inappropriate and unsupported by medical evidence.

VI. CONCLUSION & AUTHORIZATION DETAILS
Based on the clinical evidence, plan criteria, and California law, the prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED.
