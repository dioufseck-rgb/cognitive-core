## I. FINAL DETERMINATION
Pursuant to the California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.30 et seq., and following a comprehensive clinical and administrative review of the medical record, the initial determination by HealthFirst Utilization Management is hereby **UPHELD**. 

The request for prior authorization of CPT Code 22551 (ACDF C5-C6) does not meet the necessary clinical criteria under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Policy CC-SPINE-2024, nor does it qualify for statutory overrides under California state law (CIC § 10169.5, DMHC APL 22-014) or generally accepted medical standards (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, AO Spine Guidelines 2021). The member’s condition represents mild, stable cervical myelopathy without evidence of myelomalacia or acute progressive clinical deterioration, for which a supervised course of conservative therapy remains the clinically indicated primary management pathway.

## II. CASE HISTORY & CLINICAL PRESENTATION
Mr. Bernard Achebe is a 61-year-old male with a 6-month history of stable, mild cervical myelopathy at the C5-C6 level. 
* **Clinical Symptoms:** Mild bilateral spasticity and hand clumsiness. Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) remain fully intact and independent. No gait instability (tandem gait is fully intact).
* **Neurological Examination (2024-10-05):** Bilateral Hoffman signs are present; mild hyperreflexia bilaterally. Hand grip strength is graded at 4+/5 bilaterally, with no frank motor weakness or dermatomal sensory deficits. Modified Japanese Orthopaedic Association (mJOA) score is calculated at **15 out of 18**, confirming **mild myelopathy** (scores 15–17 indicate the mild category).
* **Imaging (MRI dated 2024-10-01):** Moderate central cord compression at C5-C6. Canal diameter is measured at 8mm. Cord contact is present without intrinsic cord compression. Critically, there is **no T2-weighted signal change and no myelomalacia**.
* **Conservative Treatment Completed:**
  1. *Physical Therapy:* 3 weeks completed (9 sessions total), halted prematurely by Dr. Osei.
  2. *Pharmacotherapy:* 1 agent (Baclofen 5mg BID for 6 weeks), which yielded mild spasticity improvement.
  3. *Interventional Treatment:* None.
* **Treating Provider Position:** Dr. Osei's appeal letter (dated 2024-11-01) declares "surgical urgency" and asserts that further delay is medically contraindicated, warning of "irreversible neurological deterioration."

## III. EVIDENCE-BASED EVALUATION & FINDINGS

### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy Review (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
The initial denial notice issued by Dr. Alan Foster on November 5, 2024, was audited for compliance with California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b). 
* **Findings:** The denial notice explicitly cited the clinical criteria applied (HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024), provided a detailed clinical explanation for the determination (noting that the member completed only 3 of the 6 required weeks of physical therapy, trialed only 1 pharmacotherapy agent, and had no interventional procedures), and enclosed the mandatory Independent Medical Review (IMR) disclosure and application forms.
* **Determination:** **Procedurally Sound**. The review proceeds to Clinical Evaluation.

### Step 2: Clinical Classification & Diagnostic Verification
To determine the appropriate review pathway, the clinical record was analyzed to differentiate between cervical radiculopathy (nerve root compression) and cervical myelopathy (spinal cord compression).
* **Findings:** The medical record documents bilateral hand clumsiness, mild bilateral spasticity, and objective upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (bilateral Hoffman signs and mild hyperreflexia). MRI findings confirm moderate central cord compression at C5-C6 with an 8mm canal diameter.
* **Determination:** **Cervical Myelopathy Confirmed** (primary diagnosis). The review proceeds to the Step 3 State Regulatory and Clinical Override evaluation.

### Step 3: State Regulatory Compliance & Override Evaluation (CIC § 10169.5 & DMHC APL 22-014)
Under the Hierarchy of Authority, California state law and Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) regulations supersede internal health plan criteria. 

1. **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy Prerequisites):**
   * *Statute:* Plans cannot condition surgical approval on physical therapy completion if the condition involves "objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address" (§ 10169.5(a)(3)).
   * *Clinical Application:* Under generally accepted spinal standards, structural compromise that PT cannot address is represented by cord signal changes (myelomalacia) or severe cord compression with progressive functional decline. Mr. Achebe's MRI explicitly notes "No T2 signal change" and "No myelomalacia." His symptoms are stable (6-month duration), and he remains fully independent. No structural compromise meets the statutory definition to trigger a § 10169.5(a)(3) waiver.
   
2. **DMHC All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014 (Time-Sensitive Neurological Conditions):**
   * *Statute:* Plans must waive conservative treatment requirements when objective imaging shows progressive pathology (such as myelomalacia or progressive herniation on serial imaging) AND the physician documents conservative failure.
   * *Clinical Application:* The member has a single MRI (no serial imaging demonstrating progression), which is negative for myelomalacia. The physical therapy notes indicate that the member’s mild symptoms were "stable," and therapy was not halted due to clinical deterioration or failure to benefit, but rather by Dr. Osei's clinical apprehension. Objective exam parameters (mJOA of 15, intact tandem gait, independent ADLs) confirm a stable clinical state. Dr. Osei’s subjective assertions of "surgical urgency" are in direct conflict with his own objective clinical examination notes.

* **Determination:** **No Legal Override Triggered**. The clinical and imaging findings do not satisfy the requirements of CIC § 10169.5 or DMHC APL 22-014. The review proceeds to Step 4.

### Step 4: Plan Criteria Evaluation — Myelopathy Exception Pathway (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2A)
HealthFirst clinical coverage criteria CC-SPINE-2024 contains a modified "Myelopathy Pathway" exception that lowers the threshold for surgical authorization when myelopathy is the primary diagnosis.
* **Criterion (a) Myelopathy as Primary Diagnosis:** **MET** (confirmed in Step 2).
* **Criterion (b) Physical Therapy (Reduced to 4 weeks minimum):** **NOT MET**. The member completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy (9 sessions). 
  * *Functional Plateau Review:* Section 5 of CC-SPINE-2024 allows a PT waiver if a "functional plateau" is documented (defined as <10% improvement over 3 sessions or therapist attestation of maximum therapeutic benefit). No such documentation exists; the physical therapist noted "mild symptoms stable" and recorded that therapy was halted solely on Dr. Osei's directive.
* **Criterion (c) Pharmacotherapy (Trial of two agents for 4+ weeks each):** **NOT MET**. The member trialed only one agent (Baclofen 5mg BID for 6 weeks). No second agent (such as an NSAID, oral corticosteroid, or gabapentinoid) was trialed.
* **Criterion (d) Interventional Treatment (Waived via documented risk):** **MET**. Dr. Osei's clinical narrative sufficiently documented the potential risk of interventional procedures (e.g., epidural injections) in the setting of cord contact.

* **Determination:** **Myelopathy Exception Not Met**. Because the member failed to complete the modified 4-week physical therapy requirement and failed to trial a second pharmacotherapy agent, the request fails the plan's internal medical necessity criteria. The review proceeds to Step 6 to resolve conflicts with clinical practice guidelines.

### Step 6: Clinical Standard Evaluation (CHSC § 1374.32(b) & AANS/CNS 2023 / AO Spine 2021)
Under CHSC § 1374.32(b), the health plan must verify if generally accepted clinical standards support immediate surgery for this presentation, which would override the plan's more restrictive criteria.

1. **AANS/CNS Joint Section Guidelines (2023 Update):**
   * *Tier 1 (Strong Surgical Indication — PT Waived):* Indicated for "moderate to severe myelopathy with objective cord compression on MRI" or "myelomalacia on MRI (cord signal change)." 
   * *Tier 2 (Reasonable Option):* Indicated for "mild myelopathy with stable symptoms after an adequate conservative trial."
   * *Application:* With an mJOA of 15 (mild category) and no myelomalacia on MRI, Mr. Achebe's presentation falls squarely into **Tier 2**. The clinical standard does *not* support bypassing conservative trials for mild, stable disease.

2. **AO Spine Clinical Practice Guidelines (2021):**
   * *Guideline:* The AO Spine Guidelines state that "conservative treatment may be considered for mild cervical myelopathy as initial management, with close monitoring for progression." While AO Spine notes that mandatory physical therapy has no high-quality evidence base for *moderate-to-severe* myelopathy, it affirms that conservative management is appropriate and standard for mild, stable myelopathy.

3. **North American Spine Society (NASS) Guidelines (2020):**
   * *Grade B Recommendation:* "Conservative treatment may be considered for mild cervical myelopathy as initial management, with close monitoring for progression."

* **Determination:** **Clinical Standard Not Met**. There is no medical consensus or clinical guideline supporting immediate surgical decompression for mild (mJOA 15), stable cervical myelopathy in the absence of myelomalacia, functional ADL deficits, or progressive gait impairment. A conservative treatment trial is medically appropriate and clinically indicated.

* **Summary of Findings:**
  1. **The Initial Denial was Procedurally Sound** under CHSC § 1374.31(b).
  2. **State Regulatory Overrides are Inapplicable** under CIC § 10169.5 and DMHC APL 22-014, as there is no objective imaging evidence of myelomalacia or progressive compromise to legally excuse the conservative treatment requirements.
  3. **Plan Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2A) are Unmet**, as the member completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy (without a documented functional plateau) and trialed only 1 pharmacotherapy agent.
  4. **Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023, AO Spine 2021)** endorse initial conservative management for mild, stable myelopathy (mJOA 15) and do not support immediate surgical intervention.

To proceed toward surgical authorization, the clinical record must document either:
1. Completion of the remaining conservative therapy requirements under CC-SPINE-2024 (at least 1 additional week of physical therapy to reach the 4-week myelopathy pathway threshold, and a 4-week trial of a second pharmacotherapy agent, such as a gabapentinoid or NSAID); OR
2. Objective, documented clinical or imaging progression (such as a drop in mJOA score, new gait impairment, or the appearance of myelomalacia on repeat MRI) that would clinically elevate this case to a Tier 1 surgical indication or trigger a statutory regulatory waiver.

The initial denial of the prior authorization request for **CPT Code 22551 (ACDF C5-C6)** is **UPHELD**.
