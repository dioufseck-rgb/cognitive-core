## II. CLINICAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE SUMMARY

On October 20, 2024, the plan issued a medical necessity denial for a requested single-level C6-C7 ACDF. The denial (Reason Code: MED-003) cited a failure to meet the plan’s clinical coverage criteria, specifically stating that the member's MRI showed only mild cord contact without measurable compression, that no myelopathy was present on physical examination, and that physical therapy (PT) had not yet been completed to a documented "functional plateau."

The member, through Dr. Karen Liu, appealed this decision on the basis that the member has a confirmed diagnosis of persistent, symptomatic C6-C7 cervical radiculopathy that has failed to improve despite exhaustive conservative treatment, including 7 weeks of supervised physical therapy (which reached a documented functional plateau), 4 months of dual-agent pharmacotherapy, and a C7 selective epidural steroid injection.

---

## III. STEP-BY-STEP EVALUATION & ANALYSIS

Pursuant to the California statutory hierarchy of authority, this review evaluates the clinical and administrative evidence by applying the following framework:
$$\text{California State Law (CIC/CHSC/DMHC APL)} > \text{Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS, AO Spine)} > \text{Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)}$$

### STEP 1: Procedural Adequacy and Defect Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Review of the initial denial notice dated October 20, 2024, issued by Dr. Patricia Huang.
*   **Finding:** The denial notice specified the exact internal clinical criteria applied (*HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024*), cited the specific clinical reasons for the denial (lack of measurable compression/myelopathy, incomplete PT), and enclosed the mandatory state-required Independent Medical Review (IMR) notification documents. 
*   **Conclusion:** The denial notice is procedurally valid under **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)**. *Proceed to Step 2.*

### STEP 2: Clinical Diagnosis Classification & Objective Evidence Verification
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Audit of the submitted clinical records and Stanford Medical Center imaging to classify the underlying pathology.
*   **Evidence Review:**
    *   *Imaging (MRI dated August 1, 2024):* Demonstrates a C6-C7 disc herniation with mild cord contact, *no* measurable spinal cord compression, *no* T2 cord signal changes, *no* myelomalacia, and mild right C7 foraminal stenosis.
    *   *Neurological Exam (dated August 5, 2024):* Shows right C7 dermatomal distribution numbness, a diminished right triceps reflex, and 7/10 pain at rest. There are *no* bilateral sensory or motor deficits, and *no* upper motor neuron (UMN) signs (e.g., negative Hoffman sign, normal gait, no hyperreflexia).
*   **Conclusion:** The member’s clinical presentation and imaging do not support a diagnosis of cervical myelopathy. Rather, the clinical findings establish a definitive diagnosis of **Cervical Radiculopathy (C6-C7)**, characterized by objective, unilateral dermatomal sensory deficits and corresponding reflex changes. *Proceed to Step 3 (Radiculopathy/Axial pathway).*

### STEP 3: Plan Criteria Application & Compliance Review (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Comparison of the clinical record against the medical necessity requirements for cervical radiculopathy outlined in *HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2*.
*   **Analysis of Compliance:**
    1.  **Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement):** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy (C6-C7) supported by an MRI (within 6 months) showing structural pathology (C6-C7 herniation and C7 foraminal stenosis) correlating directly with right C7 dermatomal symptoms and reflex changes. **(MET)**
    2.  **Section 2.2(a) (Physical Therapy):** The member completed 7 weeks of supervised physical therapy (totaling 21 sessions), which exceeds the plan’s minimum requirement of 6 weeks. **(MET)**
    3.  **Section 2.2(b) (Pharmacotherapy):** The member completed trials of two qualifying agents for $\ge 4$ weeks each:
        *   *Pregabalin 150mg BID* (neuropathic agent) for 4 months. **(MET)**
        *   *Meloxicam 15mg* (NSAID) for 3 months. **(MET)**
    4.  **Section 2.2(c) (Interventional Treatment):** The member underwent a right C7 epidural steroid injection on September 20, 2024 (resulting in 30% temporary relief for 3 weeks, with a subsequent return to baseline). **(MET)**
*   **Evaluation of Denial Rationale:** The initial reviewer denied the request on the grounds that "PT was not yet completed to a functional plateau." This finding is directly refuted by the physical therapy discharge summary, which explicitly states that a **functional plateau was reached** because the patient experienced no therapeutic or functional improvement over the final 3 consecutive sessions, with radicular symptoms persisting unchanged.
*   **Conclusion:** The member has fully satisfied the conservative treatment requirements of **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2**. The plan's initial clinical review erred in its factual determination. *Proceed to Step 4 for statutory reinforcement.*

### STEP 4: Statutory and Regulatory Override Assessment (CIC § 10169.5)
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Assessment of whether California statutory provisions restrict the plan from enforcing further physical therapy prerequisites.
*   **Statutory Application:** Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2)**, a health plan *shall not* condition the approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of further physical therapy if the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating clinician documents that the member has reached a **functional plateau** without adequate resolution of symptoms. 
*   **Findings:** The clinical record contains an explicit statement from the treating physical therapist documenting that a functional plateau was reached (less than 10% progress over the final 3 sessions, with symptoms remaining at baseline). 
*   **Conclusion:** Under **CIC § 10169.5**, the plan is legally barred from requiring any additional physical therapy or denying the procedure based on the physical therapy requirement. The statutory criteria for an override/satisfaction of conservative therapy are met. *Proceed to Step 5.*

### STEP 5: Clinical Standard Override Assessment (CHSC § 1374.32 & Clinical Standards)
*   **Evaluation Activity:** Assessment of the surgical request against generally accepted clinical standards.
*   **Standard Applied:** Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)**, the standard for medical necessity is determined by generally accepted standards of medical practice, which override more restrictive internal plan criteria.
*   **Clinical Guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023 & NASS 2020):**
    *   For patients presenting with objective cervical radiculopathy and correlating structural pathology on imaging who have failed an exhaustive conservative treatment course of $\ge 6\text{ to }12$ weeks, surgical decompression via a single-level anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) is classified as a **Tier 2 (Reasonable/Indicated)** standard-of-care intervention.
    *   Thomas Reed has experienced persistent, severe radicular pain (7/10 at rest) and functional numbness for over 4 months, failing multiple pharmacotherapies, 21 sessions of PT, and an interventional epidural block. 
*   **Conclusion:** The requested CPT 22551 ACDF is clinically indicated, medically necessary, and aligned with generally accepted clinical standards (**AANS/CNS 2023**; **NASS 2020**).

## IV. RATIONALE FOR DECISION

1.  **Factual Error in Plan Review:** The plan’s initial denial was based on the assertion that physical therapy had not been completed to a functional plateau. However, the structured clinical record confirms that the member completed **7 weeks of physical therapy (21 sessions)**, and the PT discharge summary explicitly documented a **functional plateau** over the final three sessions.
2.  **Compliance with Plan Policy:** The member met all medical necessity criteria under *HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2*, including diagnosis confirmation (C6-C7 radiculopathy), duration of conservative therapy (7 weeks PT, 4 months Pregabalin, 3 months Meloxicam), and interventional therapy (C7 epidural steroid injection).
3.  **California Statutory Mandate:** Pursuant to **CIC § 10169.5**, a health plan cannot mechanically enforce physical therapy requirements once a clinical "functional plateau" is documented. The plan is legally prohibited from denying coverage on this basis.
4.  **Alignment with Clinical Standards:** Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)**, the clinical guidelines of the **AANS/CNS (2023)** and **NASS (2020)** govern. These guidelines designate ACDF as the standard-of-care surgical intervention for patients with persistent, objective radiculopathy who have failed a comprehensive conservative treatment trial.

Therefore, the initial denial is **OVERTURNED** and the requested C6-C7 ACDF is **APPROVED**.
