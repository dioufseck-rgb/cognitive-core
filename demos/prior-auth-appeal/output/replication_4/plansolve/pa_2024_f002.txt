### FINAL DISPOSITION: OVERTURN

The initial denial of prior authorization for single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) is **OVERTURNED**. The requested surgical procedure is **APPROVED** for immediate clinical authorization.

---

## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

On October 10, 2024, the plan issued an initial denial of CPT 22551 based on a clinical documentation discrepancy under Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024. The initial submission contained incomplete records from Coastal Physical Therapy, showing only 4 weeks of therapy, which conflicted with the member's claimed 10 weeks of total therapy and failed to satisfy the plan's 6-week minimum conservative management threshold. 

On appeal, the member and treating provider submitted complete medical records resolving this administrative discrepancy. The supplemented record establishes that the member completed two distinct 6-week courses of physical therapy (12 weeks, 36 sessions total) and reached a documented functional plateau at both facilities. Furthermore, the clinical record confirms severe, medically refractory cervical radiculopathy correlating with MRI findings of severe nerve root compression and objective motor and reflex deficits. 

Applying the statutory provisions of **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5**, the clinical standards of the **AANS/CNS (2023)**, and the plan's own **CC-SPINE-2024** criteria, the conservative treatment requirements have been fully satisfied. The requested surgical intervention is medically necessary, and the initial denial is overturned.

## II. NUMBERED EVALUATION STEPS

### Step 1: Procedural Defect Check
Under **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b)**, a health plan's denial notice must specify the clinical criteria applied, the specific clinical reason for the denial, and provide notice of Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights. 
*   **Finding:** The initial denial notice dated October 10, 2024, cited the correct plan clinical criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) and detailed the specific clinical reason for the denial (the administrative discrepancy between the 6 weeks of PT from Riverside PT and the 4 weeks from Coastal PT). The notice was procedurally compliant and not legally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b). The appeal must therefore be decided on its substantive clinical and statutory merits.

### Step 2: Clinical Classification & Diagnostic Verification
The medical record must be evaluated to distinguish between Cervical Myelopathy and Cervical Radiculopathy, as these conditions govern distinct clinical pathways.
*   **Diagnostic Findings:** 
    *   **Imaging (MRI dated August 15, 2024):** Demonstrates a large C5-C6 disc herniation resulting in severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. Crucially, there is no evidence of spinal cord compression, cord signal change, or myelomalacia.
    *   **Neurological Exam (dated August 20, 2024):** Demonstrates right C6 dermatomal numbness, right bicep weakness rated at 4/5, an absent right bicep reflex, and a positive right Spurling sign. There are no upper motor neuron signs (e.g., no Hoffman sign, no hyperreflexia, no gait instability, and no bilateral symptoms).
*   **Classification:** The member's condition is clinically classified as **Cervical Radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment**. The standard radiculopathy pathway applies; the myelopathy exception under Section 2.2(A) is not applicable.

### Step 3: Statutory Physical Therapy Waivers Review (State Law Override)
Under **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5**, a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning surgical approval on the completion of further physical therapy under specific statutory circumstances.
*   **Statutory Finding:** **CIC § 10169.5(a)(2)** provides that a plan cannot mandate additional physical therapy when "the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
*   **Clinical Integration:** 
    *   The member completed an initial 6-week course (18 sessions) at Riverside Physical Therapy (April 1, 2024, to May 10, 2024) where a clinical plateau was documented. 
    *   The member subsequently completed another 6-week course (18 sessions) at Coastal Physical Therapy (June 1, 2024, to July 12, 2024) where the physical therapist documented a "functional plateau — maximum therapeutic benefit reached" with no improvement in the C6 neurological deficit.
*   **Statutory Conclusion:** Because the member completed supervised physical therapy and achieved a documented functional plateau, the physical therapy requirements are legally satisfied under California law. The plan cannot demand further conservative physical therapy trials as a prerequisite to surgery.

### Step 4: Clinical Standards & Evidence-Based Necessity Review
Under **CHSC § 1374.32(b)**, clinical reviews must align with generally accepted standards of medical practice, which supersede restrictive or mechanical plan criteria.
*   **Clinical Guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023 / NASS 2020):** These guidelines designate single-level ACDF (CPT 22551) as a **Tier 2 (Reasonable Indication)** surgical intervention for patients presenting with cervical radiculopathy who exhibit persistent dermatomal pain, objective neurological deficits (motor weakness, reflex loss), and corresponding nerve root compression on MRI, who have failed 6 to 12 weeks of structured conservative management.
*   **Evidence-Based Compliance:** The member exhibits a 4/5 bicep motor deficit and reflex loss that directly correlates with the severe right-sided C5-C6 compression on MRI. The member has failed a total of 12 weeks of physical therapy, 5 months of Gabapentin (600mg TID), 3 months of Duloxetine (60mg daily), and a right C6 epidural steroid injection (which provided only 60% temporary relief before returning to baseline within 3 weeks). Delayed surgical decompression in the presence of persistent motor weakness (4/5) risks permanent neurological deficit. Surgical intervention is medically necessary under Tier 2 guidelines.

### Step 5: Plan Criteria and Alignment
The submitted appeal documentation is evaluated against HealthFirst's internal Clinical Coverage Criteria **CC-SPINE-2024**:
1.  **Section 2.1(a) Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. Confirmed diagnosis of C5-C6 cervical radiculopathy supported by an MRI within 6 months demonstrating severe nerve root compression and correlating clinical exam.
2.  **Section 2.2(a) Physical Therapy:** Met. The administrative discrepancy has been fully resolved on appeal. The complete records from Coastal Physical Therapy confirm 6 weeks (18 sessions) from June 1, 2024, to July 12, 2024. Combined with the 6 weeks completed at Riverside Physical Therapy, the member has completed a total of 12 weeks of verifiable PT, exceeding the plan's 6-week minimum requirement.
3.  **Section 2.2(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Met. Trial of two qualifying neuropathic agents completed: Gabapentin (5 months) and Duloxetine (3 months), both exceeding the 4-week trial requirement.
4.  **Section 2.2(c) Interventional Treatment:** Met. A right C6 epidural steroid injection was performed on August 28, 2024.
5.  **Section 2.3 & 2.4 Clinical Documentation:** Met. Current imaging (August 15, 2024) is within 6 months, and Dr. Nguyen has provided the required clinical rationale, documentation of functional limitations, and surgical risk statement.

The member satisfies all internal medical necessity criteria under CC-SPINE-2024.

## III. DETERMINATION SUMMARY & AUTHORIZATION

The clinical documentation submitted on appeal resolves the prior documentation gap. The member has completed 12 weeks of physical therapy, failed to sustain relief from interventional injections, and continues to suffer from C5-C6 radiculopathy with objective bicep motor weakness (4/5) and reflex loss.

Because the member meets all internal criteria of **CC-SPINE-2024**, meets generally accepted clinical guidelines (**AANS/CNS 2023**), and satisfies the physical therapy plateau protections under California statutory law (**CIC § 10169.5**), the requested procedure is medically necessary.
