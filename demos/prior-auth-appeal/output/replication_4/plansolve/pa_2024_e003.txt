### **I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY OF DECISION**

Following an exhaustive clinical and regulatory review of the appeal submitted on behalf of member George Harris, the Clinical Appeals Division has **OVERTURNED** the initial decision of October 30, 2024, which denied coverage for CPT Code 22551 (Revision ACDF C5–C6). 

The initial denial was based on a narrow, factually incorrect determination that the member did not exhibit "hardware failure." This determination ignored clear, objective diagnostic imaging documenting **pseudarthrosis (failed fusion)** at the C5–C6 level. Under the governing authority of **California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b)** and **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5**, the plan’s restrictive criteria must yield to generally accepted clinical standards. 

The clinical evidence confirms that Mr. Harris has symptomatic, CT-proven pseudarthrosis with progressive neurological deficits (right bicep weakness 4/5, diminished reflex, C6 dermatomal numbness) that have failed to respond to a comprehensive, multi-modal course of conservative therapy. Accordingly, the requested revision surgery at UCSF Medical Center is medically necessary and is **authorized immediately**.

### **II. EVALUATION OF CASE EVIDENCE & CLINICAL HISTORY**

#### **1. Surgical and Presenting History**

*   **Mid-2022 (18 Months Post-Op):** Recurrence of right-sided C6 neck and upper extremity symptoms.
*   **September 2024:** Progression to severe, localized right-arm pain, numbness, and motor weakness.

*   **MRI (September 1, 2024):** Limited by hardware artifact. Noted possible non-union at C5–C6, but could not definitively confirm or exclude residual disc material/recurrent herniation.
*   **CT Scan without Artifact (September 15, 2024):** Demonstrated **incomplete bridging bone formation** and **lucency around hardware** at the C5–C6 level. The radiologist and treating specialist interpreted these findings as consistent with **failed fusion/pseudarthrosis**. The hardware itself was noted to be intact without fracture.

#### **3. Neurological Examination (September 20, 2024)**
*   Objective physical findings showed a clear return of right C6 radiculopathy:
    *   **Motor:** Right biceps weakness graded at **4/5**.
    *   **Reflexes:** Diminished right biceps reflex.
    *   **Sensory:** Numbness in the right C6 dermatomal distribution.
    *   *Clinical Context:* The physical exam confirms neurological deficits that are worse than the patient's preoperative baseline from 2021.

#### **4. Conservative Treatment Inventory**
The clinical record documents an exhaustive, multi-modal conservative trial that exceeded the plan's standard requirements:
*   **Physical Therapy:** Completed **8 weeks (24 sessions)** of supervised PT. The physical therapist and treating physician documented a **functional plateau with no improvement**, noting that PT cannot resolve structural mechanical failure (non-union).
*   **Pharmacotherapy:** Failed a **4-month** course of Naproxen 500mg BID (partial pain relief only) and a **3-month** course of Gabapentin 300mg TID (partial neuropathic relief), satisfying the plan's requirement for two agents tried for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
*   **Interventional Treatment:** Underwent a cervical epidural steroid injection on **October 10, 2024**, resulting in minimal improvement. The treating physician noted that lack of response to injections is expected when structural pseudarthrosis is present.

### **III. PROCEDURAL COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY EVALUATION**

#### **1. Procedural Adequacy Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))**
The initial denial notice issued by Dr. Patricia Huang on October 30, 2024, cited reason code **MED-003**, stating: *"Revision surgery not covered under standard ACDF criteria... Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology... Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure."*

While the denial notice technically cited plan criteria, it represents a **procedural and substantive defect** because it misapplied its own guidelines. The plan's criteria are written disjunctively: revision is covered if there is evidence of hardware failure, **OR** pseudarthrosis, **OR** new pathology. 

By denying coverage solely based on the absence of "hardware failure" (fracture) while completely ignoring the CT scan of September 15, 2024, which explicitly confirmed pseudarthrosis, the reviewer created an arbitrary barrier. Under **CHSC § 1374.31(b)**, a denial that ignores clear clinical records to misapply criteria is substantively deficient and invalid.

#### **2. Physical Therapy Statutory Exception (CIC § 10169.5)**
The plan’s denial implies that standard conservative pathways must continue to be exhausted. However, under **California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)**, a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning surgical approval on further physical therapy when:
1.  **§ 10169.5(a)(1):** The treating physician has documented in writing that physical therapy is unlikely to benefit the member given the structural nature of the condition (Dr. Williams documented that PT cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure).
2.  **§ 10169.5(a)(2):** The member completed a course of physical therapy and reached a functional plateau (member completed 8 weeks/24 sessions with 0% functional improvement).
3.  **§ 10169.5(a)(3):** The condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise (right biceps weakness 4/5, reflex loss) that physical therapy cannot address.

Consequently, any plan requirement for further conservative delay is legally preempted and waived under California law.

### **IV. CLINICAL STANDARDS & MEDICAL NECESSITY ANALYSIS**

#### **1. Governing Hierarchy of Authority**
As mandated by the Evaluation Plan, the following hierarchy of authority governs this appeal:
$$\text{California State Law (CIC, CHSC)} > \text{Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS, NASS)} > \text{HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024}$$

#### **2. Alignment with Generally Accepted Clinical Standards**
*   **AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023 Update):** Under these guidelines, surgical revision is a **Tier 1 (Strongly Indicated)** recommendation for patients presenting with symptomatic cervical pseudarthrosis (failed fusion) accompanied by correlating, progressive radiculopathy and motor deficits (4/5 biceps weakness) that have failed conservative management. 
*   **North American Spine Society (NASS) Guidelines (2020) & AO Spine Guidelines (2021):** These authorities state that symptomatic pseudarthrosis is a structural mechanical failure. While mild, stable symptoms may be observed, progressive neurological deficits (such as Mr. Harris’s diminished reflex and motor weakness) require decompression and rigid stabilization to prevent permanent nerve root damage. Extending conservative trials in the face of structural non-union and progressive motor weakness is contrary to clinical standards and risks irreversible nerve injury.
*   **Plan Criteria Overrides (CHSC § 1374.32(b)):** Under California’s Independent Medical Review standard, health plans cannot apply medical necessity criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted medical practice. To the extent that the plan's reviewer interpreted "hardware failure" to mean only physical breakage of metal plates/screws, this interpretation is overly restrictive. Pseudarthrosis represents a functional and structural failure of the prior fusion construct. Generally accepted standards mandate that CPT 22551 is medically necessary for this presentation.

#### **3. Evaluation Plan Decision Tree Pathway**
Following the structured decision tree:
1.  **Step 1 (Procedural Check):** The denial notice was substantively defective/invalid under CHSC § 1374.31(b) due to a failure to evaluate the submitted clinical evidence of pseudarthrosis. 
2.  **Step 2 (Pathology Classification):** The diagnostic check reveals radiculopathy with progressive motor weakness and CT-confirmed pseudarthrosis (no active myelopathy). 
3.  **Step 3 & 4 (Legal/Clinical Review):** The member fully met and exceeded all standard plan conservative requirements (Section 2.2 of CC-SPINE-2024) AND meets the statutory exceptions under **CIC § 10169.5**. Furthermore, the request meets **Tier 1 surgical indications** under **AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines** for symptomatic pseudarthrosis. 
4.  **Result:** The pathway leads directly to **OVERTURN**.

### **V. FINAL DETERMINATION**

Pursuant to California Health & Safety Code § 1374.32, the Clinical Appeals Division hereby **OVERTURNS** the initial denial of prior authorization for Case ID PA-2024-E003. 

The plan’s initial reviewer erred by failing to recognize that pseudarthrosis (confirmed by the September 15, 2024 CT scan) meets the plan's own coverage criteria for revision spine surgery, and that the member’s clinical presentation of progressive right C6 radiculopathy meets the standard of medical necessity under California law and national neurosurgical guidelines.
