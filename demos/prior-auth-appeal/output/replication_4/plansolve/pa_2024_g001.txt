### FORMAL DETERMINATION

Pursuant to California Health and Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.30 et seq., California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5, and generally accepted clinical standards of care, the initial denial of prior authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) is hereby **OVERTURNED**. 

The requested surgical procedure is determined to be **medically necessary**. HealthFirst Health Plan is hereby ordered to authorize and cover the requested service immediately.

---

### I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

On November 1, 2024, HealthFirst Utilization Management denied prior authorization for an anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7, citing a failure to satisfy the conservative treatment criteria under clinical policy **CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2**. Specifically, the plan claimed the member completed only three (3) weeks of physical therapy (PT) and failed to document a functional plateau.

An independent review of the complete administrative and clinical record demonstrates that **the plan’s clinical denial was based on a critical factual error**. The member actually completed nine (9) weeks of supervised physical therapy (27 total sessions) across two distinct providers, with both sets of records submitted alongside the initial authorization request. Furthermore, the second physical therapy provider explicitly documented a functional plateau in its discharge summary. 

The member has also completed a compliant trial of two pharmacotherapeutic agents (>4 weeks each) and a targeted interventional procedure (cervical epidural steroid injection). Because the member has fully satisfied both the plan's internal criteria and the statutory protections of California law, the denial of coverage cannot be sustained.

---

### II. CLINICAL & ADMINISTRATIVE HISTORY

The clinical record presents a highly consistent, objectively verified case of persistent right-sided C7 radiculopathy that has failed to respond to a comprehensive course of conservative management.

1.  **Clinical Presentation & Physical Exam (August 25, 2024):** The member presents with persistent, right-sided cervical radiculopathy. Objective neurological examination by Dr. Sarah Okonkwo, MD, revealed right C7 dermatomal numbness, a diminished right tricep reflex, and right grip strength of 4/5. No upper motor neuron (UMN) signs, bilateral deficits, or gait disturbances were noted, classifying this clinically as severe, persistent radiculopathy rather than myelopathy.
2.  **Objective Imaging (August 20, 2024):** A cervical spine MRI demonstrated a large C6-C7 disc herniation causing moderate right C7 nerve root compression and right foraminal stenosis. Consistent with clinical exam findings, there was no cord contact, cord compression, or myelomalacia.
3.  **Conservative Management Timeline:**
    *   **Physical Therapy Provider 1 (Valley Physical Therapy):** Completed 3 weeks (9 sessions) from June 1, 2024, to June 21, 2024. Outcome: Partial improvement; symptoms persisted.
    *   **Physical Therapy Provider 2 (Capital Physical Therapy):** Completed 6 weeks (18 sessions) from July 8, 2024, to August 16, 2024. Outcome: Functional plateau achieved. Discharge summary dated August 16, 2024, explicitly states: *"Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."*
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Failed a 4-month trial of Gabapentin (600mg TID) and a concurrent 3-month trial of Naproxen (500mg BID), both of which exceeded the plan's 4-week trial requirement.
    *   **Interventional Treatment (September 15, 2024):** Received a right-sided C7 cervical epidural steroid injection. The procedure provided temporary (35%) pain relief, but symptoms returned to baseline within three weeks.

### III. EVALUATION STEPS & DETAILED ANALYSIS

Consistent with the Governing Source Hierarchy, this appeal is reviewed under California regulatory mandates (highest authority), generally accepted clinical standards (intermediate authority), and the plan’s clinical coverage criteria (lowest authority).

#### Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Disclosure Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
The initial denial letter dated November 1, 2024, was procedurally adequate. It explicitly cited the applied policy (**CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2**) and outlined the specific clinical reasons for denial (alleged completion of only 3 weeks of PT and a lack of documented functional plateau). Because the denial was procedurally sufficient, a remand is not indicated, and the evaluation proceeds to a substantive review of the facts.

#### Step 2: Factual Validation of Conservative Treatment History
The sole basis for the plan’s initial denial was that the member completed only 3 weeks of physical therapy. This factual claim is **demonstrably wrong**.
*   The administrative record contains undisputed, objective treatment records from **Valley Physical Therapy** confirming 3 weeks of therapy (9 sessions) from June 1 to June 21, 2024.
*   The record also contains treatment records and a discharge summary from **Capital Physical Therapy** confirming an additional 6 weeks of therapy (18 sessions) from July 8 to August 16, 2024.
*   The cumulative total is **9 weeks and 27 sessions** of supervised physical therapy directed at the cervical spine.
*   Both sets of records were stamped as submitted with the original prior authorization request. The initial reviewer, Dr. Foster, failed to aggregate the treatment durations of the two providers, focusing exclusively on the Valley PT files.

The plan’s factual premise is false. This finding alone, under the disposition framework, warrants overturning the denial.

#### Step 3: Diagnostic Classification & Plan Criteria Exception Alignment
Under **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1**, a patient must have a confirmed diagnosis of either cervical radiculopathy (2.1(a)) or cervical myelopathy (2.1(b)).
*   **Myelopathy Check:** Because the member does not exhibit upper motor neuron signs (e.g., positive Hoffman, hyperreflexia, gait disturbance, bilateral weakness) and the MRI shows no cord compression or myelomalacia, the member does not qualify for the Myelopathy Exception under Section 2.2(A).
*   **Radiculopathy Standard Pathway:** The member's primary diagnosis is right-sided C7 cervical radiculopathy. Therefore, the standard conservative treatment requirements of **Section 2.2** apply:
    1.  *Physical Therapy (2.2(a)):* Minimum 6 weeks required. **Met** (9 weeks completed).
    2.  *Pharmacotherapy (2.2(b)):* Trial of at least two classes of medications for a minimum of 4 weeks. **Met** (Gabapentin [neuropathic] for 4 months, Naproxen [NSAID] for 3 months).
    3.  *Interventional Treatment (2.2(c)):* Minimum of 1 cervical epidural steroid injection. **Met** (Right C7 epidural completed on September 15, 2024).

The member fully satisfied all aspects of the plan's standard internal coverage criteria for cervical radiculopathy at the time of the initial prior authorization submission.

#### Step 4: Statutory Exception and Waiver Evaluation (CIC § 10169.5)
Under **California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2)**, a health plan is legally prohibited from conditioning the approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of additional physical therapy if:
> *"The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician [or physical therapist] documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."*

The discharge summary from Capital Physical Therapy explicitly documented a functional plateau, using clear and clinical terminology: *"Maximum therapeutic benefit achieved. No further improvement expected from continued PT."* 

By ignoring this documentation and demanding additional PT beyond this documented plateau, the plan's denial directly violates **CIC § 10169.5**. A mechanical application of a 6-week continuous-course requirement that fails to account for a documented functional plateau across multiple providers is legally invalid in the State of California.

#### Step 5: Clinical Standard of Care Alignment (AANS/CNS 2023 & NASS 2020)
Where plan criteria are applied in a manner more restrictive than generally accepted medical practice, state law dictates that the clinical standard of care must govern (**CHSC § 1374.32(b)** and **DMHC APL 22-014**).

According to the **Joint Section of the AANS/CNS Guidelines for the Management of Degenerative Cervical Radiculopathy (2023)** and the **North American Spine Society (NASS) Guidelines (2020)**:
*   Surgical decompression (ACDF) is indicated for patients with cervical radiculopathy who exhibit objective neurological deficits (sensory loss, diminished reflexes, and dermatomal weakness) that have failed to resolve after 6 to 12 weeks of conservative therapy.
*   The member has experienced persistent, severe radicular pain and progressive physical limitations for over 5 months (June 2024 to November 2024), despite completing 9 weeks of physical therapy, 4 months of dual-agent pharmacotherapy, and a targeted epidural steroid injection.
*   The clinical presentation meets the **AANS/CNS Tier 2 Surgical Indications** (surgical intervention is highly reasonable and indicated for persistent radiculopathy with objective deficit failing conservative trial).

Enforcing additional conservative measures when the clinical standard of care has been fully met, and when objective nerve root compression exists, poses an unnecessary risk of prolonged neurological dysfunction (grip weakness, sensory loss).

---

### IV. CONCLUSION & APPEALS ORDER

Based on a comprehensive review of the clinical evidence and the governing authorities, the plan's denial is clinically and legally unsupportable:
1.  **Factual Error:** The plan miscounted the member's physical therapy duration, overlooking 6 weeks of completed therapy at Capital PT.
2.  **Plan Criteria Met:** The member fully completed 9 weeks of PT (exceeding the 6-week requirement), completed compliant trials of two distinct medication classes, and underwent a targeted cervical epidural.
3.  **Statutory Compliance:** The member reached a documented functional plateau, triggering the statutory protections of **CIC § 10169.5**, which bars the plan from mandating additional PT.
4.  **Clinical Standards met:** The procedure is fully supported by the **AANS/CNS 2023 Guidelines** for a patient with persistent C7 radiculopathy and 4/5 motor weakness who has failed comprehensive conservative care.

Therefore, the initial determination is **OVERTURNED**. HealthFirst is ordered to immediately approve prior authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C6-C7) at UCSF Medical Center as requested by Dr. Sarah Okonkwo, MD.
