### **II. CASE SUMMARY & APPEAL BACKGROUND**
On November 1, 2024, HealthFirst issued an initial denial (Reason Code: MED-003) for a requested two-level anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) at C5–C6 and C6–C7. The initial reviewer, Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, approved the clinical necessity for the C5–C6 level but denied the overall request because the C6–C7 level did not meet surgical criteria. 

The treating physician, Dr. Lisa Park, MD, appealed this decision on three grounds:
1. Both levels are symptomatic per clinical evaluation.
2. Fusing adjacent levels simultaneously is necessary to prevent accelerated adjacent-segment degeneration.
3. The member meets the plan’s full criteria for both levels.

This independent review evaluates the clinical records, imaging reports, and physical therapy summaries against California statutory law, national clinical guidelines, and the plan’s clinical criteria (CC-SPINE-2024).

---

### **III. EVALUATION STEPS & CLINICAL ANALYSIS**

#### **Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Regulatory Compliance Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))**
A review of the initial denial notice dated November 1, 2024, confirms that the notice is **procedurally adequate** under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b). The denial letter:
*   Explicitly cited the governing plan criteria applied (**HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024**).
*   Provided a clear, specific clinical reason for the adverse determination (lack of correlating clinical or imaging pathology at C6–C7).
*   Contained the required statutory notices regarding California Independent Medical Review (IMR) rights and expedited appeal options.
Consequently, no procedural defect exists to warrant an administrative overturn, and the case must be decided on its clinical merits.

#### **Step 2: Diagnostic Classification and Clinical Profile Analysis**
To determine the appropriate medical necessity standard, the member's clinical presentation must be classified as either *Cervical Radiculopathy* or *Cervical Myelopathy*:
*   **Myelopathy Indicators:** Requires objective signs of spinal cord compression (such as a positive Hoffman sign, hyperreflexia, clonus, bilateral upper/lower extremity weakness, or gait disturbance) coupled with cord signal changes (myelomalacia) on MRI.
*   **Radiculopathy Indicators:** Characterized by unilateral nerve root compression matching a specific dermatomal distribution, lower motor neuron signs (hyporeflexia, localized weakness), and dermatomal sensory loss.

**Clinical Record Evaluation:**
*   **Imaging (MRI - Sept 15, 2024):** 
    *   *C5–C6:* Shows a large disc herniation, moderate-to-severe right C6 nerve root compression, and foraminal stenosis. **No spinal cord compression, cord contact, or myelomalacia is present.**
    *   *C6–C7:* Shows a small disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing. **There is no identifiable nerve root compression, no cord contact, and no cord signal changes.**
*   **Neurological Exam (Sept 20, 2024):**
    *   *C6 Level:* Right bicep weakness (3+/5), absent right bicep reflex (0/4), and right C6 dermatomal numbness/tingling.
    *   *C7 Level:* Slightly diminished right triceps reflex. No definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms are documented. Dr. Park noted that mild C7 symptoms were "possibly present but not clearly documented."
*   **Diagnostic Classification:** The clinical profile is a classic presentation of **unilateral C6 radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits**. There are no upper motor neuron findings, no bilateral symptoms, no gait abnormalities, and no MRI evidence of cord compression or myelomalacia. Therefore, the diagnosis is strictly **cervical radiculopathy**, not cervical myelopathy.

#### **Step 3: Statutory Exception and Physical Therapy Waiver Analysis (CIC § 10169.5 & DMHC APL 22-014)**
Under California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 and Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014, a health plan's conservative therapy and physical therapy (PT) requirements are waived only if there is objective imaging evidence of progressive structural neurological compromise (e.g., myelomalacia, cord compression) or if the physician documents that PT is contraindicated or risks irreversible harm.

*   **Waiver Determination:** Because the member does not have cervical myelopathy, spinal cord compression, or myelomalacia, **the statutory conservative therapy waiver does not apply**.
*   **C5–C6 Standard Pathway Analysis:** The plan's standard conservative treatment criteria (CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2) require 6 weeks of supervised PT, 2 pharmacotherapy agents for 4+ weeks, and 1 interventional procedure.
    *   *PT:* Completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised PT targeting C6 symptoms (fully satisfies the 6-week requirement).
    *   *Pharmacotherapy:* Trialed Pregabalin (4 months) and Naproxen (3 months) (fully satisfies the requirement of 2 agents for 4+ weeks).
    *   *Interventional:* Completed a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 15, 2024, which provided temporary relief (70%) before symptoms recurred (fully satisfies the interventional requirement).
    *   *Conclusion:* The C5–C6 level **fully satisfies** all plan conservative treatment criteria.
*   **C6–C7 Pathway Analysis:** There was no C7-specific interventional procedure performed. PT notes from week 8 indicate that C7-related symptoms were "minimal and possibly resolving." Because the C6–C7 level has no objective nerve root compression on imaging and lacks corresponding clinical symptoms, a conservative therapy failure cannot be established for this level.

#### **Step 4: Clinical Standards and Medical Necessity Alignment (AANS/CNS & AO Spine Guidelines)**
Under CHSC § 1374.32(b), medical necessity must be evaluated against generally accepted national clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, and AO Spine 2021 Guidelines) which override more restrictive or mechanically applied internal plan criteria.

*   **C5–C6 Level (Approved):** 
    *   The member has a large C5–C6 disc herniation with moderate-to-severe right C6 nerve root compression and a corresponding severe neurological deficit (3+/5 bicep weakness, absent bicep reflex). 
    *   He failed a comprehensive 8-week course of physical therapy, multiple neuropathic and anti-inflammatory medications, and a targeted epidural steroid injection. 
    *   Under **AANS/CNS 2023 (Tier 2/Radiculopathy Surgical Indications)** and **NASS 2020 guidelines**, surgical decompression and fusion at C5–C6 is standard, highly effective, and medically necessary to prevent permanent motor deficit.
*   **C6–C7 Level (Denied):** 
    *   The MRI reveals only a small disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing, with **no nerve root compression or cord contact**. 
    *   The clinical exam shows no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms, and PT notes indicate that any mild C7 symptoms were resolving.
    *   Under **AANS/CNS 2023 (Tier 3)** guidelines, surgery is explicitly **not indicated** for mild degenerative imaging findings that lack correlating clinical signs or symptoms.
    *   *Addressing the Appeal Argument:* The appellant's argument that the C6–C7 level must be fused simultaneously to prevent adjacent-segment degeneration is clinically invalid. Generally accepted spine guidelines indicate that prophylactically fusing a non-symptomatic, structurally stable adjacent level actually *increases* adjacent segment stress at the remaining mobile levels. Standard clinical practice dictates sparing mobile segments whenever possible. Fusing C6–C7 in the absence of pathology or matching symptoms is not medically necessary and violates accepted standards of medical practice.

#### **Step 5: Plan Criteria Reconciliation (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)**
*   **C5–C6 Level:** Satisfies CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.1(c) (confirmed diagnosis of cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit), Section 2.2 (completed conservative therapy), and Section 2.3 (imaging pathology matches symptoms).
*   **C6–C7 Level:** Fails CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.3 and is excluded under Section 3.3, which mandates denial when "imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with clinical presentation."

---

### **IV. DETERMINATION RATIONALE**

1.  **C5–C6 ACDF (CPT 22551) is APPROVED:** The clinical record demonstrates severe right-sided C6 radiculopathy caused by a large C5–C6 disc herniation with moderate-to-severe C6 nerve root compression. The member has an objective motor deficit (3+/5 bicep weakness) and has failed an exhaustive course of conservative therapy (8 weeks of PT, 2 medications, and an epidural steroid injection). Decompression and fusion at this level meet HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 criteria, AANS/CNS 2023 guidelines, and NASS 2020 clinical standards.
2.  **C6–C7 ACDF (CPT 22552) is DENIED:** Imaging of the C6–C7 level shows only a minor disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing with **no nerve root compression or cord contact**. There are no correlating C7 clinical symptoms or objective dermatomal deficits. Fusing a clinically silent and radiographically mild level is not supported by AANS/CNS guidelines, is excluded under Section 3.3 of CC-SPINE-2024, and does not meet the definition of medical necessity under California Health & Safety Code § 1374.32(a). Prophylactic adjacent-level fusion is medically unsupported.
