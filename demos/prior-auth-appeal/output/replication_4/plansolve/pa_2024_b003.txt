### I. FINAL DETERMINATION

**DECISION:** **OVERTURN** the initial adverse determination and **APPROVE** the requested procedure (CPT 22551) at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center.

---

### II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BASIS FOR OVERTURN

This appeal review was conducted in accordance with the **Hierarchy of Authority** mandated for health plan reviews in the State of California: 
$$\text{California State Law \& Regulations (CIC, CHSC, DMHC APL)} > \text{Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS, AO Spine)} > \text{HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024)}$$

The initial denial dated 2024-10-01 is **overturned** based on three independent and compounding grounds:
1. **Procedural Defect:** The initial denial notice is procedurally defective under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b) for failing to cite the specific clinical coverage criteria utilized or reconcile the clinical findings.
2. **Clinical Criteria Compliance:** A rigorous clinical audit demonstrates that the member has fully satisfied all diagnostic, imaging, and conservative therapy criteria under the plan's policy, **CC-SPINE-2024**. The initial denial incorrectly ignored a diagnostic, gold-standard CT myelogram that overcame severe MRI hardware artifact.
3. **Statutory and Regulatory Mandate:** California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 and Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014 legally prohibit the plan from maintaining a denial when there is documented structural neurological compromise (3+/5 motor weakness) and a documented functional plateau.

---

### III. DETAILED EVALUATION PLAN STEPS

#### STEP 1: Procedural Adequacy Review (CHSC § 1374.31(b))
* **Statutory Requirement:** Under California Health & Safety Code § 1374.31(b), any medical necessity denial must explicitly disclose the specific clinical criteria applied and provide the specific clinical reasons for the determination.
* **Findings:** The initial denial letter dated 2024-10-01 stated: *"Member meets conservative treatment requirements. However, imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4 to support surgical intervention. MRI shows mild degenerative changes. Procedure not medically necessary."* 
* **Procedural Defect:** The letter failed to cite the applicable clinical policy (**CC-SPINE-2024**). Furthermore, it completely omitted any clinical discussion of the diagnostic **CT Myelogram dated 2024-09-10**, which was performed specifically because the MRI was obscured by metal artifact. 
* **Conclusion:** The denial notice is procedurally defective under CHSC § 1374.31(b) and must be treated as invalid. **This finding independently mandates an OVERTURN of the denial.**

---

#### STEP 2: Diagnostic Classification (Myelopathy vs. Radiculopathy)
* **Clinical Review:** The member’s clinical history and physical examination dated 2024-08-20 were evaluated to classify the pathology:
  * **Myelopathic Signs:** Absent. There is no documentation of gait instability, bilateral upper extremity weakness, hyperreflexia, positive Hoffman sign, or bowel/bladder dysfunction.
  * **Radicular Signs:** Present and severe. The neurological exam reveals a unilateral, left C4 dermatomal distribution deficit: shoulder and lateral arm numbness, significant deltoid weakness graded at **3+/5** (unable to resist gravity), and a diminished left bicep reflex. These findings are new since 2023.
* **Conclusion:** The pathology is classified as **Cervical Radiculopathy** secondary to adjacent segment disease at C3-C4 following a successful C4-C5 fusion in 2019. (Plan Criteria Section 2.1(a) is met).

---

#### STEP 3: Objective Imaging & Pathology Verification
* **MRI Limitations:** The C-spine MRI dated 2024-08-15 showed "significant artifact from the prior C4-C5 hardware limiting evaluation." The radiologist noted "mild degenerative signal changes visible" but explicitly concluded that they "cannot exclude moderate compression due to artifact."
* **CT Myelogram Resolution:** Because the MRI was non-diagnostic due to metallic artifact, a CT myelogram was performed on 2024-09-10. This is the clinically accepted gold standard in post-fusion patients. The CT myelogram confirmed:
  * **C3-C4:** Moderate-to-severe left C4 nerve root compression.
  * **Stenosis:** Significant foraminal stenosis.
  * **Inference:** The MRI vastly underestimated the pathology due to the hardware artifact.
* **Conclusion:** Objective pathology is verified. The imaging demonstrates severe structural pathology (nerve root compression) at the C3-C4 level correlating directly with the member’s left C4 radicular symptoms and 3+/5 deltoid weakness.

---

#### STEP 4: State Regulatory & Clinical Standards Override Analysis
Even if the plan's internal criteria were interpreted to deny this service, California law and clinical standards override such a decision:
* **CIC § 10169.5 Compliance:** This statute prohibits conditioning surgical approval on physical therapy when there is objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address (subdivision (a)(3)) or when a member has reached a functional plateau (subdivision (a)(2)). 
  * The member completed **10 weeks (30 sessions) of physical therapy** with zero improvement in her left C4 radicular symptoms, establishing a definitive **functional plateau** under Section 5 clinical guidelines.
  * The member has **3+/5 motor weakness** and severe nerve root compression, representing profound structural neurological compromise that PT cannot anatomically resolve.
* **DMHC APL 22-014 Compliance:** Requires health plans to waive rigid conservative management rules when objective imaging shows progressive pathology and the surgeon documents conservative failure. 
* **Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS 2023 / NASS 2020):** Under AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines, progressive or persistent radiculopathy with objective motor weakness (3+/5) and correlating advanced imaging compression (CT myelogram) following conservative failure represents a **Tier 1 (Strong) surgical indication**. Delaying surgical decompression in the presence of motor weakness risks permanent, irreversible axonal loss and deltoid atrophy.

---

#### STEP 5: Plan Criteria Compliance Analysis (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)
An audit of the clinical record against **HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2** reveals complete compliance:

1. **Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement):** Met. Confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with dermatomal numbness, 3+/5 deltoid weakness, and a diminished bicep reflex.
2. **Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement):** Met in full.
   * *(a) Physical Therapy:* Member completed **10 weeks (30 sessions)** of physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week policy minimum) with documented failure to improve.
   * *(b) Pharmacotherapy:* Member completed trials of **two** neuropathic agents: Pregabalin 300mg BID for 6 months (partial benefit) and Duloxetine 60mg for 3 months (minimal benefit). This exceeds the policy requirement of 2 agents for 4 weeks each.
   * *(c) Interventional Treatment:* Member underwent a **left C4 epidural steroid injection on 2024-09-25** with transient (60%) relief returning to baseline by week 3. This satisfies the 1-injection minimum.
3. **Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement):** Met. The CT myelogram dated 2024-09-10 (within 6 months) provides definitive, high-resolution proof of moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression, overriding the artifact-limited MRI.
4. **Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation):** Met. Dr. Paul Nguyen provided detailed clinical examinations, documentation of conservative therapy failure, and a clear surgical rationale for adjacent segment disease.

---

### IV. CONCLUSION & AUTHORIZATION DISPOSITION

The initial denial of coverage for the C3-C4 ACDF was medically and procedurally unsound. The initial reviewer’s reliance on a limited MRI while ignoring a diagnostic CT myelogram violates both California regulatory standards and generally accepted neurosurgical practice. 

The clinical record demonstrates that Angela Morrison has a severe, structurally correlated, and medically refractory left C4 radiculopathy with objective motor weakness (3+/5) that has failed exhaustive conservative therapy (including 10 weeks of physical therapy, two neuropathic medications, and an epidural injection).

Therefore, the initial denial is **OVERTURNED**.
