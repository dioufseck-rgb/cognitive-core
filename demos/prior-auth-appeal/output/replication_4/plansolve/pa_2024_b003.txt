### **CLINICAL & REGULATORY EVALUATION**

#### **Step 1: Procedural Adequacy and Defect Check (CHSC § 1374.31(b))**
The initial denial notice issued on October 1, 2024, by Dr. Alan Foster cited the clinical reasoning that "imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4" based on a "mild degenerative changes" finding on MRI. 

* **Procedural Finding:** While the initial denial technically met the minimum threshold of stating a clinical reason, it committed a material error by selectively evaluating a limited MRI study while entirely ignoring the diagnostic CT myelogram. To prevent administrative delay and protect the member’s right to timely care, we bypass a procedural remand under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b) and proceed directly to a binding clinical determination on the merits.

#### **Step 2: Diagnostic Classification and Clinical Symptom Correlation**
The clinical record was evaluated to differentiate between primary Cervical Myelopathy and Cervical Radiculopathy:
* **Neurological Examination (August 20, 2024):** Documents severe left-sided C4 dermatomal deficits, including shoulder and lateral arm numbness, a diminished left bicep reflex, and objective deltoid weakness graded at **3+/5 (moderate weakness against gravity)**. These deficits are new since 2023.
* **Classification:** There is no documentation of upper motor neuron signs (e.g., Hoffman sign, hyperreflexia, clonus, or gait disturbance) that would classify the presentation as primary Cervical Myelopathy. 
* **Determination:** The member presents with severe, progressive **Cervical Radiculopathy** and adjacent segment disease at C3-C4 (following a successful C4-C5 fusion in 2019). The evaluation must proceed under the standard radiculopathy conservative and surgical pathways.

#### **Step 3: Objective Imaging Evidence and Pathology Verification (C3-C4)**
The core justification for the initial denial was that the MRI dated August 15, 2024, showed only "mild degenerative changes." 
* **MRI Limitations:** The MRI report explicitly states: *"SIGNIFICANT ARTIFACT from C4-C5 hardware limiting evaluation... Cannot exclude moderate compression due to artifact."*
* **CT Myelogram Resolution:** Because the MRI was diagnostically obscured by metal hardware artifact from the prior 2019 fusion, a CT myelogram was appropriately performed on September 10, 2024. The CT myelogram confirmed: **"moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression confirmed"** and **"significant foraminal stenosis at C3-C4."** The radiologist explicitly noted that the previous MRI had underestimated the pathology due to hardware artifact.
* **Pathology Verification:** The CT myelogram provides definitive, high-resolution proof of severe structural pathology at C3-C4 that perfectly correlates with the member's left C4 radiculopathy and 3+/5 deltoid weakness.

#### **Step 4: Compliance with Plan-Specific Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)**
A review of the member’s clinical history reveals that she has not only met but significantly exceeded the standard conservative treatment requirements outlined in Section 2.2 of HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024:

1. **Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1(a)):** Satisfied. The member has confirmed cervical radiculopathy with correlating unilateral deficits and a supporting CT myelogram within 6 months.
2. **Physical Therapy (Section 2.2(a)):** Satisfied. The member completed **10 weeks (30 sessions)** of supervised physical therapy (exceeding the plan's 6-week requirement) with documented "no improvement" in her radicular symptoms.
3. **Pharmacotherapy (Section 2.2(b)):** Satisfied. The member completed trials of two qualifying agents:
   * **Pregabalin 300mg BID** for 6 months (partial relief).
   * **Duloxetine 60mg** for 3 months (minimal benefit).
4. **Interventional Treatment (Section 2.2(c)):** Satisfied. The member underwent a **left C4 epidural steroid injection on September 25, 2024**, which yielded 60% temporary relief but saw symptoms return to baseline by week 3.
5. **Imaging Requirement (Section 2.3):** Satisfied. The CT myelogram dated September 10, 2024, is within 6 months and demonstrates moderate-to-severe structural pathology consistent with the symptoms and the proposed surgical level.

**Conclusion of Plan Audit:** The initial reviewer's assertion that the member does not meet imaging requirements is clinically incorrect. The plan's criteria explicitly accept CT myelograms under Section 2.1. The member meets 100% of the plan's medical necessity criteria.

#### **Step 5: Application of State Regulatory Mandates and Clinical Standards (Overrides)**
Pursuant to the **Governing Hierarchy of Authority**, California state law and generally accepted clinical standards supersede any restrictive or erroneous interpretations of plan criteria:

* **California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) & (3):** A health plan cannot condition surgical approval on physical therapy or conservative trials where the member has already reached a "functional plateau" without resolution of symptoms, or where there is objective evidence of structural neurological compromise (deltoid weakness of 3+/5 and severe nerve root compression) that physical therapy cannot remediate.
* **DMHC All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014:** Plans are prohibited from applying internal medical necessity criteria mechanically. When a diagnostic tool (such as an MRI) is compromised by hardware artifact, plans must evaluate alternative definitive imaging (such as a CT myelogram) to avoid the denial of medically necessary care.
* **Clinical Standard Guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023 / NASS 2020):** Surgical decompression (ACDF) is a Tier 1/2 strong indication for patients with cervical radiculopathy who present with progressive, objective neurological deficits (motor weakness of 3+/5) and correlate with severe structural compression on imaging, having failed appropriate conservative care. Delaying surgery in the presence of objective motor weakness risks permanent neurological deficit.
