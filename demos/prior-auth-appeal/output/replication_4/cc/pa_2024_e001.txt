1. FINAL DETERMINATION DISPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After a comprehensive clinical and regulatory review of the prior authorization appeal, HealthFirst has issued a PARTIAL DISPOSITION:

- CPT Code 22551 (ACDF, C5-C6 level): OVERTURNED (APPROVED)
- CPT Code 22552 (ACDF, C6-C7 level): REMANDED (DEFERRED PENDING ADDITIONAL CLINICAL DOCUMENTATION)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CLINICAL RATIONALE FOR C5-C6 LEVEL (CPT 22551) — OVERTURNED (APPROVED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The prior authorization denial for the C5-C6 level is overturned because the clinical record demonstrates that the member fully satisfies all medical necessity criteria under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria Document CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2, as well as generally accepted standards of medical practice.

- Diagnosis Requirement (Section 2.1): The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy and cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit. The neurological exam dated September 20, 2024, documents significant right bicep weakness (3+/5), an absent right bicep reflex, and right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling.
- Imaging Requirement (Section 2.3): A cervical MRI dated September 15, 2024 (within the required 6-month window), demonstrates a large disc herniation at C5-C6 causing moderate-to-severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. This structural pathology directly correlates with the member's objective C6 neurological deficits.
- Conservative Treatment Requirement (Section 2.2): 
  (a) Physical Therapy: The member completed 8 weeks (24 sessions) of supervised physical therapy, exceeding the 6-week minimum required under Section 2.2(a). The physical therapy summary notes that C6 symptoms persisted despite compliance.
  (b) Pharmacotherapy: The member completed a 4-month trial of Pregabalin 150mg BID (neuropathic agent) and a 3-month trial of Naproxen 500mg BID (NSAID), satisfying the requirement under Section 2.2(b) for two agents trialed for at least 4 weeks each.
  (c) Interventional Treatment: The member underwent a right C6 epidural steroid injection on October 15, 2024, which provided 70% temporary relief before symptoms fully returned within 4 weeks, satisfying Section 2.2(c).
- Clinical Standards Alignment: Under the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for the Management of Degenerative Cervical Radiculopathy (2023), surgical decompression via ACDF is a Tier 2 (Reasonable Indication) for patients with persistent radiculopathy and objective neurological deficits who have failed 6 to 12 weeks of conservative care. 

Therefore, denying the C5-C6 level constitutes a violation of the plan's own clinical criteria and generally accepted medical standards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CLINICAL RATIONALE FOR C6-C7 LEVEL (CPT 22552) — REMANDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The prior authorization denial for the C6-C7 level is remanded because the current clinical record contains insufficient objective evidence to support the medical necessity of fusing this level under CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.3 and Section 3.3, as well as clinical guidelines.

- Imaging and Clinical Mismatch (Section 2.3 & Section 3.3): The MRI dated September 15, 2024, shows only a small disc bulge and mild foraminal narrowing at C6-C7, with no identifiable nerve root compression and no spinal cord contact. 
- Lack of Correlating Symptoms: The neurological exam dated September 20, 2024, notes a slightly diminished right tricep reflex but "no definitive C7 dermatomal symptoms." The treating physician's notes state that mild C7 symptoms were "possibly present but not clearly documented." Furthermore, the physical therapy discharge summary at week 8 states that C7 symptoms were "minimal and possibly resolving."
- Incomplete Conservative Trial: No C7-specific interventional treatment (such as a selective C7 nerve block or epidural injection) has been performed to confirm that the C6-C7 level is a primary pain generator.
- Clinical Standards Alignment: Under AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) Tier 3, surgical intervention is not indicated for imaging findings that lack correlating clinical symptoms. The treating physician's appeal basis—that adjacent levels should be addressed simultaneously to prevent accelerated degeneration—is not supported by high-quality clinical evidence as a primary indication for prophylactic fusion in the absence of active clinical and radiographic pathology.

REMAND INSTRUCTIONS FOR C6-C7 RECONSIDERATION:
To establish medical necessity for the C6-C7 level, the requesting provider must submit:
1. Updated clinical documentation demonstrating persistent, active C7 dermatomal symptoms (pain, numbness, or weakness in the C7 distribution) that did not resolve.
2. An updated objective neurological exam showing a clear, persistent C7 deficit (e.g., progressive tricep weakness or sensory loss).
3. Consideration of a diagnostic, level-specific selective nerve root block or epidural injection targeting C7 to confirm the level as a symptomatic source of pain.

4. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE AND HIERARCHY OF AUTHORITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This determination is governed by the California Regulatory Framework, which establishes a strict hierarchy of authority where state law and clinical standards supersede restrictive internal plan policies.

- California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32 (Medical Necessity Standard): Under California law, "medically necessary" services are those in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice and clinically appropriate in extent and site. The plan's practice of issuing a blanket denial for an entire multi-level procedure because one level does not meet criteria is non-compliant with CHSC § 1374.32. The plan is legally obligated to authorize the medically necessary portion of the request (C5-C6) while separately addressing the borderline level (C6-C7).
- California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy Prerequisites): The member's completion of 8 weeks of physical therapy and subsequent functional plateau for C6 symptoms fully satisfies the statutory requirements. The plan cannot mechanically enforce further conservative trials for the C5-C6 level.
- CHSC § 1374.31(b) (Defective Denial Notice): The initial denial notice issued by Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, on November 01, 2024, failed to cite the specific sections of the clinical coverage criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) applied, rendering the denial procedurally defective under California law. This partial overturn corrects this procedural defect by providing explicit citations and clinical reasoning.

5. MANDATORY NOTICE OF INDEPENDENT MEDICAL REVIEW (IMR) RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because the request for the C6-C7 level (CPT 22552) has not been approved at this time, you have the right to request an Independent Medical Review (IMR) through the California Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC) or the California Department of Insurance, as applicable.

Under CHSC § 1374.30, you may submit an application for an IMR if your health care service has been denied, modified, or delayed by the plan on the basis of medical necessity. The IMR is conducted by independent medical specialists who are not affiliated with HealthFirst. If the IMR reviewer decides that the C6-C7 fusion is medically necessary under generally accepted standards of medical practice, HealthFirst is legally bound to accept that decision and authorize the procedure, regardless of internal plan criteria (per CHSC § 1374.32(b)).

You do not need to exhaust the plan's internal appeal process further before requesting an IMR. There is no charge to you for this review. Application forms and instructions are enclosed with this letter, or you may contact the DMHC Help Center directly at 1-888-466-2219 or visit www.dmhc.ca.gov.
