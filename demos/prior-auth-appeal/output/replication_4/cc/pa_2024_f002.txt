Dear Mr. Clark and Dr. Nguyen,

The Appeals and Grievances Department of HealthFirst Health Plan has completed its review of the first-level appeal submitted on October 25, 2024, regarding the prior authorization denial for the requested Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at the C5-C6 level (CPT 22551).

After a comprehensive review of the complete clinical record, the plan's clinical coverage criteria, California regulatory requirements, and generally accepted clinical standards, HealthFirst Health Plan has determined that the initial denial issued on October 10, 2024, is OVERTURNED. The requested surgical procedure is approved.

I. BACKGROUND AND INITIAL DENIAL
On October 10, 2024, HealthFirst Health Plan initially denied the prior authorization request for CPT 22551. The denial, issued under reason code MED-003 by Dr. Patricia Huang, MD, cited "conflicting physical therapy documentation." Specifically, the plan noted that while Riverside Physical Therapy records documented 6 weeks of therapy, the Coastal Physical Therapy records initially submitted by the member only documented 4 weeks. Because of this discrepancy, the plan concluded that the total verifiable duration of physical therapy was uncertain and failed to meet the plan's conservative treatment requirements.

On October 25, 2024, an appeal was filed on the basis that both sets of records are authentic, and the discrepancy was due to an incomplete initial submission of Coastal Physical Therapy records (where the final 2 weeks were faxed separately). The complete 6-week records for Coastal Physical Therapy have now been submitted and verified.

II. CLINICAL PRESENTATION AND FINDINGS
The clinical record establishes the following objective findings for Mr. Steven Clark:
1. Presenting Diagnosis: Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment.
2. Objective Imaging: An MRI of the cervical spine dated August 15, 2024, demonstrates a large disc herniation at C5-C6, causing severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis.
3. Neurological Examination: A physical examination dated August 20, 2024, reveals objective neurological deficits, including right C6 dermatomal numbness, bicep weakness graded at 4/5 on the right, an absent right bicep reflex, and a positive right Spurling's sign.

III. ANALYSIS OF PLAN CLINICAL COVERAGE CRITERIA (CC-SPINE-2024)
Under HealthFirst Health Plan's Clinical Coverage Criteria for Spinal Surgery — Cervical Procedures (Document: CC-SPINE-2024), a request for CPT 22551 is approved when all criteria in Section 2 are satisfied:

1. Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement): Satisfied. Mr. Clark has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with nerve root compression and corresponding radicular symptoms (pain, numbness, and weakness in a dermatomal distribution), supported by an MRI within 6 months (August 15, 2024) demonstrating structural pathology consistent with his symptoms (C5-C6 large disc herniation and severe right C6 nerve root compression).
2. Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement): Satisfied.
   - (a) Physical Therapy: Satisfied. Section 2.2(a) requires a minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy with documented functional outcomes.
     - First, the undisputed records from Riverside Physical Therapy (April 1, 2024, to May 10, 2024) document 6 weeks (18 sessions) of supervised therapy directed at the cervical spine, concluding with a documented functional plateau and persistent symptoms. This course independently satisfies Section 2.2(a) in full, rendering any documentation dispute regarding other providers irrelevant to the clinical determination.
     - Second, the complete records now submitted for Coastal Physical Therapy (June 1, 2024, to July 12, 2024) document an additional 6 weeks (18 sessions) of supervised therapy, also concluding with a documented functional plateau and no improvement in neurological deficits. In total, Mr. Clark has completed 12 weeks (36 sessions) of documented physical therapy.
   - (b) Pharmacotherapy: Satisfied. Section 2.2(b) requires a trial of at least two specified agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. Mr. Clark completed a 5-month trial of Gabapentin (600mg TID) and a 3-month trial of Duloxetine (60mg daily), both of which are neuropathic agents listed under Section 2.2(b) and were trialed well beyond the 4-week minimum.
   - (c) Interventional Treatment: Satisfied. Section 2.2(c) requires at least one interventional treatment. Mr. Clark underwent a right C6 cervical epidural steroid injection on August 28, 2024, which provided 60% temporary relief before symptoms returned to baseline within 3 weeks.
3. Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement): Satisfied. The cervical MRI dated August 15, 2024 (within 6 months of the request) demonstrates severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis at the proposed surgical level (C5-C6).
4. Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation): Satisfied. Dr. Sarah Nguyen, MD, has provided the necessary clinical rationale, description of functional limitations, and discussion of surgical risks and alternatives.

IV. ANALYSIS OF CALIFORNIA REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS
Under California law, which supersedes any conflicting or more restrictive plan policies, the initial denial is legally and procedurally invalid:

1. California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite): Under § 10169.5(a)(2), an insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau. Furthermore, § 10169.5(c) prohibits plans from mechanically applying a minimum week requirement without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau.
   By denying the request based on a clerical discrepancy in the Coastal PT records—while completely ignoring that the verified Riverside PT records independently documented 6 weeks of therapy and a functional plateau—the plan mechanically and unlawfully applied its physical therapy requirement in violation of CIC § 10169.5.
2. California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32 (Standard of Review for IMR): Under § 1374.32(b), the standard for medical necessity is based on generally accepted standards of medical practice and does not require consistency with a plan's internal, more restrictive coverage criteria. Because Mr. Clark's clinical presentation meets generally accepted medical standards, the procedure is medically necessary.
3. California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31 (Plan Obligations on Denial): The initial denial notice was procedurally defective under § 1374.31(b) because it failed to provide a valid clinical reason for denial, focusing instead on an administrative record discrepancy that did not impact the clinical sufficiency of the undisputed portion of the record (the 6-week Riverside PT course).

V. ANALYSIS OF CLINICAL EVIDENCE AND GUIDELINES
According to generally accepted standards of medical practice, which govern under California DMHC All-Plan Letter (APL) 22-014 and CHSC § 1374.32:
1. AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) & NASS Guidelines (2020): Under Tier 2 (Reasonable Indication) of the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines, surgical decompression via ACDF is strongly indicated for patients with cervical radiculopathy who exhibit objective neurological deficits (such as Mr. Clark's 4/5 bicep weakness and absent bicep reflex) and whose symptoms have failed to resolve after 6 to 12 weeks of conservative management.
2. Functional Plateau: As defined by the clinical standard (Section 5 of the Clinical Reference Summary), Mr. Clark reached a functional plateau because he achieved no improvement in his objective neurological deficits (weakness and reflex loss) after a combined 12 weeks of physical therapy. Neurological deficits of this nature are structurally mediated and are not expected to respond to continued physical therapy. Delaying surgery to resolve an administrative discrepancy or to demand further conservative treatment poses a risk of permanent neurological damage.

VI. RESOLUTION OF CONFLICTS (HIERARCHY OF AUTHORITY)
In accordance with utilization review standards, conflicts between sources are resolved using the following hierarchy: California State Law > Generally Accepted Clinical Standards > Plan Clinical Criteria.
1. Under California State Law (CIC § 10169.5), the mechanical denial of coverage based on a physical therapy duration dispute is prohibited when a functional plateau has been documented.
2. Under Generally Accepted Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS Guidelines), surgical intervention is indicated based on Mr. Clark's objective neurological deficits and failure of conservative treatment, and further delay is clinically contraindicated.
3. Under the Plan's Clinical Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), the criteria are fully met on the undisputed record alone (the 6-week Riverside PT course).
Therefore, under all levels of the hierarchy, the initial denial cannot be sustained and must be overturned.

VII. CONCLUSION AND AUTHORIZATION DETAILS
Based on the foregoing analysis, the prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED. Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 (CPT 22551) is approved as medically necessary.
