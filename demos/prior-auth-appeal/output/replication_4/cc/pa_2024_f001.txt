Dear Ms. James,

HealthFirst Health Plan has completed its review of the first-level clinical appeal submitted on your behalf by Dr. Paul Adams, MD, regarding the prior authorization request for a single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT code 22551) to be performed at UCLA Medical Center. 

After a comprehensive review of the complete clinical record, including physical therapy records from Mountain Physical Therapy that were not available during the initial review, HealthFirst has determined that the initial denial is OVERTURNED. The requested surgical procedure is medically necessary and is hereby APPROVED.

Below is a detailed explanation of the clinical, regulatory, and plan-specific criteria used to reach this determination.

1. BACKGROUND AND REASON FOR INITIAL DENIAL
On November 2, 2024, HealthFirst initially denied the prior authorization request for CPT 22551 under Reason Code MED-003. The initial denial was based on the plan's internal clinical coverage criteria requiring a minimum of 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy. At the time of the initial review, the plan had only received records from Valley Physical Therapy documenting 4 weeks of treatment (June 1, 2024, to June 28, 2024). Records from your second provider, Mountain Physical Therapy, documenting an additional 4 weeks of treatment, were not received by the plan, preventing verification of the completed conservative treatment.

On November 16, 2024, an appeal was filed enclosing the complete records from Mountain Physical Therapy, confirming that you completed a total of 8 weeks of physical therapy across two providers.

2. CLINICAL PRESENTATION AND IMAGING FINDINGS
The clinical documentation establishes a primary diagnosis of bilateral C6-C7 cervical radiculopathy:
* Presenting Symptoms & Neurological Exam (Date: September 5, 2024): Documented bilateral C7 dermatomal symptoms, including bilateral tricep weakness (graded 4/5), diminished bilateral tricep reflexes, and bilateral finger tingling.
* Objective Imaging (MRI Date: September 1, 2024): Demonstrates a large disc herniation at C6-C7, bilateral C7 nerve root compression, bilateral foraminal stenosis, and mild spinal cord contact without compression.

3. EVALUATION OF PLAN CLINICAL COVERAGE CRITERIA (CC-SPINE-2024)
Under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2, authorization for cervical spinal surgery (CPT 22551) is approved when all of the following criteria are satisfied:

* Section 2.1 (Diagnosis Requirement): Satisfied. You have a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with correlating nerve root compression and objective radicular symptoms (bilateral tricep weakness, diminished reflexes, and tingling in a C7 dermatomal distribution).
* Section 2.2 (Conservative Treatment Requirement): Satisfied.
  - (a) Physical Therapy: Satisfied. The complete record now documents 8 weeks of supervised physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week minimum requirement) across two providers:
    1. Valley Physical Therapy (June 1, 2024, to June 28, 2024): 4 weeks, 12 sessions, documenting moderate improvement followed by a functional plateau.
    2. Mountain Physical Therapy (July 15, 2024, to August 9, 2024): 4 weeks, 12 sessions, documenting no improvement in neurological symptoms and a functional plateau at discharge.
    Note on Continuity: Although there was a 17-day gap (June 28 to July 15) between the two therapy courses, CC-SPINE-2024 Section 2.2(a) requires a "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy" and does not mandate continuous, uninterrupted treatment.
  - (b) Pharmacotherapy: Satisfied. You completed trials of two qualifying agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each:
    1. Pregabalin (neuropathic agent), 150mg BID for 3 months, yielding only partial relief.
    2. Naproxen (NSAID), 500mg BID for 2 months, which was inadequate.
  - (c) Interventional Treatment: Satisfied. You underwent a bilateral C7 epidural steroid injection on September 20, 2024, which provided only temporary relief (50% on the left, 30% on the right) before symptoms returned to baseline.
* Section 2.3 (Imaging Requirement): Satisfied. The MRI dated September 1, 2024 (within 6 months of the request) demonstrates structural pathology (large C6-C7 disc herniation and nerve root compression) consistent with your bilateral C7 radicular symptoms.
* Section 2.4 (Physician Documentation): Satisfied. Dr. Paul Adams, MD, provided the required clinical rationale, description of functional limitations, and discussion of surgical risks and alternatives.

4. EVALUATION OF CALIFORNIA REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS
Under California law, which supersedes any conflicting or more restrictive internal health plan policies, the mechanical enforcement of physical therapy duration is prohibited when clinical evidence demonstrates a functional plateau:

* California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2): States that a health insurer shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on the completion of physical therapy where the member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician or therapist documents that the member reached a "functional plateau" without adequate resolution of symptoms.
* CIC § 10169.5(c): Prohibits plans from mechanically applying internal requirements for a minimum number of weeks of physical therapy without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau.
* Application: Both Valley Physical Therapy and Mountain Physical Therapy documented that you reached a functional plateau, with no further improvement in your neurological symptoms (tricep weakness and tingling). Under CIC § 10169.5, further physical therapy cannot be mandated, and the completed 8 weeks fully satisfies the statutory standard.
* California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b): Notes that a denial notice must specify the clinical criteria and specific clinical reasons applied. The initial denial's reliance on "missing records" that were submitted but not received constitutes an administrative issue rather than a valid clinical basis for denying medical necessity.

5. EVALUATION OF CLINICAL EVIDENCE STANDARDS
Under California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32, medical necessity determinations must align with generally accepted standards of medical practice:

* AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines for the Management of Degenerative Cervical Disorders (2023) & NASS Evidence-Based Clinical Guidelines (2020): Recommend surgical decompression (such as ACDF) for patients with cervical radiculopathy who present with objective neurological deficits (e.g., 4/5 motor weakness, diminished reflexes) and persistent symptoms that have failed to respond to an appropriate course of conservative management (typically 6 to 12 weeks).
* Clinical Application: Your clinical presentation meets the Tier 2 (Reasonable Indication) criteria under these guidelines. You have persistent, objective C7 radiculopathy with correlating MRI findings of severe nerve root compression that has failed to resolve despite 8 weeks of supervised physical therapy, 3 months of neuropathic medication, 2 months of NSAIDs, and a targeted epidural steroid injection.

6. RESOLUTION OF CONFLICTS
Applying the hierarchy of authority (California State Law > Generally Accepted Clinical Standards > Plan Internal Criteria):
1. State Law (CIC § 10169.5) protects members from arbitrary or mechanical physical therapy requirements once a functional plateau is documented.
2. Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS & NASS Guidelines) establish that surgical decompression is the standard of care for persistent radiculopathy with objective motor weakness after conservative failure.
3. Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) are fully met once the Mountain Physical Therapy records are integrated into the clinical file. The 17-day gap between physical therapy providers does not invalidate the cumulative 8 weeks of therapy, as the plan criteria do not require continuous sessions, and state law prohibits mechanical continuity barriers when a plateau is documented.

CONCLUSION
Because you have met all diagnostic, imaging, conservative treatment, and documentation requirements under HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, California Insurance Code § 10169.5, and generally accepted clinical guidelines, the prior authorization denial is OVERTURNED. 

The requested single-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C6-C7 (CPT 22551) is APPROVED. This authorization is valid for 90 days from the date of this letter (valid through February 18, 2025) per CC-SPINE-2024 Section 3.5.
