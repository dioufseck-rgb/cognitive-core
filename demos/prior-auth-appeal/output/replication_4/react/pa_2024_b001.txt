**DISPOSITION: PARTIAL OVERTURN**

The initial denial for a two-level Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 and C6-C7 is **PARTIALLY OVERTURNED**.

*   **Authorization is GRANTED for a single-level ACDF at C5-C6.**
*   **The denial is UPHELD for the requested second level at C6-C7.**

---

**WARRANT**

This appeal determination is based on a comprehensive review of the member's clinical record, HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024), relevant California state laws and regulations, and generally accepted clinical standards of medical practice (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021). The three-source hierarchy (State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria) was applied, with higher authority governing in cases of conflict.

**I. Clinical Presentation: Cervical Radiculopathy, Not Myelopathy**

The clinical record consistently demonstrates cervical radiculopathy at the C5-C6 level, and explicitly rules out cervical myelopathy.

*   **Imaging (MRI 2024-09-01):** Shows "C5-C6: large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, moderate foraminal stenosis. Clear correlation with C6 radicular symptoms." For C6-C7, it notes "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable."
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-09-05):** Reveals "Right C6 dermatomal numbness and tingling, bicep reflex diminished right, grip strength 4/5 right." Crucially, it states "No C7 findings documented. No bilateral findings. No upper motor neuron signs."
*   **Analysis:** Per "DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE — PRIOR AUTHORIZATION APPEAL REVIEW" and "CLINICAL REFERENCE SUMMARY - SECTION 1.1", myelopathy requires spinal cord compression plus upper motor neuron signs and/or bilateral symptoms. These are absent. Myelomalacia is also absent. Therefore, the myelopathy exceptions in the plan criteria (Section 2.2(A)) and state regulations (DMHC APL 22-014) are not applicable to this case. The primary diagnosis is cervical radiculopathy C5-C6.

**II. Medical Necessity for C5-C6 Level (Approved)**

The requested ACDF at C5-C6 meets both HealthFirst's plan criteria and generally accepted clinical standards.

*   **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Met. The member has "Cervical radiculopathy — nerve root compression with radicular symptoms" (2.1(a)) and "Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit" (2.1(c)) at C5-C6, with clear C6 radicular symptoms correlating to the MRI findings.
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** Met. The member completed 8 weeks of physical therapy (exceeding the 6-week minimum), a trial of two pharmacotherapies (Gabapentin for 4 months, Cyclobenzaprine for 6 weeks), and one interventional treatment (Right C6 epidural). Symptoms persisted despite these interventions, with only partial/temporary relief from the epidural, indicating failure of conservative management.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Met. MRI within 6 months clearly demonstrates structural pathology (large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression) at C5-C6 consistent with symptoms.
    *   **2.4 Physician Documentation:** Met, as evidenced by the surgeon's note and appeal basis.

*   **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020):**
    *   The member's presentation of persistent C6 radiculopathy symptoms after an adequate trial of conservative treatment meets the "TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION" for ACDF, specifically "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx" (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1).
    *   The "CLINICAL REFERENCE SUMMARY - SECTION 2.1" further clarifies that conservative treatment is effective for radiculopathy, but when symptoms persist after 6-12 weeks, surgery becomes a reasonable option.

*   **State Law:** No California state law or regulation contradicts the medical necessity for ACDF at C5-C6. CHSC § 1374.32(b) reinforces that services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice (which C5-C6 does) shall be approved regardless of potentially more restrictive plan criteria (though, in this instance, plan criteria are met).

**III. Medical Necessity for C6-C7 Level (Denied — Upheld)**

The requested ACDF at C6-C7 does not meet HealthFirst's plan criteria or generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention.

*   **Initial Denial Reason (MED-003):** "Imaging shows pathology at C5-C6 only. C6-C7 shows mild degenerative changes without significant compression or neurological deficit attributable to this level." This reasoning is consistent with the evidence and plan criteria.

*   **HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Not met for C6-C7. There are no C7 radicular symptoms or objective neurological deficits documented. The imaging shows only "mild disc bulge, minimal foraminal narrowing, no cord contact, no nerve root compression identifiable" for C6-C7. This does not confirm a diagnosis of radiculopathy or disc herniation with objective neurological deficit attributable to C6-C7.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement:** Not met for C6-C7. Imaging does not demonstrate structural pathology *consistent with the member's symptoms* at C6-C7, as no C7 symptoms are present.
    *   **3.3 Exclusion:** Applies. "Imaging does not demonstrate pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with clinical presentation" for C6-C7.

*   **Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023):**
    *   The clinical record indicates "No C7 findings documented." ACDF is not indicated for asymptomatic levels or "imaging findings without correlating symptoms" (Clinical Reference Summary, Section 3.1, TIER 3).
    *   The surgeon's rationale for addressing C6-C7 is "prophylactically stabilize C6-C7 to prevent adjacent segment disease." While adjacent segment disease is a known concern, prophylactic fusion of an *asymptomatic* and *non-pathologic* level is not supported as a "TIER 1 — STRONG INDICATION" or "TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION" for ACDF under generally accepted clinical guidelines (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021). The focus of these guidelines is on treating existing, symptomatic pathology, not preventing potential future pathology at an currently healthy segment.

*   **State Law:** No California state law (including CIC § 10169.5, DMHC APL 22-014, or CHSC § 1374.32(b)) mandates approval for a prophylactic fusion at an asymptomatic level when it does not meet generally accepted standards of medical practice or plan criteria.

**IV. Conclusion**

The evidence supports surgical intervention for the symptomatic and pathologically confirmed C5-C6 level due to persistent radiculopathy despite conservative management. However, there is no clinical evidence of C7 radiculopathy, objective neurological deficit at C6-C7, or significant imaging pathology at C6-C7 that correlates with symptoms. Prophylactic fusion of an asymptomatic level for prevention of adjacent segment disease is not considered medically necessary under current plan criteria or generally accepted clinical guidelines.

Therefore, the request for a two-level ACDF (CPT 22551) is partially approved for one level (C5-C6), and the denial for the second level (C6-C7) is upheld.
