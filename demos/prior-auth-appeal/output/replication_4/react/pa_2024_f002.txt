### CASE SUMMARY:

The member, Steven Clark, is a 46-year-old male presenting with cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6 with functional impairment. An MRI dated 2024-08-15 demonstrates a large disc herniation at C5-C6 with severe right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis, consistent with symptoms. Neurological exam reveals right C6 dermatomal numbness, 4/5 bicep weakness, absent right bicep reflex, and a positive Spurling sign on the right.

The initial prior authorization request for ACDF C5-C6 was denied on 2024-10-10 due to "Conflicting physical therapy documentation." The denial stated that records from Riverside PT showed 6 weeks of therapy, while records submitted by the member for Coastal PT showed 4 weeks, leading to uncertainty regarding the total verifiable duration of PT. The plan requires verification of claimed PT duration.

The member's appeal clarifies that both Riverside PT and Coastal PT records are authentic. The discrepancy was due to an incomplete initial submission from Coastal PT, with the final 2 weeks faxed separately. The appeal now includes the complete Coastal PT records, confirming 6 weeks of therapy from that provider.

The clinical record, as updated by the appeal, now reflects:
*   **Physical Therapy:** 6 weeks at Riverside PT (complete records, 18 sessions, plateau documented) + 6 weeks at Coastal PT (complete records, 18 sessions, no improvement in neurological deficit, functional plateau reached). Total verifiable PT: 12 weeks, 36 sessions.
*   **Pharmacotherapy:** Gabapentin 600mg TID for 5 months (partial relief) and Duloxetine 60mg daily for 3 months (partial neuropathic benefit).
*   **Interventional Treatment:** Right C6 epidural on 2024-08-28 (60% temporary relief, returned to baseline in 3 weeks).

### CLINICAL & REGULATORY ANALYSIS:

The review will adhere to the hierarchy of authority: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria**.

**1. Primary Clinical Presentation:**
Based on the presenting diagnosis ("Cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with functional impairment"), MRI findings (nerve root compression, foraminal stenosis, *no mention of cord compression or myelomalacia*), and neurological exam (unilateral dermatomal numbness, weakness, absent reflex, positive Spurling, *no upper motor neuron signs*), the member's condition is consistent with **Cervical Radiculopathy**. The specific provisions for Myelopathy, including associated exceptions in plan criteria and state law (e.g., DMHC APL 22-014 on myelomalacia), are not directly applicable as the primary basis for this case.

**2. Application of HealthFirst PPO Gold Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with nerve root compression and radicular symptoms, supported by MRI and neurological exam. **MET.**
*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** The plan requires a minimum of 6 weeks of supervised PT. With the appeal, the member has completed a total of 12 weeks of supervised PT (6 weeks from Riverside PT + 6 weeks from Coastal PT). This clearly **MET (exceeds)** the 6-week minimum requirement. Both providers documented a functional plateau.
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** The plan requires at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. The member completed 5 months of Gabapentin and 3 months of Duloxetine. This **MET** the requirement.
    *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** The plan requires one interventional treatment. The member received a right C6 epidural. This **MET** the requirement.
    *   **Myelopathy Exception 2.2(A):** This exception is not applicable as the member's primary diagnosis is radiculopathy, not myelopathy.
*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** MRI dated 2024-08-15 (within 6 months) demonstrates pathology at C5-C6 consistent with symptoms. **MET.**
*   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** The appeal addresses the core concern of conservative treatment documentation, which is paramount to Section 2.4(a).

**Initial Denial Justification Re-evaluation:** The original denial reason ("Conflicting physical therapy documentation... uncertain duration") has been directly and fully resolved by the appeal, which provided complete and verified documentation of 12 weeks of physical therapy. Therefore, the original basis for denial is no longer valid.

**3. Application of California Regulatory Requirements:**

*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This state law specifies conditions under which a plan cannot require PT completion.
    *   Subsection (a)(2) states that a plan shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of PT where "The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms."
    *   The clinical record explicitly states that a functional plateau was reached at both Riverside PT and Coastal PT after a combined 12 weeks of therapy, with "no improvement in neurological deficit" and "maximum therapeutic benefit reached."
    *   Subsection (c) further clarifies that a plan's minimum PT duration "shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau."
    *   **Conclusion:** Even if the plan were to attempt to re-deny based on perceived insufficiency of PT duration (which is now demonstrably met), California law explicitly overrides such a denial when a functional plateau has been documented, as is the case here. The plan's concern regarding PT duration is legally superseded.

*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard is NOT Plan Criteria):** This provision mandates that if a service meets generally accepted standards of medical practice, it SHALL be approved regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive. This provides a legal basis for prioritizing clinical standards over plan criteria if a conflict were to exist, though in this case, the plan's criteria are met.

**4. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **Conservative Treatment for Radiculopathy:** Clinical guidelines recommend surgery for radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment (AANS/CNS 2023 Tier 2; NASS 2020 Grade B Evidence).
*   **Member's Status:** Steven Clark has undergone an extensive course of conservative treatment (12 weeks of PT, 5 months of Gabapentin, 3 months of Duloxetine, and one epidural steroid injection) which significantly exceeds the generally accepted minimum durations for radiculopathy. Despite this, he continues to experience persistent symptoms, objective neurological deficits, and has reached a documented functional plateau with no further improvement expected from conservative measures.
*   **Conclusion:** The member's condition and failed conservative treatment meet generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention for cervical radiculopathy.

### CONFLICT RESOLUTION AND DISPOSITION:

The initial denial was solely based on a procedural documentation issue concerning the duration of physical therapy. The appeal successfully resolved this issue by providing complete records, verifying 12 weeks of physical therapy.

*   **Plan Criteria:** With the updated documentation, the member *fully meets* all conservative treatment requirements as outlined in HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024 (12 weeks of PT > 6 weeks minimum; two pharmacotherapy agents for adequate duration; one interventional procedure).
*   **State Law:** CIC § 10169.5 explicitly prohibits denial based on PT duration when a functional plateau has been reached, which is clearly documented in this case.
*   **Clinical Standard:** The member has completed an adequate and prolonged course of conservative treatment for radiculopathy, meeting the generally accepted clinical guidelines for surgical intervention.

Since the original denial reason has been addressed and rectified, and the requested procedure meets plan criteria, state law requirements, and generally accepted clinical standards, the denial is no longer supported.

### DISPOSITION: **OVERTURN**
