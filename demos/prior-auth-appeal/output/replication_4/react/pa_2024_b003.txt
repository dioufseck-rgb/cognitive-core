**1. Summary of Initial Denial:**
The prior authorization request for ACDF C3-C4 was initially denied on 2024-10-01 by Dr. Alan Foster, MD (Denial Reason Code MED-003). The denial stated that while the "Member meets conservative treatment requirements," imaging (MRI) "does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4 to support surgical intervention." Specifically, the MRI was cited as showing "mild degenerative changes," leading to a conclusion that the "Procedure not medically necessary."

**2. Summary of Appeal Basis:**
The appeal was submitted with the following key points:
*   Member completed all conservative treatment requirements.
*   Surgeon confirms C3-C4 as the symptomatic level based on clinical examination.
*   CT myelogram confirms compression that was not visible on the MRI due to hardware artifact from prior surgery.

**3. Clinical Record Review:**
*   **Presenting Diagnosis:** Adjacent segment disease at C3-C4 following a prior C4-C5 ACDF in 2019.
*   **Prior Surgical History:** Successful C4-C5 ACDF in 2019 with good outcome for 4 years.
*   **Imaging:**
    *   **MRI (2024-08-15):** Found "SIGNIFICANT ARTIFACT from C4-C5 hardware limiting evaluation." Noted "Mild degenerative signal changes visible. Cannot exclude moderate compression due to artifact."
    *   **CT Myelogram (2024-09-10):** Performed specifically due to MRI limitations. Findings: "C3-C4: moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression confirmed. Significant foraminal stenosis. MRI underestimated pathology due to hardware artifact from prior fusion."
*   **Neurological Exam (2024-08-20):** Objective findings consistent with left C4 radiculopathy: "Left C4 dermatomal distribution: shoulder and lateral arm numbness, deltoid weakness 3+/5 left, bicep reflex diminished left. New since 2023."
*   **Conservative Treatment:**
    *   **Physical Therapy:** 10 weeks (30 sessions), documented "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms."
    *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Pregabalin 300mg BID for 6 months (partial relief); Duloxetine 60mg for 3 months (minimal benefit).
    *   **Interventional:** Left C4 epidural steroid injection (2024-09-25) provided "Good initial relief (60%), returned to baseline by week 3."

**4. Application of HealthFirst Plan Clinical Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024):**

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:**
    *   The clinical record confirms "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "Significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4, with correlating "Left C4 dermatomal distribution: shoulder and lateral arm numbness, deltoid weakness 3+/5 left, bicep reflex diminished left." This clearly meets criterion 2.1(a) for "Cervical radiculopathy — nerve root compression with radicular symptoms."
*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** Member completed 10 weeks of PT, exceeding the 6-week minimum. The note "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms" indicates failure of this therapy to address the primary neurological deficit.
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Member trialed Pregabalin (6 months) and Duloxetine (3 months), meeting the requirement for at least two agents for minimum 4 weeks each.
    *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** Member received a left C4 epidural steroid injection, meeting the requirement for one interventional procedure, which provided only temporary relief.
    *   *Myelopathy Exception 2.2(A):* The clinical presentation (unilateral C4 radicular symptoms, no UMN signs or gait disturbance) indicates radiculopathy, not myelopathy as a primary diagnosis. Therefore, the myelopathy exception does not apply, and standard conservative treatment requirements were correctly reviewed and met.
*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:**
    *   The initial denial's reliance on the MRI findings ("mild degenerative changes," "cannot exclude moderate compression due to artifact") was incomplete. The subsequent CT myelogram, performed due to the MRI's limitations, definitively demonstrated "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4, consistent with the member's symptoms. This current imaging (within 6 months) *does* demonstrate structural pathology at the proposed surgical level consistent with symptoms.
*   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** The appeal basis and clinical record confirm the physician's rationale, functional limitations, and attestation of conservative treatment completion.

*   **Section 3 — EXCLUSIONS:**
    *   **3.2 Conservative treatment requirements not met:** This exclusion is not applicable, as the initial denial acknowledged, and our review confirms, that conservative treatment requirements were met.
    *   **3.3 Imaging does not demonstrate pathology:** This exclusion is not applicable. The CT myelogram clearly demonstrates significant pathology consistent with the clinical presentation.

**Conclusion under Plan Criteria:** Based on a comprehensive review including the CT myelogram, Angela Morrison meets all applicable HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria for Cervical Spinal Surgery. The initial denial was based on an incomplete and consequently erroneous assessment of imaging findings.

**5. Application of California Regulatory Framework:**

*   **CHSC § 1374.31(b) (Denial Notice Adequacy):** The initial denial relied on MRI findings while failing to incorporate the more definitive CT myelogram, rendering the stated "specific clinical reason for the denial" (lack of sufficient pathology) factually inaccurate and thus potentially defective.
*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):**
    *   **(a)(2):** The member completed 10 weeks of PT, and the documentation indicates "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms," which signifies that a functional plateau was reached for the relevant neurological symptoms that PT cannot address.
    *   **(a)(3):** The condition involves "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis," which are objective evidences of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot resolve.
    *   While the plan acknowledged conservative treatment was met, this state law reinforces that further PT would not be beneficial and cannot be a barrier to surgery for this condition.
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard of Review):** This statute mandates that services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice SHALL be approved, regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive. This provides an overarching standard, even if plan criteria were not met (which they were in this case upon full review).
*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Myelopathy and Urgent Conditions):** While this APL primarily addresses myelopathy, its general principle in II.3 that "Plans shall not apply internal criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted standards of medical practice" is relevant.

**6. Application of Clinical Standard Knowledge (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **1.1 Cervical Radiculopathy vs. Myelopathy:** The clinical presentation is classic for cervical radiculopathy (unilateral dermatomal symptoms, weakness, diminished reflex) as confirmed by the neurological exam and imaging (nerve root compression). It does not present as myelopathy.
*   **2.1, 2.2, 2.3 Conservative Treatment Evidence:** For radiculopathy, conservative treatment is typically recommended, and the member has completed a robust course, including 10 weeks of PT, two pharmacotherapy agents, and an epidural injection, all of which have failed to provide lasting relief or improve neurological deficits. This aligns with clinical guidelines for proceeding to surgical evaluation after failed conservative care for radiculopathy.
*   **3.1 Accepted Indications for ACDF (AANS/CNS 2023):** The member's condition falls under TIER 2 "Reasonable Indication" for surgery:
    *   **(b) Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx:** Angela has persistent C4 radicular symptoms with objective weakness and diminished reflex after 10 weeks of PT and other conservative measures.
    *   **(c) Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care:** The persistent numbness and 3+/5 deltoid weakness constitute significant functional impairment.

**7. Resolution of Conflicts and Determination of Medical Necessity:**

The initial denial was based on an incomplete and flawed assessment of imaging. The MRI report explicitly noted artifact limiting evaluation and stated it "Cannot exclude moderate compression." The subsequent CT myelogram definitively confirmed "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4, directly refuting the denial's premise of "insufficient pathology."

Applying the hierarchy: State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria:
1.  **Plan Criteria:** When all imaging (especially the clarifying CT myelogram) is considered, the member clearly meets HealthFirst's own medical necessity criteria for ACDF, specifically for cervical radiculopathy with objective pathology and failed conservative treatment. The exclusion based on imaging not showing pathology is incorrect.
2.  **Clinical Standard:** The member's persistent, objectively documented left C4 radiculopathy with moderate-severe nerve root compression and failed extensive conservative treatment meets generally accepted clinical standards (AANS/CNS, NASS) for surgical intervention (Tier 2 indication).
3.  **State Law:** California law (CIC § 10169.5) explicitly allows for waiver of mechanical PT duration requirements when structural neurological compromise exists or a functional plateau has been reached, both of which apply here. CHSC § 1374.32(b) dictates that if a service meets generally accepted medical standards, it should be approved regardless of more restrictive plan criteria. In this case, the plan's denial reason was factually incorrect based on the available clinical evidence, thus conflicting with the standard of medical practice.

There is no conflict where a higher authority would uphold the denial. All sources, when properly applied to the complete clinical picture, support approval.

**FINAL DISPOSITION: OVERTURN DENIAL**

**WARRANT:**
The denial for ACDF C3-C4 for Angela Morrison is hereby **OVERTURNED**. The initial denial was based on an incomplete review of imaging, specifically relying on an MRI that was limited by hardware artifact. A subsequent CT myelogram definitively confirmed "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4, consistent with the member's documented left C4 radiculopathy (shoulder and lateral arm numbness, deltoid weakness 3+/5, diminished bicep reflex).

The member has also completed an appropriate and extensive course of conservative treatment, including 10 weeks of physical therapy (with no improvement in radicular symptoms), two pharmacotherapy agents (with partial/minimal benefit), and an epidural steroid injection (with only temporary relief). This demonstrates a failure of conservative management.

This clinical presentation with objective pathology, neurological deficit, and failed conservative treatment meets all applicable HealthFirst Clinical Coverage Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024, Sections 2.1(a), 2.2, and 2.3) and does not meet the specified exclusion 3.3. Furthermore, the requested ACDF is medically necessary and consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for cervical radiculopathy with persistent symptoms and objective nerve root compression after failed conservative therapy, as outlined in the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023, Section 3.1, Tier 2). The findings of structural neurological compromise and a functional plateau after PT also align with California Insurance Code § 10169.5 (a)(2) and (a)(3).
