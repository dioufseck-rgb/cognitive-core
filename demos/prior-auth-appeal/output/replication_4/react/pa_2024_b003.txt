**I. BACKGROUND**

Angela Morrison, a 67-year-old member with HealthFirst PPO Gold, was denied prior authorization for an ACDF at C3-C4. The initial denial, issued by Dr. Alan Foster, MD, cited "MED-003: Member meets conservative treatment requirements. However, imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4 to support surgical intervention. MRI shows mild degenerative changes. Procedure not medically necessary."

The member's appeal asserts that conservative treatment requirements were met, the surgeon clinically identifies C3-C4 as the symptomatic level, and a CT myelogram confirms compression not visible on MRI due to hardware artifact.

---

**II. CLINICAL PRESENTATION & DIAGNOSIS**

**Presenting Diagnosis:** Adjacent segment disease at C3-C4 following prior C4-C5 fusion (2019).
**Symptoms:** Left C4 dermatomal distribution: shoulder and lateral arm numbness, deltoid weakness 3+/5 left, bicep reflex diminished left. These symptoms are new since 2023.

Based on the unilateral dermatomal symptoms, objective weakness, and diminished reflex, the primary clinical presentation is **Cervical Radiculopathy** affecting the left C4 nerve root. There is no evidence in the clinical record (e.g., upper motor neuron signs, bilateral symptoms, gait disturbance, or myelomalacia) to support a diagnosis of cervical myelopathy. This distinction is critical as treatment algorithms and conservative treatment requirements differ between radiculopathy and myelopathy.

---

**III. DENIAL REASON & APPEAL BASIS ANALYSIS**

**Initial Denial Reason:**
1.  "Member meets conservative treatment requirements."
2.  "Imaging does not show sufficient pathology at C3-C4 to support surgical intervention. MRI shows mild degenerative changes. Procedure not medically necessary."

**Appeal Basis:**
1.  Member completed all conservative treatment requirements.
2.  Surgeon says C3-C4 is symptomatic level based on clinical examination.
3.  CT myelogram confirms compression not visible on MRI due to hardware artifact.

---

**IV. APPLICATION OF HIERARCHY OF AUTHORITY**

This appeal determination applies the established hierarchy: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria.**

**A. Application of Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024)**

The requested procedure (CPT 22551) is covered under HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024. We will evaluate the member's case against Section 2: Medical Necessity Criteria.

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **Plan Requirement:** Confirmed diagnosis of Cervical Radiculopathy (2.1a) with objective neurological deficit (2.1c) and supporting MRI or CT myelogram.
    *   **Case Finding:** The member presents with left C4 radicular symptoms (numbness, weakness 3+/5 deltoid, diminished bicep reflex). The CT myelogram (2024-09-10) confirmed "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4.
    *   **Conclusion:** **MET.** The member has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit and supporting imaging.

*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **Plan Requirement:** All of the following: (a) minimum 6 weeks PT, (b) trial of at least two pharmacotherapy agents for 4+ weeks each, (c) one interventional treatment. (Myelopathy Exception 2.2(A) does not apply as this is a radiculopathy case).
    *   **Case Finding:**
        *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** Member completed 10 weeks (30 sessions) of PT, which is greater than the 6-week minimum. Documented "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms."
        *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Member completed a 6-month trial of Pregabalin (partial relief) and a 3-month trial of Duloxetine (minimal benefit). Both trials meet the duration and agent requirements.
        *   **(c) Interventional:** Member received a left C4 epidural (2024-09-25) with good initial (60%) relief, which returned to baseline by week 3.
    *   **Conclusion:** **MET.** All conservative treatment requirements as per HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2, have been satisfied. The initial denial itself acknowledges this.

*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **Plan Requirement:** Current imaging (within 6 months) must demonstrate structural pathology consistent with symptoms and proposed surgical level.
    *   **Case Finding:** The initial MRI (2024-08-15) noted "SIGNIFICANT ARTIFACT from C4-C5 hardware limiting evaluation. Mild degenerative signal changes visible. Cannot exclude moderate compression due to artifact." Due to this limitation, a CT myelogram was performed (2024-09-10). The CT myelogram specifically found "C3-C4: moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression confirmed. Significant foraminal stenosis. MRI underestimated pathology due to hardware artifact from prior fusion." This imaging directly correlates with the member's left C4 radicular symptoms.
    *   **Conclusion:** **MET.** The CT myelogram clearly demonstrates the structural pathology consistent with the member's symptoms at the proposed surgical level. The initial denial's reliance solely on the limited MRI findings, despite the more definitive CT myelogram, represents an incomplete and flawed assessment of the available clinical evidence against HealthFirst's own criterion.

**B. Application of California Regulatory Framework (State Law)**

*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This statute prohibits a plan from requiring completion of PT where (a)(2) the member has completed PT and reached a functional plateau, or (a)(3) there is objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that PT cannot address.
    *   **Case Finding:** The member completed 10 weeks of PT with "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms," which constitutes reaching a functional plateau per CIC § 10169.5(b). The CT myelogram confirms moderate-severe nerve root compression, representing structural neurological compromise.
    *   **Conclusion:** Even if the plan's 6-week PT requirement were somehow deemed unmet (which it was not), this state law would prohibit denial based on PT duration, as the member reached a functional plateau and has objective structural neurological compromise.

*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard – Generally Accepted Standards of Medical Practice):** This code mandates that a service meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice shall be approved regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive.
    *   **Conclusion:** This provision reinforces that the clinical standard takes precedence if there's a conflict or if plan criteria are unduly restrictive.

*   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Prior Authorization Requirements for Time-Sensitive Neurological Conditions):** While this APL primarily focuses on myelopathy, Section II.3 states: "Plans shall not apply internal criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted standards of medical practice for conditions identified in this guidance. Where a conflict exists between plan criteria and generally accepted standards, the clinical standard governs."
    *   **Conclusion:** This APL reinforces the overarching principle that clinical standards supersede overly restrictive plan criteria.

**C. Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021)**

*   **Diagnosis (Section 1.1):** The member's presentation of unilateral dermatomal symptoms, weakness, and diminished reflex is clearly consistent with Cervical Radiculopathy.
*   **Conservative Treatment Evidence for Radiculopathy (Section 4):** For radiculopathy, physical therapy and conservative management are typically tried for 6-12 weeks before considering surgery.
*   **Surgical Indications (Section 3.1):** ACDF is indicated for:
    *   **TIER 2 — REASONABLE INDICATION:** "(b) Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx; (c) Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care."
    *   **Case Finding:** The member has persistent left C4 radicular symptoms, objective weakness (deltoid 3+/5) and numbness, confirmed moderate-severe nerve root compression via CT myelogram, and has failed an extensive course of conservative treatment (10 weeks PT, 6 months pharmacotherapy, 1 injection). This clearly aligns with the Tier 2 indications for surgery.
*   **Conclusion:** Based on generally accepted clinical standards, surgical intervention is medically necessary for the member's cervical radiculopathy given the failure of an adequate and prolonged course of conservative treatment, correlating objective imaging findings, and persistent neurological deficit.

---

**V. CONFLICT RESOLUTION**

The initial denial's reasoning regarding insufficient pathology is directly contradicted by the subsequent and more definitive CT myelogram, which explicitly revealed moderate-severe C4 nerve root compression at C3-C4. The MRI was limited by hardware artifact, necessitating the CT myelogram. The denial therefore failed to consider all available and conclusive imaging evidence.

Furthermore, the member has fully met HealthFirst's own conservative treatment requirements, and even if there were any perceived shortcomings, California state law (CIC § 10169.5) would supersede, given the documented functional plateau in physical therapy and objective structural neurological compromise.

Finally, the clinical presentation, failed conservative therapy, and objective imaging findings align squarely with generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention in cervical radiculopathy. California state law (CHSC § 1374.32(b)) dictates that services meeting these standards shall be approved.

---

**VI. DISPOSITION**

**OVERTURN**

---

**VII. WARRANT**

The denial of prior authorization for CPT 22551 (ACDF C3-C4) for Angela Morrison is hereby OVERTURNED. This decision is based on a comprehensive review of the clinical record and application of the hierarchy of authority:

1.  **Failure to Accurately Apply Plan Criteria:** The initial denial asserted that imaging did not show sufficient pathology, citing mild degenerative changes on MRI. However, a subsequent CT myelogram, performed specifically to overcome MRI artifact, definitively showed "moderate-severe left C4 nerve root compression" and "significant foraminal stenosis" at C3-C4. This finding directly contradicts the denial's imaging assessment and meets HealthFirst's own criteria for demonstrating structural pathology consistent with symptoms (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.3).
2.  **Conservative Treatment Requirements Met:** The member completed 10 weeks of physical therapy, trials of two pharmacotherapy agents for adequate durations (Pregabalin for 6 months, Duloxetine for 3 months), and one interventional procedure (C4 epidural). This meets and exceeds HealthFirst's conservative treatment requirements (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2).
3.  **Adherence to State Law:** Even if conservative treatment criteria were challenged, California Insurance Code (CIC) § 10169.5(a)(2) states that a plan cannot deny based on PT completion where the member has completed therapy and reached a functional plateau, which is documented here by "No improvement in left C4 radicular symptoms" after 10 weeks.
4.  **Consistency with Clinical Standards:** The member's persistent left C4 radiculopathy, objective neurological deficit (3+/5 deltoid weakness), confirmed moderate-severe nerve root compression on advanced imaging, and failure of an extensive course of conservative treatment meet generally accepted clinical standards for surgical intervention (AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines 2023, Section 3.1, TIER 2(b) & (c); NASS Guidelines 2020). California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.32(b) requires approval when services meet such standards.

Based on the evidence, the requested ACDF at C3-C4 is medically necessary according to both HealthFirst's clinical criteria (when all evidence is considered), California state law, and generally accepted standards of medical practice.
