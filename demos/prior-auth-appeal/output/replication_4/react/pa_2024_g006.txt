**1. Appeal Disposition:** **OVERTURN**

**2. Warrant for Determination:**

The initial prior authorization denial for an ACDF C5-C6 for Ms. Theresa Kamau is **OVERTURNED** for the following reasons, applying the hierarchy of State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria:

a.  **Procedurally Flawed Denial (State Law Violation):** The initial denial was explicitly based on an invalid criterion. The denial notice itself states: "Denial basis: Prior PA history — NOT a valid criterion under CC-SPINE-2024" and "denial is procedurally flawed: true." This constitutes a clear violation of California Health & Safety Code (CHSC) § 1374.31(b), which mandates that a denial notice specify the clinical criteria applied and the specific clinical reason for the denial. A denial notice that fails to do so is defective and shall be treated as if no denial was issued. Using prior authorization history as a primary basis for denying a new, distinct request, especially when the prior request was for a different procedure (three-level vs. one-level fusion), is not supported by HealthFirst's own policy (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 3) nor by generally accepted medical practice.

b.  **Compliance with Plan Criteria:** Even setting aside the procedural flaw, a comprehensive review of the submitted clinical record demonstrates that Ms. Kamau's request for ACDF C5-C6 **fully meets all medical necessity criteria outlined in HealthFirst's own Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024** for cervical spinal surgery for radiculopathy:
    *   **2.1 Diagnosis Requirement (Met):** Ms. Kamau has a confirmed diagnosis of cervical radiculopathy C5-C6 with objective neurological deficits (right C6 dermatomal numbness, absent bicep reflex right, bicep weakness 3+/5 right), and a large disc herniation at C5-C6 with moderate-severe right C6 nerve root compression on current MRI.
    *   **2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement (Met):**
        *   **Physical Therapy:** Ms. Kamau completed a NEW 8-week course of supervised physical therapy (July-September 2024), exceeding the 6-week minimum required by the policy. The therapist explicitly documented a "functional plateau: 'no further improvement expected, neurological deficit persistent and worsening.'"
        *   **Pharmacotherapy:** Ms. Kamau completed trials of Gabapentin (6 months) and two Methylprednisolone dose packs, satisfying the requirement for at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each.
        *   **Interventional Treatment:** Ms. Kamau received a right C6 epidural injection (November 2024), meeting the requirement for one interventional procedure.
    *   **2.3 Imaging Requirement (Met):** A current MRI (October 2024) is provided, demonstrating structural pathology (C5-C6 large disc herniation with nerve root compression) consistent with symptoms and the proposed surgical level.
    *   **2.4 Physician Documentation (Assumed Met):** The request was submitted by a spine surgeon, Dr. Victor Osei, implying the necessary attestations and rationales were provided.

c.  **Support from State Law (CIC § 10169.5 & DMHC APL 22-014):**
    *   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy Prerequisite):** Even if the plan had attempted to deny for insufficient physical therapy, this state law would override such a denial. The clinical record clearly shows Ms. Kamau (1) completed 8 weeks of PT and reached a functional plateau, and (2) has objective evidence of structural neurological compromise (large disc herniation with moderate-severe nerve root compression, 3+/5 weakness) that PT cannot address. These conditions trigger § 10169.5(a)(2) and (a)(3), mandating that the plan cannot condition approval on further PT.
    *   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Conservative Treatment Exceptions):** Although the primary diagnosis is radiculopathy, Section II.2 of this APL supports exceptions to conservative treatment requirements when objective imaging shows progressive pathology (e.g., "interval increase in C5-C6 herniation size" on Ms. Kamau's MRI) and the physician documents conservative treatment failure. This further reinforces the inadequacy of the original denial's reasoning.

d.  **Consistency with Clinical Standards (AANS/CNS, NASS, AO Spine):** The requested procedure is medically necessary under generally accepted standards of medical practice. Ms. Kamau presents with progressive cervical radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit (worsening weakness, absent reflex, significant pain 8/10, unable to work full-time) after a comprehensive and documented failed course of conservative treatment. Her condition meets **TIER 1 Strong Indication** (progressive neurological deficit despite conservative treatment) and **TIER 2 Reasonable Indication** (radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative treatment, and significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care) for surgical intervention as per the AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023).

In summary, the original denial was invalid due to procedural flaws and was contrary to HealthFirst's own established criteria, California state law, and generally accepted clinical guidelines. The requested ACDF C5-C6 is medically necessary.

---

**3. Action:**
The prior authorization denial for CPT code 22551 (ACDF C5-C6, one level) for member Theresa Kamau (M-G006) is **OVERTURNED**. Approval for the requested service is granted.
