## HealthFirst Health Plan – Prior Authorization Appeal Determination

**Case ID:** PA-2024-B004
**Member:** Kevin Watkins (M-889900)
**Procedure Requested:** CPT 22551 (ACDF C5-C6, one level)
**Requested By:** Dr. Sarah Martinez, MD
**Date of Review:** 2024-11-15

### Summary of Request and Denial

Mr. Kevin Watkins, a 41-year-old professional violinist, requested prior authorization for an Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) at C5-C6 due to acute cervical radiculopathy with significant functional impairment affecting his right hand. The request was initially denied by HealthFirst on 2024-11-10 (reason code MED-003) stating, "Member meets conservative treatment requirements. However, surgical request is premature. Member's symptoms have been present for only 6 weeks. Plan criteria require at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration. Recent onset radiculopathy has a high rate of spontaneous resolution."

The appeal argues that Mr. Watkins is a professional musician whose right hand weakness is career-ending, all conservative treatment was completed and failed within the 6-week period, a functional plateau was reached, and his physician recommends surgery given the functional impairment.

### Clinical Summary

Mr. Watkins presents with acute cervical radiculopathy at C5-C6 with objective neurological deficits. MRI dated 2024-10-01 shows a large disc herniation with moderate right C6 nerve root compression and foraminal stenosis. Crucially, there is **no cord contact and no myelomalacia**, indicating this is a case of radiculopathy, not myelopathy. His neurological exam from 2024-10-01 demonstrates right C6 dermatomal numbness, diminished right bicep reflex, 4/5 right grip strength, and inability to perform violin fingering tasks, which is critical to his livelihood.

Conservative treatment consisted of:
*   **Physical Therapy:** 6 weeks (18 intensive sessions), with documentation of a functional plateau and no improvement in fine motor deficits or radicular symptoms. Occupational Therapy confirmed inability to perform professional duties.
*   **Pharmacotherapy:** A 6-week course of Methylprednisolone dose packs (temporary partial relief) and 5 weeks of Gabapentin 600mg TID (partial pain relief, no motor improvement).
*   **Interventional:** One right C6 epidural steroid injection (2024-10-28), providing only 25% temporary relief, no motor improvement, and returning to baseline in 2 weeks.

### Review Findings and Analysis

This appeal determination applies the established hierarchy of authority: **State Law > Clinical Standard > Plan Criteria**.

1.  **Clinical Classification:**
    Based on the MRI findings (large disc herniation with nerve root compression, *no cord contact, no myelomalacia*) and neurological exam (right C6 dermatomal numbness, diminished bicep reflex, 4/5 grip strength, *absence of upper motor neuron signs or bilateral symptoms*), Mr. Watkins' condition is definitively classified as **cervical radiculopathy**, not cervical myelopathy. This distinction is critical as treatment algorithms and conservative treatment requirements differ significantly for these two conditions.

2.  **Application of Plan Criteria (HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024):**
    *   **Section 2.1 Diagnosis Requirement:** Mr. Watkins meets criteria 2.1(a) (Cervical radiculopathy) and 2.1(c) (Cervical disc herniation with objective neurological deficit), supported by MRI and clinical findings.
    *   **Section 2.3 Imaging Requirement:** The MRI from 2024-10-01 (within 6 months) demonstrates structural pathology consistent with symptoms. *This criterion is met.*
    *   **Section 2.4 Physician Documentation:** The appeal basis indicates the treating physician recommends surgery due to functional impairment. *Assumed met.*
    *   **Section 2.2 Conservative Treatment Requirement:** This is the basis of the plan's denial.
        *   **2.2(a) Physical Therapy:** Plan criteria require "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy." Mr. Watkins completed 6 weeks (18 sessions) of intensive PT. *This criterion is met by duration.*
        *   **2.2(b) Pharmacotherapy:** Plan criteria require "at least two... for minimum 4 weeks each." Mr. Watkins completed 6 weeks of Methylprednisolone and 5 weeks of Gabapentin. *This criterion is met.*
        *   **2.2(c) Interventional Treatment:** Plan criteria require "one of the following." Mr. Watkins received one right C6 epidural steroid injection. *This criterion is met.*
        *   **Denial Reason Misinterpretation:** The plan's denial states, "Member's symptoms have been present for only 6 weeks. Plan criteria require at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration." This is an incorrect interpretation or application of its own policy. HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2(a), specifically requires "minimum 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy," not a minimum duration of symptoms. Mr. Watkins completed the required 6 weeks of PT.
        *   **Functional Plateau:** The plan's criteria (Section 2.2 NOTE) define functional plateau as "<10% improvement over 3 sessions OR therapist documents maximum benefit reached." The clinical record unequivocally states, "Functional plateau documented at 6 weeks. No improvement in fine motor deficits or radicular symptoms. OT confirms inability to perform professional duties." This fully satisfies the plan's definition of a functional plateau.
        *   **Myelopathy Exception (2.2(A)):** This exception does not apply, as Mr. Watkins' primary diagnosis is radiculopathy, not myelopathy.

    *   **Conclusion on Plan Criteria:** Mr. Watkins has *met all* the conservative treatment requirements of HealthFirst CC-SPINE-2024, both in terms of duration and the documented failure of said treatments (functional plateau). The plan's denial reason misrepresents its own criteria and ignores the documented functional plateau.

3.  **Application of California State Law:**
    *   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** This state law explicitly governs conditions for requiring physical therapy. It states a health insurer "shall not condition approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where: (2) The member has completed a course of physical therapy and the treating physician documents that the member reached a functional plateau without adequate resolution of symptoms; OR (3) The condition involves objective evidence of structural neurological compromise that physical therapy cannot address."
        *   Both subsections apply directly to Mr. Watkins' case. He completed a 6-week course of intensive PT, and a functional plateau was documented by his treating therapists with persistent neurological deficits and functional impairment (inability to perform professional duties). His condition involves objective structural neurological compromise (large disc herniation with nerve root compression) causing motor weakness and fine motor deficits that PT has demonstrably failed to address.
        *   Subsection (c) further mandates that a plan's minimum PT requirement "shall not be applied mechanically without reference to whether the member has achieved a functional plateau or whether the condition involves neurological urgency."
        *   **Impact:** Under California state law, HealthFirst is prohibited from requiring further conservative treatment or denying the surgery based on the duration of symptoms/PT, given the documented functional plateau and neurological compromise.

    *   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** This section dictates that "If the service meets the standard in subdivision (a) [generally accepted standards of medical practice], the reviewer SHALL recommend approval regardless of whether the plan's internal criteria are more restrictive." This reinforces that even if the plan's criteria *were* correctly applied to deny, generally accepted clinical standards can override them.

    *   **DMHC APL 22-014 (Conservative Treatment Exceptions):** While primarily addressing myelopathy, II.3 states: "Plans shall not apply internal criteria that are more restrictive than generally accepted standards of medical practice for conditions identified in this guidance." This general principle supports overturning denials based on overly restrictive or evidence-lacking plan criteria.

4.  **Application of Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**
    *   **Conservative Treatment Duration for Radiculopathy:** Clinical guidelines generally suggest 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment for radiculopathy before considering surgery (Clinical Reference, Section 4 Summary Table). Mr. Watkins has completed 6 weeks of *intensive and failed* conservative treatment.
    *   **Functional Plateau:** The clinical standard definition of functional plateau (less than 10% improvement over 3 sessions OR therapist documentation of maximum benefit reached) is met by the treating therapist's documentation.
    *   **Surgical Indications for ACDF (CPT 22551):** The AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) identify Tier 2 as a "Reasonable Indication" for surgery when:
        *   (b) "Radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks conservative tx." Mr. Watkins has persistent significant symptoms after 6 weeks of intensive, failed conservative care.
        *   (c) "Significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care." Mr. Watkins, a professional violinist, has career-ending right hand weakness and fine motor deficits, a clear case of significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care.
    *   **Conclusion on Clinical Standard:** The requested ACDF is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for a patient with cervical radiculopathy presenting with persistent, severe functional impairment and objective neurological deficits despite an adequate and failed course of conservative treatment.

### Resolution of Conflicts

The HealthFirst denial is based on a misinterpretation and misapplication of its own criteria, specifically regarding the "6 weeks" requirement for symptoms versus physical therapy duration. Even if the plan's denial were based on a valid interpretation of its own criteria, it would be superseded by both California state law and generally accepted clinical standards.

*   **Conflict 1: Plan Criteria vs. State Law:** CIC § 10169.5 directly overrides the plan's ability to deny based on further PT requirements when a functional plateau has been reached with neurological compromise.
*   **Conflict 2: Plan Criteria vs. Clinical Standard:** The clinical standard supports surgical intervention for radiculopathy with persistent and severe functional impairment after 6 weeks of failed conservative treatment. HealthFirst's stated denial reason for "premature" surgery is not supported by the clinical evidence when a functional plateau has been reached and significant objective deficits persist.

### Disposition

**OVERTURN** the denial.

### Warrant

The prior authorization denial for CPT 22551 for Mr. Kevin Watkins is **OVERTURNED** based on the following:

1.  **Plan Criteria Met and Misapplied:** The member has met all conservative treatment requirements as stipulated in HealthFirst's Clinical Coverage Criteria CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2. He completed 6 weeks of intensive physical therapy, a trial of two pharmacotherapy agents for over 4 weeks each, and one interventional procedure. Furthermore, a functional plateau was clearly documented by his treating therapists at 6 weeks of PT, aligning with both the plan's own definition of functional plateau (CC-SPINE-2024, Section 2.2, NOTE) and clinical standards. The plan's denial reason, citing "only 6 weeks of symptoms" and a "plan criteria require at least 6 weeks of symptoms before surgical consideration," misstates and misapplies its own policy, which specifies 6 weeks of *physical therapy*, not symptom duration.
2.  **State Law Override:** California Insurance Code § 10169.5(a)(2) and (3) explicitly prohibits a health plan from conditioning approval of a surgical procedure on completion of physical therapy where the member has completed therapy and reached a functional plateau with inadequate resolution of symptoms, or where objective evidence of structural neurological compromise exists that PT cannot address. Both conditions are met in this case, making the plan's denial, as stated, contrary to California state law.
3.  **Consistency with Clinical Standard:** The requested ACDF is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for cervical radiculopathy. AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023) categorize radiculopathy with persistent symptoms after 6-12 weeks of conservative treatment and significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care as a "Reasonable Indication" (Tier 2) for surgery. Mr. Watkins has severe, career-ending functional impairment with objective neurological deficits that have not improved despite an adequate and failed course of conservative treatment.
4.  **Functional Urgency:** While his condition is radiculopathy and not myelopathy, his occupation as a professional violinist makes his right hand weakness an urgent functional impairment with significant impact on his livelihood, warranting timely surgical intervention after documented conservative treatment failure.

For these reasons, the requested procedure is medically necessary and should be approved.
