**HEALTHFIRST HEALTH PLAN — PRIOR AUTHORIZATION APPEAL DETERMINATION**

**Case ID:** PA-2024-E003
**Member:** George Harris, M-889922, Age 48
**Plan:** HealthFirst PPO Gold — Group Plan 77441, California
**Procedure:** CPT 22551 – Revision ACDF C5-C6
**Requesting Provider:** Dr. Karen Williams, MD
**Original Denial Date:** 2024-10-30
**Reviewer:** Appeals Reviewer, HealthFirst Medical Policy Department

**DISPOSITION: OVERTURN**

**WARRANT:**

The initial denial (MED-003) stated, "Revision criteria require documentation of hardware failure, pseudarthrosis, or new pathology at previously treated level. Documentation submitted does not confirm hardware failure." However, a comprehensive review of the submitted clinical record reveals a clear misapplication of HealthFirst's *own stated criteria* for revision surgery. The appeal is therefore overturned based on the following findings, adhering to the hierarchy of State law > Clinical standard > Plan criteria.

**1. Misapplication of Plan Criteria in Original Denial:**
The denial explicitly stated that "pseudarthrosis" is a criterion for revision surgery. The submitted CT scan (2024-09-15) unequivocally documents "incomplete bridging bone formation. Lucency around hardware suggesting pseudarthrosis. Findings consistent with failed fusion/pseudarthrosis." The denial's focus solely on the *absence of hardware failure* while ignoring the *documented presence of pseudarthrosis* represents an internal inconsistency and misinterpretation of the plan's stated requirements against the available clinical evidence. The documentation *does* confirm one of the criteria for revision (pseudarthrosis).

**2. Member Meets HealthFirst Plan Criteria (CC-SPINE-2024) for Medical Necessity:**

*   **2.1 DIAGNOSIS REQUIREMENT:** The member presents with "Return of right C6 symptoms: bicep weakness 4/5 right, bicep reflex diminished right, C6 dermatomal numbness. Worse than preoperative baseline." This constitutes objective neurological deficit consistent with cervical radiculopathy (2.1(a) or 2.1(c)), stemming from the confirmed pseudarthrosis at C5-C6. This criterion is met.
*   **2.2 CONSERVATIVE TREATMENT REQUIREMENT:**
    *   **(a) Physical Therapy:** The member completed 8 weeks of supervised PT, exceeding the plan's minimum 6-week requirement. The treating physician documented "No improvement — treating physician notes PT cannot address structural hardware/fusion failure," indicating a functional plateau. This criterion is met.
    *   **(b) Pharmacotherapy:** The member underwent trials of Naproxen (4 months) and Gabapentin (3 months), meeting the requirement for at least two agents for a minimum of 4 weeks each. This criterion is met.
    *   **(c) Interventional Treatment:** The member received an epidural injection, which provided "minimal improvement," meeting the requirement for one interventional procedure. This criterion is met.
    *   **Myelopathy Exception (2.2(A)):** This exception is not applicable as the member's symptoms are consistent with radiculopathy, not primary myelopathy. However, the standard conservative requirements were nonetheless satisfied.
*   **2.3 IMAGING REQUIREMENT:** The CT scan dated 2024-09-15 (within 6 months) definitively demonstrates "Lucency around hardware suggesting pseudarthrosis. Findings consistent with failed fusion/pseudarthrosis" at the proposed surgical level, consistent with the clinical presentation. This criterion is met.
*   **2.4 PHYSICIAN DOCUMENTATION:** The requesting physician's submission, along with the appeal basis, adequately documents the rationale for surgery, functional limitations, and conservative treatment failure. This criterion is met.

**3. Alignment with California Regulatory Framework (State Law):**

*   **CHSC § 1374.31(b) (Defective Denial):** The initial denial notice is procedurally flawed because it failed to accurately specify the clinical reason for the denial. By acknowledging pseudarthrosis as a valid criterion for revision yet denying on the basis of lacking hardware failure, it overlooked the documented evidence that *did* meet its own standard.
*   **CIC § 10169.5 (Physical Therapy as Prerequisite):** Although the member met the plan's PT duration, this section supports the treating physician's assessment that PT cannot address the underlying structural neurological compromise (pseudarthrosis causing nerve root compression). The member's documented "no improvement" after 8 weeks of PT also indicates a functional plateau, rendering further PT unlikely to benefit per § 10169.5(a)(2).
*   **CHSC § 1374.32(b) (IMR Standard):** This provision dictates that medically necessary services meeting generally accepted standards of medical practice SHALL be approved, regardless of more restrictive plan criteria. As detailed below, the service meets clinical standards.

**4. Alignment with Clinical Standard (AANS/CNS 2023, NASS 2020, AO Spine 2021):**

*   **Diagnosis:** The member's presentation of unilateral C6 weakness, diminished reflex, and numbness, combined with imaging showing pseudarthrosis, is consistent with cervical radiculopathy due to a structural pathology.
*   **Conservative Treatment Efficacy:** For structural issues like pseudarthrosis causing persistent radiculopathy with objective neurological deficits, the clinical literature (e.g., AANS/CNS Tier 2 indications) supports surgical intervention after an adequate trial of conservative management. The member's trial of 8 weeks of PT, 3-4 months of pharmacotherapy, and an epidural with documented "no improvement" and "minimal improvement" constitutes a failed and adequate course of conservative treatment.
*   **Surgical Indication:** Per AANS/CNS Joint Guidelines (2023), persistent radiculopathy with significant functional impairment not responding to conservative care (Tier 2 indication) is a reasonable indication for surgery. Pseudarthrosis causing persistent radiculopathy with objective neurological deficit after failed conservative treatment clearly falls within generally accepted standards for surgical correction.

**Conclusion:**

The initial denial was based on a flawed interpretation of the submitted clinical evidence against the plan's own criteria. The member's clinical presentation, objective imaging findings of pseudarthrosis, and documented failure of a comprehensive conservative treatment course all meet HealthFirst's medical necessity criteria for cervical spinal surgery. Furthermore, the requested revision ACDF is consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice for treating symptomatic pseudarthrosis causing persistent radiculopathy.

For these reasons, the prior authorization for CPT code 22551 for George Harris is **OVERTURNED**. The requested procedure is deemed medically necessary.
